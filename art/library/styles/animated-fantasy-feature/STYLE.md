# Animated Fantasy Feature

ID: `animated-fantasy-feature`

## Reusable style block

Polished hand-drawn fantasy feature-animation cartoon. Clean tapered
dark-colored contours with confident curves and selective internal lines;
smoothly sculpted believable anatomy and architecture with restrained appealing
exaggeration—slightly emphasized heads, hands, paws, expressions, and landmark
shapes, never chibi or childish. Three-to-five clean color and value shapes with
crisp cel boundaries and very limited soft painted occlusion only where forms
overlap. Broad simplified material shapes, readable silhouettes, and expressive
posing.

## Rendering axes

- **Brushwork:** Clean hand-drawn tapered contours and selective internal lines;
  no noisy painterly stroke field, mechanical vector uniformity, or comic
  hatching.
- **Edge handling:** Crisp cel boundaries and confident silhouettes dominate.
  Softness is limited to small overlap shadows and distant atmosphere.
- **Shape simplification:** Believable forms are smoothly sculpted into three
  to five clear color/value masses with appealing, restrained exaggeration.
- **Detail density:** High at faces and creature heads, medium on bodies and
  main buildings, sparse in atmospheric backgrounds.
- **Material rendering:** Stone, cloth, fur, scale, wood, and foliage are
  distinguished by broad designed shapes, selective internal lines, and a few
  highlights—not microtexture.

## Color block

Use vivid natural local colors with controlled complementary accents. Palette,
lighting, weather, and mood follow the subject rather than the anchor. No neon,
forced shared palette, or indiscriminate oversaturation.
