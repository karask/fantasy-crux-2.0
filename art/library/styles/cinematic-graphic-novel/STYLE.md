# Cinematic Graphic Novel

ID: `cinematic-graphic-novel`

## Reusable style block

Mature painted graphic novel. Semi-realistic believable anatomy and
architecture built from decisive brush-ink drawing; variable-width dark
sepia-indigo contours with dry-brush breaks; restrained directional
cross-hatching only in core shadows; four-to-six broad matte gouache-like color
and value shapes with a few translucent glaze transitions; crisp focal edges
with selectively lost or broken shadow edges; angular readable silhouette
simplification; materials rendered through designed shape rhythms rather than
photographic microtexture. Cinematic sequential-art finish, neither superhero
comic nor photorealistic concept painting.

## Rendering axes

- **Brushwork:** Brush-ink drawing establishes structure; matte gouache-like
  color shapes and a few translucent glazes sit beneath and across it.
- **Edge handling:** Variable dark contours are strongest at focal silhouettes,
  broken by dry-brush gaps, and selectively lost inside deep shadows.
  Directional cross-hatching is restricted to core-shadow emphasis.
- **Shape simplification:** Semi-real forms become angular, readable
  silhouettes and four to six designed value masses without losing anatomical
  or architectural credibility.
- **Detail density:** High at faces, hands, eyes, creature heads, and key
  material junctions; medium across bodies and main structures; sparse and
  atmospheric in backgrounds.
- **Material rendering:** Materials use designed mark and shape rhythms,
  restrained hatch direction, matte color, and selective highlights rather
  than photographic texture or evenly distributed linework.

## Color block

Use natural local colors, moderate saturation, warm-light/cool-shadow
separation, and at most one restrained focal accent family appropriate to the
subject. Do not force a shared palette or mood.
