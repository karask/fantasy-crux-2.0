# More Naturalistic

ID: `more-naturalistic`

## Reusable style block

Illustrated fantasy realism: believable anatomy and architecture with only
subtle cartoon-like shape design; four to six broad value shapes; matte opaque
gouache brushwork; natural soft modeling with crisp focal edges and selected
lost edges; restrained grouped material texture without photographic
microdetail. Mature hand-painted illustration, neither photorealistic nor
glossy.

## Rendering axes

- **Brushwork:** Broad, controlled gouache strokes build form in opaque matte
  layers. Strokes remain visible enough to feel handmade but do not dissolve
  the drawing.
- **Edge handling:** Crisp at focal overlaps and silhouette changes, soft across
  secondary modeling, and selectively lost into atmosphere or adjacent
  same-value shapes.
- **Shape simplification:** Credible anatomy and perspective are gently designed
  into four to six large value/color masses. Cartoon influence is limited to
  clearer silhouettes and plane changes.
- **Detail density:** Fine detail is reserved for faces or heads, hands or
  claws, and one narrative/material accent. Bodies and structures use grouped
  mid-frequency detail; backgrounds remain broad.
- **Material rendering:** Materials are separated by believable plane changes,
  restrained texture rhythms, and controlled highlights rather than pores,
  individual hairs, repeated scales, masonry inventory, or glossy effects.

## Color block

Use natural local colors with restrained-to-moderate saturation. Lighting,
weather, and mood belong to the content brief. Avoid a forced house palette,
cinematic color wash, neon accents, or global sepia/desaturation.
