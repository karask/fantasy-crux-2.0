# Vivid Inked Adventure Comic

ID: `inked-adventure-comic-vivid`

## Production status

This is the sole active production and website style. Generate all new artwork
only in this style unless the user explicitly requests an exception. The four
inactive systems retain one reference anchor and a detailed brief for future
recreation, but no published image sets.

## Reusable style block

Hand-inked Western fantasy adventure comic: expressive variable-weight brush
contours, simplified believable anatomy and architecture, bold silhouette
design, flat opaque colors, two or three hard-edged shadow shapes, sparse
dry-brush texture, and selective line detail at faces and focal materials.
Mature, handmade, and readable; no painterly blending and no glossy 3D.

## Rendering axes

- **Brushwork:** Decisive brush-ink contours swell through shadow and taper
  around lit or delicate edges. Interior marks are sparse, directional, and
  occasionally dry-brushed rather than uniformly clean.
- **Edge handling:** Outer silhouettes and important overlaps are crisp.
  Secondary interior boundaries are stated with a few broken lines or hard
  color joins. There are no airbrushed transitions.
- **Shape simplification:** Believable anatomy and perspective are reduced to
  chunky, readable silhouettes and two or three major light/shadow masses.
  Exaggeration supports clarity without becoming childish.
- **Detail density:** Highest at faces, hands, creature heads, and key material
  junctions; moderate across the main subject; sparse in backgrounds. Avoid
  evenly covering an image with small marks.
- **Material rendering:** Materials read through designed shape rhythms,
  selective highlights, and a few characteristic ink marks—not hair-by-hair,
  scale-by-scale, ring-by-ring, or stone-by-stone microdetail.

## Color block

Use vivid but disciplined natural color: clear saturated local colors, strong
warm/cool separation, and chromatic shadows. Selectively draw from jewel-toned
teal, cobalt, jade, vermilion, amber, violet, and warm cream according to the
actual subject and environment. Preserve believable material colors. No neon,
psychedelic rainbow, forced uniform palette, destructive oversaturation, or
washed-out sepia.
