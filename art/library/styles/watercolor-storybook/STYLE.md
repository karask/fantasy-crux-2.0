# Watercolor Storybook

ID: `watercolor-storybook`

## Reusable style block

Traditional fantasy storybook watercolor: believable but gently exaggerated
forms, transparent layered washes on cold-press paper, soft color blooms and
granulation, mostly soft edges with a few crisp inkless focal edges, large
uncluttered shapes, and minimal colored-pencil accents only at faces and key
materials. Mature and lyrical; no opaque digital painting, no cel shading, and
no glossy rendering.

## Rendering axes

- **Brushwork:** Transparent washes overlap visibly; pigment blooms,
  back-runs, and granulation are controlled parts of the image. Paper white
  supplies light.
- **Edge handling:** Most edges feather or soften into damp washes. A small
  number of dry, crisp, inkless edges clarify the focal subject. No enclosing
  contour system.
- **Shape simplification:** Forms stay believable but are gathered into large,
  gently exaggerated silhouettes and uncluttered color masses.
- **Detail density:** Detail concentrates at faces, eyes, hands, and one or two
  material accents. Secondary forms dissolve into wash rhythm and atmosphere.
- **Material rendering:** Material differences come from pigment behavior,
  wash layering, reserved paper, and minimal colored-pencil accents—not opaque
  texture painting or repeated microdetail.

## Color block

Use natural local colors with restrained-to-moderate saturation and luminous
transparent pigment. Reserve the strongest chroma for small focal accents. No
forced shared palette, artificial global desaturation, dense jewel-toned comic
color, or neon.
