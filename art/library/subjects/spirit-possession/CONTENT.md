## Content block

In an abandoned shrine, exhausted Mara kneels and presses both hands firmly against her own temples while concentrating through the anguish of an active possession. A translucent hostile spirit rises through and overlaps her upper back, shoulders, and head as a displaced spectral double, its ghostly arms spreading through empty air without solid contact. Ilyra confronts it from several steps away with her amber-tipped staff directed toward them. Mara's solid body and the separate incorporeal spirit remain clearly distinguishable.

## Pose and composition

Mara is the central low figure, bent slightly forward with both hands holding only the sides of her own head, eyes narrowed and jaw clenched in focused anguish. The spirit twists upward and backward from her silhouette with selectively broken, vaporous edges; neither Mara nor Ilyra touches or grasps it. Ilyra stands at a controlled distance with her staff held diagonally toward the possession. Opposed diagonals show an internal struggle rather than physical wrestling.
