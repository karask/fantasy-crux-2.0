## Content block

At a ruined fort, a massive wooden door stands fully closed and fixed within its stone arch. Mara walks toward it with her shield carried normally and sword sheathed. Corin crouches beside the path, urgently raises an open hand to stop her, and points near—but does not touch—a concealed tripwire across her intended next step. Mara checks her stride and looks back toward his warning. Ilyra stands behind Corin and holds her amber staff-light low near the subtle clue. Cracked masonry and weeds establish consequences without showing dice.

## Pose and composition

Mara halts naturally in mid-approach several steps from the closed door, separated from it by clear space; she does not touch, brace, lift, or carry it. Ilyra supports the warning from behind with her staff angled toward the clue. The hair-thin dark tripwire is mostly lost among cracks and weeds, with only one tiny amber glint.
