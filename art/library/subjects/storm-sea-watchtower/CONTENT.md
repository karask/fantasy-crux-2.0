# Storm sea watchtower

ID: `storm-sea-watchtower`

Website placement: GM Tools chapter

Alt text: A solitary stone watchtower stands above a stormy moonlit sea as rain
sweeps across the cliff path.

## Content block

A solitary squat medieval stone watchtower on a high sea cliff during heavy
wind-driven rain at night. A narrow slick stone stair path leads to one closed
wooden door. Bent coastal grass clings to wet black rock. Below, dark ocean
waves break violently against the cliff. Low storm clouds cross a faint moon.
No occupants.

## Composition and setting

Wide 3:2 establishing view. Keep the complete tower, cliff edge, stair path, and
a readable band of sea and sky safely framed. Use cold moonlit night values,
severe weather, and readable dark forms.

## Exclusions

No people, animals, creatures, ships, lightning bolt, lamps, glowing windows or
door, fire, smoke, magic, dramatic colored sky, text, logo, watermark, frame,
or border.
