# Drowned Causeway homepage illustration

ID: `home-hero`

Website placement: homepage opening image.

Alt text: Four adventurers cross a rain-soaked marsh toward a lonely candlelit tower at sunset while a creature lurks behind a dead tree.

## Content block

Exactly four medieval adventurers, all seen from behind, stand ankle-deep on a broken stone causeway through a flooded reed marsh in thin rain. Drowned posts, exposed roots, mud, black water, and pale marsh flowers lead to one narrow leaning stone tower on a low island with one amber window. Heavy indigo storm clouds part over a vermilion-gold sunset; night is close.

The party consists of a broad fighter in battered mail with shield and sword; a lean jack-of-all-trades with bow, rope, satchel, compact tools, and map case; a tall scholarly mage in a cobalt mantle with a crooked staff; and a shorter field mage in a vermilion weather cloak with a shuttered arcane lantern.

## Pose and composition

Wide landscape composition with all four figures and the tower inside the central seventy percent. All four adventurers face away from the viewer toward the tower. The causeway, reeds, roots, and reflections lead toward the destination. Keep the upper-left sky comparatively quiet for the live HTML heading.

## Exclusions

No front-facing figures, extra people, additional towers, fire, lightning, unrelated magical effects, baked-in text, logo, watermark, frame, border, or panel divisions.
