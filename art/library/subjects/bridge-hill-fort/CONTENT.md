## Content block

A rain-washed medieval frontier valley seen from a high path. An ancient narrow
stone bridge crosses a black rocky gorge toward a ruined hill-fort. Wind-bent
pines and wet heather occupy the foreground; low clouds break over distant
mountains. One ruined tower contains a faint unnatural amber glow.

## Composition and setting

Wide 3:2 landscape. The bridge is a strong leading diagonal from the high
foreground path to the distant fort. Keep the complete bridge, essential fort
silhouette, gorge, foreground vegetation, and mountain distance safely framed.
The weather is wet and unsettled, but the scene remains readable.
