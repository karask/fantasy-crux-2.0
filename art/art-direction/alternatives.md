# Alternative illustration style log

## Status

These directions were explored on 2026-07-23 and were **not selected**. Preserve them for future reference, special-edition discussion, or a formally recorded art-direction change. Do not mix them into normal production artwork by accident.

The accepted direction is [Grounded Painterly Fantasy](README.md).

## Reconsideration rule

Selecting an alternative for the overall game requires a new decision record that supersedes ADR-006. A deliberately different treatment for a bounded product—such as a special edition or in-world handout—should be documented as an explicit exception rather than silently changing the canonical style.
