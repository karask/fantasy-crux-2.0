# Homepage Hero Candidates — Vivid Inked Adventure Comic

These four landscape illustrations were created as candidates for the opening of
the main index page. Variant A is selected and integrated into the website.

## Shared content lock

- Exactly four medieval fantasy adventurers, seen from behind.
- Every adventurer is looking toward one creepy, isolated tower in the distance.
- The party consists of:
  - a broad fighter in battered mail with shield and sword;
  - a lean jack-of-all-trades with bow, rope, satchel, compact tools, and map case;
  - a tall scholarly mage in a cobalt mantle with a crooked staff;
  - a shorter field mage in a vermilion weather cloak with a shuttered arcane lantern.
- The time is sunset. Some light remains, but night is close.
- Wet, muddy, flooded, or windswept terrain makes the journey feel physical.
- No text is baked into the image.
- The party and tower remain within the central crop-safe area.
- Quieter sky or dark terrain is available for a small HTML heading.

The repeated “mage” in the original request is represented as two visually
different magic-users. If that was not intended, revise the fourth role before
selecting the canonical content.

## Style lock

Hand-inked Western fantasy adventure comic with expressive variable-weight black
brush contours, simplified believable anatomy, bold silhouette design, flat
opaque color shapes, two or three hard-edged shadow shapes, sparse dry-brush
texture, and selective hatching at focal materials. Mature handmade print-comic
finish; no painterly blending and no glossy 3D.

The current vivid-color default uses clear saturated local colors, strong
warm/cool separation, and chromatic shadows. Jewel-toned teal, cobalt, jade,
vermilion, amber, violet, and warm cream are selected according to the scene.
Avoid neon, psychedelic rainbow color, washed-out sepia, global desaturation,
and oversaturation that destroys the value hierarchy.

## Layout target

Every candidate is 1672 × 941 pixels, approximately 16:9. It is composed as a
full-bleed responsive hero, with the essential party and tower inside the
central 70 percent. This supports a wide desktop crop and a narrower mobile
crop. The smaller “Roll under. Read the situation. Live with the consequence.”
heading should remain live HTML, not image text.

## Variants

### A — Drowned Causeway

The party stands ankle-deep on a broken stone causeway crossing a flooded reed
marsh. Thin rain dimples black water. Drowned fence posts, exposed roots, boot
tracks, mud, and pale marsh flowers lead toward a narrow leaning stone tower on
a low island. One amber window answers the party’s lantern. A vermilion-gold
sunset slit burns beneath heavy indigo storm clouds.

### B — Cypress Fen

The party pauses on a warped timber boardwalk in a black cypress swamp. Hanging
moss, lily pads, half-submerged carved stones, rain rings, and teal mist surround
them. The tower is a cracked, tilted observatory standing in the fen, with a
broken copper dome and one turquoise slit of light. Amber-violet sunset haze
glows through fine rain.

### C — High Moor

The party reaches a windswept ridge above a peat bog. Rain-polished standing
stones, purple heather, reflective wagon ruts, and capes pulled hard by the wind
fill the foreground. Across the bog, a crooked black tower clings to a basalt
plug, reached by a threadlike switchback path. A fierce amber-vermilion sunset
breaks beneath torn indigo storm clouds.

### D — Drowned Village

The party stands under a broken chapel arch, looking across a flooded,
mud-choked village lane. Roofless cottages sink into reeds; stepping stones,
half-buried wagon wheels, cracked gargoyles, and rainwater frame the route. A
severe square tower rises alone from a wooded knoll beneath a jade, teal, amber,
and violet sunset break.

## Cross-style recreation prompt

After a variant is selected, preserve the shared content lock and that variant’s
specific environment, tower architecture, party silhouettes, role equipment,
camera position, composition, crop-safe placement, sunset direction, weather,
and narrative beat. Treat the selected image as a content and composition
reference only. Replace only the style and medium with the next approved style;
do not redesign the scene, add subjects, inherit unrelated mood, or copy content
from the new style reference.
