# Homepage Hero — Cross-Style Set

This folder recreates the selected Drowned Causeway homepage composition in
every Fantasy Crux style that has a complete 57-creature candidate set.

The website remains wired to the vivid Inked Adventure Comic version. The other
four images are art-only comparisons.

## Content and composition lock

- Exactly four medieval adventurers, all seen from behind.
- A broad fighter carries battered mail, a round shield, and a sword.
- A lean jack-of-all-trades carries a bow, rope, satchel, compact tools, and a
  map case.
- A tall scholarly mage wears a cobalt mantle and carries a crooked staff.
- A shorter field mage wears a vermilion weather cloak and carries a shuttered
  arcane lantern.
- The party stands ankle-deep on a broken stone causeway through a flooded reed
  marsh in thin rain.
- Drowned posts, roots, mud, black water, and pale marsh flowers lead toward one
  narrow leaning tower on a low island.
- One amber tower window and the party's lantern answer a vermilion-gold sunset
  under heavy indigo storm clouds.
- The essential party and tower remain inside the central crop-safe area, with
  quieter upper-left sky for the live homepage heading.
- No text is baked into the artwork.

## Prompt method

The selected vivid Inked Adventure Comic image was supplied as the sole content
and composition reference. Each established style anchor was supplied only for
its medium, brushwork, edge language, shape simplification, detail density, and
material rendering. Prompts explicitly prohibited inheriting subjects, objects,
layouts, palette, daylight, weather, or mood from style anchors.

Every generation used the common content lock above and one of these style
locks:

- **More Naturalistic:** Illustrated fantasy realism with believable anatomy,
  four to six broad value shapes, matte opaque gouache brushwork, natural soft
  modeling, crisp focal edges, selected lost edges, and restrained grouped
  material texture without photographic microdetail.
- **Watercolor Storybook:** Traditional mature fantasy watercolor with
  believable but gently exaggerated forms, transparent layered washes on
  cold-press paper, blooms and granulation, mostly soft edges, selected crisp
  inkless focal edges, large uncluttered shapes, and minimal colored-pencil
  accents.
- **Cinematic Graphic Novel:** Semi-realistic painted sequential art built from
  decisive brush-ink drawing, variable sepia-indigo contours with dry-brush
  breaks, restrained hatching, four to six matte gouache-like value shapes,
  occasional translucent glazes, crisp focal edges, and broken shadow edges.
- **Animated Fantasy Feature:** Polished hand-drawn feature-animation cartoon
  with tapered dark-colored contours, restrained appealing exaggeration, three
  to five clean cel-color shapes, crisp boundaries, broad simplified material
  shapes, readable silhouettes, and sparse atmospheric backgrounds.
- **Vivid Inked Adventure Comic:** The original selected and website-integrated
  image; expressive variable-weight black brush contours, flat opaque colors,
  hard-edged shadow shapes, selective hatching, and vivid disciplined local
  color.

All five files are 1672 × 941 pixels.
