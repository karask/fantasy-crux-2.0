# Cinematic Graphic Novel creature set

Art-only candidate set for all 57 Fantasy Crux creature entries. Nothing in this
directory is integrated into the website.

## Style lock

Mature painted graphic novel. Semi-realistic believable anatomy and architecture
built from decisive brush-ink drawing; variable-width dark sepia-indigo contours
with dry-brush breaks; restrained directional cross-hatching only in core shadows;
four-to-six broad matte gouache-like color and value shapes with a few translucent
glaze transitions; crisp focal edges with selectively lost or broken shadow edges;
angular readable silhouette simplification; materials rendered through designed
shape rhythms rather than photographic microtexture. High detail at faces, hands,
eyes, creature heads, and key material junctions; medium detail across bodies and
main structures; sparse atmospheric backgrounds. Cinematic sequential-art finish,
neither superhero comic nor photorealistic concept painting.

## Color lock

Natural local colors, moderate saturation, warm-light/cool-shadow separation, and
at most one restrained focal accent family. Palette and mood follow the subject;
they are not inherited from the style anchor.

## Reference policy

- Content reference: the matching image in `../more-naturalistic/`, used for
  creature identity, stats/ability interpretation, anatomy, pose, proportions,
  temperament, and environmental interaction.
- Style reference: `../../exploration-03/cinematic-graphic-novel/anchor.png`, used
  only for rendering language.
- `bear.png` and `dragon.png` reuse the already approved Graphic Novel exploration
  images. Every other asset was generated with the two-reference policy above.

## QA

Each asset was checked against the current creature profile and the naturalistic
content blueprint. Anatomy-sensitive creatures were inspected for their exact
limb plan; the roster was reviewed for varied actions and camera angles. Rejected
Skeleton, Werewolf, and Wyvern generations were replaced before acceptance.
