# Fantasy Crux art direction

## Status

**Accepted on 2026-07-23 and clarified on 2026-07-23.** The canonical
illustration style is **Grounded Painterly Fantasy**, selected from visual
study 7.

The canonical images are authoritative. Use this direction for new Fantasy
Crux illustrations unless a later recorded decision supersedes it. The
website's **Cold Iron** interface is not a mandatory illustration palette;
artwork may choose colors appropriate to its scene.

## Visual principles

### Grounded fantasy illustration

- Treat each subject as a believable figure or place inside a fantasy world.
- Use realistic proportions simplified into broad, confident painted forms.
- Favor natural, asymmetrical poses over theatrical display poses.
- Keep characters and creatures capable, dignified, weighty, and dangerous.

### Paint and detail

- Use matte, opaque traditional-painting texture with visible but restrained
  brushwork.
- Group hair, fur, scales, cloth, stone, and foliage into larger masses.
- Vary edges naturally: clearer at the focal point and softer elsewhere.
- Keep detail selective. Avoid promotional-card polish and microdetail spread
  evenly across the image.
- Do not use black contour lines, cel shading, graphic-novel hatching, glossy
  rendering, or airbrushed gradients.

### Color and light

- Build scene-specific palettes from muted earth colors, smoke blues, ochres,
  weathered reds, charcoal, and restrained natural accents.
- Use a clear warm/cool relationship without automatic teal-orange grading.
- Keep contrast low to moderate, with highlights reserved for the focal point.
- Fire, magic, and dramatic skies may provide narrative light, but must not
  turn the image into a glow-heavy spectacle.

### Characters, creatures, and equipment

- Faces, anatomy, equipment, and creature structure must remain credible.
- Armour and weapons should be practical, worn, and mechanically believable.
- Check hands, feet, claws, limbs, wings, heads, and joints explicitly before
  accepting generated artwork.
- Avoid glamour poses, sexualized armour, exaggerated sexual anatomy,
  weightless equipment, and cute or juvenile creature treatment.

### Environments and magic

- Paint environments as atmospheric places rather than detailed backdrops.
- Use loose depth layers, softened distance, and a few narrative landmarks.
- Weather and lighting should support the subject without overpowering it.
- Keep magical effects specific, restrained, and materially integrated.

## Reusable style block

Append and adapt this block when generating new artwork:

> **Style/medium:** Grounded Painterly Fantasy for an adult tabletop fantasy
> RPG, matching the canonical study-7 warrior and scenery references. Matte,
> opaque traditional-painting finish with visible restrained brushwork;
> believable proportions simplified into broad calm shape masses; selective
> detail; natural asymmetry; practical materials; softly varied edges; muted
> earth, smoke-blue, ochre, weathered-red, and charcoal palette; restrained
> warm/cool lighting; quiet narrative atmosphere; slightly rough handmade
> older-rulebook character-illustration quality.
>
> **Avoid:** epic promotional card-art spectacle; jewel-tone glow; cinematic
> rim lighting; symmetrical display poses; evenly distributed microdetail;
> individually rendered fur or scales; black outlines; cel shading;
> graphic-novel hatching; glossy 3D; anime or manga conventions; chibi or
> childish treatment; text, logo, watermark, or decorative border unless
> explicitly required.

Every asset prompt must still describe its subject, anatomy, composition,
lighting, palette, continuity requirements, and exclusions. This block defines
style; it is not a complete prompt.

## Continuity rules

- Reuse an approved image as an identity reference when depicting that subject
  again.
- State which input is the subject/composition anchor and which is only a style
  reference.
- Preserve established anatomy, facial features, proportions, equipment, and
  signature colors unless a deliberate redesign is requested.
- Compare each candidate directly with the canonical warrior reference before
  publication.
