# Fantasy Crux visual style studies

> **Decision:** Study 7, **Grounded Painterly Fantasy**, was accepted as the canonical Fantasy Crux illustration style on 2026-07-23. Production guidance now lives in the [art-direction guide](../art-direction/README.md). The remaining studies are preserved as alternatives.

These studies compare five rendering directions against the same two anchors:

- **Scenery:** a rain-washed frontier valley, ancient bridge, and ruined hill-fort.
- **Warrior:** Mara, a capable and battle-worn frontier warrior in practical late-medieval gear.

The content, composition, identifying details, and core palette remain as consistent as possible so that the rendering style is the variable being tested.

## 1. Grounded painterly realism

| Scenery                                                          | Warrior                                                          |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ![Grounded painterly scenery](01-grounded-painterly-scenery.png) | ![Grounded painterly warrior](01-grounded-painterly-warrior.png) |

Mature oil-and-gouache illustration with natural anatomy, tactile materials, restrained magic, and visible brushwork. This is the most balanced match for the game's combination of danger, capable heroes, and occasional epic reversal.

## 2. Cinematic dark-fantasy realism

| Scenery                                                  | Warrior                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| ![Cinematic dark scenery](02-cinematic-dark-scenery.png) | ![Cinematic dark warrior](02-cinematic-dark-warrior.png) |

More naturalistic and ominous, with lower-key values and stronger weather. It sells lethality and hardship well, but risks becoming too dark for small web placements and can make the game feel harsher than its Hero Point economy suggests.

## 3. Stylized graphic-novel realism

| Scenery                                                | Warrior                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| ![Graphic-novel scenery](03-graphic-novel-scenery.png) | ![Graphic-novel warrior](03-graphic-novel-warrior.png) |

Credible anatomy and materials rendered with expressive ink, dry-brush shapes, and restrained color washes. It is the clearest at small sizes and the easiest to make visually distinctive across a large body of rules artwork.

## 4. Luminous epic high fantasy

| Scenery                                                | Warrior                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| ![Luminous epic scenery](04-luminous-epic-scenery.png) | ![Luminous epic warrior](04-luminous-epic-warrior.png) |

Grand, romantic light and elevated heroic presence. Strong for covers, magic, and major reversals, but as an all-purpose direction it softens the grounded danger and preparation-first identity.

## 5. Engraved woodcut with oxide spot color

| Scenery                                                      | Warrior                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| ![Engraved woodcut scenery](05-engraved-woodcut-scenery.png) | ![Engraved woodcut warrior](05-engraved-woodcut-warrior.png) |

Carved linework, bone paper, iron ink, and a restrained oxide-red accent. This has the strongest fit with the existing **Cold Iron** graphic identity and reproduces well in print, though very dense scenes require careful simplification.

## 6. Painterly animated fantasy

| Scenery                                                           | Warrior                                                           |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ![Painterly animated scenery](06-painterly-animation-scenery.png) | ![Painterly animated warrior](06-painterly-animation-warrior.png) |

Hand-painted animation shapes, expressive simplification, and a freer petrol, moss, coral, and apricot palette. This retains much of the atmosphere and material richness of grounded painterly realism while making faces, silhouettes, and environments more immediately approachable.

## 7. Grounded painterly fantasy

| Scenery                                                                  | Warrior                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| ![Grounded painterly fantasy scenery](07-bold-adventure-cartoon-scenery.png) | ![Grounded painterly fantasy warrior](07-bold-adventure-cartoon-warrior.png) |

Matte traditional paint, believable forms, broad shape grouping, selective detail, muted earth and smoke colors, and restrained natural light. This is a grounded older-rulebook illustration language with a quiet narrative atmosphere.

## 8. Loose folk-storybook fantasy

| Scenery                                                  | Warrior                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| ![Folk-storybook scenery](08-folk-storybook-scenery.png) | ![Folk-storybook warrior](08-folk-storybook-warrior.png) |

Crooked ink, dry gouache, watercolor, visible paper grain, surprising plum and lavender color, and modestly exaggerated forms. It gives the game a warmer, more handmade and idiosyncratic personality, at the cost of some physical severity.

## Recommendation

Use **grounded painterly realism** as the canonical full-color direction, but adopt two disciplines demonstrated by the graphic-novel study: decisive silhouettes and restrained value groups. Keep the existing Cold Iron palette—bone, charcoal iron, slate, wet umber, oxide red, and dull brass—as the recurring color system.

If a more ownable, unmistakable identity matters more than broad market familiarity, choose the **engraved woodcut with oxide spot color** instead.

For a cartoon-forward direction without the Cold Iron constraint, the strongest candidate is **#7, bold adventure cartoon**. It sits naturally beside the previously favored #1 and #3: the material credibility of #1, the readable linework of #3, and substantially more stylized shape and color design.

## Reusable prompt specification

All images were produced with the built-in image-generation workflow.

### Shared environment anchor

> A rain-washed medieval frontier valley seen from a high path: an ancient narrow stone bridge crosses a black rocky gorge toward a ruined hill-fort, with wind-bent pines, wet heather, low clouds breaking over distant mountains, and a faint unnatural amber glow within one ruined tower. Wide landscape, bridge as a strong diagonal, scenery only, no text, logo, watermark, border, modern objects, or excessive fantasy clutter.

### Shared warrior anchor

> Full-body portrait of Mara, a capable battle-worn human frontier warrior in her early thirties: angular weathered face, olive skin, dark brown shoulder braid, gray eyes, thin scar through the left eyebrow, vigilant tired expression. Practical late-medieval riveted mail, battered leather brigandine, quilted sleeves, mud-stained trousers, worn boots, helmet at hip, sword point-down, shield on back, torn oxide-red half-cloak. One character only; no text, logo, watermark, border, magic effects, sexualized armor, or exaggerated anatomy.

### Style modifiers

1. **Grounded painterly realism:** hand-painted oil and gouache, tactile visible brushwork, naturalistic anatomy and materials, restrained detail, mature European tabletop RPG illustration.
2. **Cinematic dark-fantasy realism:** highly naturalistic concept art, lower-key storm values, subtle film grain, historical materials, cold damp atmosphere, no crushed blacks or glossy fantasy polish.
3. **Stylized graphic-novel realism:** varied ink contours, dry-brush black shapes, angular simplified planes, sparse cross-hatching, flat gouache color washes, warm paper texture, mature rather than cute.
4. **Luminous epic high fantasy:** grand hand-painted key art, sweeping brushwork, romantic depth, pearl-and-gold storm light, heroic scale, rich but disciplined detail, no glossy CGI or gaudy color.
5. **Engraved woodcut:** contemporary relief-print and copperplate language, carved black lines, dense directional hatching, bone-paper negative shapes, iron-gray wash, oxide-red spot color, maximum four apparent inks.
6. **Painterly animated fantasy:** premium hand-painted animation, designed silhouettes, softened gouache texture, selective ink, simplified expressive anatomy and geography, colorful petrol, moss, coral, and apricot palette.
7. **Bold adventure cartoon:** western fantasy animation and adventure-comic language, varied contours, chunky shapes, two-to-three tone cel shading, indigo, teal, jade, vermilion, and gold palette.
8. **Loose folk-storybook fantasy:** crooked ink, dry gouache and watercolor, playful asymmetry, patterned materials, visible paper grain, plum, turquoise, mustard, coral, lavender, and lemon-gold palette.
