# Creature thumbnail production

Fantasy Crux creature portraits use dedicated square thumbnails rather than
cropping larger illustrations at runtime.

## Delivery format

- Canvas: square (`1:1`)
- Website export: `640 × 640` WebP, quality 84
- Composition: one creature, centered safe area, readable at `96–132px`
- Background: restrained environment cue, subordinate to the silhouette
- No text, logo, watermark, border, montage, or multiple views

## House-style prompt

> Grounded Painterly Fantasy matching the canonical study-7 woman portrait.
> Matte, opaque traditional-painting finish; visible restrained brushwork;
> believable anatomy simplified into broad calm masses; selective detail;
> natural asymmetry; softly varied edges; practical material rendering; muted
> earth, smoke-blue, ochre, weathered-red, and charcoal palette; restrained
> warm/cool light; quiet narrative atmosphere; slightly rough handmade
> older-rulebook illustration quality.

Avoid epic promotional card art, jewel-tone glow, cinematic rim lighting,
symmetrical display poses, evenly spread microdetail, individually rendered fur
or scales, black outlines, cel shading, graphic-novel hatching, glossy 3D,
anime or manga, chibi or childish treatment, text, logos, watermarks, borders,
and cropped identifying features.

Each prompt must state the expected count of heads, eyes, limbs, wings, tails,
hands, feet, toes, or claws where those details are visible. Inspect anatomy
before publishing.

## Approved anchors

- `bear.webp` — massive brown bear on a mossy mountain rock; five claws on
  each visible front paw.
- `dragon.webp` — weathered russet dragon in a natural asymmetrical stance;
  four legs, two partly folded wings, one head, and one tail.
