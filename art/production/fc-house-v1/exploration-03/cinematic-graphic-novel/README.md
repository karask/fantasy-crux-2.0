# Cinematic Graphic Novel

This is an art-only Fantasy Crux style candidate. It is not wired into the website.

## Style lock

Mature painted graphic novel. Semi-realistic believable anatomy and architecture built from decisive brush-ink drawing; variable-width dark sepia-indigo contours with dry-brush breaks; restrained directional cross-hatching only in core shadows; four-to-six broad matte gouache-like color and value shapes with a few translucent glaze transitions; crisp focal edges with selectively lost or broken shadow edges; angular readable silhouette simplification; materials rendered through designed shape rhythms rather than photographic microtexture. High detail at faces, hands, eyes, creature heads, and key material junctions; medium detail across bodies and main buildings; sparse atmospheric backgrounds. Cinematic sequential-art finish, neither superhero comic nor photorealistic concept painting.

## Color lock

Natural local colors with a deep ink-value structure, moderate saturation, warm-light/cool-shadow separation, and at most one restrained focal accent family appropriate to each subject. The style is not permanently dark and does not force a shared palette or mood across unrelated scenes.

## What makes it different

- More realistic anatomy, material structure, and value modeling than Inked Adventure Comic.
- More explicit variable brush-ink and directional hatching than More Naturalistic.
- Matte, designed color planes rather than watercolor transparency or glossy digital paint.
- Selectively lost edges and dry-brush contour breaks prevent the work from reading as cel-shaded animation.
- Cinematic graphic-novel finish without speech balloons, captions, panels, halftone dots, or superhero visual language.

## Consistency method

`anchor.png` establishes the common style across the recurring female warrior, bear, dragon, and village in one 2x2 board.

The four independent images use two references:

1. The matching image in `../../calibration/revision-01/more-naturalistic/` supplies subject, anatomy, pose, camera, framing, equipment, scene layout, and narrative intent only.
2. `anchor.png` supplies brush-ink language, hatching logic, matte color construction, edge hierarchy, shape simplification, detail density, surface treatment, and material rendering only.

The transfer prompt explicitly excludes the anchor's objects, panel layout, composition, palette, lighting, weather, and mood.

## QA

- `warrior.png`: complete adult female warrior; coherent face, hands, limbs, sword, shield, and full-body framing.
- `bear.png`: four anatomically coherent legs; plausible paws and forepaw claw count; no humanlike features.
- `dragon.png`: exactly four legs and two wings; coherent attachments and safely framed extremities.
- `village.png`: environment-only; required houses, central well, watchtower, paths, and vegetation.
- All five PNG files are 1254 × 1254.
