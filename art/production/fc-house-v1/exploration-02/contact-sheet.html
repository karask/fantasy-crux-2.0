House style exploration 02

Five deliberately different illustration systems, tested against the same warrior, bear, dragon, and village subjects.

Each row used its own anchor as a style-only reference. The same more-graphic calibration images were used as content and composition-only references. Palette and mood were not inherited from either reference.

Full comparison

Five style rows, four shared subjects

1. Inked adventure comic

Varied confident ink contours, broad painted shapes, economical hatching, and crisp focal details designed to remain readable.

2. Watercolor storybook

Transparent layered washes, lightly drawn structure, softened transitions, paper texture, and selective crisp accents.

3. Hand-colored woodcut

Carved line rhythms, simplified block shapes, directional hatching, and flat hand-applied color with slight registration irregularity.

4. Illuminated chronicle

Flattened pictorial space, manuscript contours, patterned materials, selective modeled faces, and disciplined ornamental detail.

5. Painted miniature diorama

Sculpted forms, hand-painted physical surfaces, tactile material marks, staged depth, and the controlled detail of display miniatures.
