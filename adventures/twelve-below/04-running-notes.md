# Running notes

GM only. A one-page reference for the table, then a rules script for new players, spotlights, scaling, rules notes and convention advice.

## At the table

**The job:** find twelve miners and a pony, and bring them up before the Water reaches box 12.

| The Water                                  | Change        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------- |
| A scene below ground begins (2, 3, 4, 5)   | Fill 1        |
| Any other round trip of the cage           | Fill 1        |
| A long task                                | Fill 1        |
| Five real minutes of debate (once a scene) | Fill 1        |
| The gas explodes                           | Fill 2        |
| The pump is repaired                       | Erase 3, once |

| Box | The water                                                                                  |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 4   | Level 3 tunnel waist-deep: `-1P` to physical tests there.                                  |
| 8   | Level 3 tunnel flooded to the roof; the Chapel is an island.                               |
| 10  | Level 2 floods; the pump can no longer be repaired; the Chapel's platform goes under.      |
| 12  | **Dawn.** Level 1 floods and the cage stops. Anyone below Level 1 is lost unless escaping. |

**Long tasks:** bringing a casualty up the ladders by one level; hauling a casualty up the Chimney; retrying a failed repair; digging; resealing; resting.

**The cage** holds four; a stretcher or the pony counts as two. **Casualties, who cannot climb:** Brask, Della, Corrie (until woken) and Old Wat. Biscuit goes by cage or not at all.

**The bells:** 1 stop, 2 raise, 3 lower, 5 read the slate, 9 emergency. **Every lowering rings three, and three calls the troll** to wherever the cage stops, `1D6` rounds later. Not again in the same scene once it has been driven off. Never again once it is fed or walled in.

**The gas:** from 10 m along the Level 3 tunnel to the end of the Chapel. Any naked flame there: `3D6` to everyone in it, ignoring armour; prone; the crawl collapses; fill 2; the gas is gone.

**The troll:** HP 23, MWL 12, AP 3, Order 7, Move 23 m. Claw 40%, `1D6 + 2D6`, Heavy. Dodge 25%. Regenerates `1D6` at the start of its turn, even at 0 HP, unless burned since the start of its last turn. Fire ignores its hide. Heat sight.

**The pump:** shift the beam (Athletics, or Nerin's shove), then fit the valve (Mechanisms `-1P`; Ama gives `+1B`). Hulda does the rest.

## Rules in five minutes

Hand out the pregens and [How to play](03-players-and-handouts.md#how-to-play). Then say this, or something like it, and stop as soon as the players are nodding:

1. **"Roll under your skill on D100."** Have everyone roll Perception at once. Point out the Criticals and the Fumbles.
2. **"Bonus and Penalty dice change your tens die."** Have someone roll with `+1B`, keeping the better of two tens dice with the same units die.
3. **"Opposed tests: better result wins; if you both succeed, higher roll wins."**
4. **"You have two Hero Points."** Show the four uses. Tell them that tonight they will want them.
5. **"In a fight, you get one action, one move and one reaction each round."** Show the Major Wound line on a sheet. Say: _"Hits hurt in this game. The thing you will meet tonight hits very hard."_
6. **"Underground, your lamp lights three metres."** That is all they need. Explain climbing, swimming and breath when they come up.

Everything else, explain when it happens. Start Scene 1 by the fifteen-minute mark.

## Rules notes

### Darkness and heat sight

A safety lamp lights 3 m, like a lantern. From 3 m to 9 m is partial darkness: `-1P` to sight-based Perception and attacks. From 9 m to 15 m is darkness: `-2P` and half Movement. Beyond that, with no other light, it is pitch black. Each station has one lamp.

Seris treats partial darkness as light, and darkness as partial darkness. **Blind Sight (heat)** lets Pebb, Dori, Hulda, Ama, Ketter, Sprat and the troll see warm bodies in any darkness, through nothing solid. A body under cold mud or soaked sacking counts as heavily insulated and hides from it. That ruling is this adventure's, and the players can use it.

### Swimming, breath and drowning

Swim at half Movement. The stope and the flooded tunnels are rough water, so test Athletics; armour and heavy loads each add `-1P`. A failed test means no progress that round.

Anyone can hold their breath for as many rounds as their CON. After that, test Resilience each round, with a cumulative `-1P` up to `-3P`. On the first failure the swimmer breathes water and takes `2D6` damage at once, ignoring armour, and again at the end of every round until they reach air. Once they reach air, a failed Resilience test means they can only cough and gasp for `1D4` rounds.

The 60 m swim through the flooded Level 3 tunnel takes about eight rounds at half Movement. That is possible for almost everyone, and terrifying for all. A guide rope gives `+1B`.

### Climbing and falling

Climb at a quarter of Movement. An Athletics success doubles that when speed matters. A fall deals `1D6` damage for each full 3 m, ignoring armour, and leaves the faller prone. The landings every 6 m in the shaft mean that a slip deals `2D6`. A conscious faller who can control the landing tests Athletics: success removes `1D6` and they land on their feet.

### Fatigue

After a hard climb, swim or fight, call for a Resilience test. Failure means **Fatigued**: `-1P` on every test, three-quarters Movement, Combat Order -3. Another hard effort while Fatigued is tested at `-1P`. Failure then means **Exhausted**: `-2P`, half Movement, Combat Order -6. A fumbled test knocks the character out for `3D6` minutes. Nobody recovers from Fatigue tonight.

### Treating the miners

- **Battlefield treatment:** a full round beside a patient who stays still, and a Healing test. Success stops Bleeding and Dying, but restores no HP.
- **After a fight:** one Healing attempt per patient before they rest restores `1D4` HP, but only to someone who is not Wounded.
- **Brask, Della and Hulda are Wounded.** Ordinary Healing gives them no HP; Surgery or Tamsin's magic can. A splint and a shoulder are enough to move them.
- **Corrie** wakes on any successful Healing test.
- Each Healing attempt uses one Healing Kit use, success or not. Without a kit, the test is at `-2P`.

### Fire and gas

Fire damage applies every round of exposure. A torch used as a weapon attacks as a club at `-1P` and deals `1D4` fire damage. Lamp oil spilled and lit burns as a small fire, `1D6` a round, for a minute. Metal armour does not help against fire, and in this adventure neither does the troll's hide.

**The firedamp explosion** is this adventure's own hazard. It is meant to be avoidable, so warn the players once, clearly. Safety lamps are safe. Any other naked flame is not, and that includes an ordinary lantern opened or smashed. Shaping makes no flame unless it is fire magic, so Nerin's force bolts are safe in the gas. So are arrows.

### The troll in rules terms

- **Regeneration.** At the start of each of its turns, even at 0 HP, it recovers `1D6` HP, unless it has taken fire damage since the start of its previous turn. Regeneration also stops Bleeding and Dying. If its HP rises above 0, it wakes and may act that turn.
- **Killing it.** At 0 HP it is Dying like anyone else, and dies at the end of the third round. Regeneration will save it unless something burns it every single round.
- **Its claw** deals `1D6 + 2D6`, around 10 damage. Most pregens have 10 to 15 HP and a Major Wound Level of 5 to 8, so one claw usually means a Major Wound. Remind the players about Hero Points before the first fight.
- **Parrying the claw.** It counts as Heavy. A Heavy or larger shield, such as Mara's large shield, blocks all of it on a winning Parry. A Medium weapon or small shield blocks half. A Light weapon blocks nothing.
- **Holding someone under.** It seizes with Unarmed Combat 40%; the target can spend its Reaction to Dodge or Parry. While it holds someone, neither can take a Movement Action, and both take `-1P` on every test. Underwater, the victim is holding their breath. To escape, the victim spends a Combat Action and wins an opposed Unarmed Combat test. Friends beside them can give `+1B` by helping. **In this adventure, burning the troll makes it let go at once.**

### Nerin in the mine

- **Measured shove** (M3) moves one person-sized subject 3 m: the cage timber, the pump beam, a falling timber, or a miner pulled out of the troll's reach. An unwilling subject resists with Resilience. The troll is far bigger than a person, so moving it needs extra Reach and overreaches: M4 at `-1P`.
- **Hold to the wall** (M3) lets one willing climber climb bare rock for ten minutes, instead of the Chimney's rotten rungs.
- **Force bolts** (M3) deal `2D6`, and the troll's hide stops 3 of it. They do not stop its regeneration.

## At the convention

**Content.** Drowning, confined spaces, darkness, serious injury, a child in danger, and animals in danger. A mule dies offscreen before the company arrives; a goat, or worse, may be fed to the troll. Mention these in your pitch and use whatever safety tools your table prefers. The goat can always be fed offscreen.

**Props make this adventure.**

- **A real bell.** A hand-bell, or a spoon and a glass. Ring every signal for real. By the second hour, the players will flinch at three.
- **The Water track** in the middle of the table. Cross the boxes off loudly.
- **Twelve tokens and a pony** on the map. Count them out loud at the end.

**Pace.** Begin the boxed text within fifteen minutes of sitting down. When the table debates for five minutes, fill a box and say why. When the table stalls, ring the bell from above: Hulda wants to know what is happening.

**Quiet players.** Give them the bell rope. Give them Sprat to look after. Ask them what their character hears in the dark.

**Ending on time.** Keep fifteen minutes for Dawn. The count of tokens and Linnet's three bells at the Goat Grate are the ending; do not cut them for one more fight.

## Setting names used

Everything a player needs is in the brief. The setting supplies only names:

- **Thornwick** and **the Thornwick Deep** are new, and can sit in any hill country.
- **The Ironspines** are the northern mountains of the Crownless Realms, home to dwarves and goblins.
- **The Hammer** is one of the Seven Lamps, the patron of honest work, and the miners' favourite.
- **SP** are silver pieces.
- **The Last Wagon Company** is the pregens' own company, with its promise that nobody gets left behind.
