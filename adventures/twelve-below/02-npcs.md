# People of Twelve Below

GM reference for **Twelve Below**. Named NPCs use the compact notation of [Minor NPCs](../../src/content/rules/gm-tools/minor-npcs.md): **Name — people and role Rating (equipment; abilities)**, followed by their skills grouped by percentage. The troll uses its published profile, with this adventure's notes. Encounter balance has not been playtested.

Most of these people never need a roll. They need a name, a voice and a reason to be saved.

## At the pithead

### Hulda Brannoch

_The captain._ A broad dwarf in her fifties with iron-grey braids pinned up under a leather cap. Her right arm is splinted against her chest. She runs the winding engine one-handed, swearing at it in a steady, affectionate stream. She has buried miners before and will not do it tonight if she can help it.

**Dwarf Mine Captain 70 (Hammer as Club `1D6`; Blind Sight (heat), Earth Sense).**

| Skill % | Skills                              |
| ------- | ----------------------------------- |
| 70      | Mechanisms; Lore (Mining).          |
| 60      | Influence; Perception; Persistence. |
| 50      | Athletics; Resilience; Healing.     |
| 40      | Close Combat; Dodge; Trade.         |

**Wants:** all twelve up, and the truth told afterwards. She does not know about the goats.

**Does:** winds the cage, reads the slate, sends down whatever is asked for, and repeats every signal back down the wire so the company knows she heard. She cannot go below and will not leave the engine.

**Play her:** short sentences and exact numbers. _"Four in the cage. Not five. I don't care who's asking."_

### Corvin Dask

_The owner._ A soft-handed man of forty-five in a good coat with mud to the knees, grey-faced, turning his signet ring. He inherited a mine, its debts and a story he thought was a joke. He is a coward and a liar, and he loves his daughter more than anything in the world.

**Human Mine Owner 60 (no weapon).**

| Skill % | Skills                       |
| ------- | ---------------------------- |
| 60      | Deception; Influence; Trade. |
| 50      | Lore (Mining); Persistence.  |
| 40      | Perception; Riding.          |

**Wants:** Linnet back alive. After that, for nobody to read the ledger.

**Hides:** the order, the cancelled goats, and the brass plate he unscrewed from the lamp-room wall this evening, after the alarm. If the company comes back up talking about a monster, he goes grey and slips away to his office to burn the goat-dealer's bill. The plate will not burn.

**Arc:** liar, then coward, then a father who might finally do something brave. If the Water reaches box 10 with Linnet below, he goes down himself (see [Scene 5](01-adventure.md#scene-5-the-rising)).

**Play him:** promises and money. _"Name your price. Just bring her up."_

### Others at the pithead

- **Mother Tuck**, Ama and Ketter's mother, a tiny goblin who stokes Hulda's boiler and asks about her children every time the cage comes up.
- **Mags Barrow**, Rolf's wife, who organises blankets, soup and fifty frightened neighbours.
- **The crowd**: families, off-shift miners, the smith with a torch. They carry stretchers and pull ropes, but none of them will go below.

## Level 1: the Upper Drift

### Brask Ironjaw

_The joker._ A huge orc miner of thirty with a jaw like a shovel, pinned against the wall where a prop fell on his leg. He is in great pain and making jokes about it, mostly bad ones.

**Orc Miner 50 (Pick as Club `1D6`; Adrenaline Surge).** Athletics 60%; Resilience 60%; Close Combat 50%; Lore (Mining) 50%.

**Needs:** two people or the cage. Splinting the leg (Healing) lets him lean on someone, but he still cannot climb.

**Play him:** _"Leave me the lamp and a good story. No? Fine. Lift with your legs."_

### Hobb Lane

_The witness._ Sixteen, thin, and hiding in an empty ore tub with his knees up to his chin. He was leading Clover the mule when the troll took her, and he has not stopped shaking.

**Human Miner 30 (no weapon).** Persistence 30%; Athletics 40%; Deception 40% (hiding).

**Knows:** what it looked like. _"Big as a cart. Grey. It took her like a sack and threw her down the shaft. Then it went down after her."_

**Arc:** terrified, then brave. Once someone has been kind to him, he will do one brave thing later: run a message, hold a rope, or go back for something.

## Level 2: the stope

### Ama Tuck

_The fitter._ A sharp-faced goblin of twenty-two in a leather apron full of tools, furious at the pump for breaking and at her brother for pretending he can swim.

**Goblin Pump Fitter's Mate 60 (Spanner as Club `1D6`; Blind Sight (heat)).** Mechanisms 60%; Perception 50%; Athletics 50%; Lore (Mining) 40%.

**Knows:** the pump. She can shout instructions across the water and give the repair `+1B`, or do it herself once she is across.

### Ketter Tuck

_The brother._ Eighteen, all bravado, cannot swim a stroke and keeps saying he can.

**Goblin Miner 30 (Pick as Club `1D6`; Blind Sight (heat)).** Athletics 20%; Perception 40%.

**Needs:** a rope, the tub, or someone to tow him.

### Rolf Barrow

_The big man._ Forty, soaked, shivering and silent. He has been holding the pony's head for two hours.

**Human Miner 50 (Pick as Club `1D6`).** Athletics 60%; Resilience 50%; Close Combat 40%.

**Will:** hand Biscuit to someone he trusts, then help anyone else across.

### Biscuit

_The pony._ A small, round, dappled pit pony with a white blaze and very strong opinions about water. She bites people she does not know.

**Biscuit** does not fight. She swims if someone she trusts leads her, and counts as two in the cage. Calming her takes a Riding or Natural Lore test, or Rolf. A 7-damage hit is a Major Wound. Players will risk their lives for her. Let them.

## Level 3: the Chapel

### Linnet Dask

_The daughter._ Nineteen, ink-stained, in her father's old overseer's coat with the sleeves rolled up. She has kept seven people calm for hours in the dark, and she is about to stop being calm.

**Human Apprentice Overseer 50 (no weapon).** Influence 50%; Persistence 60%; Lore (Mining) 50%; Perception 40%; Athletics 40%.

**Carries:** the shift ledger, in oilcloth inside her coat ([Handout 3](03-players-and-handouts.md#handout-3-the-shift-ledger)).

**Arc:** loyal daughter, then betrayed daughter, then the person who will run Thornwick properly. She argued against the order and went down anyway. Learning about the goats breaks something in her, and the pact at dawn mends it.

**Play her:** brisk and precise, until Wat tells his story. Then very quiet.

### Old Wat Fenner

_The memory._ Sixty-four, coughing, with forty-nine years down this hole. He is the last man who remembers the goats, and he bought one himself, because nobody else would.

**Human Old Miner 60 (no weapon).** Lore (Mining) 70%; Persistence 60%; Perception 40%.

**Knows:** the whole story ([Scene 4](01-adventure.md#the-truth)), the Chimney, and every timber in the Deep.

**Needs:** a shoulder on every rung, or the cage.

**Play him:** slow, certain, and a little proud that he was right. _"Tale for children. Hah."_

### Tolly Fenner

_The grandson._ Twenty-three, big-eared, holding his grandfather's arm. He will do anything for Wat, and nothing that takes him away from Wat.

**Human Miner 40 (Pick as Club `1D6`).** Athletics 50%; Close Combat 40%.

### Sprat

_The door-boy._ A twelve-year-old goblin with huge ears and a cap three sizes too big. He opens and shuts the air doors in the tunnels. With his heat sight he sees the troll before anyone else, and he will not stop talking about it.

**Goblin Door-boy 40 (no weapon; Blind Sight (heat)).** Perception 50%; Deception 50% (hiding); Athletics 40%.

**Gives:** warning. Whenever the troll is near, Sprat sees it first: _"It's coming. Left side. It's warm."_ Use him to telegraph the troll.

**Arc:** frightened, then useful, then reckless. In Scene 5 he may run back for Biscuit.

### Della Marsh, Corrie Stone and Nan Gorrow

- **Della Marsh**, thirty-five, a timberer with a broken arm that she splinted herself. Wounded, sour and sensible. _"Don't you dare drop me."_ Human Miner 50; Athletics 50%; Healing 40%.
- **Corrie Stone**, twenty-eight, unconscious from the bad air. A Healing test wakes her, and then she can walk, Fatigued. Until then, she needs a stretcher. Human Miner 40.
- **Nan Gorrow**, forty, an orc hewer who will carry anyone anywhere and has been singing quietly all night to keep the others calm. Orc Miner 50 (Pick as Club `1D6`; Adrenaline Surge); Athletics 60%; Resilience 60%; Close Combat 50%.

## The troll

_Old Grey._ Huge, stooped and grey-green, with moss and stone dust in its long hair, and eyes milky with age. Old burn scars cross its shoulders: Aldo Dask's torches, sixty years ago, and the only wounds on it that never healed. It smells of wet animal and old caves. It is not cruel. It is hungry.

**Use the published [Troll](../../src/content/rules/creatures/troll.md) unchanged:** HP 23, MWL 12, AP 3 hide, DM `+2D6`, Order 7, Move 23 m. Claw: Unarmed Combat 40%, `1D6 + 2D6`, Heavy. Club: Close Combat 40%, `1D6 + 2D6`, Light (it picks up a pit prop). Dodge 25%, Persistence 25%, Resilience 60%, Athletics 20%, Perception 20%. **Regeneration** and **Blind Sight (heat)** as published.

### The troll in this adventure

- **Water:** it holds its breath for 20 rounds (its CON). It knows every flooded tunnel and swims them at half Movement, about 11 m a round, without a test.
- **Climbing:** it climbs the shaft ladders at a quarter Movement, about 6 m a round. It cannot fit in the cage, up the Chimney, or through the crawl until it tears it apart.
- **Fire:** its hide does not insulate against fire, so fire damage ignores its 3 AP. A torch deals `1D4`; burning oil `1D6` a round for a minute, until it dives into water.
- **Three bells:** see [the bells](01-adventure.md#the-bells).
- **Morale:** in Scene 3 it withdraws at half HP (11) or after any fire. Later it is hungrier: it fights until burned or down to 5 HP, then flees into the water. Once fed, it carries the food home and does not come back tonight.
- **Threat:** Threat Intensity 4. One hit can drop a pregen. Remind the players about Hero Points before the first fight.
