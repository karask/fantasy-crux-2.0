The existing [Lamp adventure](../the-lamp-that-went-dark/README.md) and [Hollow Ward adventure](../the-patients-of-hollow-ward/README.md) supply the detailed local scenes, procedures, and profiles used by A01 and A03. Their existing endings and actual table outcomes take precedence over an assumed clean campaign opening. The [horse-fair adventure](../the-dragon-at-the-horse-fair/README.md) and [Last Wagon Company background](../../pregenerated-characters/00-company-background.md) are optional continuity, not prerequisites.

## Campaign inventions

This folder adds Master Aveline Sere, Marshal Rovan Kest, their personal histories, Nema Vale, Edrin Pell, other explicitly local supporting people, the House of Tomorrow, and the organised custody scheme. It develops the Ash Chancellor and Varkesh without declaring their additions universal setting canon. It names particular monsters and sites while retaining published creature capabilities except where an explicit local variant is stated.

The command couplers, their separability from local service, their specific M8 operator links, the master-only Hall interface, the six-hour sequence, the brake, the incomplete-acknowledgement safety rule, and the proposed failed-override history are **campaign truths**. They turn the proposal’s open distributed-ritual possibility into a runnable scenario. They do not settle the emperor’s fate, the truth of the gods, or the final ontology of Shaping.

The exact campaign days, seasonal coincidence, local site distances, ferry subsidy, practical relay delivery rate, loan arrears, commissions, force strengths, and NPC skill ratings are play assumptions. The running chapter labels them. The published gazetteer omits the proposal’s exact White Span length; this campaign uses the route to Gull’s Rest without reinstating a precise bridge measurement.

## Mechanical decisions

- Use normal starting characters and normal HP. Optional Epic Characters rules are not silently applied to PCs or villains.
- Use current armour ENC for derived Order and current weapon damage. The old Minor NPCs shortsword example is not used as a weapon-table override.
- Shaping100 is a Master; skill80 is an Expert. Maximum Intensity and Safe Magnitude remain distinct from total Magnitude. High skill does not grant infinite PP, actions, or automatic success.
- Active Magnitude uses permanent INT. The vampire profile retains an older reference to substituting Blood Reserve for POW in an active limit; this campaign explicitly follows the current general INT rule for the Chancellor, limit13, while Reserve still funds costs and commitments.
- Existing supernatural infrastructure is operated or physically disconnected through its established mechanisms. Recreating an enchantment still requires a fully costed build, appropriate capacity or commitment, and any mythic price. The campaign does not award seven portable magic batteries.
- Body destruction and final defeat differ only where actual creature abilities say so: vampire Death Mist and the hag’s anchored reformation are disclosed in usable clues.
- Celand’s anchor destruction/banishment consequence and the dragon’s Reach2 size classification are explicit local rulings. They are not additions to the published creature catalogue.
- Advances use the published 3–5 IP per adventure guidance. Twenty-five sessions do not require a guaranteed particular final skill score.

## Editorial review record

The delivery review checks local file and heading links; adventure numbering; clock names and dates; sample session sums; villain derived statistics and prepared Shaping arithmetic; supporting talent prerequisites; redundancy of critical conclusions; and whether each adventure provides independent entry, meaningful choices, late-state consequences, and persistent rewards.

**Delivery results:** all 12 campaign Markdown files pass targeted Prettier and markdownlint checks. All 74 local links and their four heading fragments resolve. A01–A15 occur once each as adventure headings; all three sample pacing budgets sum to 25 sessions. Recalculated HP, MWL, and Order match Sere, Kest, Nema, Jessa, and the Chancellor. Editorial review corrected a supporting guard’s shield strength requirement, made Sere’s return journey explicit, recorded Jessa’s original Veiled result, and distinguished the Hall’s mechanical reset interval from magical Duration.

The repository-wide `npm run check` was also attempted. It stopped at the existing formatting gate, reporting 152 files outside this campaign; subsequent build and browser stages were not reached. Those unrelated files were not reformatted. The campaign-specific checks above passed independently.

No website routes, canonical rules, existing adventures, or character sheets are changed. This is a Markdown campaign packet with linked source adventures, not a new published site or an illustrated PDF.

**Playtest status:** authored and reviewed, not table-playtested. Creature and boss lethality remains real. The GM should assess the actual party before choosing an optional direct confrontation, especially with Varkesh, and disclose visible danger and credible alternatives as written.
