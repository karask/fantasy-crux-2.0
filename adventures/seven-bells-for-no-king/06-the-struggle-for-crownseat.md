## A12: Supper with the Ash Chancellor

**Place:** a discreet townhouse above the Gate of Names’ service crypt, Crownseat. **Play:** 2 sessions. **On site:** one afternoon and night, with optional extra scouting. **Pressure:** T08; after Day 56 the house enjoys the protection of appointed custodians.

**Entrances:** Neris arranges an introduction; a missing clerk’s family asks for help; an old signature from A03/A10 matches the host’s modern seal; or the Chancellor invites the company to discuss Sere. The invitation is sincere: it wants the company to weaken its rival.

**Situation:** the Chancellor hosts a dinner to assemble clients among the new custodians. Three appointments are compromised through debt and blackmail, not enchanted obedience. Below the house nine captive clerks supply blood and signatures. Steward **Merren Ash** (Deception70, Influence60, Persistence55) arranged the dragon’s false collateral schedule and would surrender it in exchange for protection from the vampire.

**House connections:** front vestibule → dining room → study → roof stair. The kitchen joins the dining room to the pantry stair, which descends to the cell corridor. The study has a concealed but physically discoverable service door to the Gate crypt. Roof vents connect the study and stairwell to outside. Record their actual open/closed state.

1. **The dinner:** three minor officials, Merren, a nervous musician, and the vampire attend by default. A named faction leader appears only if travel and prior arrangements permit it. The Chancellor serves excellent food it does not eat. It gives the party a genuine Office payment list that leads to A13, then proposes a mutual protection agreement. It will not accept inspection of its cellar as an innocent after-dinner tour.
2. **The study:** the forged dragon schedule, appointment bribes, and two stolen Gate registers. A third register is already below. The vampire’s early portraits always omit the Rose Lamp, while a trial note says its presentation injured the Chancellor. These are explicit weakness clues. The roof maintenance plan shows which shutters admit natural sunrise into the study and central stair.
3. **The kitchen and cells:** the cook knows that “special guests” never leave by the front door. A humane appeal or clear evidence wins her help; threatening her children does not create a better shortcut. Two Cabinet-guard-profile private guards watch the cells. They believe the captives are debt prisoners, but the feeding wounds make that excuse untenable. A key at Merren’s belt, an ordinary lock, or the narrow laundry hatch gives entry. The nine captives can walk except for two who need carrying and Surgery afterward.
4. **Gate service crypt:** stolen registers are linked anchors of the local denial workings, not magical voting ballots. Missing volumes have let marked imperial corpses pass. Returning them to labelled slots and having a burial clerk reconcile duplicate entries restores the existing register linkage. It takes two hours and gives the local ward its intended valid list again; it does not conjure a new empire-wide Ward. Two [skeletons](../../src/content/rules/creatures/skeleton.md) guard the service landing under the command “admit register bearers; stop removal without a countersigned receipt.” A real register grants passage; copying the visible receipt allows lawful removal for repair. Use their full profiles if fought.

**A playable hunt:** establish sunrise time—use 06:00 for this local campaign morning. The vampire sits down at 22:00. Exposing the cells, securing exits, and confronting it are independent operations. It does not know the party is a threat until it sees intrusion or receives a report. If alerted, it takes its sword from the study and spends Reserve on protection only if time permits. It tries to reach the crypt, whose shutters admit no sun, or escape through an open roof vent. Sealing every observed vent and opening the roof shutters require real positions and actions. Use Mist Form and Death Mist exactly as explained in [Adversaries](03-adversaries.md#the-ash-chancellor); a drop to 0 HP alone may not finish it.

**Competing objectives:** the Chancellor attempts to leave with a register while Merren flees with the collateral schedule and frightened captives need help. The party can recruit the kitchen staff, block the service passage beforehand, or accept one escape. Do not invent a second escape route after a successful reconnaissance.

**Outcomes:** kill or capture the vampire; expose it and drive it from office; accept its evidence while refusing alliance; or make a dangerous pact it will interpret literally. Destroying it ends T08’s predatory control, but blackmailed officials still need protection from other opportunists. If it escapes, locate its next refuge through its actual contacts and need for blood; no automatic midnight return to full strength.

**Clues and rewards:** Sere’s payment ledger opens A13; the crypt has an independent Hall plan for A15; the false debt schedule weakens Varkesh’s claim at A11. A major commission, rescued administrative witnesses, and a secured Gate are possible gains. The vampire’s private treasure uses its published plunder rating; captive wages and public registers are identified property.

## A13: The House of Tomorrow

**Place:** an Office workshop, school, and relief depot in Crownseat. **Play:** 2 sessions. **On site:** one day’s investigation and one operation. **Pressure:** Sere moves the complete Concordance records to the Hall on Day 77 if still following her original plan.

**Entrances:** Rusk’s addresses from A02/A04; a student asks the company to find a missing teacher; the Chancellor provides a payment ledger; or Sere invites the party to review an impressive public-works model. An invitation permits ordinary inspection, not the basement’s secret records.

**Situation:** many workers sincerely believe they are saving the realms. The school teaches surveying to poor apprentices. Its relief depot supplies real grain. Sere uses those achievements to recruit loyal staff and conceal a programme of engineered emergencies. Her assistant Nema suspects that one “evacuation exercise” was real and concealed civilian deaths.

**Site:** public yard → lecture hall → model gallery → design office. The yard also opens to the relief store and workshop floor. A service stair from the design office reaches the **restricted archive** and **coupler test chamber**; an ordinary coal delivery passage reaches the archive corridor from outside.

1. **Lecture hall:** Sere or Nema demonstrates the difference between local operation and command coupling with working scale machinery. Asking a direct technical question can obtain the key insight without burglary. Sere claims coupling merely coordinates repairs. A successful careful comparison of her explanation with earlier evidence exposes the missing authority condition; no single opposed roll compels her confession.
2. **Relief store:** lists show genuine supplies, then “exercise casualties” charged as replacement labour. A survivor among the porters can name the abandoned district. Paying and protecting this witness produces testimony independent of stolen documents.
3. **Design office:** H7’s draft appoints the Office permanent custodian. The royal names have spaces for endorsement rather than selection. Complete notes describe the seven acknowledgements, Hall controls, six-hour sequence, and abort procedure. Copies take four hours or one hour with Nema’s help; carrying the locked folio is faster but conspicuous. Rusk may be here if he escaped earlier. He knows who issued his orders and will trade testimony for a fair trial.
4. **Restricted archive:** classified payment orders prove deliberate sabotage, with originals corresponding to A02 and A08. A list of detained dissenting engineers gives three names and a real depot address. They are in the building’s locked apprentice dormitory and can be freed, rather than being a tease for an unwritten rescue.
5. **Test chamber:** a room-sized mechanical model of the command couplers and the Brass Bailiff. Its posted instructions are: **“On three bells evacuate unbadged persons by the south door. Protect the operator against assault. Leave persons in marked safety alcoves unless they attack.”** Its one programmed skill is Mechanisms100; add Perception30 for identifying badges and positions. Its human operator can order a safe inspection but cannot make it improvise a new goal. Borrowed badges, safety alcoves, redirecting an actual evacuation, or using the coal passage can bypass it. It is the full published golem if fought.

**Nema’s decision:** she cooperates when shown both the casualty list and a matching independently sourced order, or when Sere openly orders harm to witnesses. She supplies directions, the folio, and one bounded escape assistance. She does not instantly gain master skill or become the party’s unlimited caster. Without convincing evidence she attempts to stop theft and summon a supervisor rather than attack strangers with lethal magic.

**A confrontation with Sere:** she is based here after returning from the opening rescue until Day 77, except for recorded trips. Two guards protect her; the Bailiff remains at the test chamber. She cannot bring all three into every room at once. The gallery has chest-high stone model plinths, two doors, a roof vent wide enough for human flight, and a side stair. Put those features on paper. Her spell tactics and exact PP example are in Adversaries. A good ambush can catch her unprepared. If her escape route has been blocked, honour that.

**What she says when exposed:** “Those people died because no one would share a granary. I made sure the next granary would have one key.” Her original justification is partly true; the subsequent murders remain chosen acts. She may offer to suspend the ceremony and submit evidence to a joint court in exchange for protection of the school. Require concrete relinquishment of power before treating that as a resolution.

**If late:** records are at the Hall but copies, witnesses, model, and Nema remain. An abandoned room does not mean the investigation failed. If Sere was killed or arrested earlier, play this as securing her institutions and preventing looting, not a replacement boss fight.

**Reward:** major commission, complete technical evidence, rescued engineers, and the possibility of ending the master-led conspiracy immediately. Safe release of the relief grain improves A14’s supply alternatives.

## A14: The city beneath the open hand

**Place:** Vellbridge, its approach road, and Kest’s camp. **Play:** 2 sessions, or more for a player-led military campaign. **On site:** three days of ultimatum by default. **Pressure:** T10 on Day 70 if its prerequisites survive.

**Entrances:** a Council plea; a personal message from Hesta; requisitions discovered elsewhere; or the company returns home to soldiers repairing its roads. The occupation is framed as protection. Its immediate benefits are visible, alongside detention and food seizure.

**If Kest never obtained the resources to occupy:** run a negotiation at his increasingly hungry camp, or close this node as an allied success. Do not create an invasion anyway.

**Situation:** Kest requires the Council to approve permanent inspection of its charters and surrender control of the north approach. He holds twelve adult civic representatives, including a councillor and guild delegates, as “guarantors.” No beloved NPC is automatically among them. His quartermaster Edrin warns that another week’s provisions would require taking winter grain from villages.

**Map:** camp and supply wagons outside North Gate; barracks and guarantors beside the gate; council hall uphill; independent granary on the river quay; relief depot at the Brazen Mule. A maintained drain from the river passes beneath the barracks yard but cannot carry a wagon or army.

**The three-day ultimatum:** Day 1 inspections and invited negotiations; Day 2 announcement of permanent grain requisition; Day 3 at noon transfer of guarantors to the field camp if no agreement. Announce each order. A negotiated standstill can buy time; a successful hostage rescue removes the transfer objective.

**Operations the company can choose:**

- **Free the guarantors:** the barracks holds four guards in two pairs, one at the outer entrance and one in the yard. The drain reaches a storeroom with a normal locked door into the yard. Forged transfer paperwork can work until checked; a lawful release signed by the councillor who issued the invitation also works if Edrin enforces it. Prisoners can walk, and allies can receive them at the quay.
- **Secure the granary:** a public inventory can prove there is enough food for a funded withdrawal but not permanent occupation. Ysabet or Sal may guarantee its price in exchange for published repayment terms. Diverting the wagons secretly takes two hours and drivers; it leaves Kest’s soldiers angry and hungry unless a withdrawal option accompanies it.
- **Break the invitation:** show the Council actual sabotage orders and secure a public revocation. Influence can sway uncertain delegates, while blackmailed ones need protection. Three identifiable delegates are undecided, two support occupation, two oppose it. The chair breaks ties. No one must clear seven personal side quests; a convincing public case can move the three together.
- **Confront the commander:** parley in his tent, challenge him under witnesses, or isolate him during a gate inspection. His default escort is two veterans. A fair duel is optional; the company may prefer an ambush or negotiation. His printed defeat and withdrawal conditions apply.

**If armies fight:** baseline **100 Kest troops** versus **60 town defenders**, commanded by a Council captain with Lore (Military Tactics)55 and Influence55. Maud can add 20 wardens; Korva can add 40 soldiers, only after their real travel and negotiated agreement. A PC may command instead. Apply the published Mass Combat modifiers for actual numbers, fortifications, training, special forces, and equipment; use morale through the published rout procedure, and Kest’s Tactician. Resolve a decisive party mission before the battle roll; its result changes a concrete factor such as gate possession or arrival of reinforcements. Never give five abstract bonuses for different descriptions of the same advantage. Use actual casualties and rescue work afterward.

**Twist and choice:** if Kest learns Sere manufactured emergencies, he offers to arrest her and continue as sole military custodian. This can remove one enemy while installing another. A withdrawal contract can instead require release of guarantors, local gate control, a supplied route out, and independent observers. If the party defeats him, ordinary soldiers can surrender without being slaughtered for their commander’s ambitions.

**Links:** his orders include the Hall’s security roster and abort timetable (A15), independent of Sere’s archives. A coded dinner invitation points to the Chancellor if A12 remains unresolved. His baggage contains witness receipts that can corroborate A10.

**Reward:** major commission or debt cancellation, a secure home, and control of how Vellbridge enters any final settlement. If it falls, survivors can establish a company base at Hollow Ward, Gull’s Rest, or an allied road abbey; a liberation campaign remains possible.
