## A01: The lamp that went dark

**Place:** White Span, south of Vellbridge. **Play:** 1 session. **On site:** one evening and the following morning. **Pressure:** people on an unstable crossing, followed by T01–T03.

Run the existing [complete adventure](../the-lamp-that-went-dark/01-adventure.md), with its [handouts](../the-lamp-that-went-dark/02-handouts.md), [NPCs](../the-lamp-that-went-dark/04-npcs-and-stat-design.md), and [supporting cast](../the-lamp-that-went-dark/07-supporting-npcs.md). Its rescue, chamber puzzle, recovery crew, and ending remain intact.

**Three entrances:** a Council commission; a personal connection to missing Survey Team Hallow; or witnessing the failing lamp while crossing. If played previously, take the actual rod custody, survivors, evidence, and public account as established facts.

**Objectives:** rescue Hallow; keep travellers from the dangerous approach; recover the original route-rod and notes; establish who may hold them. These are separable successes. Hallow recovered the rod after discovering the copy—they did not steal it. Maelin Voss supplied the replacement unknowingly under Cale Rusk’s restoration order. Her paperwork is evidence of a supply chain, not proof the entire Castaine house is guilty.

**Campaign addition, after the existing danger resolves:** Sere arrives with relief labourers. She expends magic to help free a cart from damaged approach masonry, then needs others to carry its passengers. This is an introduction, not a demonstration that she could have solved the whole adventure. She offers professional praise and asks who will supervise the repair. If attacked on specific evidence, use her actual profile and available resources; do not protect her because it is early.

**Connections visible in the recovered material:**

- Rusk’s order names **Blackglass’s acoustic conduit** as the transport route for replacement fittings: A02.
- Hallow’s dispatch list shows three versions of one warning, all authenticated through **Central Measure**: A04.
- A margin note says **“service bedding sound; command collar to be measured separately.”** It introduces the possibility that public service and central control are separable; corroborate through A07, A09, or A13.
- A medical supply chit names **Hollow Ward**, a useful lead if the company already knows someone missing there: A03.

The main adventure ends with the bridge **closed**, not magically restored. Survey workers can make a protected maintenance passage after two days if the rod is secured and the company supplies labour; general traffic still needs a real repair plan. The subsidised river ferry in the running guide remains available.

**If the party declines:** the Council evacuates the approach and another crew rescues some surveyors; the original rod goes into disputed custody. Survivors and the copied notebook still lead to A02/A04. Do not kill the entire team to punish an alternate opening.

**Company reward:** its agreed existing payment, not a second overlapping commission, plus Dena as a potential ally if she survives. If the company prevented the theft but cannot keep the rod, its evidence still matters.

## A02: A share in Blackglass

**Place:** quarry east of Vellbridge. **Play:** 1–2 sessions. **On site:** half a day to one day. **Pressure:** T02, sale on Day 8.

**Entrances:** Hesta asks the company to escort an auditor; Hallow’s notes name the conduit; or quarry workers seek help finding **Miri Flint**, a goblin rigger missing below. The three requests can come from opposing parties.

**Situation:** League auditor **Pell Dorr** (Trade/Lore (Contracts) 65, Persistence 50, Deception 40) thinks a purchase will finally fund safe work. Forewoman **Vessa Marr** (Mechanisms 60, Influence 50, Persistence 60) wants the workers to hold their own lease. Rusk has used an Office loan to install a reversed screw-jack in the conduit. Its operation can transfer damaging vibration toward the Span. The giant spider below is an opportunistic resident.

**Site:** the weigh yard opens to the office and a descending haul ramp. The ramp reaches a split: the **pump gallery** on the left, the **jack chamber** ahead, and the **old air shaft** on the right. The air shaft rejoins the chamber behind the spider’s webbed hunting hollow.

1. **Weigh yard:** workers debate whether to let Dorr in. Showing the escort commission gets an audience; a reasonable rescue plan gets tools. Winning Influence against Vessa can secure her own participation, not permission to ignore safety.
2. **Office:** the loan, payment dates, and original jack diagram are available to the lawful auditor or anyone admitted by the workers. H2 reproduces the key order. Comparing the installed jack with the drawing reveals the reversal without a test. Trade success identifies the clause that suspends foreclosure for undisclosed defective collateral.
3. **Pump gallery:** Miri is alive on a ledge beyond silk. She warns of the spider. A secured rope from the air shaft can reach her; distracting the spider with a carcass creates the same opportunity. Its printed venom and web rules apply if fought. It pursues prey within the gallery, not into daylight after a retreat.
4. **Jack chamber:** two recovery hirelings have orders to take the signed plate and leave. They offer 150 SP for permission to remove it. If opposed, they shove a tool cart across the ramp and try the air shaft; use ordinary object and combat rules. They flee after one is Wounded. The jack’s safe shutdown is posted: **brace load → disengage drive belt → lower tension → lock screw**. Following it with supplied tools takes an hour without a roll; Mechanisms under pursuit can shorten this to ten minutes. Destroying the loaded jack without bracing risks the visible suspended conduit, announced before the act.

**Evidence:** the signed plate links Rusk to deliberate interference; a dispatch address identifies **Sere’s House of Tomorrow** (A13); delivery invoices list **Station Black Measure** (A08). A scratched service diagram separates local anchor casing from a smaller command collar (coupler evidence). The loan alone does not prove the League knew about the sabotage.

**Choices and consequences:** expose the defect and workers win a hearing; settle the arrears and choose a guarantor; let the sale proceed under a safety covenant; take the evidence by force and risk a lawful warrant. Miri’s rescue and shutdown remain successes whatever the ownership outcome. The sale can be halted with proof sent by courier; the PCs need not attend personally.

**Late arrival:** after Day 8, six Cabinet-guard-profile trust guards control the yard. They admit an auditor or a party with a safety complaint; a secret air-shaft approach remains. Dorr can become an ally when shown he bought a trap. The spider and evidence do not disappear because the auction ended.

**Reward:** local commission; Miri can guide one later underground survey. A sound lease supplies quarry bracing materials for A08 or A14 if transport is arranged.

## A03: The patients of Hollow Ward

**Place:** fort north of Vellbridge. **Play:** 1 session. **On site:** an evening. **Pressure:** current captives, not a new scheduled conspiracy attack.

Run the existing [adventure](../the-patients-of-hollow-ward/01-adventure.md), [NPCs](../the-patients-of-hollow-ward/02-npcs.md), and [handouts](../the-patients-of-hollow-ward/03-player-brief-and-handouts.md). Its [running notes](../the-patients-of-hollow-ward/04-running-and-setting-notes.md) explain the ward-closure sequence and accessible alternative routes.

**Entrances:** Sava Dorn is missing; Corporal Mira Senn commissions a rescue; or a returned patient’s discharge token reaches the company through Ancor. Preserve the adventure’s motives: Gentle Hands follows an old literal task; Doctor Oren Vey concealed his involvement in historical experiments. Neither was planted here by Sere.

**Campaign additions to the surviving records:**

- The **Red Field transport list** gives the old unit, witness names, and a current archive address at Banner Ford: A10. It provides corroboration, not an instant verdict on every claimant.
- An attached **Gate transfer receipt** shows the Ash Chancellor’s former office receiving medical detainee registers: A12. The handwriting and seal can be matched to modern appointment papers.
- A surgeon’s note records a coronation-era directive: **“Maintain local wards if the command permission is suspended.”** Another independent clue to separating service and command.

These are kept in a distinct administrative folder, allowing the company to preserve evidence while leaving private patient histories protected. Oren will help identify signatures if the group distinguishes the two. His cooperation can be won through argument, evidence, or assurances; his historical wrongdoing is not erased by one helpful interview.

**Aftermath choices:** publish the administrative evidence; entrust authenticated copies to Korva, Maud, or Brindle; or conceal it to protect a patron. A public report can cause hearings without automatically inciting ethnic reprisals. Give people agency in how they respond.

**Late or delegated visit:** the fort can hold the receiving entrance shut and post warnings while awaiting a rescue team. The ongoing danger is known; use the original adventure’s actual patient situation when played, not an invisible accumulating death count. A team that rescues without closure has achieved rescue and left a clearly described remaining task.

**Reward:** existing adventure payment, Mira’s trust, and a reliable place to stage travel north if the ward is secured. Gentle Hands does not become a portable combat minion or an infinite source of healing.

## A04: The letter with seven seals

**Place:** Vellbridge’s Central Measure annex, Station Seven, and a relay 12 km west. **Play:** 1 session. **On site and local travel:** one day; an archive-only operation can take half a day. **Pressure:** T01, records burned at midnight on Day 6.

**Entrances:** Pella has contradictory warnings; Tal asks the company to recover a “misfiled duplicate”; or a goblin printer offers money for the censored dispatch register. H1 makes the audit known even without A01.

**Situation:** three warnings have valid authentication and different instructions. They were altered **before** the Post received them. Tal ordered destruction of duplicate technical records for secrecy; Rusk expanded the destruction list to erase the revision history. Tal does not know that alteration until shown the two lists.

**Locations:** Station Seven connects by city streets to the annex and by road to the grounded relay. Inside the annex, the public counter leads to the copying room and the locked audit store; a loading door enters that store from the yard.

1. **Station Seven:** Pella provides H3 and the posting times. Comparing them shows that all three packets arrived sealed from Central Measure. No cipher puzzle gates this conclusion. She wants an authenticated correction, not an advantageous rumour.
2. **Copying room:** clerk **Iven Rell** (Deception 45, Persistence 40) added Rusk’s supplemental list because it carried a valid Office signature. He will show it if given protection against punishment for testifying. The public destruction schedule itself can be copied openly. A Council inspection order, Tal’s authorisation, disguise, or burglary reaches the store.
3. **Audit store:** four crates, each plainly labelled by date. The red-string crate holds routing logs; the blue holds ordinary duplicates; the white holds patient forms that should not be published; the black holds coupler measurements. Removing a crate takes two people; copying the relevant red and black pages takes two hours, or one hour with a competent clerk. At midnight two labourers burn authorised crates in the yard. If shown conflicting orders, they stop and fetch their supervisor—they are not assassins.
4. **Grounded relay:** two giant hawks are hunting the exposed approach. Each is large enough to lift a person, as the crushed shelter roof and a witnessed pass make clear. Their shadows and discarded courier tubes warn of them. The covered road shelter is safe from their claws, and no essential evidence requires a fight. A covered escort, distracting food, or moving at dusk lets the pixies leave; use the published [giant hawk](../../src/content/rules/creatures/giant-hawk.md) only if a fight occurs. Courier **Petal Wick** has the first dispatch copy and a note naming Rusk’s Crownseat return address. Her testimony survives an archive fire.

**Clues:** the revision trail links sabotage cover to Rusk and the Office; his return address opens A13; a scheduled visit names Storm Needle keeper **Jessa Kelm** (A06); the black crate shows that command-collar specifications were deliberately omitted from public repair plans. These do not require believing Tal.

**Hard choice:** releasing every document immediately exposes private patients and refuge routes. Redacting those pages takes four hours with a printer, or allies can do it while the party leaves. Destroying evidence to protect privacy is not the only option.

**If late:** recover the courier’s early copy, interview Iven, and take ash impressions identifying the burned crate numbers. These prove suppression and lead to A13 even though complete local data is gone. The loss matters: future work needs an additional survey day or an ally’s measurements.

**Reward:** local commission; Brindle authenticates the company’s evidence if it preserves chain of custody. An exposed Rusk goes to A13 by the actual Crownseat route or seeks surrender to a patron; he does not vanish across the world.

## A05: Five hours to Gull’s Rest

**Place:** port, tide gates, and treaty hall. **Play:** 1–2 sessions. **On site:** two days for full preparation; the final surge is a five-hour crisis. **Pressure:** T03 around Day 10.

**Entrances:** H1’s tide notice; a rescue-house request carried by Oloa; or a charter to escort a harbour negotiator. The title refers to the final local warning, not an obligation to cross a closed Span in five hours. The group can arrive days ahead by the published routes or ferry.

**Situation:** the treaty pearl remains seated, but the Pearl Compact withdrew consent to the old treaty. This mismatch makes the gates choose dangerous thresholds. Harbourmaster **Beren Tull** (Influence 55, Persistence 55) insists on protecting property; rescue captain **Luma Sar** (Sailing 65, Athletics 60) wants everyone out of the lower ward. Luma is Oloa’s cousin, willing to challenge her publicly over rescue delays.

**Layout:** the quay reaches the upper market by a broad ramp. The breakwater contains the **air-filled treaty hall** and, below it, the **gate machinery gallery**. A safe upper walkway leads to the **western spillway**. Low-ward alleys connect the quay to the **refuge school**. A lantern mast is visible from all four sites.

**Preparation objectives:** clear the school and prepare beds (half a day with residents); clear debris from the western spillway (one day with six workers); negotiate temporary operating consent and halt unlawful dredging (half a day once both decision-makers attend). The council owns a workboat and has twenty volunteers if the group organises tasks. Delegation can genuinely complete all three.

**Five-hour crisis if preparation is incomplete:**

- **Hour 0:** gauges rise and bells sound the wrong sequence. State which gates are shut and which streets are still dry.
- **Hour 1:** a barge strikes the quay, isolating six workers on an accessible deck. A boat rescue takes an hour; ropes from the upper ramp can do it in half an hour with prepared anchors.
- **Hour 3:** the low street floods. Remaining residents must use roofs and upper windows. Cleared spillways reduce the force; prepared evacuation means nobody is waiting here.
- **Hour 5:** the surge peaks. If the western spillway is open, it carries water into the empty marsh channel. If not, the lower ward floods. Use the actual storm and drowning rules for exposed characters; do not convert a planning failure directly into unavoidable death.

**Ways to reach the machinery:** permission from the harbourmaster; entry with a repair crew; or the dry inspection stair from the upper walkway. A merfolk-only underwater solution is never required. Two impatient reef wardens block removal of the pearl; they accept Oloa’s temporary agreement, can be talked past, or use Cabinet-guard-equivalent equipment and skills if violently opposed. They fight to stop tampering and retreat to warn their assembly.

**Repair truth:** reseating the pearl does nothing. A documented thirty-day temporary consent plus corrected gate thresholds can stabilise operations; a permanent treaty needs authorised parties and proper restoration. The inspection panel has distinct **LOCAL WATER** and **IMPERIAL DIRECTION** circuits. Isolating the command coupler takes a Survey-qualified operator four hours and does not erase consent requirements.

**Clues:** the weather all-clear carries the Storm Needle’s selector code (A06); an old treaty lists the dragon as a witnessed creditor rather than owner of all Crownworks (A11); the dual circuit offers another independent discovery of command separation. Oloa can introduce delegates for A15.

**After failure:** play rescue, relief, and a contested investigation. The port remains a meaningful hub. Salvage witnesses carry evidence inland; trade shifts to the League. Blaming all merfolk makes Oloa less able to help, while publishing the actual fault can preserve negotiations.

**Reward:** local or regional commission depending on the agreed scope; rescue-house allies, temporary ferry priority, and practical gratitude from people who will recognise the company later.
