## Before preparing the finale

- Does Sere still have a master, Hall access, seven operable connected couplers, and seven willing or physically coerced crews?
- If not, record the plan as stopped or postponed and identify the real remaining conflict.
- Which rulers and affected communities will recognise the company’s proposed settlement?
- Which enemies can actually reach the Hall, and what do they know?
- Which earlier rescues, evidence, and alliances will be visible in the ending?
