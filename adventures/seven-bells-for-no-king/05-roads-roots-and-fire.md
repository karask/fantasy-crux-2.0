# Roads, roots, and fire

These nodes can be approached through local patrons without any Whitewater investigation. Rival expeditions may arrive first; they create people to bargain with and different site conditions, not empty rooms where all adventures have expired.

## A06: The Storm Needle

**Place:** a Wester Cape, one day from the League port. **Play:** 1–2 sessions. **On site:** a day and night. **Pressure:** the next forecast will misdirect a convoy; its departure is in two days when the company receives the local harbour notice. If this node is skipped, the convoy takes shelter after a navigator objects, but the false-signal network remains.

**Entrances:** the false all-clear from A05; Ysabet wants the source of contradictory forecasts found; Tessara needs accurate historical records for A09. The tower can be reached along its tidal causeway or by a boat landing at the sheltered service stair. The causeway is passable for three hours around each low tide; give the actual next two windows on arrival.

**Situation:** Keeper Jessa Kelm falsified warnings to wreck a rival’s ships. Rusk then threatened exposure unless she falsified selected Crownwork warnings. Her active Veiled Shaping biases the selector gears; the tower’s observation chamber still records real weather. She wants escape, and two Cabinet guards have arrived to make that difficult.

**Site:** causeway → gatehouse → spiral service stair → selector room → observation dome. The stair also opens onto the harpy balconies; a ladder from the dome descends to the sea landing.

- **Gatehouse:** three drowned sailors’ memorial plaques identify an early false warning and its date. Guards demand a signed inspection order but fear being trapped here by the tide. They can be bribed with passage, deceived with a real survey seal, or fought as two guards. They withdraw to the landing after one falls.
- **Selector room:** the brass pointer disagrees with the observation log. Jessa can voluntarily dismiss her working. Alternatively expose the housing and Dispel it, or disengage the selector and use the manual signal lanterns. The posted manual gives the real signal sequence; reading it is sufficient to warn the convoy.
- **Observation dome:** sound storm records, a Saffron copy request, and a column headed **“Concordance safe test, Day 84”** provide the timing lead. The notation also says **“cancel on missing station acknowledgement.”** It proves the ceremony has prerequisites, not an unavoidable celestial destiny.
- **Balconies:** two [harpies](../../src/content/rules/creatures/harpy.md) nest beyond closed maintenance screens. They want food, nesting security, and the dead keeper’s silver whistle. Bargain for the safe upper approach; otherwise use the service stair. Their presence is audible before the screen is opened.

**Jessa’s working:** individual human, all characteristics 10 except INT16/POW16; HP10, MWL5, PP16 before commitment, Order13, AP0. Shaping80, Persistence60, Dodge35; cell Bend·Force/Motion; Talents Shaping and Veiled. I1, touch R0, D4, Reach0, Veiled +1 = **M6**. She committed six maximum PP in an earlier six-hour ritual and has **10 available maximum/current PP**, active M6. Record the original successful casting result as 42 for opposed detection; Veiled applied its extra casting penalty when the ritual was performed. Its Tell briefly pulls shadows toward the false selector setting; Veiled hides it only under its actual rules. Touch Dispel M6 is possible for sufficiently capable Shapers and uses the ordinary opposition against her Shaping80. Novices can use the manual mechanism without Dispel.

**Choices:** protect Jessa while requiring testimony; hand her to a court; let her flee with one copy after securing the originals; bargain with Ysabet about compensation. A court can punish a real crime while recognising coerced later acts.

**Clues and rewards:** Rusk’s payments point to A13; accurate storm cycles improve A09’s safe plan; the convoy captain knows the route to the dragon’s treaty court at A11. Regional commission and League passage. Securing the manual selector ends this sabotage even if Jessa escapes.

## A07: The root crypt of the Orchard Bell

**Place:** near Leth Arain, Greenward. **Play:** 2 sessions. **On site:** one to three days, with treatment work delegable. **Pressure:** T04, hard frost expected Day 28.

**Entrances:** Serel requests help with samples; Synod pilgrims seek missing orchard families; a tenant council asks the company to stop outsiders deciding its future. The company can take any side or propose its own arrangement.

**Situation:** the root anchors have a treatable mundane blight. The Court’s deep-grove faction would welcome the end of imperial monoculture. The dryad **Eren-of-the-Graft** wants an older tree spared. Hag **Mother Celand** inflames the dispute, promising to protect individual families if they betray their neighbours. She has no power to guarantee that protection.

**Site:** tenant village → roofless abbey → root crypt. A deer track from the village reaches Celand’s limestone caves; an old water channel connects their outer chamber to the abbey’s spring yard, not directly to the Bell’s anchor.

1. **Village:** four families repeat the same unusual phrase from different supposed sources: “the Bell drinks children’s winters.” Comparing accounts reveals that each met an unfamiliar woman after sunset. Natural Lore examination finds ordinary blight in roots from different households; magical ancestry is not a cause.
2. **Abbey:** hundreds of petitions obscure the Bell’s root. Careful removal takes two hours with pilgrims. Burning them risks the exposed dry wood, visibly and obviously. Behind them is H4, an old maintenance hymn illustrating the removable command collar. A margin names the Hall’s chapel archive, another route into A15.
3. **Root crypt:** Eren stands beside the living graft. She accepts pruning diseased imperial branches if the elder tree is protected and tenants retain access to their homes. She can distinguish old graft from command tendrils. Bracing and pruning by trained workers take three days; it preserves the surviving local protection through the coming frost. Full restoration remains a separate project. Threatening her tree brings a warned [dryad](../../src/content/rules/creatures/dryad.md) confrontation; withdrawal up the crypt stair is clear.
4. **Celand’s cave:** a lit outer camp, a low passage, and a dark inner hearth. Stolen knives and staged “elven” letters expose her fabricated accusations. She first offers the party one truthful fact about Sere in exchange for leaving her local victims alone. Refusing does not trigger unavoidable magic; she uses her published Practice and Tell.

**The hag hunt:** her anchor is the split limestone hearth-stone, visibly repeated in victims’ charcoal sketches. Before entering, an abbey notebook explains that bodily destruction returns her to it and she reforms after **2D6 days**. The hearth is a boulder-sized object, AP4/HP40. **Campaign anchor ruling:** breaking it and opening the hearth to natural daylight ends Celand’s bond here, banishing her from this campaign region; it does not destroy the Orchard Bell. This consequence is discoverable in the notebook. Merely killing her body buys time. She can Create Shade only in darkness within her domain, one at a time, at the published cost. A party can map the cave by day, open a roof fissure, illuminate the inner room, or confront her at night. Retreat through the lit spring yard is possible; she cannot leave her domain by day.

**Clues:** a Cabinet sample request proves Sere knew the blight’s mundane cause; Celand’s stolen letters identify the Chancellor’s steward (A12); the hymn and roots independently establish service/command separation. Celand did not invent the Court’s legitimate ecological objection.

**End states:** maintain the Bell with joint stewardship; phase into local protections while supporting livelihoods; evacuate voluntarily; or lose outer orchards while preserving the inner community. If late, hungry displaced families and disputed land claims replace the urgent frost rescue. Serel’s workers, a living-domain delegate, and a regional commission are possible rewards.

## A08: The hand beneath the mountain

**Place:** Station Black Measure below Dor-Morak. **Play:** 2 sessions. **On site:** two days for investigation; four for full shoring by a crew. **Pressure:** T05, jack operation on Day 35.

**Entrances:** Ekkit sends shifting survey marks; Droma hires a discreet inspection; A02’s invoices name the foundation jack. Locals know a troll has been striking the platform, an explanation Compact officials find convenient.

**Situation:** the troll knocks because inspectors bring food. Saboteurs use its visits to mask deliberate jack adjustments. Droma has prepared an evacuation plan without informing residents. Cabinet Crew North, if it arrived, offers to repair the damage in exchange for permanent station access.

**Site:** workers’ hall → main bridge → suspended station. The station branches into the **chain house**, **anchor platform**, and **warm drain**. A narrow foot inspection gallery joins the workers’ hall to the chain house, bypassing the main bridge. Mark ropes and railings before danger starts.

- **Workers’ hall:** ninety residents still think the closure is a tax dispute. Publicly announcing the actual risk gives them a choice. A gathering can obtain volunteers, not instant trust in the officials who concealed it.
- **Warm drain:** Knuckle-in-the-Drain sits beside healed bloody handprints. A butcher’s offal cart and a warm disused kiln beyond the station offer a viable relocation. It understands food and territory better than promises. If fought, use the full troll, including Regeneration and heat Blind Sight. The narrow inspection gallery is too small for it, offering a retreat route.
- **Chain house:** three Cabinet guards protect foreman **Uld Rake** (Mechanisms65, Persistence45, other guard skills). He has Rusk’s weekly adjustment instructions and knows they worsen movement. He claims the sacrifice will secure funds for a larger repair. He yields if shown that Sere intends to blame his crew, or can be captured. Stopping him before he engages the drive stops the operation.
- **Anchor platform:** immense chains cross a vertical fault. Tools, worker marks, and the intact foundation anchor prove its movement is artificial. **Safety sequence:** attach the independent brace; slack the drive chain; pin the jack; then survey. Four people with proper gear take four hours; a trained crew can do it while PCs secure access. If the jack moves during fighting, metal screams and the deck tilts before the next displacement. Give a full round to reach the already described safety galleries. No surprise unavoidable fall.

**Evidence:** orders with Sere’s countersignature; the decision to sacrifice occupied galleries; a transport docket sending spare command collars to A13. Under the platform a tablet explains that **all seven acknowledgements are required** for a full network command. Ekkit’s copy of the old service-route atlas points to A15 even if the tablet is lost.

**Choices:** expose Droma immediately or negotiate a public evacuation she leads; publish the safe atlas while withholding vulnerable warren routes; retain a Cabinet repair crew under independent supervision or expel it. The engineers have useful skills despite their employer.

**Late arrival:** if the operation occurred unopposed, the main bridge is gone and survivors occupy the workers’ hall. The foot gallery reaches the surviving chain house. The anchor is still recoverable; rescue and an alternate route replace prevention. Name losses only after resolving actual warning and evacuation efforts. The Cabinet cannot use a nonoperable coupler for T11 until it spends real work restoring access.

**Reward:** regional commission, Compact engineers, Ninefold access, and bracing expertise that can help A14. The troll’s removal alone is not closure of the sabotage front.

## A09: Water at two noons

**Place:** Lantern Aqueduct and nearby observatory in the Saffron Reach. **Play:** 2 sessions. **On site:** two to four days. **Pressure:** T07, seasonal transfer on Day 49.

**Entrances:** Tessara’s survey commission; patients with waterborne illness; or A06’s request for weather records. The dry magistrate will sponsor an independent expedition if the PCs fear becoming the queen’s agents.

**Situation:** three newer districts draw through unprotected connectors beyond the old water Ward’s Reach. An imperial chart’s political boundary became an engineering assumption. Sere knows the cause and delays publication while the Office offers emergency custodians. Meanwhile the observatory can reveal the safe timing for water diversion and the proposed Concordance.

**Route through the site:** aqueduct inspection court → distributary gates → unprotected connector houses. A separate 25 km canal towpath reaches the observatory: flood stair → north gallery → sun court → instrument cabinet.

1. **Inspection court:** the original boundary plan and present connections can be compared without magic. Isolating polluted intake and distributing clean water is an immediate mundane response. A permanent extension of enchanted protection is a larger restoration project, not one free apprentice spell.
2. **Connector houses:** one sluice keeper accepted Office pay to omit warning notices. He supplies the payment record if protected from an angry crowd. Clean water wagons and repairs need two days’ labour; Ashal can keep them funded after Tessara’s term if given the plan before noon on Day 49.
3. **Flood stair:** fish skeletons, unwounded rats, and carved eye warnings announce the basilisk. A survey cache contains bronze sight-baffles, a long bait pole, and the iris handle. The north gallery has no direct sight line into the warmed nesting bowl.
4. **Sun court:** the Noon Jewel guards its clutch. Lure it into the outer court with prey, then close the iris from the gallery. Averting eyes prevents Death Gaze under the published restrictions; the creature still bites. Reflections do not bypass or reverse its gaze. If blood contaminates a weapon, the cache has abundant water for the published cleaning action. It will not pursue into the cold flood stair unless cornered.
5. **Instrument cabinet:** records describe past failed overrides, station acknowledgement intervals, and a **six-hour Hall procedure** planned for Day 84. They establish how to cancel safely, not a single mandatory ritual outcome. A claimant expedition of three guards led by scholar **Hadris El** (Lore75, Influence50) wants to take the portable mirror; the mirror is a structural component. Recording it preserves evidence without extraction. If they already arrived, bargaining over copies can avoid a fight.

**Competing objectives:** early diversion exposes the dome but floods occupied farms unless evacuation occurs; waiting permits safe access with a smaller boat crew but threatens Tessara’s exclusive claim. The PCs can survey by the north gallery at the ordinary low-water window, avoiding diversion entirely, but need two extra days. Give that option before they commit.

**Clues:** Sere’s delayed notice proves exploitation of a real engineering fault; H5’s old testimony corroborates the warning against overriding permissions; timing records and the distinction between local protection and command couplers make A15 operable from an independent source. A customs docket points toward A11’s debt witnesses.

**After the transfer:** Ashal becomes the decision-maker. Documents remain valid evidence; access must be renegotiated. A destructive diversion creates relief work and an inquiry. The site does not vanish forever. Regional commission, water engineers, and a credible seasonal delegate are the lasting rewards.

## A10: What the Red Field kept

**Place:** Banner Ford and the old battlefield 20 km away. **Play:** 1–2 sessions. **On site:** two days, or one for a narrow rescue. **Pressure:** T06, hearing on Day 42, transfer Day 43, potential raid Day 46.

**Entrances:** Korva seeks an authenticated register; Maud asks for an independent witness escort; Hollow Ward’s transport list leads here. The threatened witness is **Reva Den**, a living elderly archive custodian, not a conveniently immortal eyewitness. She inherited the unit records and can authenticate how they were preserved.

**Situation:** the tribunal charges Reva with selling military records. She did sell copies to support her household, but the death-order pages are genuine. Her testimony threatens claimant property interests. A militant Banner captain wants to rescue her by burning the courthouse, with prisoners still inside.

**Site:** Banner Ford’s court and archive; the battlefield’s boundary stone, burial trench, and ruined pay station. Court cells have a public visitors’ door and a laundry service exit; no player must become a legal expert to discover them.

- **Court:** Reva explains the records’ chain of custody and agrees to testify if her daughter is protected. The company can secure a lawful stay with two corroborating sources, argue for neutral jurisdiction, negotiate bail of 800 SP, or extract her from custody. Bail is a published court option, not a roll to invent one.
- **Boundary stone:** the Last Witness is a guardian spirit, anchored here with a 17 m boundary. Its inscription permits **“names spoken, testimony recorded, remains returned.”** Removing remains or defacing names triggers its audible alarm; it manifests on its next turn under its profile. Respecting the binding lets a scholar take rubbings and hear its limited testimony about witnessed acts. It cannot read modern suspects’ minds.
- **Pay station:** a sealed metal chest holds duplicate orders, payment tallies, and the names of officials who countersigned the massacre. Pick the ordinary lock with Mechanisms, obtain the archive key from Reva, or open the corroded hinge with two hours’ careful tools. Two looters offer the chest for 300 SP if they reached it first. They flee from organised opposition.

**The difficult discovery:** the order is real and some of the land titles created afterward remain valid under current claimant law. Proof enables a restitution case; it does not instantly feed displaced people or determine what every descendant deserves. Korva can restrain the raiders if given a protected witness and a dated hearing. She does not require the PCs to promise a verdict.

**Links:** a countersignature identifies the Ash Chancellor’s historical office (A12); a supply account contradicts Kest’s claim that his current requisitions are merely inherited obligations (A14); one military diagram records a Hall withdrawal passage used during the failed coronation (A15).

**Late arrival:** pursue the prison convoy by its actual route, or visit Reva in custody. If a raid occurred, investigate who ordered it and protect surviving witnesses. The original chest remains where its last recorded holder put it. Evidence is never teleported away because the party chose another adventure.

**Reward:** regional commission or equivalent Banner support, Korva’s escorts, and a possible coalition force with its own negotiated terms.

## A11: The court of cinders

**Place:** a volcanic treaty court 60 km from Banner Ford. **Play:** 2 sessions; a planned dragon hunt can take more. **On site:** one to three days. **Pressure:** T09, first seizure on Day 63.

**Entrances:** Varkesh’s witnessed demand H6; Sal requests debt arbitration; Oloa or Tessara supplies a treaty reference. Varkesh invites representatives under safe conduct, which it honours unless they attack or steal. The PCs may decline and investigate the claim elsewhere.

**Situation:** the dragon financed imperial relief and military transport. Part of its debt is valid. A later collateral schedule, supplied by the Ash Chancellor’s agent, adds the Crownworks as security without the required witnesses. Varkesh has noticed the defect but prefers nobody forces it into public discussion. It values being regarded as a scrupulous creditor.

**Site:** memorial avenue → open audience court → covered counting hall. A low servant passage links the hall to the caravan yard. Beyond the court, a long abandoned **toll furnace** has two great freight gates and high side inspection galleries. The hoard cavern is farther uphill and is neither empty nor safe to enter casually.

1. **Memorial avenue:** bronze names include merfolk navigators, goblin accountants, and imperial officials. Witness families can be traced through Oloa, Ekkit, or temple records. A rubbing proves which seals belonged on the valid agreement.
2. **Counting hall:** clerk **Enna Silt** (Trade80, Persistence60) keeps the originals because Varkesh enjoys rigorous accounting. She will permit supervised comparison under safe conduct. A missing seal and different page ruling expose the added schedule. She wants the record accurate even if it displeases her employer.
3. **Audience court:** Varkesh offers a fourteen-day standstill for 6,000 SP, recognition of the valid debt with a credible payer, or a public hearing with surviving witness institutions. Proof of the defective schedule wins exclusion of Crownworks from collateral if presented before at least two recognised witnesses; it does not erase the valid debt. He may demand an alternative revenue stream. The company can reject that demand and organise resistance.
4. **Caravan yard:** if T09 occurred, hostages are held here behind a guarded ordinary gate, outside the dragon’s permanent line of sight. Two veteran-profile retainers serve for wages, not racial loyalty. A false transfer order, bribery, a night extraction, or openly negotiated release can save the captives.

**Clues:** the forged schedule came through the Chancellor’s steward (A12); the authentic contract names an independent Hall inspection entrance (A15); Enna’s accounts prove the Cabinet has purchased **operator appointments**, not a royal bloodline. She can testify at a congress if her route is protected.

**If the PCs choose a dragon hunt:** survey the toll furnace before committing. Varkesh fits its long floor but cannot spread its wings inside. Its breath and attacks remain fully dangerous. The two freight gates each use the castle-gate object profile, AP4/HP120; winches in the side galleries take two people a full round to close, after which they latch. The human-sized galleries provide complete stone cover but have firing apertures that create a route for attacks both ways. A dragon can batter a gate or leave before it closes; shutting it requires a real lure and correct timing. No roll merely announces “the dragon is trapped.”

The party can evacuate the yard, obtain fire protection that actually covers its wearers, recruit competent Shapers, and use prepared siege equipment through ordinary rules and agreed custom engineering if desired. Rebuild Shapings for the dragon’s Reach2 size. Do not grant a free scale-breaking poison or an automatic-kill relic. Fighting here separates the monster from its hostages and restricts flight; it does not make the battle safe. With no workable damage plan, advisors say so before the lure. A failed attempt can end in retreat through the low passages or a costly surrender under witnessed terms.

**Other victories:** exclude the Crownworks from the claim; free the hostages and deny profitable occupation; make Varkesh enforce its contract against the Chancellor; or settle the debt through a willing coalition. Regional commission, neutral safe conduct, and a dragon removed from the enemy alliance are substantial rewards. A friendly dragon is not an on-demand combat summon.
