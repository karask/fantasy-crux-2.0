# People of the Last Errand

GM reference for **The Last Errand**. Named NPCs use the compact notation of [Minor NPCs](../../src/content/rules/gm-tools/minor-npcs.md), as in [The Lamp That Went Dark](../the-lamp-that-went-dark/04-npcs-and-stat-design.md): **Name — people and role Rating (equipment; abilities)**, followed by a table of skills grouped by percentage. Creatures use their published profiles unchanged. Encounter balance has not been playtested.

## Notation defaults

| Omitted entry        | Use                                                                                                                                           |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Body and resources   | HP 10/10, MWL 5, PP 10/10; characteristics 10.                                                                                                |
| Actions and movement | Combat Order 10, DEX 10, Movement 15 m; one Combat Action, Movement Action and base Reaction.                                                 |
| Protection           | AP 0, DM `+0`. No Hero Points, Talents or Shaping unless listed.                                                                              |
| Other skills         | 20%.                                                                                                                                          |
| Language             | Everyone at each stop has someone who speaks River Crown or Crown Speech well enough to talk. Do not roll Language for ordinary conversation. |
| Equipment            | Weapon damage is **before DM**. Add DM once. A weapon's Size governs Parry; armour gives AP.                                                  |

**Order is final:** armour ENC is already subtracted. Roll `1D10 + Order` for initiative.

## Aster Vael

_The courier._ A slight young woman of twenty-four in the long blue coat of the Imperial Errand, a courier's horn at her hip, her dark hair cut short for the saddle. At first she is only a cold spot, a doubled shadow and a taste of copper. She grows clearer with every delivery. She is brisk, proud of her route, kind to horses, and terrified underneath of having failed.

**Use the published [Ghost](../../src/content/rules/creatures/ghost.md):** Incorporeal Spirit; PP 11; Spirit Combat 40% (Spectral Grasp, `1D6` PP); Dodge 40%; Persistence 50%; Deception 50%; Perception 40%; dominant possession. Manifesting or fading costs her Combat Action.

**Echoes of life:** Athletics 60%, Riding 50%, Lore (Crownroads) 60%, Language (Crown Speech) 70%. Her speech is old-fashioned, but River Crown speakers follow it.

**Anchored** to the courier's satchel: she cannot move more than 10 m from it. If the satchel is destroyed, she is banished and cannot reform, and its seals are lost with it.

**She does not cast.** Her warrant opens each arch, as a key opens a lock. See [the gate in rules terms](04-running-notes.md#the-gate-in-rules-terms).

**What she knows grows with each letter delivered:**

| Letters delivered | Aster                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0                 | A chill and a whisper. She speaks only route words: _"Errand resumed. Western Wood. Stand within the circle."_                                                        |
| 1                 | Visible and clear. She names herself, talks and answers questions about the empire as it was. She can command the imperial dead at the Station of Hanging Lamps.      |
| 2                 | She remembers coronation night: the bell rang backward as she stepped into the arch, and "every road refused the Crown in the same breath".                           |
| 3                 | She remembers dying alone at the Milestone, writing to Tam, and promising him the fireworks. She notices the company's strange clothes and asks how long it has been. |

**Before the Hall,** she will not change the order of her route; persuasion fails automatically, because her duty outranks her doubts. She can be persuaded to go back for someone (see [left behind](01-adventure.md#the-bells-and-the-seals-how-the-gate-works-at-the-table)).

## The Gilded Spur Company

A three-person chartered company from Crownseat that has had a bad year. Their charter, a gilded spur on a red field, is two months behind on fees.

### Captain Sabel Ardent

_The rival._ Tall, sharp-boned and sunburnt, with a soldier's braid and a scar through one eyebrow. Her ringmail is mended with wire, and she wears a former Castaine household knight's sword. She is dryly polite under any pressure.

**Human Company Captain 65 (Arming sword `1D8`, Medium; Medium shield `1D6`, Heavy; Ringmail 3; Protector).**

| Skill % | Skills                                                  |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| 65      | Close Combat; Influence.                                |
| 55      | Dodge; Perception; Riding.                              |
| 45      | Athletics; Deception; Persistence; Resilience.          |
| 35      | Ranged Combat; Unarmed Combat; Lore (Military Tactics). |

**Wants:** the satchel, for the 600 SP that House Castaine's archivist promised. After the first jump, she wants her people home alive more than she wants it.

**Arc:** rival, then truce-partner, then a temptation at the Castaine door. If the company saved Tarn or Lissa tonight, she keeps faith. If not, she tries for the door.

**Play her:** offer a deal before drawing steel. _"Nobody needs to bleed for a box of old paper."_

### Tarn Gorvash

_The oath-breaker._ A huge orc with grey-green skin and a shaved head, the Banner brand scraped from his forearm. He carries a great axe and speaks rarely and gently, especially to the horses.

**Orc Mercenary 60 (Great axe `2D8`, Heavy; Leather 2; Adrenaline Surge).**

| Skill % | Skills                                                |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| 60      | Close Combat; Athletics; Unarmed Combat.              |
| 50      | Resilience; Riding; Natural Lore; Language (Varkesh). |
| 40      | Dodge; Perception; Persistence.                       |
| 30      | Influence; Ranged Combat.                             |

**Wants:** to stay away from the Emberplain. Three years ago he swore to guard a caravan through wyvern country and fled when the wyvern came; three people died.

**Arc:** dread, then judgment on the Red Switchback. If he swears a new oath there, he fights the wyvern like a man settling a debt.

### Lissa Fenn

_The frightened Shaper._ Nineteen, thin and freckled, with soot under her fingernails and a flint ring on her right hand. She is clever and brave until things go wrong, and then she reaches for fire.

**Human Shaper 55 (Dagger `1D4+1`, Light; Shaping 55%, Veteran: maximum Intensity 3, Safe Magnitude 3).**

| Skill % | Skills                                 |
| ------- | -------------------------------------- |
| 55      | Shaping.                               |
| 45      | Lore (Magic); Perception; Dodge.       |
| 35      | Persistence; Deception; Ranged Combat. |

**Cells:** Unmake·Fire (her specialty) and Ward·Fire.

**Practice:** she strikes her flint ring against her knuckles while counting a rhyme under her breath. **Tell:** every flame nearby leans towards her and her target, and the air smells of hot iron.

**Ready workings:**

- **Firebolt:** Unmake·Fire, Intensity 2 + Range 1 = **M3**. `2D6` Projected Harm within 15 m. Dodge or shield Active Guard at -1P; armour applies; Impact Size Medium.
- **Fire ward:** Ward·Fire, Intensity 2 + Duration 1 = **M3**. 4 AP against elemental harm for a scene, on herself or a touched subject.

**Arc:** a threat in the grove, useful light in the deep road, and the person most likely to be seized on the Red Switchback.

## The passengers

### Master Oswin Brack

_The client._ A soft, sweating man in a good coat gone shiny at the elbows, with three rings, one of them real. He is charming, cowardly and endlessly hopeful. He calls everyone "my friend" just before he tries to sell them something.

**Human Antiquarian Broker 60 (Walking stick, used as a club at -1P).**

| Skill % | Skills                                     |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| 60      | Trade; Deception; Lore (Imperial History). |
| 50      | Influence; Perception.                     |
| 40      | Persistence; Language (Crown Speech).      |
| 30      | Dodge; Riding.                             |

**Wants:** money. He bought the satchel from salvagers, knowing they had robbed a sealed vault, and paid more than Sabel offered. A League collector will view it at dawn in Vellbridge.

**Use him:** at every stop he tries to sell that stop's letter to whoever wants it most, at the worst possible moment. He is funny until the moment he is dangerous.

### Sister Mirelle Dawn

_The novice._ Nineteen, round-faced and steady-eyed, in the grey habit of a Synod novice with a small brass Star lamp at her belt. She is on her way to begin service at the House of the Rose Lamp in Vellbridge.

**Human Synod Novice 50 (Healing Kit, 5 uses).**

| Skill % | Skills                                          |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| 50      | Healing; Lore (Theology); Persistence.          |
| 40      | Perception; Influence; Language (Crown Speech). |
| 30      | Dodge; Resilience; Natural Lore.                |

**Wants:** to help. She sees Aster first and is not afraid of her. The Star Lamp guides the dead, and Mirelle believes this one is lost.

**Use her:** as the voice of kindness, a second healer, and someone who walks into danger to help the hurt. Her rite in the finale is worth +1B.

### Gannet Moss

_The driver._ A wiry old goblin with a white moustache, a waxed hat and a pipe he never lights in the coach. He was born in the Nine Warrens and has driven the Rill Post Road for thirty years. His horses are **Dumpling**, broad and patient, and **Sorrow**, nervous and fast.

**Goblin Coach Driver 60 (Sling `1D6`, 50 m; Blind Sight [heat]).**

| Skill % | Skills                                 |
| ------- | -------------------------------------- |
| 60      | Driving.                               |
| 50      | Riding; Natural Lore; Perception.      |
| 40      | Dodge; Ranged Combat; Language (Kesh). |
| 30      | Unarmed Combat; Athletics.             |

**Wants:** his coach and horses, in that order, then his passengers. He will not leave the horses anywhere. **Dumpling and Sorrow** use the published [Horse](../../src/content/rules/creatures/horse.md) profile.

**Use him:** for comic grumbling, speaking Kesh with Kettle, and driving the Red Switchback.

### Wenna Cray

_The extra passenger._ A League deckhand pushed into the circle during the Pearlwater flash, soaked and coughing, with a knife in her teeth. **Human Sailor 50:** Sailing, Athletics and Close Combat 50%; Dodge 40%. She is grateful, salty and useful in a fight.

## The Consulting Oak

### Mother Rhull

_The voice of the land._ A woman of bark and moss with leaves for hair. She sits among the oak's roots, and her eyes are the colour of old sap. She is patient, ironic and ancient. She remembers the imperial foresters and the levies, and she will not forgive easily.

**Use the published [Dryad](../../src/content/rules/creatures/dryad.md):** HP 11, PP 22, Persistence 60%, Shaping 60% (Bend·Mind, Alter·Mind, Conjure·Fire, Bend·Paths); Grove Anchor, Tree Passage and One with the Grove.

**Wants:** the empire never to pass through her tree again. A letter that remits the timber levy and recalls the foresters is the first imperial thing in ninety-three years to ask her pardon.

**Her price:** carry an acorn home and plant it where the courier died.

### Speaker Aelin Thorne

_The presiding advocate._ A silver-haired elf in a green advocate's sash, with a voice used to calming a crowded assembly. **Elf Verdant Court Advocate 60:** Influence, Lore (Verdant Law) and Language (Ilyr) 60%; Persistence, Perception and Language (Crown Speech) 50%; Natural Lore and Performance 40%. Low-Light Sight. She wants the Court's procedures respected and secretly hopes the road will reopen.

### Warden Hob Ashby and the grove wardens

**Hob Ashby — Human Woodland Warden 50 (Shortspear `1D6`, Medium; Leather 2; Order 7).** Close Combat, Perception and Natural Lore 50%; Athletics, Dodge and Persistence 40%. He is hot-headed and from a charcoal town, and he dislikes elves who are slow to decide.

**Six grove wardens — Human and elf Wardens 40 (Shortspear `1D6`, Medium; Leather 2; Order 7).** Close Combat and Perception 40%; Dodge 30%.

**Use the published [Ghoul](../../src/content/rules/creatures/ghoul.md)** for all three: HP 12; Unarmed Combat 60% (Claw `1D4+1D4`, Medium; Bite `1D6+1D4`, Medium); Dodge 40%; Persistence 30%; Ghoul Howl; Ghoul Venom (Potency 22, paralysis for `1D10` hours or until healing restores at least 1 HP).

**Hesk** still wears a rotten road-guard tabard and speaks old Crown Speech. He is intelligent and starved, and he is still a soldier. He wants to know whether the Emperor was crowned. What he needs is an order.

## The Red Switchback

### Rider-Captain Urza Ironvow

_The Banner captain._ A grey-haired orc with a lined, watchful face, a riding braid and a ringmail coat polished by years of road dust. She keeps a written copy of every oath she has witnessed in a leather case at her saddle.

**Orc Banner Rider-Captain 65 (Shortbow `1D8`, 75 m; Arming sword `1D8`, Medium; Ringmail 3; horse).**

| Skill % | Skills                                              |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| 65      | Riding; Ranged Combat.                              |
| 55      | Close Combat; Influence; Lore (Banner Law).         |
| 45      | Perception; Persistence; Dodge; Language (Varkesh). |
| 35      | Natural Lore; Athletics.                            |

**Wants:** the wyvern dead or driven off, the oath-breaker answered, and the law kept. The Eastern Banner letter is worth more to her than anything the company could offer.

**Five Banner riders — Orc and human Riders 50 (Shortbow `1D8`, 75 m; Shortspear `1D6`, Medium; Leather 2; Order 7; horses).** Riding and Ranged Combat 50%; Close Combat and Dodge 40%.

### The wyvern

**Use the published [Wyvern](../../src/content/rules/creatures/wyvern.md):** HP 23, AP 5, flies 30 m; Unarmed Combat 60% with Multiattack (Bite `1D10+2D6`, Claw `1D6+2D6` and Sting `1D6+2D6`, all Heavy); Wyvern Venom (Potency 60, `1D6` HP and -4 CON).

**Run it as described in [the climb](01-adventure.md#the-climb):** one pass per leg, with a single Bite or Claw; full Multiattack only at the Top Turn. It flees after three hits from anyone, 5 or more damage after armour, or any Critical. A wyvern that wanted a fair fight would have killed the company. This one wants a horse.

## The Couriers' Gallery

**Claimant guards — Human Guards 50 (Arming sword `1D8`, Medium; Medium shield `1D6`, Heavy; Chainmail 5; Order 3).** Close Combat and Perception 50%; Dodge, Persistence and Resilience 40%.

They pour through the broken door at the third bell, in fours. They want prisoners and someone to blame; they kill only those who fight.

**The three captains** are voices behind bronze: Veyran's precise and furious, Ordel's loud and suspicious, and Castaine's smooth and offering a deal.
