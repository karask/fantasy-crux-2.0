# The Last Errand

An introductory one-shot for **Fantasy Crux 2.0 Beta** that crosses the Crownless Realms in a single night: from a storm-bound posting-house in the Whitewater March, through a sacred grove, a haunted deep road and a wyvern's mesa, to the sealed Hall of Measures itself.

**Players:** four recommended; three or five work. **Length:** one four-hour session, including a break. **Characters:** the [Last Wagon Company](../../pregenerated-characters/README.md) pregens. **Rules:** the full rules or [Fantasy Crux Lite](../../player-handbook/lite/README.md); every number is the same. **Status:** complete scenario and NPCs; not yet playtested. No maps needed.

> _A storm drives your coach into a ruined imperial posting-house. A rival company is waiting for the old satchel your client is carrying. Then the great arch wakes, a bell rings backward, and the floor is suddenly somewhere else._

Ninety-three years ago, on the night the emperor vanished, a young courier left the Hall of Measures carrying proclamations of a coronation that never happened. Tonight her ghost resumes the route, and the company, its passengers and its rivals go with her.

At every stop the gate leaves on its **third bell**, and anyone outside the circle stays behind. The satchel's **seven seals** burn one per jump, with one to spare. Each stop shows a different people and its law. The company can deliver each letter, ninety-three years late, to whoever holds its office now. And at the end of the road, beside the empty throne and the crown of ash, the company must persuade a grieving ghost to take everyone home.

## Why it plays well

- **Two clocks the players can see.** The bells at every stop, and the seal ribbon on the table all night. "We have one spare seal" is the line the players will keep repeating.
- **The company's own promise is the stakes.** Nobody gets left behind, including the client, the novice, the horses, the rivals and a ghost.
- **Every stop is different:** a standoff in a storm, a trial before a sacred assembly, horror in the dark with repairs under attack, a chase up a cliff road under a wyvern, and a siege and a persuasion beside the empty throne.
- **It introduces the setting by showing it:** the backward bell, failing imperial works, the Seven Lamps, and peoples who are powers with their own laws. A chartered company is the only thing that can pass between them.
- **Failure complicates and never ends the game.** Missed deliveries make rough landings; people left behind come home later; even a refused ghost leaves the Courier's Last Road.

## Preparation

1. Read [the adventure](01-adventure.md) and [the people](02-npcs.md). Twenty minutes with the [running notes](04-running-notes.md) covers the rest.
2. Choose four pregens. **Mara, Pebb, Tamsin and Ilen** are recommended; see [who shines where](04-running-notes.md#who-shines-where).
3. Print [the handouts](03-player-brief-and-handouts.md), especially the seal ribbon. A real handbell and a one-minute sand timer make the third bell unforgettable.
4. Have D100 dice and counters for Hero Points ready.
