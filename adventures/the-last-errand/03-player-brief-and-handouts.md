# Player brief and handouts

The **player brief** is safe to share before play. Each **handout** is revealed only when the GM's note says so. Print them on rough paper, or read them aloud.

## Player brief: a night job

You are the **Last Wagon Company**. Six weeks ago, a flooded caravan was ordered abandoned with wounded passengers still aboard. You refused, and you brought everyone home. Afterwards you registered a charter with equal shares and one promise: **nobody gets left behind.**

Tonight's job is simple. **Master Oswin Brack**, an antiquarian with a coach, a strongbox and a nervous manner, has hired you to escort him from Rillmarket to Vellbridge overnight. The pay is **50 silver each**, on arrival at dawn. He will not say what is in the box, only that it is "old paper, very valuable to the right person".

Also aboard:

- **Old Gannet Moss**, the goblin driver, with his horses Dumpling and Sorrow. He has driven this road for thirty years and has opinions about all of it.
- **Sister Mirelle Dawn**, a Synod novice on her way to serve at the House of the Rose Lamp. She prays a lot and asks good questions.

It is raining hard. The road floods in places. Somewhere ahead is an old imperial posting-house that drivers call "the Ninth Milestone" and do not like to stop at.

**Bring:** your character sheet and your two Hero Points. Everything on your sheet is with you.

## Handout 1: the escort contract

_Show at the start, if the players ask to see their contract._

> **AGREEMENT OF ESCORT**
>
> Between Master Oswin Brack, dealer in antiquities, of Rillmarket, and the chartered **Last Wagon Company**, of Vellbridge.
>
> The Company shall escort Master Brack, his coach, his driver, his passengers and his goods from Rillmarket to Vellbridge, departing at the eighth bell, arriving by dawn.
>
> In consideration, Master Brack shall pay fifty silver pieces to each member of the Company upon safe arrival.
>
> The Company shall not open, inspect, or ask after the goods.
>
> _Signed:_ O. Brack, _with a flourish_. _Witnessed at the Rillmarket claimant court, third bench._

## Handout 2: the arch inscription

_Give when someone reads the arch at the Ninth Milestone. Every station's arch carries the same words._

> **OF THE IMPERIAL ERRAND**
>
> THE ERRAND DOES NOT WAIT.
>
> STAND WITHIN THE CIRCLE AT THE THIRD BELL.
>
> ONE SEAL CARRIES ONE ROAD.
>
> THE STAR SEAL BRINGS EVERY COURIER HOME.

## Handout 3: a Crowning Letter

_Give when the Western Wood letter is opened or read aloud. The other letters follow the same form; their gifts are below._

> **CALDRAS**, Fourth of the Name, by the measure of the Seven Lamps Emperor of Ordan and the Pearlwater, **to Our Steward of the Western Wood**, greeting.
>
> Know that at the ninth bell of this night We are crowned in the Hall of Measures, before the witnesses of every province.
>
> At dawn you shall light the beacon of your stewardship and read these words aloud, that all who live along Our roads may know that the Crown is whole.
>
> In token of Our coronation, **the timber levy of the Western Wood is remitted from this day, and Our foresters are recalled.**
>
> Given under Our seal, by the Imperial Errand, on the night of the Crowning.

**Gifts in the other letters,** to read aloud when they are opened:

- **To the Warden of the Deep Causeway:** _"In token of Our coronation, We confirm Warden Brakka Hollowhand of the Compact, and Gallery-Factor Snick of the Nine Warrens, to keep the Causeway together, as it has been kept."_
- **To the Captain of the Eastern Banner:** _"In token of Our coronation, the arrears of Our Banner shall be paid in full at the Crowning, with Our thanks for its long service."_

## Handout 4: a letter to Tam

_Give when the satchel is searched, or when Aster speaks of her brother. It is written in pencil on the back of a courier's waybill._

> Tam —
>
> They gave me the CROWNING ROUTE. Seven stations in one night, and the Star seal brings me home by breakfast.
>
> Keep the porch lamp lit and save me a honey bun from the festival. When the beacons go up at dawn, that's me.
>
> Don't let Mother worry.
>
> — Aster
>
> _(Below, in a shakier hand:)_ It's cold here, Tam. I'm sorry about the fireworks.
