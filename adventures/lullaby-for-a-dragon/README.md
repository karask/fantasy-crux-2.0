# Lullaby for a Dragon

A four-hour convention heist for **Fantasy Crux 2.0 Beta**. A harvest festival, a song with a secret verse, an imperial music machine under a vineyard, and a dragon who has been asleep for two hundred years. Players need to know nothing about the setting, and nothing about the rules beyond what the GM explains in five minutes.

**Players:** three to six; four is ideal. **Length:** one four-hour slot, including a break. **Characters:** the [Last Wagon Company](../../pregenerated-characters/README.md) pregens. **Rules:** the full rules or [Fantasy Crux Lite](../../player-handbook/lite/README.md); every number is the same. **Status:** complete scenario, NPCs, handouts and maps; not yet playtested.

> _Steal from a sleeping dragon. Whatever you do, don't let the music stop._

The company drives a wagon of wine to the grape-treading festival at the Warm Hill, with a second, secret job: under the hill lies a golden music-cylinder that a noble collector wants very badly. The doors to it open only to the vineyard family's harvest song. So the company must charm the family, learn every verse, and slip away at midnight with the singing still going on behind them.

Under the hill, the old song is still playing on an imperial machine the size of a church. The company finds out what it is for, and then their rival takes the cylinder anyway.

## Convention listing

Paste this into the catalogue:

Nobody mentioned what the music box is for. Nobody mentioned what is sleeping under the vines, or what happens when the music stops. A heist of whispers, harvest songs, a rival thief and the most dangerous room in the world. Bring your best singing voice; the dragon will not mind if it is terrible.
>
> _Content: fire and burns, a frail old woman in danger, a family estrangement, an ancient creature kept captive._

## Why it plays well

- **A heist where noise is the enemy.** The Waking track sits on the table all night. Whispers are free and singing is free; everything else costs. The players plan around a sleeping dragon, and the tension builds itself.
- **The song is the key.** The harvest song the players learn at the festival opens every door, calms the dream-sparks, and in the end sings the dragon to sleep. What they learned in the first hour is what saves them in the last.
- **The twist is in the loot.** The thing they came to steal is the thing that keeps her asleep. Then they have to decide what kind of thieves they are.
- **The most dangerous room in the world.** Crossing a dragon's hoard without a sound, and lifting a silver cylinder out from under her foot.
- **A finale where everyone has a job.** Chase the thief, fight on wet rock, seat the score in the cradle, and sing. Someone always sings.
- **No fight with the dragon, and no need for one.** If she wakes, the company talks to her. She has a grievance two hundred years long, and the best ending is the kind one.
- **Failure costs a box, a burn or a scare, never the game.** Every door has a second way through, and every ending has an afterwards.

## Preparation

1. Read [the adventure](01-adventure.md) and [the people](02-npcs.md), about an hour. The [running notes](04-running-notes.md) are your table reference.
2. Print the [handouts](03-players-and-handouts.md): a songsheet for every player, the commission, the fourth verse, the keeper's log, Lucan's orders, the Waking track, and [the keepers' plan](map/player-map.svg) as large as you can.
3. Print the pregens: Pebb, Dori, Orren and Seris are recommended for four players. See [who shines where](04-running-notes.md#who-shines-where). Print a copy of _How to play_ for each player.
4. Bring D100 dice, counters for Hero Points and, if you own one, a wind-up music box.

To redraw the maps after an edit, run `node adventures/lullaby-for-a-dragon/map/build-maps.mjs` from the repository root.
