# People of the Warm Hill

GM reference for **Lullaby for a Dragon**. Named NPCs use the compact notation of [Minor NPCs](../../src/content/rules/gm-tools/minor-npcs.md): **Name — people and role Rating (equipment; abilities)**, followed by their skills grouped by percentage. Creatures use their published profiles, with this adventure's notes. Encounter balance has not been playtested.

Nobody in this adventure needs to be fought. Lucan's crew can be, and the dream-sparks may have to be. Vermeil must not be.

## Vermeil

_The sleeper._ A red-gold dragon the size of a barn, coiled on her hoard like a cat in front of a fire. Her scales shade from gold at the belly to deep red along the spine. Each breath out puffs two small flames from her nostrils. Asleep she is beautiful and terrible, like a sleeping volcano. Awake she is old, proud, courteous, starving and very, very tired.

**Use the published [Dragon](../../src/content/rules/creatures/dragon.md) unchanged:** HP 50, MWL 25, AP 12 scales, DM `+7D6`, Order 18, Move 30 m on the ground and 45 m flying. Bite, Claw and Tail all at Unarmed Combat 100%. Breathe Flame: a 26 m cone dealing `4D6` fire. Fire Resistance 12. Persistence, Resilience, Influence and Perception all 100%, with Exceptional Skill.

### Vermeil in this adventure

- **Asleep,** she perceives nothing but the music and a voice speaking to her. The Waking track replaces every test against her senses.
- **Stirring** (track at 7 or more), she does one thing a beat in her sleep: a **sigh of flame** at the loudest place in the hoard (`2D6` fire, Dodge at `-1P` to halve it, armour applies), a **shift of her coils** (anyone on the hoard tests Athletics or falls and slides `1D3` m down the gold), or **a question** spoken aloud to whoever is singing.
- **Awake,** she cannot be fought. If anyone attacks her, the track fills, and she answers with one Breathe Flame across the attackers before she says a word. Remind the players about Hero Points first. See [If she wakes](01-adventure.md#if-she-wakes).
- **She cannot be lied to.** Her Perception wins against any Deception at the table. Nor can she be out-argued. A bargain works only if it is fair and honest.

**What she wants:** the sky, company, the truth about the emperor who lied to her, and something to eat after two hundred years. In that order, unless someone makes her angry.

**Play her:** slow, warm, formal and ancient, with sudden sharp curiosity. She calls people "little" anything: little keeper, little thief, little singer. _"You have my song in your mouth, little singer. Sing it properly."_

### What she says in her sleep

From box 4 on, read the next line each time the track fills a box. Use them in order.

1. _"Higher... the wind is under me... so cold up there, and so clean."_
2. _"Aubin? Is that you, little keeper? You are late with my song."_ Speak it to whoever is nearest her head.
3. _"Burn the city on the hill, he said, and then you may sleep. One night. I burned it. I was so tired."_
4. _"Is it morning yet? He promised me the morning."_
5. _"Who is singing? Sing me the one about the water."_
6. _"Too quiet. Why is it so quiet?"_
7. _"Little thief. I can hear your heart. Put back my song."_ Speak it to whoever holds the Golden Score.

If she runs out of lines, she sighs, and the hill trembles.

## Dream-sparks

_Her dreams, leaking out of the rock._ Small living flames the size of a fist, with no shape at all. They drift toward noise and warmth, and they can be beautiful: in quiet moments they dance to the Lullaby.

**Use the published [Elemental](../../src/content/rules/creatures/elemental.md), Small Salamander:** SIZ 3, HP 3, MWL 2, Move 15 m, Order 10. Engulf 1 m: Attack 15%, which must succeed and win against the target's Resilience; `1D6` fire damage on a win, armour applies. Dodge 100%, Persistence 30%, Resilience 100%. Immune to fire. Perception 80% against magic and spirits.

### Dream-sparks in this adventure

- **They come with noise.** `1D3` appear at Dreaming (box 4), near the loudest noise, and another one each time a fight breaks out under the hill.
- **The Fire verse settles them.** Anyone singing it makes every spark within 10 m sink back into the rock for the scene. No test.
- **Wine or water puts one out.** A splash from a cask, a flask or the stream ends a spark at once. They hate the festival wine.
- **Hitting one is nearly hopeless** (Dodge 100%), and fighting is loud. Sing instead.

## The thief and his crew

### Lucan Vasse

_The prodigal._ Thirty-two, lean and handsome in a dark oilskin coat, with his grandmother's sharp eyes and a smile he uses like a lockpick. He left the Warm Hill at eighteen after a shouting match with his mother: he wanted to sell the estate, and she called him a thief. He became one, and a good one. He has never forgiven any of them for being right.

**Human Retriever 60 (Shortsword `1D6`, Medium; Dagger `1D4+1`, Light; Leather 2).**

| Skill % | Skills                                                   |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| 60      | Athletics; Deception; Perception.                        |
| 50      | Close Combat; Dodge; Influence; Mechanisms; Persistence. |
| 40      | Unarmed Combat; Resilience; Lore (Crown Vale).           |

**Wants:** the Score, Harrow's money, and never to admit that his grandmother's song mattered.

**Knows:** the stream cave from boyhood, and the Warm Spring grate, which he cut this evening. He does not know the verses. He walked out before Gran could teach him the last one.

**Arc:** rival, then doubter, then, if the company gives him the chance, a grandson. The moment that turns him is the dragon calling him Aubin, or his grandmother calling him home. Proof he has seen gives `+1B` to persuading him: the log in his great-great-grandfather's hand, the scale, a look at her. Hearing her speak gives `+2B`.

**Play him:** whispered charm, then fury, then fear. _"It's bellows and a furnace. It's always been bellows and a furnace."_

### Grell

_The muscle._ A big, cheerful orc with a broken nose, carrying the rope sling and most of the crew's gear. He thinks the whole job is a lark until the hill starts breathing.

**Orc Hireling 50 (Club `1D6`, Light; Leather 2; Adrenaline Surge).** Athletics 60%; Resilience 60%; Close Combat 50%; Dodge 40%.

**Will:** carry anything and fight anyone, but not a dragon. At Stirring he drops his end of the Score and runs, unless someone gives him a better idea.

### Wick

_The hands._ A quick, neat goblin with clever fingers and a habit of humming while she works, which is why Lucan keeps telling her to stop. Her heat sight is no use under the hill: everything glows.

**Goblin Hireling 50 (Dagger `1D4+1`, Light; Blind Sight (heat)).** Mechanisms 60%; Deception 60%; Dodge 50%; Perception 40%.

**Does:** lifts the Golden Score out of its cradle when Lucan signals. It takes her one beat.

**Will:** switch sides for a share and a good reason. She hums the Warm Hill tune without knowing where she heard it.

## The Vasse family

### Gran Perrine Vasse

_The last who remembers._ Eighty-four, tiny, upright, in black lace and a red festival shawl, with a cup of new wine that never seems to empty. She was fourteen when her grandfather Aubin died, and he taught her all four verses with his hand on her head. She is sharp, funny and shamelessly nosy, and she will dance with any stranger with good manners.

**Human Singer 70 (walking stick).** Performance 70%; Lore (Warm Hill) 70%; Persistence 60%; Influence 50%; Perception 50%.

**Knows:** all four verses, the family stories about the old keepers, and that her grandfather made her promise to keep singing "because the hill is asleep, and it must stay asleep".

**Wants:** her grandson home, though she will not say so first.

**Play her:** delighted, then shrewd. _"Nobody asks an old woman about songs unless they want something. Sit down. Pour. Now, what do you want?"_

### Odile Vasse

_The vintner._ Fifty, weathered, with a ledger under one arm and purple to the elbows. She runs the estate, the festival and the family. She is fair, proud and tired, and she does not trust strangers who ask about the tower, because the last person who did was her son.

**Human Vintner 60.** Trade 60%; Influence 60%; Perception 50%; Persistence 60%; Lore (Vineyards) 60%.

**Wants:** a good harvest, and her mother not to fall down the press-house steps.

### Tobin Vasse

_The watch._ Seventeen, gangling and earnest, with a lantern, a cudgel and two enormous hounds, Pip and Must. He guards the tower path because his mother told him to, and he takes it very seriously, because nobody else does. A village girl named Rosalind has been trying to dance with him all night.

**Human Watchman 40 (Club `1D6`).** Perception 50%; Athletics 50%; Close Combat 40%.

**Will:** let the company past for a good reason, a good story, or a good distraction. If he learns the truth, he insists on coming, and his mother will never forgive the company.

## The client

### Lady Isaura Harrow

_The collector._ A cool, elegant woman of forty in grey silk, cousin to a claimant to the vanished throne. She owns two hundred imperial masterpieces, and she has heard every curse ever put on any of them. She is not cruel. She simply never believes anything she cannot buy.

**Human Collector 60.** Trade 60%; Influence 60%; Lore (Imperial Art) 70%; Perception 50%.

**At dawn** she waits at the Empty Chair, the inn at the crossroads, with a carriage and two guards. She pays for the Golden Score, if the company makes her: see [Dawn](01-adventure.md#dawn). Shown the dragon's scale, she goes pale and offers 500 SP for it on the spot.

**Play her:** perfectly polite, faintly bored. _"Every hill in the Vale has a monster in it, darling. None of them has ever sent me a bill."_
