# Running notes

GM only. A one-page reference for the table, then a rules script for new players, spotlights, scaling, rules notes and convention advice.

## At the table

**The job:** learn the song, open the doors, take the Golden Score. **The truth:** the Score keeps a dragon asleep.

**Quiet:** whispering, walking and careful work are free. **Singing the lullaby is always free.**

**The verses:** I. Water opens the tower lock. II. Stone opens the lodge gate. III. Fire settles dream-sparks within 10 m. IV. Sleep opens the engine door and, in the finale, holds the track still for a beat.

**Singing bonuses:** `+1B` each for learning verse IV properly, a score playing or the tuning fork in hand, a chorus of three or more, and a player singing at the table. Gran Perrine sings at 70%.

**The engine:** patch the pipe (climb, then Mechanisms; `+1B` with the lodge's spares) and fix the belt (Athletics to hold, Mechanisms to tighten). Both done: erase 2.

**The Silver Score:** under her right forefoot. Lift the claw (Athletics `-1P`), lever it (Mechanisms), Nerin's shove (`-1P`), or sing her into turning over (Performance).

**The finale beat:** the hill (fill if silent), the company (one thing each), the dragon (at 7+). **Sleep returns** at 3 or less with the Golden Score turning, or the Silver Score turning plus a promise to sing every harvest.

**Lucan's crew:** Lucan 60, Grell 50, Wick 50. They carry the Golden Score (40 kg) at half Movement. Lucan's Persistence is 50.

## Rules in five minutes

Hand out the pregens and [How to play](03-players-and-handouts.md#how-to-play). Then say this, or something like it, and stop as soon as the players are nodding:

1. **"Roll under your skill on D100."** Have everyone roll Perception at once. Point out the Criticals and the Fumbles.
2. **"Bonus and Penalty dice change your tens die."** Have someone roll with `+1B`, keeping the better of two tens dice with the same units die.
3. **"Opposed tests: better result wins; if you both succeed, higher roll wins."**
4. **"You have two Hero Points."** Show the four uses. Tell them that tonight they might want them.
5. **"In a fight, you get one action, one move and one reaction each round."** Show the Major Wound line on a sheet. Then say: _"Tonight, most of your problems will not be solved by fighting. Some of them will get much worse."_
6. **"Sneaking is Deception. Singing is Performance. You are all bad singers. It will not matter as much as you think."**

Everything else, explain when it happens. Start Scene 1 by the fifteen-minute mark.

## Rules notes

### Quiet and noise

The Waking track replaces every test against a sleeping dragon's senses. Do not roll her Perception while she sleeps. Tell the players what counts as loud before it happens, and be generous with whispered plans and slow, careful work. The track should rise because of their choices and their bad luck, never because of a rule they did not know.

### Heat sight under the hill

Pebb, Dori, Wick and anyone else with **Blind Sight (heat)** can use it on the festival grounds and the stair. In the engine cavern and the hoard, the rock is warmer than any body. There, heat sight shows only a glowing fog and works no further than 3 m.

### Fire

Fire damage applies every round of exposure, and metal armour does not help against it. The steam gallery deals `2D6`, and timing the bursts avoids it. A dream-spark's Engulf deals `1D6`, and the target resists with Resilience. Her sleepy sigh deals `2D6`; awake, her Breathe Flame deals `4D6`. Armour applies to both her flames, and dodging either takes `-1P` and halves the damage.

### The scores

The **Golden Score** is a metre long and weighs 40 kg. Two people carry it at half Movement, one strong person at a quarter. Lifting it from its cradle or seating it is a Mechanisms test, or one beat for Wick. It is smaller than a person, so Nerin's measured shove can move it 3 m, which is enough to drop it into the cradle from the gallery.

The **Silver Score** weighs 20 kg, and one person can carry it. It plays the tune without the enchantment and without the fourth verse.

### Falling in the stream

The stream is cold, fast and waist-deep, with deep pools. A fall from the wheel or the stepping-stones deals `1D6` and sweeps the faller `2D6` m downstream. Swimming back is an Athletics test. Anyone carrying the Golden Score into the water drops it, or goes under with it: Athletics at `-2P` to hold on.

### Gran under the hill

If the company tells Gran Perrine the truth, she asks to come. Taking her is a strong choice and a heavy one. She is slow (Move 8 m; the stair takes her twice as long and leaves her Fatigued), but in the finale she is the best singer under the hill: Performance 70%, and she knows every verse. If Lucan sees her and she calls him by name, he stops, and he fights no one standing next to her. She is eighty-four and in a cave with a dragon, and she must be protected. _Nobody gets left behind._ This is the adventure's emotional centre, if the table lets it be.

### Nerin in the hill

- **Measured shove** (M3): one subject no bigger than a person, 3 m, within 15 m: either score, a song-lock's bolts he can see, or a falling friend. Her claw needs extra Reach: an overreach at M4, `-1P`.
- **Hold to the wall** (M3): climbing the pipe ranks, the roots to the Sky Door, or the stair's broken stretches.
- **Force bolts** are loud. Every one fills a box under the hill.

## At the convention

**Content.** Fire and burns, a frail old woman in danger, a family estrangement, and an ancient creature kept captive. Nobody in this adventure needs to die. Mention these in your pitch and use whatever safety tools your table prefers.

**Props make this adventure.**

- **The songsheet,** one for every player. By the end they will know the chorus by heart.
- **The Waking track** in the middle of the table. Cross boxes off slowly; rub them out with ceremony.
- **A music box,** if you own one. Wind it in the Lullaby's cavern; when it stops, Lucan takes the Score.
- **Ask the players to sing.** Offer `+1B` for any line actually sung at the table. Somebody always does.

**Pace.** The festival can swallow an hour if you let it. Give the company the songsheet early and put the fourth verse within reach by 0:45. In the hoard, keep the description short and the silences long.

**Quiet players.** Give them the songsheet and the job of singing. Ask them what the dragon's breathing feels like through their boots.

**Ending on time.** Keep fifteen minutes for Dawn. The dragon's answer, the family at the tower, and Lady Harrow's face are the ending. Do not cut them for one more fight with Grell.

## Setting names used

Everything a player needs is in the brief. The setting supplies only names. **The Warm Hill**, **the Vasse family**, **the Crush** and **the Empty Chair** are new, and could sit in any wine country. **The Crown Vale** is the old imperial heartland of the Crownless Realms, full of vineyards, claimant estates and abandoned signal towers. **The old empire** fell ninety-three years ago, when its last emperor walked into the Hall of Measures to be crowned and never walked out; its works still run, badly. **A claimant** is one of the nobles who claim the vanished throne, and Lady Harrow is cousin to one. **SP** are silver pieces. **The Last Wagon Company** is the pregens' own company, with its promise that nobody gets left behind.
