# Handouts: The Lamp That Went Dark

**GM file: contains spoilers.** Only the quoted text under each handout is player-facing. Reveal individual handouts at the indicated moment; do not share this whole file. The record times are fictional local bells, unrelated to the session's table-time schedule.

## Handout 1: The company's contract

**Give at the briefing.** This is the rescue authority, distinct from Voss's optional private offer.

> **MARCH COUNCIL — EMERGENCY SERVICE COMMISSION**
>
> To the registered partners of the Last Wagon Company:
>
> Enter the White Span's northern maintenance works to locate Survey Team Hallow, assist the living, account for those found, and report the condition of the works. Cooperate with the road watch in keeping the public clear of danger.
>
> Payment: thirty silver pieces per company member, half on acceptance and half on return with an honest report. Treatment of injuries incurred in this service is provided through the House of the Rose Lamp.
>
> Record the custody of recovered Survey papers and Crownwork components. Such components are excluded from salvage shares. This commission does not authorize reopening the crossing or claiming its machinery.
>
> Commissioned: Chief Measurer Tal Orsic, Crown Survey.
>
> Countersigned and sealed: March Council emergency desk.
>
> Access and return arrangements to be acknowledged by the road watch.

## Handout 2: The evacuation order

**Give at the maintenance gate when the courier arrives.** The envelope was genuinely carried by the Post; the statement of evacuation is false. Rusk's office generated it, but there is no identifying signature on this copy that proves its individual author. Do not call it a forgery to players before they compare the records.

> **OFFICE OF CONTINUANCE — RESTORATION CONTROL**
>
> White Span, northern works. Issued at the second afternoon bell, this day.
>
> All personnel of Survey Team Hallow have been evacuated. No living Survey personnel remain within the northern maintenance chambers.
>
> Rescue access is therefore unnecessary. Pending reconciliation of the component inventory, admit only the appointed recovery detail and suspend other entry.
>
> Original component and accompanying field records are to be offered to the restoration clerk for receipt.
>
> By the authority of the Office restoration desk.
>
> [An official impression. A genuine Post delivery mark appears on the envelope.]

## Handout 3: Two records

**Give alongside the order.** Tal provides the copied notebook passage; Ina allows inspection of the gate ledger. Both are essential evidence and require no successful test. The fresh signal comes along an ordinary maintenance bell-cord connecting the refuge to the gate. Its local codes are posted at both ends, and the clerk records the decoded message in plain language. It is not magical long-range communication.

> **From a field notebook; copied last night**
>
> Hallow party: Dena, Fen, Tessa, Jory. Dena cannot manage the stairs unaided. Three will remain in the refuge while I go for help. We need a litter and witnesses from outside the restoration detail. We have kept the records with us.
>
> The counting walk is passable. Measure the journey, not the door.
>
> — Jory

**The gate's separate record:**

> **North maintenance gate ledger; today**
>
> Second afternoon bell: no Survey evacuation passed this gate. Recovery detail admitted on restoration papers.
>
> Third afternoon bell: refuge signal received. Hallow identification, three persons, injury, assistance required. Answered from this desk; distress sequence repeated in reply.
>
> No Survey personnel have emerged since Jory Pell's departure last night.
>
> Recorded by the duty watch clerk.

The line about no personnel passing the gate is corroboration, not a claim that this one ledger proves every possible exit was watched. The fresh identified signal is the decisive reason to continue the rescue. Bren's crew does not interrupt the refuge's bell-cord.

## Handout 4: The counting door

**Give at the Gate landing.** Read the first quotation immediately. Supply the measured results as soon as the party walks or measures the routes, or when offering the second hint. Characters who inspect the latch receive the indicator description automatically.

> **On the lintel:**
>
> THE LONG ROAD ACCOUNTS. THE SHORT ROAD ADMITS.
>
> **The corridor:**
>
> You stand at GATE, beside the locked maintenance door. A corridor runs around the same pier and returns here. One entrance reaches LAMP first. The other reaches RIVER first. Each station bears its name and a carved symbol.
>
> Chalk arrows point both ways. One is labeled eighteen; the other thirty. A portable measuring chain hangs on a peg.
>
> **At the latch:**
>
> Two small indicators are labeled LAMP and RIVER. An instruction reads: “Register both stations in passage; return to Gate.” The open-hand pointer faces the return from River. The latch clicks forward only when the stations register in the short journey's order.

**Reveal when measured or as a hint:**

> **Measured results:**
>
> GATE → LAMP → RIVER → GATE: eighteen standard paces.
>
> GATE → RIVER → LAMP → GATE: thirty standard paces.
>
> Both routes go around the same pier and return to the same landing. Floor marks let the chain confirm the distances regardless of your own stride.

**GM solution:** complete Gate → Lamp → River → Gate, then open the door. Partial journeys do not register both stations. Once opened, the ordinary door can be held or wedged for everyone. The measuring chain is a loose tool, not something that tightens by itself. Trial, measurement, supplied instructions, or asking Fen all count as solving the problem.

## Handout 5: The exchange record

**Reveal in the rod chamber and refuge.** These are two different sources. The delivery docket remains in the empty packing case even if Bren took the records tube. The field observations are in that tube; if unavailable, Tessa states them from her experience and helps the party make a fresh written account. Do not silently hand over a lost original.

> **Delivery docket: White Span / Ninth Route**
>
> Dated: two days before this inspection.
>
> Deliver replacement rod for temporary restoration exchange. Remove original to padded return case pending collection. Retain all seating labels. Independent field inspection to be completed before the replacement is put into service.
>
> Authorizing surveyor: Cale Rusk, Office of Continuance.
>
> Released by: Maelin Voss, Castaine restoration desk.
>
> Inspection exception, written at release: “Proceed on the authorizing surveyor's instruction; independent inspection to follow.” — M. Voss
>
> Related works file: Blackglass acoustic survey loan.

**The team's separate record:**

> **Hallow field observations: yesterday**
>
> Arrival: eight rods sound under comparison. Ninth is already seated but gives no answering hum. Fresh tool marks at its collar. No removal by this team.
>
> Opened return case. Original rod inside, sounding through its padded cradle. Secured it against collection. Left seated replacement untouched.
>
> Replacement bears a Ninefold archive mark. Record it exactly; do not infer manufacture or responsibility from the mark alone.
>
> Spatial lurch during inspection. Dena injured. Dena, Fen, and Tessa withdrawing to refuge with original and field record. Jory taking a copy out.
>
> Recorded by Tessa Mar; witnessed by Dena Hallow and Fen Kel.

The field record uses “yesterday” and the docket's relative date here to keep the draft independent of a campaign calendar. Assign actual consecutive local dates if the campaign already has one; retain the ordering: release and exchange, then Hallow's arrival, then today's false evacuation statement.

## Handout 7: The recovery detail's work order

**Show if the party examines Bren's papers or receives them with his surrender.** These papers are genuine. They support his presence below without supporting assault, arrest, or exclusive seizure. Voss's earlier copy can also supply the entry directions if obtained in Scene 2.

> **RESTORATION DESK — RECOVERY DETAIL**
>
> Bren Marr and assistants are employed to locate the original ninth route-rod and accompanying Survey records, record their condition, and arrange their delivery to the restoration desk.
>
> Preserve the material and account for its handling. Refer disputed custody to the responsible gate authority. This is a property recovery commission; no arrest authority is granted.
>
> Clerk responsible: Maelin Voss.
>
> Maintenance directions: at Gate, follow Lamp, then River, then return to Gate and lift the latch. Hold the door for your companions. Leave acoustic shutters secured.

Bren knows the dispute clause. His willingness to force a quick outcome comes from fear of losing the fee and his employer's favor, not from a secret legal right the PCs cannot challenge.
