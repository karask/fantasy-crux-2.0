# Supporting NPCs

**GM only.** Compact profiles for contacts and future leads. The main cast is in [04-npcs-and-stat-design.md](04-npcs-and-stat-design.md). These characters add no compulsory scenes or fights to the [four-hour adventure](01-adventure.md).

## Reading the shorthand

These use the [Minor NPCs](../../src/content/rules/gm-tools/minor-npcs.md) approach: related skills follow the specialty; others fall in ten-point steps. Skill-band numbers are percentages.

Unless overridden, use **HP 10/10, MWL 5, PP 10/10, POW 10, DEX 10, Combat Order 10, Movement 15 m, AP 0, and DM +0**. Other characteristics are 10 if an unusual situation needs one. Combat Order is the final value: do not subtract armour again. All begin without conditions, active Shapings, or committed PP.

All unlisted general nonmagical skills are **20%**; unfamiliar Lore is **10%** when some relevant knowledge is plausible, and unlisted Languages are **0%**. Impossible knowledge gets no roll. Everyone has own **Language (River Crown) 60%**, except Neris and Pella, who have learned **River Crown 60%** and own **Kesh 70%** and **Briarlilt 70%**, respectively. These language choices are individual adventure details.

No NPC has Hero Points, Talents, or Shaping unless listed. Weapon damage is the **base before DM**; add DM once for close, unarmed, or thrown attacks. Fists use Unarmed Combat, base `1D3`, Light. Listed weapons meet requirements and equipment stays within carrying capacity. No passive shield defence or unlisted manoeuvres apply.

## Doctor Samel Ancor — surgeon 80

A human with rolled sleeves and immaculate instruments. “Tell me what hurts. Then tell me what happened.” Ancor wants successful treatment and accurate observations. His pride in mundane medicine is established; his appearance and human ancestry are additions.

| Trait           | Value                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Skills          | **80:** Healing, Lore (Medicine). **70:** Perception, Persistence. **60:** Influence, Resilience, Natural Lore. **40:** Trade, Deception. **30:** Dodge. **20:** Athletics, Close Combat, Unarmed Combat.                                                 |
| Body            | Shared defaults.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Gear and attack | Two full Healing Kits, 5 attempts each; surgical instruments, clean bedding, and safe working space at the House of the Rose Lamp. Unarmed Combat 20%, `1D3`, Light; no readied weapon.                                                                   |
| Abilities       | **Physician.** Successful treatment grants the patient `+2B`, replacing the usual `+1B`, against poison or disease; a treated patient may make one further resistance test against a poison that already defeated them. **No Shaping; no Field Surgeon.** |

Ancor's ordinary Surgery uses Healing in safe conditions outside combat, restores **1 HP**, and unlocks natural recovery. It does not immediately remove Wounded unless current HP is now greater than MWL. Physician does not increase surgery's HP restoration. A failed attempt expends a kit use; neither paid care nor a high skill guarantees every roll succeeds. See [Physician](../../src/content/rules/talents/physician.md) and [Healing and Recovery](../../src/content/rules/adventuring/healing-and-recovery.md).

## Sister Irra Vale — healer and Shaper 60

A human untangling a washed cord before touching a patient. “May I examine the injury?” Irra wants proper treatment and honestly reports her remaining strength. Her ancestry and manner are additions; surgical Shaping at the Rose Lamp is established.

| Trait           | Value                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Skills          | **60:** Healing, Shaping, Persistence, Lore (Spirit World). **50:** Perception, Influence, Resilience. **40:** Lore (Medicine). **30:** Athletics, Dodge. **20:** Close Combat, Unarmed Combat, Deception.                                                  |
| Body            | Shared defaults. **Safe Magnitude 3; maximum Intensity 3; active Magnitude limit 10.**                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Gear and attack | Two full Healing Kits, 5 attempts each; washed cord and clean medical supplies. Unarmed Combat 20%, `1D3`, Light; no readied weapon.                                                                                                                        |
| Abilities       | **Shaping 60%; Flesh specialty; known cells: Alter·Flesh, Scry·Flesh, Ward·Spirit.** Universal Sense Magic and Dispel. Practice: spoken prayer while tracing the washed cord. Tell: seven faint shadows gather around her and the target. No other Talents. |

**Ready working — treat a Major Wound:** `Alter·Flesh; I3, R0, D0, Reach0 = M3`. Touch one willing patient; spend one Combat Action and roll Shaping 60%. Success restores `3D4` HP, capped at maximum, removes Bleeding and Dying, and counts as Surgery. Wounded ends only once HP exceeds MWL. A patient restored above 0 HP can act next turn. No defence roll is needed from a willing patient.

Pay **3 PP on Success, 2 PP on Critical, 1 PP on an ordinary safe Failure, or 3 PP plus Backlash on Fumble**. A critical does not maximize healing. Confirm at least 3 current PP before attempting this working. It is one immediate treatment, with no repeated healing from Duration.

For diagnosis, `Scry·Flesh; I2, touch, instant, one willing patient = M2` reveals bodily condition, not memories or honesty. `Ward·Spirit; I2, self/touch, D1, Reach0 = M3` can grant `+1B` to Persistence against possession for one scene or ten minutes on a willing souled subject. It grants neither weapon armour nor automatic removal of existing possession.

Irra's presence and current PP are not guaranteed. If she enters play fresh, use the listed full pool; otherwise record actual expenditure. Doctor Ancor remains a mundane treatment option.

## Cale Rusk — surveyor 70

A human with carefully maintained boots and a soft voice: “Distinguish cause from coincidence.” Rusk wants interference blamed on the old works while retaining control of repairs. His personality is new; his Pale Cabinet affiliation and signed orders come from GM material.

| Trait           | Value                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Skills          | **70:** Engineering, Lore (Crownworks), Lore (Law and Heraldry). **60:** Shaping, Deception, Persistence. **50:** Influence, Perception, Mechanisms. **40:** Resilience, Dodge. **30:** Athletics, Close Combat, Unarmed Combat. **20:** Healing.                        |
| Body            | Shared defaults. **Safe Magnitude 3; maximum Intensity 3; active Magnitude limit 10.**                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Gear and attack | Dagger: Close Combat 30%, `1D4+1`, Light; measuring chain, chalk, writing kit, Office papers, brass survey needle. No enchanted protective item or concealed combat weapon.                                                                                              |
| Abilities       | **Shaping 60%; Scry specialty; known cells: Scry·Earth/Stone, Scry·Paths, Ward·Paths.** Universal Sense Magic and Dispel. Practice: tap a brass needle against the measured surface while reciting a measure. Tell: nearby brass edges appear doubled. No other Talents. |

**Ready observations:** `Scry·Earth/Stone; I1, touch, instant, Reach0 = M1` detects a named natural mineral in the touched stone. `Scry·Paths` with the same ratings reveals one immediate route clue at a touched threshold, such as which approach is shortened. Neither reveals a conspirator's intentions.

**Ready protection:** `Ward·Paths; I2, self, D1, Reach0 = M3` grants `+1B` to Resilience against hostile magical relocation for one scene or ten minutes. It supplies no general Dodge bonus, weapon AP, or immunity to ordinary grappling. Rusk must cast and pay normally; he does not start with it active.

Rusk is an offscreen lead in this session. These statistics support a later interview, inspection, or escape attempt; they are not a tested boss encounter. His known cells include no Harm, mind control, or teleportation, and his signature does not reveal his superiors automatically.

## Shared magic and source notes

The two Shapers know only their listed cells. Sense Magic costs one Combat Action and 1 PP and requires a Shaping test; ordinary success identifies active magic's location and Form within 15 m. Dispel uses its normal Magnitude, cost, and opposed procedure, not an automatic dismissal. Practice is interruptible; Tell is noticeable within `10 × Magnitude` metres, minimum 3 m. No listed NPC has Veiled. At 0 PP a living Shaper falls unconscious after the effect resolves.

Use [Becoming a Shaper](../../src/content/rules/magic/becoming-a-shaper.md), [Building a Shaping](../../src/content/rules/magic/building-a-shaping.md), [Casting and Defence](../../src/content/rules/magic/casting-and-defence.md), [Effects](../../src/content/rules/magic/effects.md), and [Universal magical actions](../../src/content/rules/magic/ongoing-and-magical-actions.md) for other proposed workings. Known additional cells represent these experienced NPCs' prior learning, not a different starting-character entitlement.

Public contacts derive from the [published Gazetteer](../../src/content/rules/gazetteer/index.md); the notebook sale and Rusk's secret role use the [Crownless Realms GM material](../../potential-worlds/the-crownless-realms/the-crownless-realms.md). See [setting and design notes](06-setting-and-design-notes.md) for that distinction. Use the current [weapon table](../../src/content/rules/equipment/weapons.md) when changing equipment.
