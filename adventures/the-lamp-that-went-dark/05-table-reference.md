# Table reference

**GM only.** Use with [the full adventure](01-adventure.md). Use the completed [NPC profiles](04-npcs-and-stat-design.md) for the main cast and [supporting profiles](07-supporting-npcs.md) for contacts. Choose PCs from the [eight complete Last Wagon Company sheets](../../pregenerated-characters/README.md); their creation arithmetic is checked, while encounter balance remains unplaytested.

## The facts to remember

- Voss supplied the replacement under Rusk's restoration order and waived an independent inspection. She did not know it was mundane.
- Hallow found the copy already installed, recovered the original from its outgoing case, and kept it from collection. Three survivors remain below; the fourth, Jory, already escaped.
- The evacuation order is false. A fresh, identified refuge signal came after its claimed evacuation time. A genuine seal and delivery do not establish truth.
- Bren has legitimate recovery employment but no right of arrest or exclusive seizure. He wants a completed job and living employees.
- The session's success is rescue, evidence, custody, and a safely closed crossing. The rod is an anchor, not loot, and full repair is a later project.

## Area connections in words

**A, chapel yard:** briefing and final hearing. **B, service gate:** cordon, papers, access to the stair. **C, stair:** reaches **D, counting walk**, which loops around one pier and opens into **E, recovery gallery**. The gallery's far door reaches **G, refuge**; its side shutter reaches **F, rod chamber**. The exit follows the same short route in reverse; the counting door opens normally from inside. Gargoyle vents are secured and outside the rescue route.

**Puzzle:** Gate → Lamp → River → Gate measures eighteen standard paces and opens the door. The reverse measures thirty. Both stations must register; partial out-and-back journeys do not. Visible indicators explain this. Let everyone through once it opens. Wrong attempts do no damage. Measurement, trying both routes, a latch bypass, or help can succeed.

## Fight at a glance

Four PCs face **Bren, Kett, and Rill**; three face Bren and Kett; five add Orven. Clubs are used one-handed, with no damage bonus, ranged attacks, Charge, or special combat Talents.

| NPC                       | HP / MWL | Order | AP  | Club attack / Parry | Dodge | Other key test                                                                           |
| ------------------------- | -------- | ----- | --- | ------------------- | ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bren                      | 10 / 5   | 7     | 2   | 50%; `1D6`, Light   | 40%   | Athletics 50%; group Intimidate resistance Influence 40%, single-target Persistence 30%. |
| Each hireling             | 10 / 5   | 10    | 0   | 40%; `1D6`, Light   | 30%   | Athletics/Perception 40%; Persistence 20%.                                               |
| Tessa, protected by hatch | 10 / 5   | 13    | 0   | Not an attacker     | 30%   | Athletics 20% for the tube.                                                              |

Bren and the hirelings all have DEX 10, Movement 15 m, DM `+0`, and no Hero Points. Order already accounts for armour ENC. Dena starts at **4/12 HP, MWL 6, Wounded and stable**. Fen has **Healing 30%** and a kit with four attempts left.

Tessa shows the arriving rescuers the seal on the records tube; Bren catches its dangling strap. On his first turn, if still contested, he spends his Combat Action on one Athletics opposed by Tessa's Athletics: he wins the tube or she pulls it safely inside. Resolve earlier PC interventions first. Do not repeat tug rolls. Bren tries to leave with a won tube using the normal action rules.

Teach one Combat Action, one Movement Action, and one base Reaction per five-second round. The benches can provide partial cover where appropriate; a canvas screen cannot stop missiles. A PC can lower the baffle lever as a Combat Action, freeing conversation without settling possession of the tube.

Bren voluntarily stops when a hireling suffers a serious disabling injury, the company controls both tube and open refuge, or his exclusive seizure is shown indefensible. An acceptable custody bargain can stop the fight earlier. These are motive-based decisions, separate from forcing surrender with combat Intimidate.

## Rules to have open

- [Basic tests](../../src/content/rules/skills/basic-tests.md) and [Bonus/Penalty dice](../../src/content/rules/skills/bonus-and-penalty-dice.md): roll at or below skill; choose modifier-die candidates by grade, then higher success or lower failure. Cancel modifiers, cap at three.
- [Opposed tests](../../src/content/rules/skills/opposed-tests.md): grade first; higher within the same successful grade, lower within failed grades; exact ties use higher base skill, then defender/status quo.
- [Assistance](../../src/content/rules/skills/assistance.md): normally at least 26% relevant skill; meaningful help grants `+1B` total, not per helper. [Group tests](../../src/content/rules/gm-tools/calling-for-tests.md): weakest Deception once when everyone must pass; best observing Perception once.
- [Attacks and Reactions](../../src/content/rules/combat/attacks-and-reactions.md) and [combat result matrix](../../src/content/rules/combat/critical-hits-and-fumbles.md): resolve combat defenses with their own procedure, not generic opposed-roll comparison. Subdue, Disarm, and Trip need Talents.
- [Rounds and Actions](../../src/content/rules/combat/rounds-and-actions.md): Combat Order, Withdraw, and the particular restrictions on combat Intimidate. Full-strength enemies are difficult to force into surrender.
- [Damage and Wounds](../../src/content/rules/combat/damage-and-wounds.md): introduce Hero Points before danger. A Major Wound causes Wounded and Bleeding. At 0 HP, resolve Dying immediately; promises of mercy do not stop it.
- [Healing and Recovery](../../src/content/rules/adventuring/healing-and-recovery.md): stabilization takes a full round including movement and reactions, with a Healing test; it restores no HP. Dena starts stable. Ordinary post-encounter Healing does not treat a Major Wound as an ordinary injury.

## Close the loop

Name the survivors' destination, the rod's custodian or sealed location, the holders of originals and copies, and the public account. Pay the contract, arrange care, award the recommended 4 IP, and choose one lead: Blackglass, Jory/Neris, formal restoration, contradictory orders, or Voss's next approach. Record successes so future adventures build on them.
