# People of the darkened Span

GM reference for **The Lamp That Went Dark**. The profiles below have fixed, usable game values, equipment, abilities, and behavior. They use a compact extension of [Minor NPCs](../../src/content/rules/gm-tools/minor-npcs.md). The [eight pregenerated PC sheets](../../pregenerated-characters/README.md) are complete and checked against character-creation budgets; encounter balance has not been playtested against them.

Tal Orsic, Hesta Coil, Doctor Samel Ancor, Pella Underleaf, Aunt Neris Skell, Cale Rusk, and Team Hallow come from the setting. Dena's first name and the team's individual members, Ina Tern, Maelin Voss, and Bren Marr are additions for this adventure. Tal's rescue contract is countersigned by the March Council for this scenario.

## Reading the compact profiles

The format is **Name — people and role Rating (equipment; abilities; exceptions)**. The rating is the NPC's specialty percentage, as in the Minor NPCs examples; it is not a level or a score in every skill. Each profile's table explicitly groups skills by percentage, normally stepping down by 10 points as they become less relevant. Use the listed skills directly; never add a characteristic base to them.

> **Bren Marr — Human Recovery Foreman 50 (Club `1D6`, Light; Leather 2; Order 7).** His table gives Close Combat and Athletics 50%, Perception and Dodge 40%, and Persistence 30%. He is good at recovering property, not equally expert at every task.

These **adventure notation defaults** extend the published shorthand; they do not change the core rules:

| Omitted entry         | Use                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Body and resources    | HP 10/10, MWL 5, PP 10/10; characteristics 10 unless a profile overrides them.                                                                                                                                      |
| Actions and movement  | Combat Order 10, DEX 10 for initiative ties, Movement 15 m per round; one normal Combat Action, Movement Action, and base Reaction.                                                                                 |
| Protection and damage | AP 0, DM `+0`. No Hero Points, Talents, or Shaping unless explicitly listed.                                                                                                                                        |
| Other general skills  | 20%. An unfamiliar Lore is 10% only when the character could reasonably know the answer; impossible knowledge gets no roll. These are the lowest applicable shorthand bands, not extra skills to add to the tables. |
| Language              | All members of this local cast speak River Crown 60%. Listed additional languages are separate; an unlisted language is not automatically known.                                                                    |
| Unarmed attack        | Unarmed Combat at the listed score, `1D3 + DM`, Light.                                                                                                                                                              |
| Equipment notation    | Weapon damage is **before DM**. Add DM once. A weapon's Size controls Parry; an armour number is AP.                                                                                                                |
| Prepared state        | Start at full listed HP and PP with no ongoing magic, except where current state is explicitly stated. All listed equipment is within carrying capacity and weapon minima.                                          |

**Order is final:** the values below already subtract armour ENC. Roll `1D10 + Order` for initiative; do not subtract armour again. Compare DEX on tied totals, then reroll if still tied. MWL always uses maximum HP, not current HP.

## Chief Measurer Tal Orsic — the patron

An exhausted dwarf of the Crown Survey, Tal is an exacting mentor who corrects a measurement before noticing his tea has gone cold. He is capable of institutional secrecy and personal kindness in the same conversation. He lies, when necessary, in complete sentences.

**Appearance:** A broad dwarf with a forked grey beard and reddened eyes, wearing a faded Survey coat with carefully patched elbows. A brass caliper hangs from a cord against his chest.

**Dwarf Crown Survey Measurer 70 (Dagger `1D4+1`, Light; survey tools; Shaping 60%; Blind Sight [heat], Earth Sense).**

| Skill % | Skills                                             |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| 70      | Engineering; Lore (Crownworks); Lore (Magic).      |
| 60      | Shaping; Perception; Mechanisms; Language (Karic). |
| 50      | Influence; Persistence; Resilience.                |
| 40      | Deception; Trade.                                  |
| 30      | Athletics; Close Combat; Dodge; Healing.           |
| 20      | Unarmed Combat; Ranged Combat.                     |

**Equipment and abilities:** Dagger attack/Parry 30%; measuring chain, brass caliper, chalk, Survey seal, document case. Talent: **Shaping**, Scry specialty; known cells **Scry·Paths**, **Scry·Earth/Stone**, **Ward·Paths**. Maximum Intensity 3, Safe Magnitude 3, active limit 16; PP 14. His measured recitation and aligned brass tools form the Practice; brass edges visibly double as the Tell. See the ready working below. He remains at the gate managing public safety and official access, rather than joining the expedition.

- **Voice:** Quiet, complete, precise. “Four people went below. The instruments can wait.” He listens closely when someone distinguishes an observation from an assumption.
- **Wants:** Team Hallow brought back alive, their readings preserved, and the Span protected from an improvised repair.
- **Leverage:** A concrete plan for getting people home, competent handling of evidence, and an arrangement in which rival witnesses can inspect without seizing control. A reminder that silence endangers workers reaches him more deeply than an accusation of cowardice.
- **Knows:** The team's assignment, the maintenance access, and the broad pattern of the Span's recent faults. The Survey has reasons to believe the Crownworks are connected, but that is background knowledge, not an answer he volunteers during the rescue briefing. He has not inspected the replacement rod and does not know the full chain of interference.
- **Limits:** He cannot grant House Veyran's road powers, waive another office's lawful responsibilities, or promise that the Central Measure will approve public release. His Council-countersigned contract authorizes the rescue and recovery of the team's evidence; it does not settle ownership of the Crownwork.
- **Play him:** He is the party's useful patron, not a concealed final villain. Pressure over the evidence can reveal his institution's secrecy without making the rescue itself fraudulent.

## Sergeant Ina Tern — the road watch

A human officer serving the Veyran road warden. Ina is humane, methodical, and painfully aware that a small act at this gate can become a claimant's grievance. She checks a signature while moving a bench into place for an injured traveller.

**Appearance:** A square-shouldered human woman with cropped black hair and a pale eyebrow scar. Her faded Veyran scarf is tucked securely away from her working hands.

**Human Road-Watch Sergeant 50 (Shortsword `1D6`, Medium; Medium Shield `1D6`, Heavy; Leather 2; Order 10).**

| Skill % | Skills                                                        |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 50      | Close Combat; Perception; Influence; Lore (Law and Heraldry). |
| 40      | Athletics; Dodge; Unarmed Combat; Resilience; Persistence.    |
| 30      | Deception; Healing; Ranged Combat.                            |
| 20      | Engineering; Trade.                                           |

**Equipment and abilities:** Shortsword or shield attack 50%; Parry 50% with either ready item, using its Size. Leather armour ENC 3 is already included in Order. Keys, whistle, watch ledger, seal, and a station Healing Kit with 5 uses. No Talents or Shaping. She can defend her post but is not an extra PC combatant below. Use **Persistence 40%** for resisting the access concession and **Perception 50%** against false credentials or a disguised entrant.

- **Voice:** Short practical statements. “Show me who takes responsibility. Then I can open the gate.”
- **Wants:** A safe road, living surveyors, and a clear account of who entered and what they brought out.
- **Leverage:** The countersigned rescue contract, an agreed return signal, credible precautions, and a clear distinction between rescuing people and claiming machinery. Good documentation helps her say yes.
- **Knows:** Who officially passed her post, which papers were presented, and the recent visible symptoms at the Span. She can recognize Voss's recovery authority as genuine while also explaining its limits.
- **Limits:** She is not a Castaine agent and has no private knowledge of the false rod. She will not surrender her post or send the whole watch into an unexamined passage. She has no reason to arrest the party merely for contradicting a clerk.
- **Play her:** Procedure gives the party something concrete to address. A persuasive, workable proposal can succeed through conversation; call for Influence only if a meaningful point remains contested.

## Maelin Voss — the clerk who delivered the wrong thing

A human Castaine restoration clerk. Voss remembers the exact wording of a receipt and straightens its edge whenever her confidence wavers. She supplied the replacement on Cale Rusk's restoration order and believes that Team Hallow stole the original.

**Appearance:** A slender human woman with neatly pinned chestnut hair and immaculate cuffs. She keeps rubbing an ink stain on one thumb as if it were the source of her trouble.

**Human Restoration Clerk 60 (Dagger `1D4+1`, Light; restoration orders, private ledger and seal).**

| Skill % | Skills                                                                         |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 60      | Trade; Lore (Law and Heraldry); Culture (Crown Vale); Language (Crown Speech). |
| 50      | Influence; Deception.                                                          |
| 40      | Perception; Persistence.                                                       |
| 30      | Dodge; Resilience.                                                             |
| 20      | Close Combat; Unarmed Combat; Athletics; Healing.                              |

**Equipment and abilities:** Dagger attack/Parry 20%; leather document case, ink and paper, Office order copy, personal seal. No Talents or Shaping. Her authority and access to records are social resources, not magical bonuses. Use **Perception 40%** against a switched sketch or hidden duplicate, and **Influence 50%** to oppose the final disputed custody concession. She withdraws behind the watch if threatened with weapons.

- **Voice:** Polite, defensive specificity. “The delivery was authorized. I can show you the order.” Under pressure she starts repeating dates instead of answering the question.
- **Wants:** The original recovered, her paperwork vindicated, and sole custody of the evidence before another claimant can turn it into a public accusation.
- **Leverage:** Separate her documented actions from the intentions of whoever issued the order. Offer witnessed joint custody, an accurate record of her cooperation, and a chance to compare the original, replacement, and delivery record without destroying or hiding anything.
- **Knows:** Her order came from Cale Rusk; she arranged the delivery and hired Bren's recovery crew. She recognizes her own handling and delivery records. She knowingly bypassed the required independent inspection to meet Rusk's deadline and conceals that embarrassing shortcut until confronted with the record. Until shown the physical and written evidence together, she does not understand that Hallow recovered the original after discovering the substitution.
- **Limits:** Voss is not a Pale Cabinet insider. She cannot explain the wider Crownwork plan, cannot identify every participant in it, and cannot prove that a claimant leader ordered sabotage. Her documents establish a recovery interest, not an exclusive claim to everything found below.
- **Play her:** She can conceal an embarrassing detail, press an unsupported interpretation, or bargain hard without knowing she helped sabotage the Span. When the evidence changes, let her change her position. Witnessed joint custody is a real concession she can make.

## Bren Marr — the recovery foreman

A human mercenary hired by Voss. Bren normally makes a living by returning valuable property intact. He checks knots twice, pays attention to exits, and becomes abruptly reckless when he thinks an entire contract is slipping away.

**Appearance:** A broad-shouldered human man with weathered cheeks and a crooked nose. His recovery order is pinned flat against his chest where nobody can miss its stamp.

**Human Recovery Foreman 50 (Club `1D6`, Light, one-handed; Leather 2; Order 7).**

| Skill % | Skills                                                    |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| 50      | Close Combat; Athletics.                                  |
| 40      | Perception; Dodge; Resilience; Unarmed Combat; Influence. |
| 30      | Persistence; Deception; Engineering; Mechanisms.          |
| 20      | Healing; Ranged Combat; Trade.                            |

**Equipment and abilities:** Manufactured club attack/Parry 50%; leather armour ENC 3 already included in Order; 10 m rope, the work order, and an empty receipt book. No Talents, Shaping, shield, or ranged weapon. His club is used in **one hand**, dealing `1D6 + 0`, not the two-handed Flex damage. For the tube, use **Athletics 50% against Tessa's 20%**; this single Combat Action settles the pull. For combat Intimidate, a single-target demand opposes his **Persistence 30%**; a demand to his whole crew opposes his higher **Influence 40%**, before the enemy-condition modifier.

- **Voice:** Blunt and possessive. “That tube goes to my employer. Nobody needs to get hurt over paper.” When frightened he talks faster, not louder.
- **Wants:** Something he can hand Voss to demonstrate that he completed the recovery. Faced with the rod's apparent value and disputed ownership, he tries to seize the field-notes tube rather than risk carrying the rod away himself.
- **Leverage:** A written receipt for evidence placed in witnessed joint custody; a clear instruction from Voss to stand down; or the visible failure of his attempted seizure. He values his crew's lives and will not spend them to win an argument about a contract.
- **Knows:** Who employed him, what the recovery papers actually say, and what he has personally seen. He knows nothing of the substitution before this expedition and nothing of the Pale Cabinet.
- **Limits:** His salvage/recovery authority is genuine. It grants neither arrest powers nor an exclusive mandate. He cannot legally invent those powers by waving his papers. He has no surprise reinforcements, combat Shaping, concealed missile weapon, or special combat Talent in this encounter design.
- **Play him:** Make his intended seizure clear before resolving it. His fight has a practical objective. If blocked, hurt, disarmed of the tube by circumstances, or confronted with a settlement that satisfies his employer, he can choose to stop. Do not make him fight to the death to protect a secret he does not know.

## Dena Hallow — the injured expedition leader

A human field surveyor whose first concern is still whether everyone else has been counted. She is already Wounded but stable when found. She is conscious, no longer Bleeding, and not Dying; avoid introducing an undisclosed three-round treatment deadline.

**Appearance:** A lean, middle-aged human woman with silver at her temples, seated with her splinted leg supported. Her gaze stays level even when her hands shake with pain.

**Human Field Surveyor 60 (Dagger `1D4+1`, Light; survey notebook; HP 4/12, Wounded and stable).**

| Skill % | Skills                                          |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| 60      | Engineering; Lore (Crownworks).                 |
| 50      | Perception; Influence; Mechanisms; Persistence. |
| 40      | Resilience; Healing; Deception.                 |
| 30      | Athletics; Dodge; Close Combat; Unarmed Combat. |
| 20      | Ranged Combat.                                  |

**Equipment and abilities:** Dagger attack/Parry 30% before Wounded's `-1P`; survey case, personal field notes, splint, and the original rod in its padded cradle. No Talents or Shaping. Custody of the anchor gives her no spell or control over the Span. Fen already stopped her Bleeding; mundane Surgery would restore 1 HP, unlock recovery, and leave Wounded until her HP exceeds 6. A qualifying magical treatment follows its own healing rules. Her visible condition does not force her to make a test to give testimony.

- **Voice:** Tired, economical, stubborn. “We found the copy in the machine. We took the original out of the packing case. Write both sentences down.”
- **Wants:** Her workers rescued and cleared of theft, the evidence preserved, and enough independent witnesses that the discovery cannot quietly disappear into one office.
- **Leverage:** Tend her companions, distinguish rescue from custody, and promise a record that can be checked. An offer to exchange the rod blindly for absolution meets a firm refusal.
- **Knows:** The installed rod was a dull replacement. She recovered the original from an outbound packing case after discovering the discrepancy. She knows where each object was found, what her team observed, and why they stopped interfering with the mechanism.
- **Limits:** She did not witness the initial substitution and cannot name its ultimate author from observation. Her account and physical evidence implicate the delivery chain, not every Castaine official. She cannot certify a safe restoration without a full survey.
- **Play her:** Her insistence on shared evidence is compatible with thanking the rescuers. She gives the facts needed to understand the twist without demanding a successful social roll. Trust determines cooperation over custody, not whether players receive the premise's essential information.

A goblin engineer with precise hands and a dry sense of humour. Fen treats a claim about a machine as something that should survive an inspection.

**Appearance:** A wiry goblin with cropped dark hair and soot ingrained around their nails. Every pocket of their tool apron has been repaired with a different piece of cloth.

**Goblin Maintenance Engineer 60 (Dagger `1D4+1`, Light; tools; Healing Kit, 4 uses left; Blind Sight [heat]).**

| Skill % | Skills                                                      |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| 60      | Engineering.                                                |
| 50      | Mechanisms; Craft (Carpentry); Perception; Language (Kesh). |
| 40      | Athletics; Resilience; Persistence.                         |
| 30      | Healing; Dodge; Close Combat; Unarmed Combat; Deception.    |
| 20      | Influence; Ranged Combat.                                   |

**Equipment and abilities:** Dagger attack/Parry 30%; carpenter's tools, wedges, chalk, padding, and a Healing Kit. One of its five uses treated Dena before play; **four attempts remain**, with no automatic Bonus die. No Talents or Shaping. Fen can provide relevant assistance or operate ordinary maintenance gear. Stabilization uses **Healing 30%**, a kit use, and a full stationary round including Movement and Reactions; it is not guaranteed rescue magic.

- **Limits:** Fen does not know who ordered the interference or possess a complete model of the Crownworks. Engineering competence does not make every concealed hazard automatically known.
- **Play them:** Use Fen to clarify what the characters can see and to confirm a sound solution. Allow the players to make the discovery and choose the action; Fen need not deliver a puzzle lecture on arrival.

## Using the completed encounter

The four-player encounter is **Bren, Kett, and Rill**. For three players, use **Bren and Kett**. For five, add **Orven**. The individual profiles do not change with party size. Named allies stay out of its action economy except for Tessa's protected tube contest and necessary rescue care.

| Participant         | Club attack / Parry | Dodge | HP / MWL | Armour | Order | Main non-attack use                                                           |
| ------------------- | ------------------- | ----- | -------- | ------ | ----- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bren                | 50%; `1D6`, Light   | 40%   | 10 / 5   | 2 AP   | 7     | Athletics 50% for the tube; Influence 40% for group resistance to Intimidate. |
| Each hireling       | 40%; `1D6`, Light   | 30%   | 10 / 5   | 0 AP   | 10    | Athletics 40%; guard a passage, operate a lever, carry a companion.           |
| Tessa, behind hatch | Not an attacker     | 30%   | 10 / 5   | 0 AP   | 13    | Athletics 20% opposes Bren's single pull; no Reaction cost.                   |

Bren voluntarily calls a halt when one hireling is down with a serious injury, the company controls the tube and open refuge, or his attempted exclusive seizure becomes indefensible. Kett and Rill follow him; Orven gives first priority to an injured colleague. An acceptable witnessed custody agreement can end the conflict before that. A won tube is something Bren tries to carry out using normal Movement or Withdraw, not a free escape.

The crew does not execute fallen characters, attack the rods, call reinforcements, or gain free Disarm, Trip, or Subdue. Clubs still cause ordinary damage, Major Wounds, and possible death. If mercy is intended, resolve Bleeding and Dying rather than assuming a declaration of surrender removes them.

These fixed values complete the NPC design for this scenario. **They have not been playtested against finished pregens.** During PC preparation, check that each character can defend themselves, that the company has credible attacks and Healing, and that Hero Points are explained. Do not silently raise enemy damage or armour because some characters favor social skills.

## Abilities and ready Shaping

| Ability                             | Table effect and limits                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Blind Sight (heat): Tal and Fen** | Detect heat-contrasting targets despite darkness. Cannot read, distinguish color or fine detail, obtain eye contact, or see through solid barriers, heavy insulation, or ambient-temperature concealment. This does not read the order through a case or see a person through the refuge door. |
| **Earth Sense: Tal**                | While touching connected earth or stone, knows approximate depth and whether the chamber is structurally unstable. Does not identify the forged rod, the culprit, or the counting-door solution.                                                                                               |
| **Shaping: Tal**                    | Only the three cells listed in his profile, plus the universal Sense Magic and Dispel actions. Shaping 60%, maximum Intensity 3, Safe Magnitude 3, active limit 16; current/max PP 14. He has no Counter, Veiled, Confluence, or other unlisted magical Talent.                                |

**Tal's inspection working:** with **Scry·Earth/Stone**, touch one stone support block and identify a hidden crack within that block. A specialist diagnosis is **I2, Range 0, Duration 0, Reach 0, no adjustments: M2**. Spend one Combat Action, perform the Practice, and test Shaping 60%. The block is unattended and gives no defense. An ordinary success gives the diagnosis and costs 2 PP; a critical costs 1 PP; an ordinary safe failure loses 1 PP; a fumble loses 2 PP and causes Backlash. This examines one object, not the entire pier or Crownwork. It supplies evidence, not an answer about who caused a failure.

The Tell is apparent around the Shaper and target within `10 × Magnitude` metres. Tal's successful M2 example therefore produces its doubled-brass-edge sign within 20 m. Changing an effect, range, duration, or subject size requires recalculating Magnitude; the example is not a free spell. Tal does not perform a complete restoration or join the rescue merely because he has these cells. His immediate work is coordinating the closure and later independent survey.

## Rules notes for these personalities

- **Influence:** Resisted persuasion normally opposes Persistence or Influence. Success changes an immediate decision, not someone's core loyalties or basic self-preservation. Voss accepting witnessed joint custody is a plausible result; Voss spontaneously knowing Rusk's entire conspiracy is not. See [Practical skills](../../src/content/rules/skills/practical-skills.md).
- **Deception:** Disguise, hiding, stealth, and misdirection use Deception, usually opposed by Perception. A successful misdirection can create access or hide an action; it does not erase documents or make an impossible authority genuine. When the whole party must pass unseen, use the weakest member's Deception once; use the best guard's Perception once. See [Calling for Tests](../../src/content/rules/gm-tools/calling-for-tests.md).
- **Information:** Give obvious observations and the testimony required to understand the case through play. Tests can gain speed, cooperation, a better position, or extra detail. They should not decide whether the group is allowed to learn the adventure's central discovery.
- **Intimidation in a fight:** Use the combat Intimidate action and its enemy-condition modifiers. Full-strength opponents resist with `+2B`; forcing their surrender at that point requires critical Influence against failed resistance. A person can also choose to yield because the objective is lost or an acceptable settlement is offered. See [Rounds and Actions](../../src/content/rules/combat/rounds-and-actions.md).
- **Attacks:** Use the combat result matrix for weapon attacks and reactions, not the general opposed-test high-roll comparison. Special disarming, tripping, and subduing attacks require their Talents. See [Attacks and Reactions](../../src/content/rules/combat/attacks-and-reactions.md).
- **Dena's care:** Stable does not mean healed. Battlefield treatment stops Bleeding and Dying without restoring HP. A Wounded patient needs Surgery or equivalent treatment before natural recovery, and the ordinary post-encounter `1D4` Healing attempt does not apply to a Wounded patient. See [Healing and Recovery](../../src/content/rules/adventuring/healing-and-recovery.md).

## The gargoyles are not introductory opponents

The source site's pair of gargoyles remain in ventilation hollows beyond closed acoustic shutters, outside the active rescue route and this session's fight. Sounds, scratches, or falling stone dust can establish their presence. Keeping those shutters closed is a clear precaution; the rescue and its evidence do not require opening them. Do not have an unrelated failed roll release the pair as extra combatants.

The existing [gargoyle profile](../../src/content/rules/creatures/gargoyle.md) has **14 HP, 6 AP, a `+2D6` Damage Modifier, and a claw dealing `1D6 + 2D6` at 50%**. Those are serious opponents for starting characters. This adventure does not weaken or replace that profile. Surveying their roosts or securing the acoustic conduit belongs in a later expedition with warning, preparation, and room to choose another approach.
