# The Last Wagon Company

**Player-safe company brief.** [Complete point-built sheets for eight members](../../pregenerated-characters/README.md) are now available, with [full descriptions and personalities](../../pregenerated-characters/01-concepts.md). Choose four characters, adding Ilen for a fifth player; Orren, Seris, and Nera provide further choices. Everyone speaks **River Crown** and begins rested, at full HP and PP, with **2 Hero Points** and no lingering wounds.

## Read aloud: who you are

> Six weeks ago, rain drowned the road outside Vellbridge. Your caravan master ordered the last wagon abandoned, although wounded travellers lay beneath its canvas. You stayed. Together you braced the wheels, carried the injured through rising water, and dragged the wagon to dry ground. You lost the cargo but brought everyone home.
>
> Doctor Ancor treated the wounded; Hesta Coil extended equipment credit. You registered the Last Wagon Company, with equal shares and one promise: nobody gets left behind. A few modest jobs later, you know each other's habits, jokes, and strengths.
>
> Now the White Span's lamps are dimming, distances lurch underfoot, and a parapet leaks salt water. Survey Team Hallow has gone missing beneath the crossing. The March Council needs an independent company to bring them home, establish what happened, and keep the public safe. This is your first substantial commission.

## Your company already works

Since the flood, you have escorted medicine, recovered a runaway draft animal, and watched a warehouse overnight. You share meals and company decisions. Your charter names equal partners. Hesta's remaining credit is manageable company debt, never a reason to abandon this rescue.

The bonds below form a circle; change their details together if desired. Nobody is a secret traitor; personal ambitions support the company. Decide who makes the tea and your shared ritual before dangerous jobs.

**For three players:** use Pebb, Dori, and Tamsin. Change Tamsin's bond to Pebb: Pebb abandoned a valuable document case to carry the last injured guard, proving the company promise mattered more than profit. Dori has Close Combat 56% and useful rescue tools, but Athletics is only 36%; plan physical rescues with ropes and assistance. Tamsin has Close Combat 51% as well as Healing 56% and Shaping 62%. Other combinations should preserve conversation, technical problem-solving, and reliable access to Healing.

## Mara Holt — the steady hand

**Human; Whitewater upbringing; former road-watch escort.** Mara checks ropes twice, notices quiet people, and expresses affection by fixing their equipment. She left watch work to choose the people she protects.

- **Wants:** Earn a reputation that makes frightened travellers glad to see your charter.
- **Trusting bond:** Pebb talked a hostile tollkeeper into opening a shelter during the flood. Mara takes Pebb's judgment seriously and gives negotiations time to work.
- **Suggested approach:** Establish safe routes, reassure frightened people, and help companions climb. In a fight, hold an approach and protect someone moving a casualty; the completed sheet provides an arming sword, large shield, Protector, and Shield Cover.
- **Likely skills:** Athletics, Close Combat, Perception, Influence, Dodge.
- **Local fact:** House Veyran appoints the road warden, but the elected March Council manages the city's markets and drains. Their responsibilities overlap awkwardly.

## Pebb Dallow — the convincing explanation

**Goblin; Whitewater upbringing; former copying-house apprentice.** Pebb learned clerical work and forgery from the same employer. Cheerful, observant, and offended by shoddy paperwork, Pebb appreciates both bluffs and honest bargains.

- **Wants:** Make the company respectable enough that people remember your work before your former trade.
- **Trusting bond:** Dori crawled under the flooded wagon when Pebb was trapped there. Pebb trusts Dori's warning about a structure without demanding proof first.
- **Suggested approach:** Offer credible agreements, read suspicious documents, cultivate sources, or improvise a cover story with the group's support. In a fight, use a ranged weapon from a clear position.
- **Likely skills:** Influence, Deception, Streetwise, Perception, Ranged Combat.
- **Local fact:** A registered charter lets authorities hire your company without recognising another power's soldiers. Presenting it can open a conversation; it cannot excuse misconduct.

## Dori Ashlar — the patient problem-solver

**Dwarf; Whitewater upbringing in a family of Moruni bridge-workers.** Dori sketches while listening, names tools affectionately, and inspects things before declaring them impossible. River Crown is Dori's own language; Moruni workshop customs shape a deep respect for careful construction. Years with March crews taught Dori to listen to everyone's observations.

- **Wants:** Help ordinary people trust their bridges again, and eventually supervise a crew that listens to its workers.
- **Trusting bond:** Tamsin kept talking Dori through the flooded wagon's lifting rhythm when panic threatened. Dori trusts Tamsin to say when courage has become exhaustion.
- **Suggested approach:** Examine loads, wear, and connections before operating machinery. Use a worker's manner or quiet approach to inspect awkward places. In a fight, defend secure footing with a close weapon.
- **Likely skills:** Engineering, Mechanisms, Perception, Deception, Close Combat, Athletics.
- **Local fact:** The White Span is a Crownwork: people walking its pale causeway count fewer steps than its surveyed length should require. Familiarity with bridges does not make its oldest workings familiar.

## Tamsin Reed — the practical lamp

**Human; Whitewater upbringing; Synod-trained healer.** Tamsin warms cold hands, asks before touching patients, and has a dry sense of humour. Faith means clean bandages, patient listening, and staying with frightened people.

- **Wants:** Bring missing people home and prove worthy of the care that trained you.
- **Trusting bond:** Mara returned for the caravan's last injured guard after everyone else reached safety. Tamsin trusts Mara to act on the company's promise when it becomes costly.
- **Suggested approach:** Ask witnesses about people as well as events; use ordinary Healing where appropriate. In combat, reach casualties safely, provide treatment, and defend yourself with a simple close weapon.
- **Likely skills:** Healing, Shaping, Influence, Perception, Close Combat.
- **Magic:** Flesh specialty, with the exact cells **Alter·Flesh** and **Scry·Flesh**: bodily treatment and bodily observation. The completed sheet funds the full 20-IP Shaping Talent and gives priced healing and diagnosis examples. Your spoken litany and washed cord accompany a visible Tell: seven faint shadows. Magic cannot be passed off as unnoticed ordinary care.
- **Local fact:** Doctor Samel Ancor, senior surgeon at the House of the Rose Lamp, treats serious injuries without Shaping. Magical and practical medicine both have a place.

## Optional fifth: Ilen Sedge — the returning messenger

**Human; Whitewater upbringing; independent courier.** Ilen is curious, punctual, and careful with confidences. A passenger on the flooded caravan, Ilen helped carry its injured travellers and signed the same charter.

**Wants:** Carry a company reputation worth defending. **Bond:** Pebb always delivers entrusted messages unopened; Ilen returns that trust. **Approach:** Scout, remember exits, reassure strangers, and provide ranged support from a clear firing position. **Likely skills:** Perception, Athletics, Ranged Combat, Influence, Deception. **Local fact:** Vellbridge's courier relays carry physical messages; storms still delay news.

## Ready for the first scene

Together you cover conversation, deception, investigation, physical rescue, mechanisms, combat, and treatment. No obstacle requires magic. Choose actions from the situation, not only the highest skills on your sheets.

You arrive with the personal equipment on your [completed sheet](../../pregenerated-characters/README.md). At the briefing, Tal issues shared rescue supplies: **two Healing Kits**, chalk, shuttered lamps and fuel, cord, charcoal, and paper; your personal ropes complete the rescue kit. These shared supplies are additional to personal inventory and must not be counted once per PC. Ancestry grants no unlisted abilities. The [creation audit](../../pregenerated-characters/03-creation-audit.md) verifies the point builds, including Tamsin's full 20-IP Shaping cost, Pebb's and Dori's required innate abilities, and everyone's own River Crown language.
