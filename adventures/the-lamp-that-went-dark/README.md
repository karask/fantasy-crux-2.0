# The Lamp That Went Dark

An introductory adventure for **Fantasy Crux 2.0 Beta**, set in Vellbridge and beneath the White Span in the Crownless Realms.

**Players:** four by default; guidance for three or five. **Length:** one four-hour session, including a break. **Characters:** an established company of pregenerated adventurers. **Status:** scenario, NPC profiles, and eight point-built PC sheets complete; encounters have not been playtested against the finished roster. No maps or illustrations have been created.

When a lamp on the White Span goes dark, the crossing rings with a bell nobody can find. A missing survey team is accused of stealing the very thing that could save the bridge. The company must get past a disputed cordon, find a way through a door that measures journeys, and bring people and evidence home before an emergency becomes somebody's claim to power.

The session has a definite ending: the missing people are accounted for, the immediate danger is contained, and the company determines who holds the evidence. Restoring the Crownwork and pursuing the sabotage can become later adventures.

## Preparation

Read the adventure and NPC file, then select four of the [eight complete character sheets](../../pregenerated-characters/README.md); Mara, Pebb, Dori, and Tamsin remain the default four. The sheets use normal point allocation, paid ancestry abilities and Talents, and an explicitly approved fixed 140 SP starting allowance. Each includes all general skills and a creation ledger. Before play, compare the chosen pregens with the supplied three-, four-, or five-player encounter; those pairings have not yet been playtested. Eight available sheets do not imply an eight-player encounter balance.

Have D100 dice, a few counters for Reactions and Hero Points, and the handout text ready. Describe positions using the area descriptions; simple distance notes suffice. There is no need for a tactical map or a separate city investigation.

The published [gazetteer](../../src/content/rules/gazetteer/index.md) supplies the public setting. This adventure also adopts selected secrets from the **Gamemaster Gazetteer** in the [Crownless Realms setting draft](../../potential-worlds/the-crownless-realms/the-crownless-realms.md). Those borrowed secrets and the adventure's additions are identified in the design notes; the public gazetteer has not been changed.
