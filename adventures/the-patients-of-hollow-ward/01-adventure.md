# The Patients of Hollow Ward

**GM only.** Read the [NPC profiles](02-npcs.md) and prepare the individual [handouts](03-player-brief-and-handouts.md). All events occur in Hollow Ward, the half-inhabited imperial fort north of Vellbridge. Start after the journey, at supper in its caravan yard.

## What makes this adventure move

The company wants to recover three living people and stop the infirmary taking anyone else. Their friend Sava is among the missing. A clay orderly follows a cruelly literal medical routine; two skeletal sentries keep its sterilizing theatre closed. Below the wards, the preserved imperial surgeon is still conscious—and wants someone to relieve him of his duties.

**The reversal:** there is no creature feeding on the patients. The orderly is genuinely treating their injuries, then refusing to release them. The frightening dead surgeon can help stop it. He is also responsible for keeping the original hospital's abuses secret. Players can cooperate with him without deciding that he was innocent.

The central question is: **can the company turn a place built to confine people back into a place that lets them leave?**

## The truth and tonight's danger

The service golem, called **Gentle Hands**, was ordered to admit the wounded after sunset, keep them safe, and release them only on an officer's discharge. Over a century of damaged instruments, its assessment has become unreliable: a person lying still after dark appears to it as an unattended casualty. It carries sleeping travelers into the infirmary, washes them, dresses real wounds, and assigns them dead soldiers' names.

The infirmary's admission shutter opens on its surviving moonless-night duty cycle. This elaborates the gazetteer's reports of the building opening from inside on moonless nights; it does not mean it has been abducting people every evening without anybody noticing.

The hospital's former surgeon, **Doctor Oren Vey**, is an intelligent mummy. He can give routine medical instructions but lacks the commanding officer's authority to discharge patients or close the ward. He preserved himself to oversee the patients left behind when the garrison withdrew. Some were victims of experiments he permitted under House Veyran's wartime patronage. His registers prove that complicity. His own account initially omits it.

The commanding officer's seal remains in a small cabinet behind the sterilizing theatre. The cabinet is secured by an old steam interlock. Its controls are mundane, and its operating instructions survive. The sentries still obey their separate guard order: keep unauthorized people out of the theatre. They are not Gentle Hands' subordinates.

Gentle Hands recognizes the genuine seal impressed on the surviving discharge board. A correctly completed **ward closure** ends its admissions until a new authorized opening. The surgeon knows the board procedure; its printed instructions also explain it, so trusting him is not mandatory.

### Events already in motion

Shortly after sunset tonight, carter **Bram Fell** was admitted and managed to crawl into an old laundry drain while the orderly fetched linens. He emerges at supper, still wearing wrist tag 04. His arrival lets the PCs learn the threat immediately. He escaped physically but has not been discharged in the records; the company must also clear his entry when closing the ward.

Three adults are currently held: **Sava Dorn**, the company's friend and a caravan cook; **Leth Arven**, a trader with a sprained wrist; and **Corin Bell**, a stable hand with a treated leg injury. Sava was taken shortly before supper after lying down with a headache. The others were taken earlier tonight. All are conscious and stable when discovered; Corin needs help walking.

At the next routine inspection, Gentle Hands will move them individually to an inner observation room. That makes access harder, not instant death. Describe its preparation before moving anyone. The creature remains governed by the same rules even if the players take longer than expected.

These are ceilings. Bank time from an early solution. At 2:40, ensure the group knows how to complete the closure whether through Oren or the printed board. At 3:20, move directly to the last discharge. Do not add a pursuit or second combat after a successful closure.

## Scene 1: A fourth place at supper

### A. The caravan yard

Timber galleries lean against the old fort wall. A communal hearth, watering trough, and stacked blanket bales surround long supper benches. The March Watch holds the gate; Banner traders occupy much of the yard. The infirmary's stone frontage forms the far side. Its windows are narrow, its main door iron-bound, and a laundry drain opens near ground level beside it.

The company has delivered Hesta Coil's blankets. Sava, who fed them after their first disastrous caravan journey, promised to join them for supper. Her place is empty.

> Something scrapes inside the laundry drain. A man pushes out headfirst, wrapped in clean white bandages. He looks at your supper table and starts to cry. “Don't lie down,” he says. Behind the infirmary door, a calm voice calls, “Private Arven. Your family has not collected you.”

Bram has ordinary injuries but no active Bleeding. Give him warmth and attention without a roll. If treated, use normal Healing. His three useful facts are automatic: a clay figure carried him inside, its treatment helped, and it refused to believe his name or let him leave. He escaped through the drain when left unattended.

**Watch Corporal Mira Senn** wants the yard evacuated and the infirmary entrance burned shut. She is frightened, not corrupt. The blanket porter confirms that Sava lay down with a headache and vanished; a trail of wet, clay-sized footprints leads inside. Nobody must roll to learn who needs rescue.

The company can persuade Mira to **hold off on fire, keep a clear return route, and lend a litter and two full Healing Kits**. A definite rescue plan and agreement to report back may settle this through conversation. If resisted, Influence opposes Persistence 40%; practical assistance may give `+1B`. On failure she still permits a short rescue attempt, but withdraws the watching crowd and offers supplies only at the gate. She will not burn a building while she knows rescuers and captives remain within it. Her proposed fire is a threat to the site and future access, not a hidden timed massacre.

Mira can authorize 25 SP per PC from the fort's emergency funds for returning the people and preventing another taking, plus treatment and lodging. The friendship is the immediate reason to act; the fee makes helping sustainable. Hesta's delivery fee is separate and already earned.

## Scene 2: The night round

### B. Receiving hall

The main infirmary door has opened for tonight's admission cycle. A long tiled hall contains coat pegs, an admission desk, an open linen cupboard, and a wooden discharge board fitted into the wall beside the entrance. One side passage leads to the patient ward; another leads to the theatre. A stair behind the desk descends to the memorial room.

White aprons and plain medical masks hang in the cupboard. A brass sign lists staff tasks. The discharge board has four patient slots and a separate socket labeled **WARD CLOSED**. Handouts 1 and 3 supply their exact text.

Before the PCs enter deeply, show Gentle Hands lifting a heavy stone washbasin as easily as a bowl. Its enormous fingers have padded tips. It moves with deliberate care and says, “Injury must be attended.” Make its scale and strength unmistakable. It is not the introductory fight.

### C. The patient ward

Four cots stand in a twelve-metre room; three are occupied and Bram's cot is empty. A wheeled linen screen divides the room without blocking its walking lanes. Each patient wears a numbered wrist tag; their belongings sit in three numbered drawers. The far door leads to a smaller observation room. A laundry chute opens behind the screen and descends to the yard drain.

The chute can pass a crouching unencumbered adult, but a rigid litter cannot fit. Slow passage after removing awkward equipment needs no roll. Under pursuit, use one Athletics test to get through quickly; failure leaves the person delayed at an accessible bend, not crushed or drowned. Companions can pull them clear. It provides a genuine escape route, not a promised exit that arbitrarily stops working later.

**Getting close to the patients:**

- Put on medical aprons, carry linens, and perform a listed task. The disguise and practical performance use Deception against Gentle Hands' Perception 30%. It is a sensory deception, not magical control of a Mindless creature. Good preparation can justify `+1B`.
- Follow its announced round and move behind the screen while it works at another cot. Use Deception opposed by Perception when avoiding notice is uncertain; group stealth uses the weakest PC once.
- Enter openly as visitors. Gentle Hands allows conversation but blocks removing a patient without discharge. This grants information without a test while leaving the central problem unresolved.

On an exposed disguise, it orders the PC to the waiting bench and escorts them toward receiving. It does not attack for a poor impersonation. If the PC obeys, they keep access to the main route. Grappling or resisting its physical escort follows the normal combat rules, with its strength clearly warned beforehand.

**The patients:** Sava recognizes the company and tries to joke: “The broth is better than the hospitality.” Leth knows their wrist numbers correspond to the board outside. Corin has already received Surgery but remains Wounded during recovery. He has heard someone beneath the floor saying, “I did not dismiss them. I never dismissed them.” All explain freely what they know.

The first twist clue is visible now: Corin's injured leg has been washed, dressed, and properly splinted. This is treatment with imprisonment, not a slaughterhouse. The patients' belongings contain their current names; their wrist tags instead carry imperial names.

Freeing them through the drain is allowed. It completes the immediate extraction but leaves future admissions to stop. The remaining operation can proceed with the patients safe outside. Never recapture them just to restore the intended finale.

## Scene 3: The empty theatre

### D. Sterilizing theatre

The theatre is ten metres across, with a broad operating table, two tall linen racks, a low instrument trolley, and an inspection window looking into the seal cabinet. Its entrance is at one end, the cabinet controls at the other. A ceiling vent takes steam outside. The floor is level and dry. The trolley can screen movement but does not stop arrows; heavy linen racks give cover only where they actually obscure a target.

Two skeletal sentries stand beside the operating table. Their faded leather armour and imperial badges are visible before entry. One turns its skull toward a raised weapon. A scratched warning on the lintel reads, **AUTHORIZED STAFF ONLY. SEAL-BEARER EXEMPT.**

These are guards, not patients and not misunderstood living people. They were ordered to stop entry into the restricted theatre, and they will fight people attempting it. They do not pursue beyond the receiving hall. They were posted with their own authority and do not accept Gentle Hands' instructions.

**Default fight:** two published skeletons for four PCs; one for three PCs, three for five. All start visible. Use the [NPC file](02-npcs.md) for their exact values and equipment. They fight with sword and shield, using one ordinary attack per round and normal Reactions; no Charge or extra off-hand attack. A serious hit causes Wounded but no Bleeding or Dying. They stop at 0 HP.

Their objective is to hold the threshold. The PCs can destroy them, draw them outside while someone reaches the controls, or withdraw and choose another plan. Each sentry leaves its position only to confront an intruder; it returns if that intruder leaves its allowed patrol area.

**Possible deception:** genuine staff garments and the correct theatre pass phrase, available from Oren downstairs, can satisfy their recognition routine. Presenting both works without a roll. A makeshift imitation can use Deception against their Perception 20%. The linen aprons alone are insufficient here, as the posted warning and their challenge make clear. If the PCs visit Oren first and obtain the phrase, reward that path by allowing the fight to be avoided. Do not substitute a surprise battle elsewhere.

**Teaching combat:** explain initiative, attack and Parry sizes, armour, and saving a Reaction. Shields can protect someone moving past, using purchased Talents where required. Introduce Hero Points before resolving the first dangerous hit. A decisive objective may end the encounter without every enemy being destroyed.

Gentle Hands does not join ordinary fighting here; its admission duty keeps it in the ward. If a wounded PC later enters its view, it offers treatment and attempts admission, which can become another disguise or discharge problem. It never teleports in or senses injuries through walls.

## Scene 4: The breathing cabinet

### E. The sterilizing cabinet

A narrow cabinet behind the theatre's inspection window contains the commanding officer's seal in a perforated brass tray. Pipes feed an old steam sterilizer around that tray. This is a surviving piece of mundane hospital equipment, kept supplied by Gentle Hands. The seal itself is not enchanted; its authenticated impression is recognized by the golem's old instruction system.

The cabinet is hot and under pressure. Its interlock will not open while either steam pressure or wash water remains inside. Three labeled handles control **HEAT**, **VENT**, and **DRAIN**. A pressure gauge shows HIGH or ZERO; a small glass window shows the water level. These indicators are legible without a Perception roll. Nobody needs modern medical knowledge.

Give Handout 2. Initial state: **heat on, vent shut, drain shut, pressure high, water visible**.

**The solution:**

1. Turn HEAT off.
2. Open VENT and wait until the pressure gauge falls to ZERO.
3. Open DRAIN and let the visible water empty.
4. Pull the cabinet handle and lift out the seal with the provided tongs.

The pressure-operated drain safety catch prevents opening the drain while the gauge is high. Turning heat on while the vent is open merely produces continued steam and prevents cooling. Pulling the door early leaves it latched. These responses make wrong attempts informative, not damaging. The three labels, two indicators, and instruction fragments give the complete solution.

**Make it a player puzzle:** explain the starting state and ask what they operate, in what order. Correct reasoning works without a test. Let different PCs watch the gauge, control the vent, and retrieve the seal. Waits last fictional minutes, not real-time delays.

An Mechanisms success supplies the operating sequence if a player prefers solving it through their character's skill. Failure gives the first safe step, and they can continue through observation. After ten minutes of table discussion, point out the heat/pressure relationship; after fifteen, make the sequence explicit. A mechanically sound alternative that achieves cold, depressurized, drained conditions also works.

Do not conceal extra traps. Breaking the cabinet's wooden outer door uses the object rules, AP 2 and HP 25, but the inner hot tray must still cool or be handled with suitable tools. Warn about escaping steam before destructive action. Properly used tongs and protective cloth can retrieve it without a damage roll; bare-handed contact with a visibly hot surface is avoidable.

This puzzle delays retrieval through a logical process; it is not a magic lock requiring a particular pregen or a test repeated until success.

## Scene 5: The doctor downstairs

### F. Memorial room

The stair behind receiving descends to a low vaulted room. Shelves contain patient registers and carefully tied bundles of personal effects. A single chair faces a row of names carved into the wall. Its occupant wears a surgeon's apron over dry linen wrappings. He is writing with a hand whose knuckles no longer have skin.

> “If you are the relief staff,” he says, “you are very late.” His eyes move to the seal, if you have it. “If you are here to discharge my patients, perhaps you are precisely on time.”

Oren is a mummy, not a disguised living man. He makes no attempt to hide that. He wants the current patients freed and his ward closed, but fears that anybody reading the old registers will know he permitted experiments on prisoners and soldiers. He first presents himself only as the physician who stayed when others fled.

**What he gives without bargaining:** the patients are alive; the seal is in the theatre cabinet; the ordinary admission task cannot be stopped by pleading with Gentle Hands. He also points to the board's printed closure instructions. Asking why he has not collected the seal reveals that he has not dared confront the sentries or risk the preservation of his own body. He spent decades rationalizing his continued vigil; tonight's living captives finally make that excuse untenable. His medical office never carried military discharge authority. He is not physically incapable of trying, and players who accuse him of leaving the risk to others have a point.

**What he offers for an agreement:** the sentries' pass phrase, a detailed explanation of the orderly's task boundaries, and a written acknowledgement that current patients are fit to be released. He asks the company to leave the historical registers in his care. Their disposal does not mechanically control the golem; his leverage is useful cooperation, not a magical hostage.

The players can accept, offer to keep personal medical details private while preserving testimony, propose supervised preservation at the Rose Lamp, or insist on reading the books first. Use Influence opposed by Oren's **Influence 50%** for a practical archival arrangement. His Persistence 80% is available for resisting threats or hostile magic; do not automatically use it for every conversation simply because it is higher.

On failure he withholds the optional pass phrase and medical endorsement. He still does not destroy the instructions or attack rescuers. The physical route and board remain usable. The company can return to the theatre if needed; do not replay cleared enemies or charge for a second journey.

**The deeper reversal:** reading the open register automatically reveals an entry in Oren's own hand approving an experimental treatment without consent. A later entry records his refusal of further experiments and his decision to remain with survivors. Both are true. The evidence implicates wartime Veyran patrons, not Mira or every modern member of that house.

Let the players judge him. He was neither a wholly innocent caretaker nor an unambiguously malicious monster. The adventure does not require forgiving him, destroying him, or settling a century of guilt in one conversation.

## Scene 6: The last discharge

Return to receiving with the seal, patient wrist tags, and any willing companions. The discharge board's procedure is fully legible. The goal is to complete the terminal ward closure and lead out anyone still inside.

### The operation

**One patient at a time:** include Bram, whose tag 04 is outside with him. Insert each numbered wrist tag into the matching slot and stamp the discharge strip with the seal. Gentle Hands releases that patient, regardless of whether their name matches the old roster. The numeric identity is the system's key; their actual identity comes from their testimony. There is no name-guessing puzzle hidden here.

**Then close the ward:** after all four recorded entries show discharged—Sava, Leth, Corin, and Bram outside, stamp **WARD CLOSED**, turn the board's handle, and remove the seal. Gentle Hands finishes setting down anything it carries, returns to its alcove, and remains inactive until an authorized reopening. Its admission shutters close. All these are established functions of this particular instruction system, not powers acquired by owning a seal elsewhere.

A simple stamp or handle operation can use a Combat Action if rounds matter. Retrieving a tag from a frightened patient's wrist needs consent or the usual handling time, not a free pickpocketing trick. Patients willingly cooperate once reassured; no mandatory Influence roll is required.

**Engage the whole company:** one person manages the board, another guides the patients, another steadies Corin or handles the litter, and another keeps Gentle Hands on a valid care task. An accepted doctor endorsement allows it to supervise cooperatively. Without one, it approaches to verify each operation and may block an unstamped patient's attempted exit.

It accepts ordinary linen changes, fresh drinking water, and injury inspection as valid duties. A genuine task is not a roll; claiming a false condition to redirect it uses Deception opposed by Perception 30%. Failure brings it back to the patient it was supervising. It does not invalidate completed discharges.

If using rounds, let it take ordinary actions in order; it cannot inspect, move a patient, and grapple a PC simultaneously. It never punches somebody merely for placing a tag incorrectly. It begins using physical restraint when someone forcibly removes an undischarged patient and answers violence according to its dangerous profile. Warn clearly before a player commits to that escalation.

### Valid alternative endings

- **Patients escaped through the drain:** recover their tags outside, register their discharges, and close the ward. The device accepts presented tags without requiring patients to return. Mira can guard them while the company finishes.
- **A tag is lost:** the admission desk has a numbered duplicate, openly listed on the patient record. Extra blank strips handle any PCs who voluntarily accepted admission; close those entries too. An authenticated seal permits substitution. Do not punish an earlier rescue by making it impossible to finish.
- **Oren did not cooperate:** read the instructions, use the seal, and close the ward. No forgiveness or promise is required for the controls to work.
- **Oren accompanies them:** he can witness the discharges from receiving. His willingness to face living people can be the emotional conclusion. If the company earlier persuaded him to take an active risk in the theatre, allow that concession to matter: use his actual profile and let the party avoid or shorten its own fight. Do not add enemies to compensate for a successful bargain.
- **The company gives up on the seal:** the drain still permits extraction with assistance. Mira clears sleeping places from the infirmary frontage and posts a waking watch through dawn, when tonight's admission cycle ends. This completes the rescue but leaves the golem problem unresolved; pay the rescue portion, 15 SP per PC, and offer the remaining 10 SP for a later safe shutdown. Do not pretend this is the same success as closing the ward.
- **The company fights the golem:** use the full published profile and its stated behavior. Destruction stops admissions but carries serious danger and damage to the site. Escape remains possible via the drain, which its body cannot enter. It cannot Sprint. Never weaken it secretly to make a bad plan safe.

## End at the supper table

Sava serves reheated stew to the people who brought her home. Bram stays seated beside the hearth and tests his real name aloud. Corin gets proper continuing care. If the ward was closed, there are no more midnight admissions; preserve that victory in any sequel.

Pay the agreed fee according to the outcome. Recommend **4 IP per PC** for completed rescue and closure, or **3 IP** for rescuing the people while leaving the admission problem unresolved. Avoiding combat earns the same reward. Record injuries and treatment under the normal rules.

The immediate ending is personal and visible. The company decides afterward what promise it made to Oren and whether to honor it. Nobody convenes an obligatory public hearing tonight.

### Continue only if the table wants to

- **The names on the wall:** Oren remembers where patients' families lived; returning personal effects becomes a journey through the March and Banner communities.
- **The Veyran register:** descendants want recognition and evidence of the wartime experiments. Some current officials want the truth; others fear what it will cost their house.
- **A living hospital:** the Rose Lamp could reopen the infirmary with human staff and a carefully revised command for Gentle Hands. The PCs must decide whether the construct should ever be entrusted with patients again.
- **The last absent patient:** one record says a patient was transferred to Hollow Ward's sealed lower stores and never arrived. This is a later investigation, not an additional captive secretly required to complete tonight's rescue.
