# Running reference and setting notes

## At the table

**Objective:** rescue Sava, Leth, and Corin; register Bram's earlier escape; close the ward. **Main fight:** the theatre skeletons. **Main danger to avoid:** Gentle Hands' powerful body. **Potential ally:** Oren, the compromised undead surgeon.

| Situation     | Essential information or next route                                                                                                                    |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Yard          | Bram describes genuine treatment and imprisonment. Sava is missing. Mira supplies practical help.                                                      |
| Receiving     | Aprons, duties, patient numbers, duplicate tags, and closure instructions are visible. Theatre, ward, and memorial stairs are all accessible branches. |
| Ward          | Three patients cooperate; their treatment is competent; drain allows escape. Bram outside is still active entry 04.                                    |
| Theatre       | Sentries guard access; destroy, distract, or use Oren's staff recognition phrase. No extra sentries appear.                                            |
| Cabinet       | Heat off → vent → pressure ZERO → drain → empty → open with tongs. Correct reasoning needs no roll.                                                    |
| Memorial room | Oren can help but wants secrecy. The open record exposes his complicity without a successful test.                                                     |
| Finale        | Clear all four active entries, including escaped Bram; stamp WARD CLOSED and turn the handle. Remove the seal.                                         |

**Areas in words:** the yard opens into receiving. Receiving branches to the patient ward, the theatre, and the stairs to Oren's memorial room. The theatre contains the cabinet. The ward's laundry chute leads back to the yard. This is descriptive connectivity, not a map.

Players may visit Oren before the sentries or evacuate patients before retrieving the seal. Preserve those successes. Scene numbers describe the expected introduction, not compulsory movement.

## Pacing and failure

Finish the opening by 0:25, exploration by 1:00, combat by 1:40, the break by 1:50, the cabinet by 2:20, the surgeon discussion by 2:55, and the closure by 3:35. Use the remaining twenty-five minutes for treatment, supper, rewards, and overruns.

- Failed Influence changes Mira's support or Oren's optional cooperation; it never seals off all routes.
- Failed Deception brings a challenge or escort to receiving. Violent resistance is a separate player choice with a telegraphed risk.
- Failed mechanism tests provide a safe first step. The physical clues remain available and no random trap appears.
- Lost wrist tags have documented duplicates. Escaped patients need not be brought back.
- Rescue without closure is a partial success with explicit precautions and a later shutdown job, not a false claim that the danger has ended.

## Pregen fit and mechanics

The existing [pregen roster](../../pregenerated-characters/README.md) now contains completed sheets. The default four are Mara, Pebb, Dori, and Tamsin: protection, disguise and negotiation, technical problem-solving, and care respectively. Their sheets give relevant skills in the 50–62% range, appropriate for the intended modest social and sentry challenges. This is a design comparison, not a completed playtest.

For three players, Pebb, Dori, and Tamsin preserve the broad skill coverage; use one sentry. For five, add a company member of the group's choice and use three sentries. The default four face two. Every route has a mundane solution; no particular Shaping cell is required.

Apply the characters' actual Talents, including Pebb's social/deception training and Dori's close-quarters abilities. Do not assign new powers to them here. Tamsin can diagnose and heal biological bodies, including the mummy under Alter·Flesh; she cannot use Flesh to repair a clay golem. Nerin's Force/Motion workings, if selected, follow their purchased effects and defenses rather than automatically overriding an enchanted creature.

Influence uses a suitable opposed social skill. Deception covers disguises, hiding, and misdirection. Mindless creatures can be fooled by sensory recognition but cannot be persuaded into new motives or affected by Mind magic. Soulless is not identical to Mindless: Oren has a mind and can negotiate, but Spirit effects cannot affect him.

Assistance normally needs at least 26% relevant skill and grants `+1B` total, not per helper. Essential observations do not require Perception rolls. Combat attacks and reactions use their own matrix; do not apply generic opposed-roll success comparisons to weapon attacks. Nonliving creatures still become Wounded but do not bleed or become Dying. Keep standard HP, Major Wound, and Hero Point procedures for living characters.

The golem has neither regeneration nor limitless treatment supplies. Healing 100% still needs the ordinary procedure and fumbles on 00. Routine hospital commands operate this particular established device; understanding them does not grant the party a new spell, permanent enchantment, or general authority over constructs.

## Gazetteer and GM sources

The published [gazetteer](../../src/content/rules/gazetteer/index.md), under **Four satellite settlements**, establishes Hollow Ward north of Vellbridge: a half-inhabited fort, the March Watch at the gate, Banner traders in the yard, and a sealed infirmary opening from inside on moonless nights.

The unpublished [Crownless Realms GM gazetteer](../../potential-worlds/the-crownless-realms/the-crownless-realms.md), **Hollow Ward infirmary** and **The patients of Hollow Ward**, provides the literal service golem, living captives, dead officer's discharge requirement, seal behind the theatre, and intelligent mummy surgeon with records of Veyran wartime experiments. This adventure adopts those secrets rather than presenting them as already published player knowledge.

New connective details are the Last Wagon Company's blanket delivery and friendship with Sava; the named NPCs; the four active admissions; the steam cabinet; theatre sentries; laundry escape route; and terminal ward-closure procedure. Oren's remorse and refusal to perform further experiments are also adventure interpretations. His surname Vey is not evidence of a familial relationship with House Veyran.

The hospital's old names and the presence of intelligent undead do not establish a universal answer to the setting's metaphysical questions. No new conspiracy is needed for the abductions. Its current horror follows an old command and a damaged assessment, while the historical wrongdoing belongs to people who made choices.

Profiles draw directly on [Skeleton](../../src/content/rules/creatures/skeleton.md), [Golem](../../src/content/rules/creatures/golem.md), and [Mummy](../../src/content/rules/creatures/mummy.md). Added recognition skills and Oren's professional skills are stated explicitly. No creature's damage or armour is quietly reduced for the introduction. The regular [object rules](../../src/content/rules/adventuring/objects.md) govern the cabinet's wooden outer door; its safety interlock is an adventure device rather than an opposed lockmaker challenge.
