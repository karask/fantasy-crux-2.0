# Player brief and revealed handouts

**Only the opening brief is safe to share in advance.** The rest contains discovered information; reveal each quoted handout when its location is examined. GM explanations outside the quotations are not part of the handouts.

## Opening brief: the Last Wagon Company

> Six weeks ago, your caravan master ordered a flooded wagon abandoned with wounded travelers still inside. You stayed, hauled the people free, and lost the cargo. Doctor Ancor treated the injured; Hesta Coil extended credit; a cook named Sava Dorn fed you until you could pay your way again.
>
> You registered the Last Wagon Company with equal shares and a promise: nobody gets left behind. Since then you have worked together, shared meals, and learned whom to trust with a rope, a ledger, or a wounded companion.
>
> Tonight you have delivered blankets to Hollow Ward, an old imperial fort north of Vellbridge. The March Watch holds its gate and Banner traders fill the caravan yard. Sava promised you supper. Her chair is empty. Across the yard stands an infirmary nobody admits to opening.

Use the existing [company character sheets](../../pregenerated-characters/README.md). If this follows another adventure, adjust “six weeks” and the shared jobs to the established history. Begin rested if it is the introductory session; otherwise retain actual wounds and resources. The short history establishes cooperation, not a fresh round of introductions between strangers.

## Handout 1: The admission desk

Reveal on reading the receiving-hall desk. The numbers are identity references used by the machine, not a code the players must guess.

> **ACTIVE PATIENT ENTRIES**
>
> 01 — Private Merrow — ward cot one.
>
> 02 — Corporal Arven — ward cot two.
>
> 03 — Trooper Pell — ward cot three.
>
> 04 — Private Hask — absent from cot; discharge not recorded.
>
> **VISITING HOURS:** Conversation permitted under supervision. No patient shall leave without an officer's discharge.
>
> **STAFF DUTIES:** Fresh linens, drinking water, wound inspection. Staff undertaking these duties may pass receiving.
>
> **DUPLICATE TAGS:** Numbered drawer beneath this desk. Use only on the responsible officer's authorization.

The patients establish the correspondence freely: 01 is Sava, 02 Leth, 03 Corin, 04 Bram. Their real names are among their belongings and match their own testimony. The machine's imperial names are errors, not secret identities.

## Handout 2: The cabinet instructions

Reveal all three fragments together; this is a sequencing puzzle, not a hunt for missing pieces.

> **THE TRAY MUST BE COLD AND EMPTY BEFORE OPENING.**
>
> Keep the drain shut while the pressure gauge reads HIGH. The drain catch releases at ZERO.
>
> Heat makes pressure. An open vent lets pressure escape, but the tray cannot cool while heat is supplied.
>
> Drain the water only after pressure is gone. Use the tongs: metal may remain warm after the gauge reads ZERO.
>
> **Handles:** HEAT — VENT — DRAIN.
>
> **Visible state:** Heat on. Vent shut. Drain shut. Pressure HIGH. Water in the tray chamber.

GM solution: heat off, vent open, wait for ZERO, drain open, wait until empty, open the cabinet and use tongs. The pressure catch blocks an unsafe drain opening; premature door operation does nothing. Sensible equivalent methods work.

## Handout 3: The discharge board

The board is in plain view in receiving. Show this whenever examined, including before meeting Oren. An NPC can read it aloud without changing its meaning.

> **DISCHARGE AND WARD CLOSURE**
>
> Fit the patient's numbered tag to the matching strip. Impress the commanding officer's seal. Release the patient from the active roster.
>
> Patients already removed must still be discharged from the roster. The patient's physical presence is not required.
>
> Replace missing tags from the numbered desk drawer under the officer's seal. Additional admissions use the spare strips.
>
> When every active entry is discharged, impress WARD CLOSED and turn the handle.
>
> The orderly will place carried patients safely down, return to its alcove, and cease admissions until authorized reopening. Remove the seal for safekeeping.

The only necessary authorizing object is the genuine officer's seal. Oren's optional medical endorsement makes Gentle Hands easier to work around but is not another mandatory key.

## Handout 4: The open register

Reveal when someone reads the book beside Oren. The handwriting is consistent between both entries. The dates are long before the present characters' lifetimes.

> Experiment series approved under Veyran patronage. Consent of the assigned patients was not sought. Results to be compared before the next convoy.
>
> — Surgeon Oren Vey

**A later entry in the same hand:**

> Further series refused. Surviving patients remain under my care. No transfer is to be made without my personal attendance. If the garrison departs, I remain.
>
> — Surgeon Oren Vey

This proves his participation and later refusal. It does not prove that every modern Veyran official knows what happened, nor require the players to judge the refusal sufficient atonement.

## Handout 5: Oren's voluntary assistance

Provide only if he agrees to help. The company can finish without either item.

> **The theatre challenge:** “Who returns the borrowed breath?”
>
> **Staff answer:** “Those entrusted with its keeping.”
>
> Wear the staff apron and mask, keep weapons lowered, and give the answer when challenged. The sentries recognize the duty, not your name.

**His medical endorsement:**

> To the attending orderly: the present patients may be conveyed by the accompanying carers for discharge. Continue essential care. Do not obstruct their preparation of the numbered discharge strips.
>
> — Surgeon Oren Vey

This uses his recognized medical authority to facilitate preparation. It cannot replace the military discharge seal or change the standing admission command.
