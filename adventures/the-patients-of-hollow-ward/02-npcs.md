# People and creatures of Hollow Ward

**GM only.** Physical descriptions and individual personalities are adventure additions. Use the published [Minor NPCs](../../src/content/rules/gm-tools/minor-npcs.md) approach: the role's rating is its specialty percentage, with related skills grouped below and others declining in ten-point steps.

For ordinary humans, default to **all characteristics 10; HP 10/10; MWL 5; PP 10/10; DEX 10; Movement 15 m; DM +0; AP 0; final Combat Order 10**. General unlisted skills are 20%; plausible unfamiliar Lore is 10%; unknown languages and Shaping are unavailable. Everyone here understands River Crown at 60%, including the old institution's surviving personnel; this is a convenience of the local adaptation, not a rule about all ancient languages. No NPC has Hero Points or Talents unless specified. Weapon damage below is before DM unless a complete sum is explicitly shown.

## Corporal Mira Senn — a frightened professional

A human woman with tired eyes, a blunt fringe, and a watch scarf knotted around her wrist. She takes names before issuing orders and counts returning people twice. Her voice becomes very quiet when afraid: “Tell me how many are still inside.”

**Human Watch Corporal 50 (Shortsword `1D6`, Medium; Small Shield `1D4`, Medium; Leather 2; Order 7).**

| Skill % | Skills                                            |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| 50      | Close Combat; Perception; Influence.              |
| 40      | Persistence; Resilience; Dodge; Athletics.        |
| 30      | Unarmed Combat; Healing; Lore (Law and Heraldry). |
| 20      | Deception; Mechanisms.                           |

Leather ENC 3 is already deducted from Order. Both weapon attack and Parry use 50%. Two full Healing Kits, a litter, and lanterns are station supplies she can lend; they are not all carried on her person.

**Wants:** the yard safe before anyone else disappears. **Leverage:** a bounded rescue plan and visible care for her people. **Knows:** the infirmary opens on moonless nights; several earlier complaints were dismissed as drunken wandering. **Limits:** no knowledge of the experiments and no orders to cover them up. She does not enter the skeleton fight or burn known living people inside.

## Sava Dorn — the person they came for

A human woman with a broad smile, grey-threaded hair, and a burn mark on one forearm from years at cooking fires. Even frightened, she notices who has not eaten. “If we survive this, you are all washing up.”

**Human Caravan Cook 50 (kitchen knife as Dagger `1D4+1`, Light, in belongings drawer; tag 01).** Shared human physical defaults.

| Skill % | Skills                                   |
| ------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| 50      | Craft (Cooking); Natural Lore.           |
| 40      | Influence; Perception; Resilience.       |
| 30      | Healing; Persistence; Athletics; Dodge.  |
| 20      | Close Combat; Unarmed Combat; Deception. |

**Wants:** herself and the other patients home, with nobody abandoned. **Bond:** she fed the Last Wagon Company after the flood that brought them together. **Knows:** the orderly's route and the other patients' real names. **Leverage:** reassuring her that Corin will be carried out. She follows a workable plan without demanding a successful social roll and is not a bonus combatant.

## Doctor Oren Vey — a witness who cannot rest

A mummy in an old linen apron, his jaw secured by a carefully replaced bandage. He moves slowly and precisely, cleaning a pen before using it. He retains the bedside courtesy of a physician and the evasiveness of someone who has rehearsed his defense for a century.

**Intelligent Mummy Surgeon 80 (no weapon; surgical instruments and registers; adapted from the published mummy).** Oren is not related to House Veyran merely because his surname begins similarly.

| Body                               | Value                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Characteristics                    | STR 23, CON 23, DEX 4, SIZ 13, INT 13, POW 0, CHA 1.                                                                                               |
| Resources                          | HP 18/18; MWL 9; PP 0; Order 9; Move 15 m; AP 2 natural; DM `+1D6`.                                                                                |
| Tags                               | Corporeal, Undead, Soulless; **not Mindless**.                                                                                                     |
| Published combat/resistance skills | Close Combat 80%, Unarmed Combat 60%, Dodge 30%, Persistence 80%, Resilience 50%, Athletics 10%, Deception 10%, Perception 40%, Lore (Undead) 65%. |
| Individual learned skills          | Healing 80%, Lore (Medicine) 80%, Influence 50%, Lore (Imperial Hospital Procedure) 70%.                                                           |
| Attack if cornered                 | Fist 60%, `1D6 + 1D6`, Light. He does not carry the generic profile's great hammer.                                                                |
| Talents and Shaping                | None. His preserved condition grants no healing spell or command over the golem.                                                                   |

He remains active at 0 PP; after a Major Wound he becomes Wounded but never Bleeding or Dying. At 0 HP he is destroyed. **Preserved Flesh:** Alter·Flesh repairs his body; Mind can affect him, Spirit cannot. **Flammable:** use the published [mummy profile](../../src/content/rules/creatures/mummy.md): fire that deals HP damage ignites him, repeating that damage at each round's end without reapplying AP until extinguished. Extinguishing uses his entire round. He warns against exposed flame near his wrappings rather than springing this property on the group.

**Wants:** current captives released, the ward closed, and his historical patients' records left with him. **Secret:** he signed off on some involuntary experiments before refusing further participation. **Leverage:** distinguish preserving private patient histories from suppressing evidence of abuses. **Voice:** “I stayed. That is true. It is not the whole truth.” **Limits:** he cannot issue military discharge, modify the golem's standing command, or cast Shaping. His learned Influence is used for negotiation; high Persistence does not make every request a magical battle of wills.

He normally chooses conversation and withdrawal over violence. His refusal to risk himself retrieving the seal is a moral failing, not an invented mechanical disability; a persuasive appeal can win concrete help. Approaching him is not an ambush encounter. A first threat makes him retreat from open flame and warn the group; deliberate attacks use his real profile, with no secret beginner-friendly reduction.

## Gentle Hands — the clay orderly

A clay figure broad enough to fill a doorway, with a painted blue sash and cloth wrapped around each immense fingertip. Its voice is low and patient. It says “This will help” before lifting someone, and cannot understand why helpful treatment would be refused.

Use the **programmed** version of the published [golem](../../src/content/rules/creatures/golem.md), with Healing as its one programmed skill. Perception 30% is an explicit adventure addition for recognizing uniforms, movement, and tasks.

| Trait           | Value                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Characteristics | STR 39, CON 29, DEX 7, SIZ 29, INT 4, POW 4, CHA 4.                                                                                                                    |
| Resources       | HP 29/29; MWL 15; PP 4/4; Order 6; Move 15 m; DM `+3D6`.                                                                                                               |
| Armour          | 8 AP, or 4 AP against crushing harm.                                                                                                                                   |
| Skills          | Healing 100%; Lore (Allocated Task) 50%; Perception 30%; Unarmed Combat 30%; Dodge 0%; Persistence 90%; Resilience 100%.                                               |
| Attack          | Fist 30%, `1D8 + 3D6`, Light as listed in the source profile.                                                                                                          |
| Body            | Corporeal, Construct, Soulless, Mindless. Does not breathe, eat, sleep, bleed, or suffer poison or disease. At 0 HP disabled/destroyed; 0 PP does not incapacitate it. |
| Other abilities | Programmed Task: obeys its instructions literally. Slow: cannot Sprint. No Talents or Shaping.                                                                         |

As a nonliving corporeal creature it can become Wounded, suffering `-1P`, but is never Bleeding or Dying. Do not replace its actual damage with harmless restraining damage. A grapple follows the grapple rules rather than automatically lifting a resisting PC; its large size may give the appropriate circumstantial advantage, clearly announced before the attempt.

### Its decision rules

| Situation                                                         | Response                                                                                                                |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A person is lying still in its visible admission area after dark  | Treat as a casualty; approach, announce assistance, and carry an unresisting person inside. It has no remote detection. |
| Recognizable staff perform a listed care task                     | Allow the task. A convincing disguise can fool recognition; Influence and Mind magic cannot change its instructions.    |
| Visitors talk with patients                                       | Allow conversation while continuing its round.                                                                          |
| A patient leaves without discharge                                | Block the exit, state the requirement, and attempt restraint if removal continues.                                      |
| A numbered discharge strip bears the genuine officer's impression | Release that entry permanently from tonight's patient roster.                                                           |
| All active entries discharged and WARD CLOSED stamped             | Put carried people down safely, enter its alcove, and stop until an authorized reopening.                               |
| Someone attacks it                                                | Defend its task using normal actions. It cannot Sprint or enter the laundry drain.                                      |

Healing uses normal kit requirements and time. Its supply cupboard contains two full usable kits; spoiled stock is visibly labeled. Corin's earlier care came from a separate now-exhausted kit. Healing 100% still fumbles on 00. Ordinary Surgery does not instantly erase Wounded. It can be redirected to a real medical task, but neither its skill nor its literal obedience gives the party infinite healing or a controllable combat ally.

## Theatre sentries — the intended fight

Use the published [skeleton](../../src/content/rules/creatures/skeleton.md) with the following explicit local additions: **Perception 20%** for staff recognition and the stated theatre patrol boundary. They retain their published strength and equipment.

| Trait             | Each sentry                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Characteristics   | STR 13, CON 4, DEX 11, SIZ 11, INT 0, POW 0, CHA 0.                                                                                     |
| Resources         | HP 8/8; MWL 4; PP 0; Order 3; Move 15 m; AP 2 leather; DM `+0`.                                                                         |
| Skills            | Close Combat 35%; Dodge 10%; Persistence 100%; Resilience 100%; Perception 20%.                                                         |
| Weapons           | Arming sword `1D8`, Medium; medium shield `1D6`, Heavy. Attack/Parry 35%.                                                               |
| Abilities         | Pierce Resistance: after Parry and AP, halve damage from piercing weapons, rounding normally. No Talents or Shaping.                    |
| Tags and injuries | Corporeal, Undead, Soulless, Mindless. Wounded applies after a Major Wound; never Bleeding or Dying; destroyed at 0 HP; active at 0 PP. |

For **three PCs use one**, **four use two**, **five use three**. They have no morale and cannot be talked into compassion or affected by Mind/Spirit effects. They can be bypassed through their recognition procedure, lured within their patrol boundary, or destroyed. They never Charge in this encounter and gain no extra shield attack. Their Order already includes leather ENC 3.
