# The Patients of Hollow Ward

A second introductory adventure for **Fantasy Crux 2.0 Beta**: an evening of haunted exploration, uneasy hospitality, and escape from a hospital that refuses to discharge its patients.

The Last Wagon Company arrives at Hollow Ward with a delivery of blankets. During supper, a missing carter crawls out of a laundry drain wearing spotless bandages. Behind the sealed infirmary doors, someone begins calling the names of soldiers who died a century ago. Then the company's friend disappears from her bed.

**Four hours; three to five PCs, four recommended.** Use an already established party. No maps. This folder contains a separate alternative adventure; the White Span adventure remains available.

Start with **Mara, Pebb, Dori, and Tamsin** from the existing [pregenerated characters](../../pregenerated-characters/README.md). Other company members can substitute; preserve medical care and a character comfortable negotiating. This is suitable as their first substantial adventure or as a later job, with current injuries and resources respected.

The main fight uses the published skeleton profile. The much stronger golem is a visibly dangerous obstacle governed by discoverable instructions. Its controller is an intelligent undead surgeon who needs help but has something shameful to hide. The ending resolves the present disappearances and stops further admissions; the surgeon's past can become a later story.

Design emphasis: creeping unease, a confined location the players learn to navigate, a physical mechanism puzzle, and a final operation involving everyone. The session does not require a custody hearing or a political investigation.
