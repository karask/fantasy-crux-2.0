Copper-brown scales, old harness scars and a long powerful tail. Alert, intent on her clutch and familiar with Serrik's routine. She neither speaks nor breathes fire. Use the published **Giant Lizard**, adding **Perception 40%** for this animal's observation and familiar nest scent.
