## Daily pace {#adventuring-daily-travel}

Daily rates assume ten hours of travel in fair conditions. When it matters which group arrives first, make an opposed Athletics test for travellers on foot or Riding test for mounted groups.

| Pace          | Distance per day | Cost                                                                                           |
| ------------- | ---------------: | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hiking        |            50 km | Steady walking on a road or path, without wagons or animals; make a Fatigue test at day's end. |
| Marching      |            30 km | An organised pace that leaves the group ready to fight; no end-of-day Fatigue test.            |
| Riding slowly |            30 km | Walking mounts, pack animals, or wagons.                                                       |
| Riding        |            50 km | Casual riding.                                                                                 |
| Riding fast   |            90 km | No wagons; both rider and mount make a Fatigue test at day's end.                              |

Multiply the chosen rate by the worst terrain crossed for a substantial part of the day.

| Terrain        | Rate |
| -------------- | ---: |
| Road or path   | 100% |
| Light brush    |  80% |
| Medium brush   |  70% |
| Light woods    |  70% |
| Rolling hills  |  70% |
| Heavy woodland |  50% |

## Navigation

Once per uncertain travel day, make a Natural Lore navigation test. Make another test after a material route change.

On a Success, make full progress. On a Failure, lose one quarter of that day's progress. On a Fumble, make no progress and become lost.

Weather, steep slopes, navigation errors, forced stops, and exceptional mounts may alter the final distance at the GM's discretion.
