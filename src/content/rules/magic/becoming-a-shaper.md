## Access {#magic-shaping-access}

The [Shaping Talent](/rules/talents/#shaping) costs **20 IP** and requires a supernatural initiation or discovery. It gives you:

- **Shaping**, a Knowledge skill with base `INT + POW`;
- one **specialty**: a Form or a Technique;
- two known cells that contain your specialty; and
- the [Sense Magic and Dispel](/rules/magic/#ongoing-and-magical-actions--magic-universal-actions) actions.

Shapers learn exact cells, not whole Forms or Techniques: knowing `Unmake·Fire` gives no other Unmake or Fire cell. Choose cells from [Forms and Techniques](/rules/magic/#techniques-and-forms).

> **Example.** Ilyra's specialty is Fire, so both her starting cells contain Fire: `Unmake·Fire` and `Ward·Fire`.

### At character creation {#magic-at-creation}

You can buy the Talent during [character creation](/rules/characters/#character-creation--characters-starting-ip) or later. At creation, declare the Talent and reserve its 20 IP before skill point allocation. Shaping starts at its normal base of `INT + POW`, and you can spend up to 30 Knowledge points on Shaping.

This remains a real sacrifice, not a discount: you must still spend the full 20 IP, usually by trading other skill points for Improvement Points.

### INT and POW {#magic-int-and-pow}

INT and POW both establish the Shaping skill. After that, permanent, unmodified INT limits total [Active Magnitude](/rules/magic/#ongoing-and-magical-actions--magic-ongoing), while POW sets maximum PP: INT is how much magic you can keep going, POW the fuel you spend.

## Improving {#magic-improving}

Shaping caps at 100% and improves normally, at most once between adventures. Learning more cells needs suitable experience.

| Advancement                                            |                       IP |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | -----------------------: |
| New cell containing the specialty                      |                        2 |
| Any other cell; needs a significant ordeal or a master |                        4 |
| Improve Shaping                                        | 1; use normal skill gain |

## Practice and Tell {#magic-practice-tell}

Every Shaper defines:

- **Practice:** what you visibly do to cast, such as speech, gestures, a focus, prayer, or trance. It can be interrupted; if it is prevented, you cannot cast.
- **Tell:** an unavoidable sign around you and any ranged target or area. It reveals magic, not its purpose.

Anyone watching the Practice notices the Tell. It is also automatically noticeable within `10 × Magnitude` metres of you and the target, minimum 3 metres. Cosmetic style is free; concealing the Tell requires the [Veiled](/rules/talents/#veiled) Talent.
