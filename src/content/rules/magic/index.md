**Shaping** is freeform magic: describe what you want, price it with a few numbers, and roll.

- **Cells.** A **Technique** is what magic does, such as Unmake; a **Form** is what it works on, such as Fire. Together they make a **cell**, written `Technique·Form`: `Unmake·Fire`. You cast only with cells you know, normally one per Shaping; the [Confluence Talent](/rules/talents/#confluence) permits exactly two.
- **Dials.** For each outcome, rate its **Intensity** (strength) from 0 to 5. Then rate the shared **Range** (distance), **Duration** (time), and **Reach** (number, area, or size) once.
- **Magnitude.** The dials plus any **adjustments** add up to **Magnitude**, which sets the PP cost and the risk.
- **Casting.** Spend a Combat Action and roll Shaping. On a success, pay Magnitude in PP; the target can still defend.
- **Limits.** Casting above your **Safe Magnitude** is risky, and all your ongoing Shapings together cannot exceed your permanent, unmodified INT.

Read [Becoming a Shaper](/rules/magic/#becoming-a-shaper), [Building a Shaping](/rules/magic/#building-a-shaping), and [Casting and Defence](/rules/magic/#casting-and-defence) first. The later sections are reference.
