## Effect

Add the **Piercing** adjustment to a Shaping for `+1` Magnitude: the effect ignores otherwise applicable mundane armour.

Ordinary Flesh damage needs no Talent; armour simply applies to it. Only internal Flesh — reaching inside a living body — requires Piercing.
