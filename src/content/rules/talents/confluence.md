## Effect

Add the **Confluence** adjustment for `+1` Magnitude to build one Shaping from exactly two known Technique·Form cells. You must know both cells, and each must contribute at least one distinct, cell-permitted outcome.

Pay every outcome's full Intensity. Add shared Range, Duration, and Reach once, followed by Confluence and any other adjustments. Confluence uses one Combat Action, one Shaping test, one Power Point payment, and one Tell.

Each outcome retains its own target, defence, delivery, protection, and other restrictions. Resolve defence rolls per subject: against one subject, outcomes using the same defence share one defence roll; outcomes using different defences resolve separately. Different subjects always defend separately. Counter stops the whole Shaping and Dispel ends the whole ongoing Shaping.

You may declare that one outcome occurs only if another outcome affects its target. This dependency never reduces Magnitude or refunds Power Points. The dependent outcome retains its own dice, limits, and valid subjects.

### Example: life drain {#talent-confluence-life-drain}

Drain a touched Living target and heal yourself:

`Unmake·Flesh I2 + Alter·Flesh I2; Reach1, Direct, Confluence; M7`

Magnitude is 2 for Direct bodily damage, 2 for healing, 1 Reach for the target and Shaper, 1 for Direct Harm, and 1 for Confluence.

After successful hostile Touch delivery, the target opposes the Direct Harm with Resilience. If it suffers actual HP damage, heal yourself for `2D4` HP, capped by the HP the target actually lost. If the damage is resisted or reduced to zero, no healing occurs. Both outcomes remain fully paid.
