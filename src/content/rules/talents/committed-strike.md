## Effect

Before making one Close Combat or Unarmed Combat attack, forfeit all Reactions for the round. Roll the attack at `+1B`; on a hit, add 2 damage before armour.

Committed Strike cannot be declared after taking a Reaction that round and is not a standard Close Combat attack for an extra off-hand attack.
