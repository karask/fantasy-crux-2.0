## Effect

Choose one skill you have at 76% or more. After ordinary Bonus and Penalty cancellation, but before the final dice cap, remove up to 1 remaining Penalty die from that skill's tests.

Buy the Talent again for the same skill to reach Mastery II, then III, removing up to 2 or 3 Penalty dice. Mastery never creates Bonus dice, raises a skill above 100%, or widens its critical range.
