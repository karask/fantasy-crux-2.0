## Effect

Make one blunt Close Combat or Unarmed Combat attack. If the hit is not stopped, roll its listed weapon or unarmed damage plus Damage Modifier, then apply any partial Parry and armour.

- **At or above the target's Major Wound Level:** the target falls unconscious but stable and loses no HP. They wake when the scene ends; any damage wakes them at once, and a successful Healing test used as a Combat Action wakes them early.
- **Below it:** the target stays conscious and loses only the weapon's minimum listed damage, reduced by any partial Parry and armour.

Talent damage bonuses do not change the threshold or this fallback damage.
