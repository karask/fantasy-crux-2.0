## Effect

Add the **Trigger** adjustment to a Shaping for `+1` Magnitude plus a second Duration rating covering the wait.

Declare the observable event when the Shaping is built. An ordinary casting is armed when cast: fix its target, cell, outcomes, Range, Reach, and all other dials, then check target, Range, and route. A Trigger cannot choose or change them when it fires. A Trigger uses one Duration for its effect and another for its wait, and fires once; hidden qualities require Scry. Counter resolves when the Shaping is cast, while a target's ordinary Dodge, shield guard, Resilience, or Persistence resolves when the effect fires against the stored Shaping result.

For an [enchanted item](/rules/gm-tools/#enchanted-items), the observable event and fixed design are set when the item is made. Activating an Activated or Charged effect arms one use; fix its target and any permitted use-specific choices then. Arming spends the bearer's PP or one stored charge even if the Trigger expires without firing.

A successful weapon hit against the already selected target may be the event. Resolve the weapon attack first and the Shaping separately; the hit triggers but does not deliver the magic or replace its defence. Magical damage gains no weapon Damage Modifier or Critical benefit, and armour applies to each result according to its own rules. A miss leaves the Trigger waiting. Damage cannot be triggered in retaliation for an attack against or harm suffered by the caster, bearer, or another protected subject, and no Trigger adds damage to repeated weapon hits.

A later event on a Fate vow needs this Talent.
