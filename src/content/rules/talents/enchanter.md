## Effect

When you bind an indefinite Shaping to an anchor, the anchor carries the commitment: its Magnitude leaves your maximum Power Points and your active total, and the [enchantment](/rules/gm-tools/#enchanted-items) persists without you.

You may also build charged and consumable items, paying their Power Points as you make them.

Without this Talent an indefinite enchantment still works, but its Magnitude stays committed against the maker for as long as it lasts.
