## Effect

After ordinary Bonus and Penalty cancellation, but before the final dice cap, remove up to 1 remaining Penalty die from Influence tests to command or threaten.

The target must be able to make you out, by word, sign, gesture, or bearing, and must have something to lose. This never creates Bonus dice, and never applies to Influence used to persuade or bargain.

Commanding Presence applies to the [Intimidate](/rules/combat/#rounds-and-actions--intimidate) Combat Action, but never alters the enemy's condition modifiers on their resisting roll.
