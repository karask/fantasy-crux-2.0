## Effect

Choose **Bow** or **Sling** when buying this Talent. Once per round, add a positive Damage Modifier to one attack with the chosen group. Buy the Talent separately to use it with the other group.

If Rapid Shot makes two attacks, Mighty Shot applies to only one. A zero or negative Damage Modifier grants no benefit, and the Talent never applies to crossbows.
