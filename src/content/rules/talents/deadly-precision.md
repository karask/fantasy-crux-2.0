## Effect

Choose one weapon type for which you have [Favoured Weapon](/rules/talents/#favoured-weapon), [Signature Weapon](/rules/talents/#signature-weapon), and [Weapon Expertise](/rules/talents/#weapon-expertise).

Once per round, before rolling a damaging attack with that weapon, double its normal critical range for that attack. At skill 76%, the range becomes **01–14**; at 90%, **01–18**; at 100%, **01–20**. The actual unmodified skill used for the attack must be at least 76%.

When rolling Bonus or Penalty dice, use the expanded critical range to grade every candidate before selecting the result. Resolve a critical normally; this Talent grants no additional damage or effects beyond the normal critical result.

This benefit can apply to an Opportunity Attack or an extra attack granted by another Talent. It does not apply to Parry, Active Guard, unarmed attacks, natural weapons, Shaping, or attacks using the weapon as an improvised weapon.

Weapon Expertise can apply to the same attack if it is eligible under that Talent's own restrictions. A primitive version of the chosen weapon qualifies, as do shields. For a weapon with both Close and Ranged profiles, check the skill used for this attack.

You may purchase this Talent for additional weapon types, meeting its prerequisites separately for each. You may use it only once per round in total, regardless of how many times you have purchased it.
