Skills range from 0% to 100%. Test one only when the outcome is uncertain and failure would matter.

[Shaping](/rules/magic/#becoming-a-shaper) is a Knowledge skill granted by the [Shaping Talent](/rules/talents/#shaping), not a general skill.
