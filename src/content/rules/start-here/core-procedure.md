## Make a test

1. **State the approach and intended outcome.** The Gamemaster clarifies what success achieves and what failure risks.
2. **Set modifiers.** Apply Bonus or Penalty dice for circumstances, then cancel opposites and cap the net at `+3B` or `-3P`.
3. **Roll D100.** A result at or below the skill succeeds. Read the dice as rolled: do not add modifiers to the percentage.
4. **Resolve the result.** Apply the established outcome. If two characters compete directly, use an Opposed Test.

Do not roll for routine actions, impossible actions, or actions whose failure has no meaningful consequence. The Gamemaster simply states the outcome.

## Result grades

| Roll                                        | Grade    |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| From `01` through the skill's tens digit    | Critical |
| At or below the skill                       | Success  |
| Above the skill                             | Failure  |
| `99–00` below 100% skill; only `00` at 100% | Fumble   |

The critical ceiling is the integer tens digit only: 59% critically succeeds on `01–05`, while 60% critically succeeds on `01–06`. A skill below 10% has no critical range.

## Let the fiction lead

Rules settle uncertain moments; they do not replace physical sense. The Gamemaster may grant dice for a strong advantage, rule that scale or anatomy makes an action impossible, or decide that an obvious approach succeeds without a test. State such rulings before the roll whenever possible.
