Fantasy Crux Lite is a gritty percentile game about capable adventurers. Violence is dangerous, preparation matters, and Hero Points leave room for epic reversals.

Read **Core Procedure** first. Players can then build characters while the Gamemaster reads Combat and Adventuring.
