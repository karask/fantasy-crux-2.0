Fantasy Crux 2.0 Beta is a gritty percentile game about capable adventurers. Violence is dangerous, preparation matters, and Hero Points leave room for epic reversals.

**Scene.** A scene is one continuous episode within a shared immediate situation: a negotiation, search, chase, or fight. The Gamemaster announces when a meaningful break or change begins a new scene.
