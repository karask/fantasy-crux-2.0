A listed **Multiattack** spends one Combat Action. Make the first attack normally and each later attack at `-1P`; the penalties are separate, not cumulative.

- Attacks can choose the same or different targets. Each resolves separately and allows its own Reaction.
- The creature keeps its base Reaction. Dodge remains limited to the base Reaction, once per round.
- Multiattack cannot be a Reaction, and cannot combine with Charge, an off-hand attack, Rapid Shot, or another extra-attack source.
