Only a creature with Spirit Combat can start it. Spend a Combat Action at Close range; no Reaction is spent. The defender opposes with its own Spirit Combat or Persistence.

- The winner deals its listed PP damage, ignoring AP. A defender using Persistence deals `1D4` PP instead.
- Criticals affect only the result grade. Soulless creatures are invalid targets.
- A Spirit reduced to 0 PP is banished from the scene, and any possession ends.

## Possession {#creatures-possession}

For hostile possession, a capable Spirit must personally reduce a Living target to 0 PP. Then, on a later Combat Action, while both stay Close and the target remains at 0 PP, it possesses the target with no new roll. Possession suspends only the PP unconsciousness; it restores no PP.

Host and Spirit act at the host's existing Combat Order and share one Combat Action, Movement Action, and Reaction. The Spirit cannot act separately. If either has spent one of these that round, it is spent for their shared turn: possession refreshes nothing.

Spirit Combat and Spirit magic can target the possessor. Ending possession restores normal 0-PP unconsciousness. The profile states who chooses the shared actions, and any further effect.
