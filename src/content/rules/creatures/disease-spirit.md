**Disease Possession:** Choose one disease when creating the Spirit. The host chooses shared actions, immediately contracts the disease, and suffers `-1P` on Resilience against disease. A successful recovery test prevents that interval's effect, but the disease remains and tests again after its Delay until the Spirit is expelled. Expulsion permits normal recovery but removes no accumulated effect.
