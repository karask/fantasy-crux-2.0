Every profile is finished and ready to use: characteristics, derived attributes, skills, attacks, and abilities are already calculated with the Lite formulas. Read the shared procedures below first, then take any profile straight to the table.

Tags carry most of the weight. They decide whether wounds, poison, Mind effects, and Spirit Combat reach a creature at all, so check them before applying any rule that assumes a living body.

Filter the bestiary by type to narrow it to the creatures a scene needs.
