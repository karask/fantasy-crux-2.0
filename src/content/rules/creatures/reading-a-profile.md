Characteristics and attributes are fixed, ready-to-play values for an average, unremarkable member of the species. HP is `(SIZ + CON) / 2`, MWL is `HP / 2`, PP normally equals POW, and Combat Order is `(DEX + INT) / 2` minus worn-armour ENC. Unlisted skills use their normal base values.

**Characteristic dice.** Most characteristics also list the dice they came from, such as `4D6` under STR 14. `4D6 drop lowest` means roll four dice and discard the lowest. Roll a listed die to vary an individual, build a named creature, or make a group whose members differ; then recalculate HP, MWL, PP, Combat Order, and Damage Modifier. A characteristic with no listed die is fixed, such as a summoned being's stats or an undead's 0 POW.

**Attacks.** An attack lists its skill, base damage, and Size. Add DM once to close, unarmed, and thrown damage; bows, slings, and crossbows never add it. Natural weapons are Light at SIZ 1–5, Medium at 6–20, Heavy at 21–40, and Huge at 41+. Ordinary fists and kicks stay Light.

**Paralysed** creatures stay conscious and make passive resistance tests, but cannot take Actions or Reactions or move voluntarily.
