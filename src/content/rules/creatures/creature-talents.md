A profile may list any [Talent](/rules/talents/). Talents work exactly as written, but creatures never pay Improvement Points or meet their prerequisites.

## Exceptional Skill (skill) I–III {#exceptional-skill}

**Exceptional Skill** preserves competence that would otherwise require a skill above 100%. Historical ratings of 101–125 become rank I, 126–150 become rank II, and 151+ become rank III; published profiles already list the resulting rank.

After ordinary Bonus and Penalty cancellation, but before the final dice cap, remove up to the rank in remaining Penalty dice from the named skill or profile roll. This can remove a Multiattack Penalty die. It never creates Bonus dice, raises a skill above 100%, or widens its critical range.
