Use a modifier only when the situation clearly warrants it. Combine different causes under the normal `+3B`/`-3P` cap.

| Close Combat situation                            |           Modifier |
| ------------------------------------------------- | -----------------: |
| Target is helpless                                | Automatic critical |
| Target is prone, flanked, or attacked from behind |    `+1B` to attack |
| Attacker or defender has higher ground            |              `+1B` |
| Attacker or defender is prone                     |              `-1P` |
| Unstable ground                                   |              `-1P` |
| Underwater                                        |              `-2P` |
| Partial darkness                                  |              `-1P` |
| Darkness                                          |              `-2P` |
| Blinded or in pitch black                         |              `-3P` |

The darkness rows use the illumination bands defined in [Light and Darkness](/rules/adventuring/#light-and-darkness).

An unaware defender cannot React. Being behind or beside a target does not itself create surprise; the fiction determines awareness. [Battle Awareness](/rules/talents/#battle-awareness) can negate the flanking or rear-attack bonus without negating genuine surprise.

## Mounted Combat

A mounted combatant gains `+1B` on Close Combat attacks and Parries against an adjacent opponent on foot. The opponent on foot suffers `-1P` on Reactions against the rider. Do not apply these modifiers when the opponent is as tall as the mounted combatant or uses a weapon at least two Sizes larger than the rider's weapon.

- Use the mount's Movement instead of the rider's.
- On a Charge with a lance, use the mount's Damage Modifier instead of the rider's.
- A mounted character's weapon skill is capped at their Riding skill.

At the start of each round, the rider of a mount unused to battle tests Riding. On a failure, the mount Sprints away at its first opportunity that round.
