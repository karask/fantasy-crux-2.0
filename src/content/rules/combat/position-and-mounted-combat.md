Use a modifier only when the situation clearly calls for it. Modifiers from different causes add together, up to the normal `+3B` or `-3P` limit.

| Close Combat situation                            |           Modifier |
| ------------------------------------------------- | -----------------: |
| Target is helpless                                | Automatic critical |
| Target is prone, flanked, or attacked from behind |    `+1B` to attack |
| Attacker or defender has higher ground            |              `+1B` |
| Attacker or defender is prone                     |              `-1P` |
| Unstable ground                                   |              `-1P` |
| Underwater                                        |              `-2P` |
| Partial darkness                                  |              `-1P` |
| Darkness                                          |              `-2P` |
| Blinded or in pitch black                         |              `-3P` |

The darkness rows use the light levels in [Light and Darkness](/rules/adventuring/#light-and-darkness).

## Awareness {#awareness}

An unaware defender cannot React. Being behind or beside a target does not by itself make it unaware; the fiction decides awareness. [Battle Awareness](/rules/talents/#battle-awareness) can cancel the flanking or rear-attack bonus, but not genuine surprise.

## Mounted Combat

A mounted combatant gains `+1B` on Close Combat attacks and Parries against an adjacent opponent on foot. That opponent suffers `-1P` on Reactions against the rider.

Neither modifier applies if the opponent on foot is as tall as the mounted combatant, or uses a weapon at least two Sizes larger than the rider's weapon.

- Use the mount's Movement instead of the rider's.
- On a Charge with a lance, use the mount's Damage Modifier instead of the rider's.
- A mounted character's weapon skill cannot be higher than their Riding skill.

**Mounts unused to battle.** At the start of each round, the rider tests Riding. On a failure, the mount Sprints away at its first opportunity that round.
