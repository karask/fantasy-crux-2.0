## Establish and Escape

To seize an adjacent target, spend a Combat Action and make an opposed test: the attacker's Unarmed Combat against the target's choice of Dodge or Parry. Parry rolls Close Combat with a ready weapon or shield, or Unarmed Combat with nothing in hand. This opposed test is the whole attack; it spends no Reaction, is not limited to one Dodge per round, and no separate Dodge or Parry is made.

If the target wins with a weapon Parry, the weapon also deals 1 damage to the attacker and ignores AP; a shield or an unarmed Parry deals none.

If the attacker wins, both characters are **grappled**:

- They cannot take a Movement Action.
- Every test they make suffers `-1P`.
- They may attack only with Unarmed Combat or a ready Light one-handed weapon.

Either participant may release the grapple freely on their own turn.

To escape, spend a Combat Action and make an opposed Unarmed Combat test against the other participant; a grapple is wrestled out of rather than parried. If the escaping character wins, the grapple ends with the participants still adjacent.

Apply Bonus or Penalty dice when Size, anatomy, footing, or extra limbs create a material advantage. The Gamemaster may rule that a grapple is impossible. The [Wrestler](/rules/talents/#wrestler) Talent adds damaging holds, forced drops, and throws.
