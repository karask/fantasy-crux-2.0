## Establish and escape {#establish-and-escape}

Only one controller can hold a target at a time.

**Seizing.** Spend a Combat Action to seize an adjacent target. You need at least one free, usable grasping limb. Before the test, commit one limb, and you can commit a second.

**Defending.** An aware target can spend **one eligible Reaction** to oppose your Unarmed Combat with **Dodge or Parry**. It must declare and spend the Reaction before rolling the opposed test; that defence roll is the Reaction, not an extra roll afterward.

- **Dodge:** spends the base Reaction and counts toward the once-per-round Dodge limit.
- **Parry:** spends an eligible Reaction, using Close Combat with a ready weapon or shield, or Unarmed Combat with nothing in hand. An extra off-hand Parry follows its normal item restrictions and `-1P`.

**No defence.** If the target is unaware, cannot spend an eligible Reaction, or chooses not to defend, you make an unopposed Unarmed Combat test. A success establishes the hold; a failure or Fumble does not. No Reaction does not mean an automatic hold.

**Weapon Parry.** If the target wins the contest with a successful weapon Parry, the hold fails and the weapon deals you 1 HP, ignoring AP. This applies only to an attempt to establish a hold. A failed weapon Parry that still wins the opposed test deals no damage, and neither does a shield or unarmed Parry.

**The hold.** A failed attempt to establish the hold leaves your limbs free. If you win the opposed test, or succeed unopposed, you are the **controller** and the target is the **held participant**. While the grapple lasts, both of you:

- cannot take a Movement Action;
- suffer `-1P` on every test; and
- can attack or Parry only with Unarmed Combat or a ready Light one-handed weapon.

A committed limb is occupied: it cannot attack or Parry, and it does not count toward an off-hand option.

## Limbs and Leverage

You can commit a second usable limb before an establishment test. During a grapple, either participant can add or release a second committed limb freely at the start of their own turn. While committed, a second limb gives `+1B` on establishment, escape, Wrestler, and opposing grapple tests.

The held participant commits one free, usable limb the first time they make an escape, Wrestler, or opposing grapple test; that first limb gives no bonus.

The controller cannot release their last committed limb without ending the grapple. All committed limbs become free when the grapple ends.

## Escape, release, and assistance {#escape-release-assistance}

Only the controller can release the grapple freely, on their own turn. The held participant must escape: spend a Combat Action and win an opposed Unarmed Combat test against the controller. Opposing an escape or a Wrestler test within an established hold spends no Reaction; only a Dodge or Parry against the initial seizure costs one.

The grapple ends on a successful escape, a separation, the controller's incapacitation, or the loss of the controller's last committed limb. After an ordinary release or escape, both stay adjacent.

**Helpers.** A target already held by one controller cannot be independently grappled. Adjacent helpers can instead give combined Assistance on one grapple test, using the normal [Assistance rules](/rules/skills/#assistance). Each helper commits one usable limb for that test, and a helper never becomes the controller.

## Size and Circumstances

Size, anatomy, footing, and similar circumstances are for the Gamemaster to judge.

- One clear circumstance gives one side either `+1B` or `-1P`, never both Bonus and Penalty dice for the same circumstance.
- An overwhelming difference can be worth `+2B`, `+3B`, `-2P`, or `-3P`, or make the attempt impossible.
- The second limb's `+1B` is separate from this judgement.

The [Wrestler](/rules/talents/#wrestler) Talent adds damaging holds, forced drops, and throws.
