summary: Fast, dangerous combat built around one action, one move, one base Reaction, decisive criticals, and lasting wounds.
---

Combat Rounds last five seconds. Position, preparation, and defence matter: a capable warrior can still be overwhelmed after their Reaction is spent.

Special techniques such as disarming, tripping, rapid shooting, and shield rushing are optional [Talents](/rules/talents/), not basic combat options.
