Combat is fast and dangerous. Each five-second round gives you one Combat Action, one Movement Action, and one Reaction. Your Reaction is your defence: once it is spent, even a skilled warrior can be overwhelmed.

Special moves, such as disarming, tripping, rapid shooting, and shield rushing, need [Talents](/rules/talents/).
