**Active Guard** is a Parry against a ranged attack. After a ranged attack or individually targeted Projected Shaping succeeds against you, you can React with Active Guard before resolution, if you are aware and have an eligible item ready.

## Using Active Guard

| Incoming attack                         | Eligible Parry    | Modifier |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------------- | -------: |
| Thrown weapon                           | Ready weapon      |    `-1P` |
| Thrown weapon                           | Ready shield      |     None |
| Bow, crossbow, or sling projectile      | Ready shield only |    `-1P` |
| Individually targeted Projected Shaping | Ready shield only |    `-1P` |

Roll Close Combat with the item.

- **Against a mundane attack,** oppose the original attack roll and use the [critical matrix](/rules/combat/#critical-hits-and-fumbles), as for a normal Parry. [Parry Size](/rules/combat/#attacks-and-reactions--parry-size) applies only after a winning Active Guard. An ordinary successful Active Guard still turns a critical into an ordinary hit, but blocks no damage.
- **Against a Projected Shaping,** oppose Close Combat against the Shaper's original Shaping result. If you win, compare your shield with the Shaping's [Impact Size](/rules/magic/#casting-and-defence--magic-cover-shields).
- **With an off-hand item,** you can use your [extra off-hand Reaction](/rules/combat/#off-hand-options) for Active Guard. Its `-1P` adds to the modifier above.

## Limits

- Arrows, crossbow bolts, and sling stones are Light. Shields are Medium (small), Heavy (medium), or Huge (large), so a winning ordinary Active Guard with any shield blocks them completely. Thrown weapons keep their listed Size.
- Weapons cannot Active Guard Projected Shapings; only shields can.
- A shield is not ready while you wield a two-handed item.
- Holding a shield gives attackers no penalty unless you have [Shield Cover](/rules/talents/#shield-cover). Terrain cover is separate.
- [Missile Guard](/rules/talents/#missile-guard) removes the `-1P` when you use a shield.
