## Choose One Option

A character holding two ready one-handed items may use one of the following options once per round. An empty hand counts as a ready one-handed item — a punch or kick is a weapon for the purpose of attacking — so a weapon and a free hand qualifies, as do a weapon and shield or two bare hands. Wielding a two-handed item leaves no hand free and does not qualify.

- **Extra attack:** immediately after a standard Close Combat or Unarmed Combat attack, make one free attack with the other item, or unarmed with a free hand, at `-1P`. It spends no Action or Reaction. Apply Damage Modifier to both attacks normally.
- **Extra off-hand Reaction:** reserve the other item for one additional Parry or Opportunity Attack at `-1P`.

The two options are mutually exclusive: after using either, the other is unavailable until the next round. The character retains their base Reaction in both cases.

The extra Reaction cannot Dodge or activate Protector. Any rule that forfeits all Reactions also suppresses it. An Opportunity Attack never produces an extra off-hand attack.

[Off-Hand Mastery](/rules/talents/#off-hand-mastery) removes the `-1P` from both options, but does not grant more uses or allow both in one round.
