## Choose One Option

If you hold two ready one-handed items, you can use **one** of these options once per round. An empty hand counts, because a punch or kick is a weapon when you attack. So a weapon and a free hand qualify, as do a weapon and a shield, or two bare hands. A two-handed item leaves no hand free.

- **Extra attack:** right after a standard Close Combat or Unarmed Combat attack, make one free attack at `-1P` with your other item, or unarmed with your free hand. It costs no Action or Reaction. Add your Damage Modifier to both attacks as normal.
- **Extra off-hand Reaction:** keep your other item ready for one extra Parry or Opportunity Attack at `-1P`. The Parry can be an [Active Guard](/rules/combat/#active-guard), which adds its own modifier.

Once you use either option, the other is unavailable until the next round. You keep your base Reaction either way.

## Limits {#off-hand-limits}

- The extra Reaction cannot be used to Dodge or to activate [Protector](/rules/talents/#protector).
- Any rule that takes away all your Reactions also takes away the extra Reaction.
- An Opportunity Attack never gives an extra off-hand attack.
- [Off-Hand Mastery](/rules/talents/#off-hand-mastery) removes the `-1P` from both options, but gives no extra uses and does not allow both in one round.
