Gamemasters carry more rules than players. These cover what happens above the scale of one character in one fight: a hoard worth looting, a crossing that turns into a storm, two armies meeting, or a cast of NPCs who each need a number.

Nothing here is required. Use the sections your campaign needs and leave the rest.
