Gamemasters carry more rules than players. These handle what happens above the scale of one character in one fight — a hoard worth looting, a crossing that turns into a storm, two armies meeting, or a whole cast of NPCs who each need a number.

Nothing here is required. Take the sections a campaign actually uses and leave the rest.
