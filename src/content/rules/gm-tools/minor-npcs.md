A minor NPC is not necessarily a weak one. They are simply NPCs who do not warrant a full profile. The shorthand below is useful for improvising during play, and even more useful for writing an adventure down.

Give a brief description, the specialty skill, then the items and abilities in brackets. A minor NPC normally has 10 HP and a Combat Order of 10; raise either when the role calls for it.

Skills close to the description sit at the specialty level, and everything else drops in `10%` steps as it grows less relevant. Reduce as far as the role deserves.

> Human Guard 50 (Shortsword `1D6+1`, Leather 2)

This guard is good at his job: 50% Close Combat and 50% Perception, 40% Dodge, Resilience, and Ranged Combat, 30% Persistence, less in anything else. He carries a shortsword and wears leather armour for 2 AP.

> Elven Scholar 70 (Dagger `1D4+1`, 2 GP, Amulet of Protection 2)

The scholar has 70% in a couple of Lore skills or languages, perhaps 60% Persistence or Healing, but only 40% Close Combat. Her amulet carries a permanent Ward enchantment worth 2 AP.

Extraordinary abilities follow the same compression. Note Talents by name, and Shaping as the cells the NPC can actually cast.

> Goblin Witch Doctor 60 (Dagger `1D4+1`; Shaping 50%: Conjure·Fire, Alter·Flesh, Bend·Mind)

He has 60% Healing, 50% Shaping, around 40% Persistence, Resilience, and Dodge, and knows the three cells listed.
