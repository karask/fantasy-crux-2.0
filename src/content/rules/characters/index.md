A character is both a person in the shared world and a set of rules that describe what they can do.
Start with a clear concept, then follow the steps below.

1. Choose a [concept](/rules/characters/character-creation/).
2. Generate the seven [characteristics](/rules/characters/characteristics/).
3. Calculate [attributes](/rules/characters/attributes/).
4. If becoming a [Shaper](/rules/magic/becoming-a-shaper/), declare access and reserve its IP.
5. Calculate and improve [starting skills](/rules/characters/starting-skills/).
6. Spend starting Improvement Points and choose [equipment and details](/rules/characters/equipment-and-details/).
7. Record [Hero Points and wound states](/rules/characters/hero-points-and-wounds/).

Characters continue to grow through [Improvement Points](/rules/characters/improvement/).
