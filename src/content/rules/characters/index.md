A character is both a person in the shared world and a set of rules that describe what they can do.
Start with a clear concept, then follow the steps below.

1. Choose a [concept](/rules/characters/#character-creation--characters-concept).
2. Generate the seven [characteristics](/rules/characters/#character-creation--characters-characteristics).
3. Calculate [attributes](/rules/characters/#character-creation--characters-attributes).
4. Calculate and improve [starting skills](/rules/characters/#character-creation--characters-starting-skills).
5. Spend [starting Improvement Points](/rules/characters/#character-creation--characters-starting-ip) on improvements and Talents.
6. Choose [equipment and details](/rules/characters/#character-creation--characters-equipment-details).
7. Record [Hero Points and wound states](/rules/characters/#character-creation--characters-hero-points).

Characters continue to grow through [Improvement Points](/rules/characters/#improvement).
