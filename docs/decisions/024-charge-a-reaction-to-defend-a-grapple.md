# ADR-024: Spend a Reaction to defend against a grapple

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-09-07

## Context

The initial grapple contest previously let the target Dodge or Parry without
spending a Reaction and exempted Dodge from its once-per-round limit. This was
an exception to the ordinary defence costs and confused the player reference.
The user requested that Dodge or Parry against a grapple attempt spend a Reaction.

## Decision

- The attacker spends a Combat Action and commits a usable grasping limb.
- An aware defender declares and spends one eligible Reaction before the
  opposed Unarmed Combat versus Dodge or Parry test.
- Dodge uses the base Reaction and counts toward the once-per-round limit.
  Parry follows normal eligibility, including the extra off-hand Parry's item
  restrictions and penalty.
- The opposed test remains the whole contest; there is no second defence roll.
- An unaware target, a target with no eligible Reaction, or one declining to
  defend makes no defence roll. The attacker must succeed at an unopposed
  Unarmed Combat test; lack of a Reaction does not automatically establish a hold.
- Opposed results, the successful weapon-Parry consequence, committed limbs,
  held restrictions and release conditions otherwise remain unchanged.
- This cost applies to defending the initial seizure, not opposing escape or
  Wrestler tests within an established hold.

## Consequences

The main rules, website build, handbook drafts, current handbook PDFs, reference
cards and pregenerated-party play reference use the same defence cost. The
statistical audit distinguishes defended and undefended initial attempts.
Earlier versioned PDFs and review records are retained as superseded snapshots,
not current rules. The site's current download source selects the corrected cards.
