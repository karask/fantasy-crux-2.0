# ADR-020: Prohibit recurring and retaliatory Shaping harm

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-09-06

## Context

Duration already prohibited repeated damage, but created hazards carried an
exception, and Trigger did not expressly distinguish an observable weapon hit
from a new weapon-delivery subsystem. This left room for target-bound recurring
harm, damaging retaliation, repeated weapon damage, deferred target selection,
and ambiguous interaction between weapon and magical defences.

## Decision

Every Shaping damage outcome resolves once. Duration cannot repeat damage on a
target, through a hazard, on every weapon hit, or in retaliation. Created magic
may still produce ordinary environmental consequences when it genuinely changes
mundane fuel, structures, or surroundings, but it deals no fixed magical damage
each round.

Keep the existing Trigger adjustment as the only one-use route. It fixes every
target and dial when cast. A later successful weapon hit against that selected
target may be its observable event, but the weapon and Shaping resolve
separately with their own damage, defence, armour, and Critical rules. The hit
does not become magical delivery and a Trigger never chooses a future target.

## Consequences

- Shaping has no recurring harm, damaging retaliation, or repeated magical
  weapon damage.
- A one-use triggered strike needs no new rider or delivery subsystem.
- Missing the declared weapon attack leaves its Trigger waiting until expiry.
- A damaging enchantment must be activated and paid anew for each one-use
  targeted effect; it never adds damage automatically to successive hits.
