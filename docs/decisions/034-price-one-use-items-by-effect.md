# ADR-034: Price one-use enchanted items by their effect

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-14

## Context

Every enchantment bound at Duration 4, including a one-charge potion or scroll.
A `1D4` healing potion was therefore Alter·Flesh I1 + D4 = Magnitude 5: a
5-hour ritual at overreach risk for a Veteran, 5 PP for an effect worth 1 PP to
cast, and 5 points of Enchantment Capacity (or committed maximum PP and active
total) until drunk. A POW 16 Enchanter could keep only three minor potions in
circulation. It was also unclear whether a maker without Enchanter paid PP for a
charge in addition to the commitment.

## Decision

- A one-use item prices its effect with the effect's own Duration and adds 1 for
  the binding. The charge keeps until used.
- Binding remains a ritual of one hour per Magnitude.
- The maker pays the PP when binding. Until used, the Magnitude occupies
  Enchantment Capacity with Enchanter, or commits maximum PP and active total
  without it.
- Reusable, multi-charge, and Continuous items keep Duration 4.

Shelf-life tiers (keeps until rest, a week, or indefinitely) were considered and
rejected: capacity and commitment already cap stockpiles, and expiry dates add
bookkeeping.

## Consequences

- A `1D4` healing potion is Magnitude 2. A POW 16 Enchanter can keep eight in
  circulation; a maker without Enchanter carries their cost in maximum PP and
  active total until each is used.
- Potions and scrolls become ordinary adventuring gear rather than luxuries,
  while multi-use items remain significant investments.
