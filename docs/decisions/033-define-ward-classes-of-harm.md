# ADR-033: Define Ward classes of harm

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-14

## Context

Ward granted AP of twice Intensity "against one defined class of harm", but no
class was defined. The NPC Warding Amulet used "weapon and natural attacks",
which is effectively all physical harm, and the stoneskin example named no class
at all. Earth, Flesh, and Force/Motion Wards therefore covered nearly every
attack for the same price as a Fire Ward that mattered only against fire. Ward,
Mind, Paths, and Fate had only passing mentions ("Ward a threshold", "Ward
against doom"), and Ward·Mind had none.

## Decision

- Ward AP protects against one of three classes: **physical** (blows, missiles,
  falls, crushing), **elemental** (fire and heat, cold, lightning, acid), or
  **spiritual** (PP damage, Ward·Spirit only).
- A class covers mundane and Shaped harm alike; there is no separate magical
  class.
- Covering both physical and elemental harm raises Intensity by 1, and the cell
  must explain both.
- The Form must explain the hardening. Fire protects only against elemental
  harm; Force/Motion only against physical harm.
- Mind, Paths, and Fate grant no AP. Ward·Mind grants a Persistence bonus or
  denies one mental intrusion, Ward·Paths denies a threshold, and Ward·Fate
  grants a defence bonus against a named doom.

## Consequences

- A single-class Ward at Intensity 3 still equals plate against that class; full
  6 AP coverage of both classes needs Intensity 4.
- Because Direct Harm ignores worn armour but not Ward AP, an elemental Ward is
  the main defence against Direct fire and cold Shaping.
- The Warding Amulet and stoneskin example now name physical harm.
