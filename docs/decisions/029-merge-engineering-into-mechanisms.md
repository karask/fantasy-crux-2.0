# 029 — Merge Engineering into Mechanisms

## Decision

Engineering is an application of Mechanisms, not a separately purchased skill. Mechanisms retains its DEX + INT base and covers locks, devices, traps, and large engineered works. Keep the existing engineering anchor for inbound links.

Projects still require suitable tools, materials, labour, and time. Craft produces appropriate goods and components; Sailing navigates and manoeuvres vessels. Weak Point now requires Mechanisms 51% and tests Mechanisms.

## Reason

The skills overlap, while engineering projects occur too infrequently in many adventures to reward a separate skill investment. The combined skill follows the broad application-based structure of Athletics.

## Reference migration

Published NPCs with both skills retain the higher score, not their sum. Pregenerated PCs retain their exact creation budgets and caps: Dori moves his former 30 Engineering allocation points to Craft (Mason), retaining Mechanisms 54%; Nerin combines his two 15-point allocations into Mechanisms +30, for 61%. Other PCs lose only the separate untrained Engineering row. Historical decision records and superseded review snapshots remain historical.
