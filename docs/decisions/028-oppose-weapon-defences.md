# ADR-028: Oppose weapon defences while retaining critical demotion

## Status

Accepted — 2026-09-13.

## Decision

Dodge, Parry, and mundane Active Guard oppose the original successful weapon
attack roll. Each participant selects modifier-dice results first. Compare
grades, then higher successful roll, higher unmodified base skill, then favour
the defender on an exact tie. A miss needs no Reaction or defence roll.

A winning Dodge blocks the hit. A winning ordinary Parry or guard applies
Size; a winning critical defence blocks all damage regardless of Size.
Losing ordinary defences against ordinary attacks have no effect. When both
rolls are critical, compare them: the winning attack retains critical damage.

The one exception is an ordinary successful defence against a critical attack:
it demotes the attack to an ordinary hit without stopping or reducing damage.
Normal damage bonuses, Damage Modifier, and armour then apply. Do not resolve
defence again. This preserves ADR-027's damage-bonus decision.

Grappling and Shaping retain their separate opposed procedures and do not use
this weapon-critical demotion exception. Reaction costs and limits are unchanged.

## Consequences

Attack quality now matters against successful ordinary defences. Critical
defences are no longer automatic protection against higher winning criticals.
Defence remains worthwhile against a critical even when it achieves only an
ordinary success. The audit enumerates weapon-specific hit outcomes separately
from general opposed contests, where matching failures can still produce a winner.
