# ADR-030: Form-linked summons, standing orders, and calling

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-12

## Context

A generic summon spent the Shaper's Combat Action for every consequential act.
Its attack therefore competed with the Shaper's own casting, and a Veteran's
Intensity 3 summon dealt about 1–1.5 damage per round where a firebolt cast
with the same action dealt about 2.9. Summoning worked only as a mobile
barrier.

Every Form produced an identical summon. The profile also omitted Size, SIZ,
Perception, PP, and creature tags.

Named beings set Intensity "by threat" without a scale and resisted only with
Persistence. Low-Persistence brutes such as the Giant and Troll (25%) were
therefore as easy to call as a Dog or Raven. Any creature with a profile could
be called. It then arrived uncontrolled and needed a second
Persistence-resisted binding outcome, and no rule named the calling cell.

## Decision

- **Standing orders.** A summon receives one standing order when cast and acts
  on its own turn immediately after its Shaper with its own Actions and base
  Reaction. Changing the order costs the Shaper's Movement Action within
  15 metres and line of effect. The one combat-capable summon limit and Active
  Magnitude cost remain.
- **Form-linked conjuring.** The generic table gains strike Size and SIZ.
  Each Form that can create a body sets its being's tags, one signature trait,
  and one drawback at no cost. Mind creates only non-combat phantasms. Spirit,
  Paths, and Fate create no beings, because Shaping never creates a soul.
- **Tag pricing.** Tags are not surcharged, because each cuts both ways. A mind
  for a Mindless being, or removing a Form drawback, adds 1 Intensity. Other
  capabilities keep their own benchmarks. Conjured beings are never
  Incorporeal.
- **Form-bound calling.** Only Spirits (Conjure·Spirit), Elementals (Conjure
  of their element), otherworldly servants of a pledged power, and linked
  individuals (Conjure·Paths) can be called. Ordinary creatures are moved with
  Paths, swayed with Mind, or replaced with a conjured being.
- **Threat Intensity.** Calling Intensity comes from a published profile method:
  average the body and best-attack ratings, round up, and add 1 once for a
  defined special ability.
- **Resistance floor and binding.** An unwilling being opposes with the higher
  of Persistence or 20% × Threat Intensity. A successful call binds it under
  standing orders for the Duration. A called being's ongoing Shapings count
  against its caller's Active Magnitude.

## Consequences

- A Veteran's Intensity 3 conjured being adds a 10 HP body and roughly 1–1.5
  damage per round for a scene at Magnitude 4. That is useful without
  replacing the Shaper's own casting. Expert Intensity 4 summons approach a
  fighter's output and should be watched in playtest.
- Calling no longer needs Confluence or a second roll, and low-Persistence
  monsters are no longer the easiest beings to call.
- Threat Intensity also serves as a general creature-danger yardstick.
- The Shaping reference cards (v03) describe the superseded command cost and
  need a new version before they are reissued.
