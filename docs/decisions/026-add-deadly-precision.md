# ADR-026: Add Deadly Precision

## Status

Accepted — 2026-09-12.

## Decision

Add Deadly Precision at 5 IP, requiring Favoured Weapon, Signature Weapon,
and Weapon Expertise for the same weapon type, plus at least 76% in the
unmodified combat skill used for the attack. The complete weapon chain costs
16 IP.

Once per round across all purchases, declare one damaging attack before rolling
and double its normal critical range. Apply the expanded range to every Bonus
or Penalty dice candidate before selection. This changes critical frequency,
not the normal critical consequences or total unmodified success chance.

Opportunity Attacks and extra attacks qualify. Defensive rolls do not.
Weapon Expertise may share the attack only under its own restrictions; the
new Talent does not remove its Rapid Shot or off-hand exclusions.

## Rationale

Critical attacks overcome ordinary Reactions and can maximise weapon damage
and positive Damage Modifier while ignoring armour. A once-per-round limit
keeps extra attacks and additional weapon purchases from multiplying this
benefit. Cost and prerequisites reward sustained weapon specialisation.
The initial price remains subject to playtesting.
