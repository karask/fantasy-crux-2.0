# ADR-016: Keep mythic prices separate from enchantment commitments

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-09-05

## Context

Duration 5 makes an enchantment permanent and requires a lasting mythic price.
Earlier wording allowed that price to free the enchantment from its creator's
active total and did not explicitly carry the Duration 4 maximum-PP commitment
into Duration 5. That made a narrative price duplicate part of the mechanical
benefit of the 10 IP Enchanter Talent.

The lasting price represents the cost of crossing a mythic boundary. The
creator's PP commitment, active limit, or Enchantment Capacity instead limits
how much active supernatural work one Shaper can leave in the world.

## Decision

Without Enchanter, both Duration 4 and Duration 5 enchantments commit maximum
PP equal to their full Magnitude and count that Magnitude against the creator's
active total until they permanently end.

Duration 5 additionally requires its declared lasting price. That price never
reduces Magnitude, PP commitment, active Magnitude, Enchantment Capacity, or
Dispel strength.

With Enchanter, either duration uses Enchantment Capacity instead of PP
commitment and active Magnitude. Duration 5 still requires its lasting price,
and its full Magnitude still occupies capacity without exemption.

## Consequences

- A non-Enchanter cannot use a mythic price to create an independently
  sustained permanent enchantment.
- Enchanter remains the sole route for moving bound Magnitude out of both the
  creator's PP commitment and active total.
- The distinction between Duration 4 and Duration 5 is persistence and mythic
  cost, not a cheaper resource burden.
- Anchors and Dispel vulnerability remain unchanged.
