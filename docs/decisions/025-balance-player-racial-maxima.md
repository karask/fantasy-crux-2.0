# ADR-025: Balance player racial maxima

## Status

Accepted — 2026-09-11.

## Decision

Player racial maxima use the approved Fantasy Races table rather than the
highest creature-generation roll plus 3. Count all seven characteristics,
including SIZ, toward a maximum total of 147. Goblins are an intentional
exception at 144, retaining CHA 21. Starting point budgets remain unchanged.

SIZ contributes to HP, Major Wound Level, and Damage Modifier despite being
fixed during ordinary advancement. Equal maximum totals constrain racial
advantages but do not establish equal power for every build.

Creature profiles and their dice remain unchanged. For random player creation,
apply the optional characteristic swap, then cap each result at its player
racial maximum without redistributing excess points. The point method keeps
its existing cap of 18 or the racial maximum, whichever is lower.

This supersedes the dice-derived player-maxima policy recorded in ADR-004 and
ADR-005; their creature-profile and other GM-tool decisions remain in effect.
