# ADR-032: Limit Shaping healing like mundane treatment

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-12

## Context

Alter·Flesh restored `1D4` HP per Intensity for 1 PP per Intensity, with no limit
on repeated castings. A starting healer could restore about 26 HP from one PP
pool, while mundane treatment allows one `1D4` Healing attempt per patient
before the next full rest. HP damage rarely carried between encounters,
undermining the gritty tone. It was also unclear whether low-Intensity healing
restored HP to a Wounded subject before Surgery.

## Decision

A subject benefits from only one HP-restoring Shaping before its next full rest,
matching mundane treatment, including enchanted items
and Confluence outcomes. Removing only Bleeding and Dying is unlimited. Below
Intensity 3, healing restores HP to a Wounded subject but neither ends Wounded
nor unlocks natural recovery.

## Consequences

- Magic heals larger amounts than mundane treatment (`2D4`–`3D4` at Intensity
  2–3) but no longer through repetition.
- Emergency stabilisation remains available without limit.
- Intensity 3 healing still counts as Surgery.
