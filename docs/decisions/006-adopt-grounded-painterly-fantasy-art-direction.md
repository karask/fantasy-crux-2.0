# ADR-006: Adopt Grounded Painterly Fantasy as the illustration style

## Status

Superseded by [ADR-014](014-adopt-vivid-ink-production-art.md)

## Date

2026-07-23

## Context

Fantasy Crux needs a consistent illustration language before rules artwork is
added. Eight matched style studies rendered the same frontier scenery and
warrior so medium, detail density, color, and tone could be compared without
changing the underlying subjects.

Study 7 was selected visually. Its first written label described it as a bold
adventure cartoon, but production tests showed that wording did not describe
the actual reference images. It led generated creature art toward heavy
contours, cel shading, saturated card-art lighting, and excess spectacle.

The canonical study-7 woman instead establishes grounded traditional fantasy
painting: believable forms, matte brushwork, muted color, selective detail,
natural poses, and quiet narrative atmosphere. The visual reference is
authoritative over a style name or prompt shorthand.

## Decision

Adopt **study 7, Grounded Painterly Fantasy**, as the canonical illustration
style.

The direction uses:

- believable anatomy simplified into broad painted masses;
- matte, opaque, visibly brushed surfaces;
- selective detail and naturally varied edges;
- grounded asymmetrical poses;
- muted earth, smoke-blue, ochre, weathered-red, and charcoal palettes;
- restrained warm/cool light and environmental drama;
- practical equipment, physical weight, and a mature tone.

Avoid epic promotional card-art spectacle, cinematic rim glow, symmetrical
display poses, evenly distributed microdetail, black contours, cel shading,
graphic-novel hatching, and glossy digital rendering.

The website's Cold Iron palette does not constrain illustration colors. The
complete production guidance and canonical references live in
[`art/art-direction/`](../../art/art-direction/).

## Alternatives considered

### Bold adventure cartoon interpretation

Strong small-size readability, but the contour-heavy cel-shaded rendering does
not match the selected study-7 references.

### Epic fantasy card-art interpretation

Offers dramatic silhouettes and color, but is too polished, saturated,
symmetrical, and spectacle-driven beside the canonical woman portrait.

### Other recorded studies

The remaining studies preserve useful alternate directions but were not
selected. They remain indexed in the alternative style log.

## Consequences

- The canonical images outrank style labels when wording and visuals conflict.
- Every candidate should be compared directly with the study-7 woman before
  publication.
- Creature prompts must include explicit anatomy counts where applicable.
- New art should favor grounded figure-study composition over theatrical
  display poses.
- The unselected studies remain preserved and indexed for reference.
- A future overall style change should supersede this ADR rather than editing
  away the decision history.
