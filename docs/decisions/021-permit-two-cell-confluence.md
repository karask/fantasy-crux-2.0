# ADR-021: Permit two-cell Shapings through Confluence

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-09-06

## Context

The Shaping rules permit several distinct outcomes within one known cell. Some
concepts, such as draining an enemy's life to heal the Shaper, require outcomes
from two different cells and therefore need a bounded learned exception.

## Decision

Add Confluence as a 5-IP Talent requiring Shaping 76%. For a +1 Magnitude
adjustment, it permits exactly two known Technique·Form cells in one Shaping.
Each cell must contribute a distinct permitted outcome, and every outcome pays
its full Intensity. Shared Range, Duration, and Reach are counted once.

Confluence resolves as one Combat Action, Shaping test, PP payment, and Tell.
Each outcome retains its applicable target, defence, delivery, and protection.
One outcome may depend on another affecting its target, without reducing the
Magnitude or refunding PP.

## Consequences

- Same-cell additional outcomes remain available without a Talent.
- Two-cell combinations require Expert Shaping, 5 IP, and +1 Magnitude.
- A linked outcome can depend on actual effect, supporting life drain without
  guaranteeing healing when its damaging outcome deals no HP damage.
