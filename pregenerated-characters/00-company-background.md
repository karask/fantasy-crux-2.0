# The Last Wagon Company

## Nobody gets left behind

Six weeks ago, a flooded caravan was ordered abandoned with wounded passengers still aboard. You refused. Some of you braced the wagon and carried passengers through the water; others found shelter, tended injuries or went back for those still missing. You brought the people home together.

Afterward, you registered the Last Wagon Company with equal shares and one promise: nobody gets left behind. You have since worked, eaten and argued together. The bonds on your character sheets grew out of that rescue; you begin play as companions who already trust one another.

Choose one of the eight characters that follow: Mara Holt, Pebb Dallow, Dori Ashlar, Tamsin Reed, Ilen Sedge, Orren Pike, Seris Vale or Nerin Quill. The unchosen companions can be elsewhere on company business.
