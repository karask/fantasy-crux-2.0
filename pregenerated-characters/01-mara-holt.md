## Character

**Human, 27, she/her. Former road-watch escort; shield-bearing protector.**

**Concept:** A methodical escort who holds dangerous ground and organises physical rescues, determined to build a company that protects people regardless of who pays.

**Appearance:** Broad-shouldered and square-faced, with close-cropped brown hair, a broken nose that healed slightly crooked, and a pale rope scar across her left palm. Her leather armour is repeatedly stitched at the right shoulder. A plain shield and carefully maintained sword look used rather than ceremonial.

**Background:** Mara left the road watch after being ordered to prioritise a merchant's cargo over stranded passengers. During the flood she braced the wagon and returned for its last injured guard. She can keep a route secure and help someone climb; she is still learning how to lead without falling back on orders.

**Personality:** Patient, practical, and quietly funny. She expresses affection by mending straps and remembering who dislikes cold tea. Under stress she starts assigning jobs before asking what others have noticed. When challenged, she can stop, listen, and change her plan.

**Loyalties and goal:** The company promise comes first. She wants frightened travellers to feel relieved when they see the charter, and wants this commission to establish that reputation.

**Bond:** Pebb persuaded a hostile tollkeeper to open a shelter during the flood. Mara trusts Pebb's judgement and gives negotiations time to work.

**Play her:** Check the footing, name an exit, and put yourself beside the person who needs protection. “Tell me when you're ready. I'll hold.”

## Abilities

### Protector — 2 IP

**Prerequisite met:** Close Combat 51%.

When an adjacent ally is hit by an attack you are aware of, spend your base Reaction to Parry for them with a ready shield using Close Combat. On success, reduce the incoming damage against your shield normally; on failure, the ally remains hit. Against a ranged attack, apply [Active Guard's](https://fantasycrux.org/rules/combat/#active-guard) eligibility and modifiers. The ally keeps their own Reaction for another trigger but cannot also React to this attack.

Protector can never use the extra off-hand Reaction.

Source: [Protector](../src/content/rules/talents/protector.md).

### Shield Cover — 3 IP

**Prerequisite met:** Close Combat 51%.

While aware and holding a ready medium or large shield, impose `-1P` on ranged attacks and any individually targeted [Projected Shaping](https://fantasycrux.org/rules/magic/#casting-and-defence--magic-cover-shields). This spends no Reaction and applies before Active Guard. Use only the best Shield Cover or terrain penalty. Dodge and [Active Guard](https://fantasycrux.org/rules/combat/#active-guard) remain available; areas and Direct Harm gain no benefit.

Source: [Shield Cover](../src/content/rules/talents/shield-cover.md).

### Favoured Weapon (Arming Sword) — 3 IP

**Prerequisite met:** Close Combat 51% or Ranged Combat 51%, as used by the chosen weapon. **Selected application/weapon:** Arming Sword.

Choose one named weapon from the Close Combat or Ranged weapons table, other than Unarmed or Improvised rock. You must have at least 51% in one combat skill used to attack with it. Record the choice with the Talent, such as **Favoured Weapon (Arming Sword)**. Shields qualify.

Every damaging Close Combat or Ranged Combat attack made with the chosen weapon adds 1 damage before Parry and armour. The bonus applies to every matching hit. If the weapon has both Close and Ranged profiles, the Talent applies to both. A primitive version of the chosen weapon qualifies. An attack that uses any weapon as an improvised club does not.

The Talent adds nothing when another rule replaces normal weapon damage, including Disarm, Trip, Shield Rush, Subdue, or an unopposed Critical. A Critical opposed by an ordinary Reaction still rolls normal damage, so the bonus applies.

Buy this Talent again to choose a different weapon, but never more than once for the same weapon. It does not apply to unarmed attacks, natural weapons, or Shaping.

Source: [Favoured Weapon](../src/content/rules/talents/favoured-weapon.md).

### Athletics Expertise (Climbing) — 2 IP

**Prerequisite met:** Athletics 51%. **Selected application/weapon:** Climbing.

Choose one Athletics application: acrobatics, brute force, climbing, jumping, running, or swimming. Record the choice with the Talent, such as **Athletics Expertise (Climbing)**.

After ordinary Bonus and Penalty cancellation, but before the final dice cap, remove up to 1 remaining Penalty die from Athletics tests using the chosen application. This never creates Bonus dice and applies to no other use of Athletics.

Buy this Talent again for a different application, but never more than once for the same one.

Source: [Athletics Expertise](../src/content/rules/talents/athletics-expertise.md).
