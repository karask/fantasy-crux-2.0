## Character

**Orc, 26, he/him. Dock labourer and salvage hand; strong rescuer and wrestler.**

**Concept:** A powerful dockworker who shifts obstacles and restrains dangerous people, seeking steady work that lets him protect his neighbours without becoming somebody's hired bully.

**Appearance:** Tall and thick through the chest, with grey-green skin, small lower tusks, and dark hair tied in a short queue. His left ear is flattened from an old wrestling bout. He handles delicate cups with conspicuous care and tests a lifting strap before trusting it.

**Background:** Orren grew up around Vellbridge's river wharves, learning salvage work and recreational wrestling from fellow labourers. He was hired to unload the last wagon; when it flooded, he stayed to lift it. He carries a great hammer for serious threats but knows wrestling and Subdue when the aim is to take someone alive.

**Personality:** Courteous, literal, and fond of memorising dreadful verse. He dislikes intimidation disguised as leadership. His flaw is accepting a difficult physical job before admitting he needs help. He responds well to someone offering a concrete share of the load.

**Loyalties and goal:** He is loyal to wharf workers and his company partners. He wants this commission to prove that his strength can earn an honest living without demanding violence at every disagreement.

**Bond:** Mara handed Orren responsibility during the rescue without treating him as expendable muscle. He trusts her to ask for his judgement as well as his strength.

**Play him:** Offer a practical hand, secure a line, and distinguish stopping an enemy from killing one. “Which end do you want lifted?”

## Abilities

### Adrenaline Surge — 4 IP (required ancestry ability)

Once per combat, immediately after becoming Wounded or Fatigued, ignore the test, Movement, and Combat Order penalties caused by Wounded and Fatigued until the end of your next turn. Also ignore the first 1 HP you would lose from Bleeding during that time. Bleeding is not removed and continues normally afterwards. Exhausted, unconscious, and Dying are unaffected.

Source: [Fantasy Races](../src/content/rules/gm-tools/fantasy-races.md).

### Wrestler — 2 IP

**Prerequisite met:** Unarmed Combat 51%.

While already [grappling](https://fantasycrux.org/rules/combat/#grappling), spend a Combat Action and make an opposed Unarmed Combat test. On a win, choose one:

- deal normal unarmed damage; armour applies;
- force one held item to be dropped at the target's feet; or
- throw the target to an adjacent space, leave them prone, and end the grapple.

The Gamemaster may bar an outcome against impossible Size or anatomy.

Source: [Wrestler](../src/content/rules/talents/wrestler.md).

### Subdue — 3 IP

**Prerequisite met:** Close Combat 51% or Unarmed Combat 51%.

Make one blunt Close Combat or Unarmed Combat attack. If the hit is not stopped, roll its listed weapon or unarmed damage plus Damage Modifier, then apply any partial Parry and armour. If the result reaches the target's Major Wound Level, the target becomes unconscious but stable and loses no HP. They wake when the scene ends; any damage wakes them immediately, and a successful Healing test used as a Combat Action wakes them early.

Otherwise, the target remains conscious and loses only the weapon's minimum listed damage, reduced by any partial Parry and armour. Talent damage bonuses do not modify the threshold or fallback damage.

Source: [Subdue](../src/content/rules/talents/subdue.md).
