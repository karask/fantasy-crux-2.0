## Character

**Elf, 25, she/her. Whitewater woodland guide; tracker and wilderness archer.**

**Concept:** A watchful guide who follows trails and finds shelter, determined to make safe routes available to people who cannot afford a private escort.

**Appearance:** Lean and angular, with copper-brown skin, dark eyes, and ash-blond hair cut just below her ears. Her green-grey travelling clothes have practical brown repairs. She becomes very still when listening, then points to the evidence she has noticed.

**Background:** Seris grew up in a mixed Whitewater household working the wooded approaches to the March. Her own language is River Crown, not Ilyr. She guided the flooded caravan towards firmer ground and later joined the charter instead of taking another season of solitary work.

**Personality:** Wry, attentive, and unexpectedly talkative over a meal. She explains tracks and weather instead of cultivating mystery. Her flaw is distrusting a convenient shortcut until she has checked it herself; she can accept a companion's reliable report when time is short.

**Loyalties and goal:** She cares about isolated households and the travellers between them. She wants the company to make a route safer, not merely survive it once.

**Bond:** Ilen returned along a dangerous path to correct a message rather than let Seris guide people with bad information. She trusts Ilen to tell her when a route has changed.

**Play her:** Find the evidence, check tomorrow's weather, and tell everyone what an animal or a footprint actually suggests. “Something passed here. Let's work out how recently.”

### Terrain Expertise (Forest) — 2 IP (required ancestry ability)

## Effect

Choose one terrain, such as forest, mountain, desert, swamp, steppe, or coast. Record the choice with the Talent, such as **Terrain Expertise (Forest)**.

After ordinary Bonus and Penalty cancellation, but before the final dice cap, remove up to 1 remaining Penalty die from Natural Lore tests made in or about the chosen terrain. This never creates Bonus dice and applies to no other terrain.

Terrain Expertise and [Tracker](https://fantasycrux.org/rules/talents/#tracker) can both apply to one test. Buy this Talent again for a different terrain, but never more than once for the same one.

The chosen terrain is **Forest**. This mandatory racial purchase waives its Natural Lore prerequisite.

Source: [Fantasy Races](../src/content/rules/gm-tools/fantasy-races.md).

### Tracker — 2 IP

**Prerequisite met:** Natural Lore 51%.

After ordinary Bonus and Penalty cancellation, but before the final dice cap, remove up to 1 remaining Penalty die from Natural Lore tests to find or follow a trail.

This never creates Bonus dice, and applies to no other use of Natural Lore.

Source: [Tracker](../src/content/rules/talents/tracker.md).

### Wayfinder — 2 IP

**Prerequisite met:** Natural Lore 51%.

While you can see the sky or read the ground, downgrade a navigation Fumble to an ordinary Failure. This prevents the group from becoming lost through that test.

Wayfinder does not prevent other travel hazards. Weather, pace, and finding food, water, or shelter still use their ordinary rules.

Source: [Wayfinder](../src/content/rules/talents/wayfinder.md).
