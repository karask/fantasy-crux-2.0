## Character

**Human, 24, she/her. Synod-trained healer; Flesh Shaper.**

**Concept:** A compassionate healer who combines ordinary medicine with costly, visible bodily Shaping, driven to return frightened people to those who love them.

**Appearance:** Tall and narrow-faced, with dark curls bound back and a small burn scar on her forearm. Her sleeves are rolled evenly and her hands are clean whenever circumstances allow. A washed section of her travelling cord serves in her prayers.

**Background:** Tamsin trained in a Synod infirmary and learned that faith includes cleaning a floor after everyone else has left. During an initiation vigil, she heard seven heartbeats within her own pulse and closed an injury beneath her hand. Further instruction turned that discovery into the exact cells **Alter·Flesh** and **Scry·Flesh**; it granted no wider catalogue of miracles.

**Personality:** Warm, direct, and dryly humorous. She asks before touching a patient and does not promise an outcome she cannot guarantee. Her weakness is treating her own exhaustion as less urgent than everyone else's. Companions can persuade her to rest by giving her a clear handover.

**Loyalties and goal:** She honours her teachers through practical care and keeps medical confidences. Her immediate goal is to bring the missing people back alive and fit for further treatment.

**Bond:** Mara returned for the last injured guard after everyone else reached safety. Tamsin trusts Mara to keep the company promise when it becomes costly.

**Magic's character:** Her Practice is an audible litany while handling a washed cord; her Tell is seven faint shadows around her and the affected subject. Interrupting that Practice stops casting. The magic always announces itself.

**Play her:** Ask what hurts, who is missing, and who can help move a casualty safely. “You can be frightened. Keep talking to me.”

## Shaping — 20 IP

**Creation declaration:** Shaping was declared and its full 20 IP reserved before Knowledge allocation. The supernatural discovery and instruction are described above. Trading 15 Resistance, 15 Combat, and 20 Practical points supplies 10 IP; the ordinary 10 IP supplies the rest.

**Shaping 62% (Critical 01–06); Veteran; maximum Intensity 3; Safe Magnitude 3; ordinary maximum Magnitude 5. Specialty: Flesh. Known cells: Alter·Flesh, Scry·Flesh.** No other cells or magical Talents; Counter is unavailable.

**Practice:** Speak an audible litany while handling a washed section of the issued travelling rope. Preventing the speech or handling prevents casting; reserve a hand for the cord.

**Tell:** Seven faint shadows gather around Tamsin and the affected subject or area. Anyone watching the Practice notices it; automatic notice extends 10 × Magnitude metres from the Shaper and ranged target/area, minimum 3 m.

### Casting, resources, and universal actions

- **Cast:** declare the effect and confirm its Magnitude, route, defence, and PP with the GM; spend one Combat Action and test Shaping. Keep Movement and eligible Reactions unless another rule prevents them. Practice must be possible.
- **Payment:** success costs Magnitude PP; Critical costs half, rounded normally, and does not maximise effect dice. A safe failure costs 1 PP with no effect. Failed overreach costs half Magnitude (rounded normally) and causes Backlash. A fumble costs full Magnitude and causes Backlash. A successful effect stopped by defence still costs its normal PP. Apply a Hero Point reroll before costs or Backlash.
- **Overreach:** M4 takes -1P; M5 takes -2P; higher requires a ritual. Maximum Intensity remains 3. Cancelling a penalty does not remove overreach risk.
- **Backlash:** the GM may choose rather than roll: take Magnitude damage ignoring armour; lose another Magnitude PP; an immediate Reach-0 effect at one lower Intensity strikes a valid nearby subject (I0 is cosmetic); or become Fatigued, escalate Fatigued to Exhausted, or collapse if already Exhausted. The full [Backlash table](../src/content/rules/magic/casting-and-defence.md) governs.
- **Ongoing:** active Magnitude starts at 0 and cannot exceed permanent INT 16. Ongoing effects count full Magnitude; instantaneous effects do not. Dismiss on your turn without an Action. Maintaining concentration or active control spends the Combat Action each round; maintain only one. Damage or serious distraction forces Persistence; failure ends maintenance. At 0 PP, resolve the casting then fall unconscious and end concentration.
- **Sense Magic:** one Combat Action, 1 PP, Shaping test; detect active magic within 15 m. Success gives location and Form; Critical also gives Technique, Magnitude, and concentrated/triggered/anchored state. Failure reveals nothing; a fumble gives a false impression and conspicuous Tell, not Backlash. Veiled magic opposes with its stored casting result.
- **Dispel:** one Combat Action; Magnitude equals target Shaping's full Magnitude + Range, Reach 0. Check normal limits, roll and pay normally, then on Success/Critical oppose the original Shaper's current unmodified Shaping (if unknown, 10 × original Magnitude, maximum 100%). You must succeed and win. Active anchored enchantments qualify; completed permanent transformations do not.
