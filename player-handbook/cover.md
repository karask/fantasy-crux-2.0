## Editorial notes

**Objective:** Identify the booklet and its audience without adding another
rules lesson. The complete edition counts the cover as page 1 so printed folios
and PDF page numbers match. See `a5/complete/content/01-cover.md`.

**Visual:** The complete edition uses the established title treatment and the
existing mundane warrior illustration at finished A5 size. The instruction panels
on the folding and spiral print proofs are not proposed cover designs. Give the
eventual front cover the same punch-safe left margin as other front-facing pages.

**Exact labels:** Use the title and subtitle lines above verbatim. Do not add
setting lore, a character-creation invitation, or a claim of complete rules.
