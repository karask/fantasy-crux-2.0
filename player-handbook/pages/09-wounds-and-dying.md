# Wounds & Dying

**One big hit can wound you. Reaching 0 HP starts a race to save your life.**

## Major Wound

After Parry and armour, compare the damage from **this one hit** with your
**Major Wound Level (MWL)**. Damage equal to or greater than MWL causes a Major
Wound. Apply the HP loss, then both conditions:

- **Wounded:** `-1P` on every test.
- **Bleeding:** lose **1 HP at the end of every Combat Round**.

**Example:** At MWL 6, a hit dealing **6 HP** wounds you. Two separate hits of
**3 HP** each do not cause a Major Wound just because they total 6.

Wounded and Bleeding do not stack with themselves. Further Major Wounds still
deal damage and can restart Bleeding after it was stopped. Do not make a
Resilience test or roll on an injury table.

## At 0 HP

Current HP stops at **0**. You fall unconscious and become **Dying**.

If still Dying at the end of the **third Combat Round after reaching 0 HP**, you
die. An adjacent helper must stabilise you in time; see page 10.

Successful stabilisation removes Bleeding and Dying but restores **no HP**.
Stable at 0 HP still means unconscious.

## Before the consequence

A Hero Point can prevent a Major Wound's conditions while keeping the damage,
or let you Avoid Death at 0 HP. These are separate uses; see page 11.

## Editorial notes

**Sources:** Damage and Wounds → Major Wounds, Dying, Hero Points; Healing and
Recovery → condition summary. The combat chapter supplies the precise countdown
wording: three rounds after reaching 0, not an invented immediate death check.

**Visual:** One large hit breaks an HP track; separate badges show Wounded and
Bleeding. A second track begins at 0 HP and marks the next three rounds, with
stabilisation interrupting the countdown.

**Exact labels:** `ONE HIT ≥ MWL`, `WOUNDED −1P`, `BLEEDING −1 HP / ROUND`,
`0 HP: UNCONSCIOUS + DYING`, `STABLE ≠ AWAKE`.

**Must retain:** Further wounds can restart Bleeding. Stopping Bleeding alone
does not remove Wounded. Never imply a new penalty or wound record per hit.
