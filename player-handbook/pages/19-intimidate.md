# Intimidate

**Spend a Combat Action to demand surrender or drive an enemy off.**

## Make the demand

Choose **one enemy or the whole group**. Make yourself understood by words,
gesture, or bearing; you do not need a shared language. The GM decides whether
the attempt is possible and who can be targeted.

Oppose your **Influence** against the target's **Persistence**. A group rolls
once using its leader's Persistence, or the leader's **Influence if higher**.

## Read the enemy's condition

Apply the **one modifier that best describes the enemy** to their resisting roll.
These condition modifiers do not add together.

- **Full strength, or no more than minor wounds:** resistance `+2B`.
- **Outnumbers your side but has lost 20% of its numbers or HP:** resistance `+1B`.
- **Fewer than your side and already wounded:** resistance `-1P`.
- **Lost over half its HP, or half the group is down:** resistance `-2P`.

## Resolve the demand

Resolve the opposed test as on page 04. While the enemy is **at full strength
or outnumbers your side**, only **Critical Influence against failed resistance**
forces surrender. A **fumbled resistance routs them outright**.

**Example:** You demand surrender from a full-strength enemy. Its resistance
gains **+2B**. An ordinary Influence Success cannot force surrender here; a
Critical against failed resistance can.

## Editorial notes

**Source:** Rounds and Actions → Intimidate; Opposed tests → comparison order.

**Visual:** One demand scene followed by enemy-confidence vignettes. Put every
modifier on the resisting side, not on the intimidating character. This is why
Intimidate needs its own page instead of a one-line action-menu entry.

**Exact labels:** `YOUR INFLUENCE`, `THEIR RESISTANCE`, `ONE CONDITION MODIFIER`,
`FULL STRENGTH +2B`, `FUMBLED RESISTANCE: ROUT`.

**Must retain:** The source first allows the group's leader to substitute
Influence, then calls the exceptional outcomes failed/fumbled Persistence.
Use "resistance" here to include that stated substitution; do not invent a
second test. Preserve the source's 20% wording, not a new threshold or rounding
policy. Do not turn this into GM morale guidance or guarantee a specific demand
is possible regardless of circumstances.
