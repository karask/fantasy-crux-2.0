# Difficult Shots

**These modifiers apply to the attacker's Ranged Combat test.**

## The target

- **Moved at least 10 m since your last turn:** `-1P`.
- **Moved at least 30 m since your last turn:** `-2P` instead.
- **Partly obscured by mist, smoke, fog, or foliage:** `-1P`.
- **Heavily obscured:** `-2P` instead.
- **Prone:** `-1P`.
- **Helpless:** `+2B`.

## The weather

- **High wind:** `-1P`.
- **Fierce wind:** `-3P` instead.
- **Hurricane:** the attack fails.

## Your position

- **You are prone:** `-2P`.
- **You are on unstable ground:** `-1P`.
- **You are engaged at Close range:** `-2P per enemy`.

## Combine different causes

Use only the **strongest version of the same condition**: moving 30 m gives
`-2P`, not the 10 m and 30 m penalties together. Different causes combine.
Cancel Bonus and Penalty dice, then cap the final modifier at **+3B or -3P**.

**Example:** A target moved 10 m (**-1P**) and stands behind a low wall (**-1P**).
Your Aim gives **+1B**. The shot ends at **-1P**.

Range and Aim: page 14. Terrain cover: page 15. Illumination is a separate
circumstance; its full rules are outside this core booklet.
