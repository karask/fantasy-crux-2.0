# Make a Skill Roll

Describe your approach and intended outcome. Roll when uncertainty, pressure,
or danger makes failure matter; routine or impossible actions need no roll.

## Read the dice

Roll two ten-sided dice: one for **tens**, one for **units**. Tens `5` and units
`9` make **59**. Tens `0` and units `7` make **07**. Two zeros mean **100**.

Set any Bonus or Penalty dice before rolling; see page 03.

## Read your result

Check Fumbles first: they override success.

- **Critical:** `01` through one tenth of your skill, rounded down. Any skill
  above 0% has at least `01` as a Critical; a 0% skill has none.
- **Success:** at or below your skill, without a Critical or Fumble.
- **Failure:** above your skill, without a Fumble.
- **Fumble:** `99` or `100` below 100% skill; at 100% skill, only `100`.

**At 60%:** `01–06` Critical · `07–60` Success · `61–98` Failure · `99–100` Fumble.

A Critical achieves your intent exceptionally well. A Fumble fails with a serious
consequence related to the risk. Skills cannot exceed 100%.

## Other dice and measures

`2D6+2` means roll two six-sided dice, add them, then add 2. The number after `D`
is the number of sides.

Distances are in metres. If using a grid, a square may represent 2 metres.
Round ordinary fractions to the nearest whole number, with halves up; the
Critical threshold above specifically rounds down.

## Editorial notes

**Sources:** D100 Percentile → Dice notation, Make a test, Result grades, Measures
and rounding; Basic skill tests → Result grades, Exact critical ranges.

**Visual:** Large dice annotated `TENS` and `UNITS`, followed by a four-colour
result track for skill 60. Keep zeros unmistakable and show 100 as a reading,
not a three-digit die. Set dice faces deterministically during composition.

**Exact labels:** `TENS`, `UNITS`, `59`, `07`, `00 = 100`, and the four grades.

**Must retain:** Fumble precedence, minimum Critical 01, the 0% exception, and
the special Fumble rule at 100%. Do not imply every lower successful roll is better.
