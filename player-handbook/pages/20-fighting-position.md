# Fighting Position

**Position changes a test when it creates a clear advantage or obstacle.**

## Attack the opening

- **Target is prone, flanked, or attacked from behind:** `+1B` to your Close
  Combat attack.
- **Target is helpless:** your Close Combat attack is an **automatic Critical**.

## Mind your own position

- **Higher ground:** the attacker or defender with the advantage gains `+1B`.
- **You are prone:** `-1P` to your melee attack or defence.
- **You are on unstable ground:** `-1P` to the affected test.

**Example:** A standing fighter attacks a prone opponent: the attacker gains
**+1B**. The prone opponent's Parry suffers **-1P**. These modifiers apply to
different rolls.

## Awareness matters

An **unaware defender cannot React**. Standing behind or beside someone does
not automatically make them unaware. Describe how you approach and whether
they can notice you.

**Example:** An alert defender attacked from behind can still React. The rear
attack gains **+1B**, but position alone does not remove the defence.

## Combine circumstances

Apply modifiers only when the situation warrants them. Combine different causes
under the usual cancellation and **+3B / -3P cap**; see page 03.

Ranged positioning uses the different modifiers on page 17. Mounted combat,
underwater combat, and illumination rules are outside this core booklet.
