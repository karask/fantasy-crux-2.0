# Ranged Attacks

**Spend a Combat Action and test Ranged Combat against a target within reach.**

## Check the range

- **Up to listed Range:** no range penalty.
- **Beyond Range, up to twice Range:** attack at **-2P**.
- **Farther than twice Range:** cannot hit the target.

**Shortbow example:** up to **75 m**, normal; beyond 75 m through **150 m**,
**-2P**; beyond 150 m, **no shot**.

## Aim for the next shot

Spend a **Combat Action** aiming at one visible target. Your next ranged attack
against that target gains **+1B**.

Lose Aim if you take **any Reaction**, lose sight of the target, or do not make
that attack with your **next Combat Action**.

## Use the right damage

**Thrown weapons add DM. Bows, crossbows, and slings do not.**

Within **2 metres**, a ranged weapon with the **Close** tag can attack using
Close Combat and its listed damage. A ranged weapon without Close used there
becomes an improvised **Light club: 1D6 + DM, attack at -1P**.

## A crowded target

Apply cover normally. An ordinary failure misses. Only a **Fumble** may hit a
nearby target, selected fairly or randomly, for normal rolled damage after armour.

Cover: page 15. Defence: page 16. Other shot modifiers: page 17.
Weapon ranges, tags, and reload costs: page 22.
