# Read Your Character

Your character is ready to play. Use the values printed on the sheet and describe
what you want to do.

## The person

- **STR — Strength:** physical power.
- **CON — Constitution:** health and endurance.
- **DEX — Dexterity:** agility and coordination.
- **SIZ — Size:** height, mass, and bulk.
- **INT — Intelligence:** reasoning and memory.
- **POW — Power:** will and presence of mind.
- **CHA — Charisma:** personal magnetism and leadership.

## The numbers you use

- **Skill %:** the number you test against. Close Combat 60% means a roll of 60
  or less succeeds, unless it is a Fumble. See page 02.
- **HP — Hit Points:** injury you can sustain. Track current HP separately from
  maximum HP.
- **MWL — Major Wound Level:** damage from one hit that causes a Major Wound.
  Losing HP does not lower this threshold.
- **Movement:** metres you can move with a Movement Action.
- **Combat Order:** add this to `1D10` when combat begins.
- **DM — Damage Modifier:** add this to melee, unarmed, and thrown damage.
  Bows, crossbows, and slings use their listed damage.
- **AP — Armour Points:** subtract these from damage after a Parry.
- **ENC — Encumbrance:** equipment's burden. Armour also reduces Combat Order;
  your pregen's listed armour is already accounted for.
- **Hero Points:** spendable chances to turn a desperate moment around. See page 11.

**Read it:** `HP 8/12` means 8 current HP out of 12 maximum. An `MWL 6` stays 6.
