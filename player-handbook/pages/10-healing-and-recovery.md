# Healing & Recovery

**Stabilise first. Treat the injury next. Recovery takes time.**

## Save a life · battlefield treatment

Stay adjacent to the patient and spend your **whole round: Combat Action,
Movement Action, and all Reactions**. Both of you remain stationary. Test Healing.

Success removes **Bleeding and Dying** but restores **0 HP**. A patient at 0 HP
remains unconscious. Use a Healing Kit or suitable improvised supplies;
without them, the test suffers `-2P`.

## Treat ordinary damage · after the encounter

A patient who is **not Wounded** may receive **one Healing attempt before their
next full rest**. Success restores **1D4 HP**, up to maximum HP. The limit is one
attempt per patient, even if that attempt fails.

## Treat a Major Wound · Surgery

Once the patient is stabilised, work **outside combat in safe conditions** and
test Healing. Successful Surgery restores **1 HP** and allows natural recovery.

A Wounded patient cannot recover HP naturally before successful Surgery or
equivalent treatment. Other Healing tests, including Surgery and ordinary
treatment, suffer `-2P` without a Healing Kit.

## Recover · light activity and rest

Recover **CON ÷ 4 HP per 24 hours**, rounded to the nearest whole number with
halves up, while doing no more than light activity. Never exceed maximum HP.

After treatment, **Wounded ends when current HP is greater than MWL**.
Merely reaching MWL is not enough.

**Example:** A treated patient with CON 14 recovers **4 HP per day**. With MWL 6,
Wounded ends at **7 HP or more**.

## Editorial notes

**Sources:** Healing and Recovery → Battlefield Healing, Treating ordinary
damage, Surgery and natural recovery; Damage and Wounds → Dying; Practical
skills → Healing.

**Visual:** Three scenes of the same patient: battlefield bandage, safe Surgery,
and resting recovery. Show the full-round cost beside the helper, not the patient.

**Exact labels:** `WHOLE ROUND`, `STOP BLEEDING + DYING`, `RESTORE 0 HP`,
`SURGERY +1 HP`, `CURRENT HP > MWL`.

**Must retain:** The combat chapter specifically permits suitable improvised
supplies for stabilisation; the general Healing rules require a kit or −2P.
Keep that distinction. Do not promise that ordinary first aid treats a Major
Wound, that stabilisation wakes a patient at 0 HP, or that natural healing starts
before Surgery.
