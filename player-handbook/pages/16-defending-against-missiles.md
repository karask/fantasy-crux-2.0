# Defending Against Missiles

**After a ranged hit, an aware defender may spend one eligible Reaction.**

## Dodge · base Reaction

Test **Dodge at -1P**. You may Dodge only once per round and only with your base
Reaction. You must perceive the danger and be able to move away.

## Active Guard · Parry the projectile

Use **Close Combat** with the eligible item ready:

- **Thrown weapon → ready weapon:** Parry at **-1P**.
- **Thrown weapon → ready shield:** Parry with **no Active Guard penalty**.
- **Arrow, crossbow bolt, or sling projectile → ready shield only:** Parry at **-1P**.

Ordinary successful Parries block damage according to Size; see page 07.
Thrown weapons retain their listed Size. Arrows, bolts, and sling projectiles
are **Light**: any ready shield is large enough to block an ordinary hit fully
on a successful Parry.

## Keep the distinctions clear

Only **one Reaction** can answer one attack. Critical attacks still use page 08:
an ordinary successful guard downgrades the Critical to normal rolled damage
after armour; only a Critical defence stops it.

An extra off-hand Parry adds its own **-1P**. For example, guarding an arrow with
that extra Reaction gives **-2P before other modifiers**. It cannot be used to Dodge.

Carrying a shield gives **no passive attack penalty**. A shield that is unavailable
while you wield a two-handed item is not ready for Active Guard.
