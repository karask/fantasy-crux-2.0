# Hero Points

**Spend 1 Hero Point when you declare its effect, then mark it off your sheet.**

## Try again

Reroll a **failed test**, including a Fumble. Declare this after seeing the roll
but **before applying consequences or costs**. The new result stands.

Each test permits **at most one reroll**, regardless of its source.

## Downgrade a Major Wound

When a hit would cause a Major Wound, keep the full HP loss but prevent **Wounded
and Bleeding from that hit**. It becomes ordinary damage and creates no Major
Wound requiring Surgery. Existing conditions are not erased.

## Avoid Death

When reduced to **0 HP**, become stable and unconscious instead of Dying. You
are not Bleeding. Awaken after the scene at **1 HP**; remain Wounded if that
condition already applied.

## Add a story benefit

With the GM's approval, establish a plausible favourable detail or coincidence.
A larger benefit may cost several points; companions may contribute their own.

**Example:** A familiar face in the crowd offers a hiding place—if the detail
fits the situation and the GM agrees.

## A wound and 0 HP are separate

You have **5 HP**, MWL **6**, and take **7 damage**. Downgrading the Major Wound
costs 1 point but still leaves you at 0 HP. Avoiding Death costs **another point**.

**Sources:** Character creation → Hero Points and wounds, Spending Hero Points;
Damage and Wounds → Hero Points. This is a play procedure despite its source
chapter. Do not include starting Hero Points or GM award guidance.

**Visual:** Four small narrative moments around the same spendable token icon.
Use two tokens in the last example to prevent conflating the two protections.

**Exact labels:** `1 POINT`, `REROLL ONCE`, `KEEP THE HP LOSS`, `AWAKEN AT 1 HP`,
`TWO EFFECTS = TWO POINTS`.

**Must retain:** A reroll may be worse. Avoid Death removes Bleeding but does not
erase an existing Wounded condition. Story benefits require agreement.
