# Terrain Cover

Cover penalises the attack. It does not reduce damage.

## Partial cover

### ATTACK −1P

A low wall protects part of the target. Apply one Penalty die to the attacker's
Ranged Combat test.

## Substantial cover

### ATTACK −2P

Battlements leave much less of the target exposed. Apply two Penalty dice to the
attacker's Ranged Combat test.

## Complete cover

### NO ATTACK

No attack line reaches the target. You cannot make the attack from here.

## Remember

The penalty belongs to the attacker. If the shot hits, cover removes no damage.
