# Close Weapons

**Use Close Combat for a held weapon; Unarmed Combat for punches and kicks.**
Add your **DM** to the listed damage. If STR or DEX is below the listed minimum,
attacks and Parries suffer **-1P**; failing both requirements still gives only -1P.

## Read the tags

**1H:** one hand. **2H:** two hands. **Flex:** may use two hands for **+2 damage**
and a **STR requirement 2 lower**. **Set:** may brace against a Charge (page 18).
**Thrown:** may use its ranged profile without an improvised-weapon penalty.

**Size:** Light → Medium → Heavy → Huge; determines Parry protection (page 07).
**SP:** Silver Pieces. **ENC:** burden. A dash means no listed value.

Primitive or improvised versions of listed weapons deal **1 less damage**, to a
minimum of 1.

**Example:** A two-handed longsword deals **1D8 + 2 + DM** and requires
**STR 11 / DEX 11**. It is still Medium, and using two hands prevents off-hand options.
