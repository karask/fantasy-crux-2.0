# Skills at a Glance

Describe the action; choose the skill that fits. Use your sheet's percentages.

## Resist danger

- **Dodge:** evade an attack or physical hazard you perceive.
- **Persistence:** resist intimidation, coercion, and fear.
- **Resilience:** endure fatigue, bodily shock, deprivation, poison, and disease.

## Fight

- **Close Combat:** attack and Parry with melee weapons and shields.
- **Ranged Combat:** shoot or throw weapons at a distant target.
- **Unarmed Combat:** punch, kick, grapple, and fight without a weapon.

## Know

- **Culture:** recall a society's history, geography, customs, and beliefs.
- **Language:** communicate; 50% means fluent everyday speech.
- **Lore:** recall specialist knowledge; useful preparation can give `+1B` to one
  directly related test soon after a successful Lore test.
- **Natural Lore:** understand nature, track, predict weather, and find food or shelter.

## Act

- **Athletics:** balance, climb, jump, run, swim, or exert force.
- **Craft:** make and repair goods within a chosen trade.
- **Deception:** sneak, disguise, misdirect, conceal, or pick pockets.
- **Driving:** handle carts, wagons, chariots, and similar vehicles.
- **Engineering:** design, build, repair, or sabotage large works.
- **Healing:** stabilise patients, treat injuries, and perform Surgery; see page 10.
- **Influence:** persuade, bargain, command, or intimidate.
- **Mechanisms:** work with locks, traps, and intricate moving devices.
- **Perception:** notice hidden things, fine details, and approaching danger.
- **Performance:** act, dance, sing, speak, or play music.
- **Riding:** control a mount in challenging circumstances.
- **Sailing:** operate boats and ships under oar or sail.
- **Streetwise:** find rumours, contacts, illicit goods, or discreet urban routes.
- **Trade:** appraise value and negotiate a sale.

Craft subjects, studied Cultures, Languages, and Lore fields are separate skills.
Routine competent work with time and suitable tools usually needs no roll.
