# Attack, Dodge & Parry

**Attack first. After a hit, an aware defender may spend one eligible Reaction.**

## 1 · Make the attack

Use **Close Combat** for a melee weapon, **Unarmed Combat** for a punch or kick,
or **Ranged Combat** at distance. Roll against the skill: success hits; failure
misses. Check Criticals and Fumbles as on page 02.

## 2 · Answer an ordinary hit

- **Dodge:** roll Dodge. An ordinary success avoids an ordinary hit. You may
  Dodge only once per round, using your base Reaction.
- **Parry:** roll Close Combat with a ready weapon or shield. An unarmed Parry
  uses Unarmed Combat and can answer an unarmed attack.
- **No successful Reaction:** the hit proceeds to damage.

Only **one Reaction** can answer a single attack or trigger. A failed Reaction
changes nothing. An unaware defender cannot React.

## 3 · Compare Parry Size

Sizes ascend: **Light → Medium → Heavy → Huge**.

Against an ordinary hit, a successful Parry with an item:

- **same Size or larger:** blocks all damage;
- **one Size smaller:** blocks half the total damage;
- **two or more Sizes smaller:** blocks none.

A **Critical Parry blocks all damage**, regardless of Size. Apply armour after
Parry. Unarmed attacks are Light.

**Example:** A Medium arming sword Parries a Heavy great axe: an ordinary Parry
blocks half. A Heavy medium shield blocks all.

## Another use: Opportunity Attack

When an adjacent enemy disengages or creates a clear opening, spend an eligible
Reaction on one Close Combat attack. It grants no extra off-hand attack.

Critical attack outcomes: page 08. Missile defence: page 16.

## Editorial notes

**Sources:** Attacks and Reactions → Reactions, Parry Size; Critical Hits and
Fumbles → Combat Result Matrix, used only to confirm Critical Parry behaviour.

**Visual:** Attack → Dodge/Parry branches, followed by three matched weapon
silhouettes for `ALL`, `HALF`, `NONE`. Draw the exact listed weapon Sizes rather
than inferring Size from apparent physical length.

**Exact labels:** `AFTER A HIT`, `ONE REACTION`, `ALL`, `HALF`, `NONE`.

**Must retain:** Ordinary attacks are not general opposed rolls. Do not grant
free disarms, trips, or other Talent manoeuvres. Name shield Size carefully:
a medium shield is Heavy, while a small shield is Medium.
