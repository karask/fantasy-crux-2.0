# Off-Hand Options

**Two ready one-handed items give one extra option per round. Choose attack OR defence.**

## What qualifies?

**Weapon + weapon · weapon + shield · weapon + free hand · two bare hands.**
An empty hand counts as ready; a punch or kick can be the attack. Wielding a
two-handed item leaves no free hand and does not qualify.

## Attack · immediately follow up

After a **standard Close Combat or Unarmed Combat attack**, make one free attack
with the other item, or unarmed with a free hand, at **-1P**.

It spends no Action or Reaction. Apply DM to both attacks normally. The first
attack need not hit. The follow-up must happen immediately.

## Defence · keep another Reaction

Reserve the other item for one additional **Parry or Opportunity Attack at -1P**.
This extra Reaction cannot Dodge.

Your base Reaction remains available with either choice. A shield Parry using
that base Reaction has **no inherent off-hand penalty**.

## One choice, not both

Once you use either extra option, the other is unavailable until the next round.
An Opportunity Attack, Charge, or Set Weapon attack never generates an extra
off-hand attack. Forfeiting all Reactions also removes the extra Reaction.

**Example:** A fighter follows a sword attack with a dagger attack at **-1P**.
The opponent answers with their **base shield Parry: no off-hand penalty**.
The fighter has used the extra attack and cannot also take the extra Reaction.
