# Armour

**Subtract Armour Points after a Parry changes damage.**

| Armour    |  AP | ENC |     Cost |
| --------- | --: | --: | -------: |
| Leather   |   2 |   3 |   500 SP |
| Ringmail  |   3 |   4 | 1,000 SP |
| Scalemail |   4 |   6 | 1,500 SP |
| Chainmail |   5 |   7 | 3,000 SP |
| Platemail |   6 |   9 | 9,000 SP |

## Protection you can recognise

- **Leather:** padded, boiled leather, or layered linen protection.
- **Ringmail:** a padded suit reinforced with sewn metal rings.
- **Scalemail:** a padded suit covered with overlapping metal scales.
- **Chainmail:** interlinked metal rings.
- **Platemail:** fitted steel plate over a chain backing.

**Example:** After any Parry, **7 damage** remains. Chainmail's **5 AP** reduces
that to **2 HP lost**. Armour never turns damage into healing.

Critical attacks have their own armour rules; see page 08.

## Burden

Armour **ENC** counts towards carried load and is also subtracted from base
**Combat Order**. Your pregen's listed armour is already included in its printed
Combat Order: do not subtract it twice. **SP** means Silver Pieces.

## Size and fit

- Armour for **SIZ 1–5:** half listed cost and ENC.
- Armour for **SIZ 21 or more:** double listed cost and ENC.
- **Ill-fitting plate:** may be worn at double ENC. Other armour must fit well
  enough for the GM to allow it.
- **No layering:** wearing more than one armour does not combine their AP.
