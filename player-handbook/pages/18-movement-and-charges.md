# Movement & Charges

**Choose the route and pay its costs. You cannot give up an action already spent.**

## Disengage or Withdraw

**Disengage:** use your Movement Action to leave. An enemy who can reach you may
spend a Reaction on an Opportunity Attack.

**Withdraw:** spend **Combat Action + Movement Action** to move up to normal
Movement without Opportunity Attacks caused by that movement. Keep your base
Reaction.

## Sprint

Spend **Combat Action + Movement Action** to move up to **twice Movement**.
Keep **only your base Reaction, and only to Dodge**. No other Reaction is
available that round.

## Charge

Spend **Combat Action + Movement Action; forfeit all Reactions**.
Move **at least 5 m and no more than twice Movement** on a straight, unobstructed,
passable route, ending adjacent to the target. Then make one Close Combat attack.
A hit gains **+1D6 damage**.

A Charge allows no other movement and no extra off-hand attack. The maximum-damage
Critical outcome replaces bonus damage as explained on page 08.

## Set Weapon against a Charge

Spend your **Combat Action** to set a ready weapon with the **Set** tag.
If someone Charges into its reach before your next turn, make **one Close Combat
attack at +1B before their Charge attack**. This spends no further Action or
Reaction, works once, and then the weapon is no longer set.

Taking any Action, Movement Action, or Reaction before the trigger unsets it.
A Set Weapon attack grants no extra off-hand attack.

**Movement 15 example:** Withdraw up to **15 m**; Sprint up to **30 m**;
Charge **5–30 m** in a straight line and then attack.

## Editorial notes

**Source:** Rounds and Actions → Common Movement Actions, Withdraw, Set Weapon,
Sprint, Charge, and incompatible-spending rule.

**Visual:** Four route diagrams anchored by the same fighter, with the page-06
spending symbols beside each. Pair Charge with a braced spear; mark the spear's
attack as occurring first. Routes are explanatory and do not create grid rules.

**Exact labels:** `WITHDRAW: MOVE`, `SPRINT: 2 × MOVE`, `CHARGE: 5 m TO 2 × MOVE`,
`CHARGE HIT +1D6`, `SET: ATTACK FIRST +1B`, `NO REACTIONS`.

**Must retain:** Sprint permits only base Dodge. Charge permits none. Set Weapon
expires on the source's timing and actions, and requires its tag. Avoid diagrams
that imply a Charge can turn a corner or grant an off-hand follow-up.
