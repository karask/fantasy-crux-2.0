# Ranged Weapons

**Thrown attacks add DM. Bows, crossbows, and slings use only listed damage.**
Below either STR or DEX minimum, attacks and Parries suffer **-1P**, not -2P if
both are too low. Range penalties and Aim are explained on page 14.

## Read the profile

**Close:** within 2 m, use Close Combat and listed damage. **Thrown:** add DM to
the ranged attack. **Missile:** bows, crossbows, and slings do not add DM.
**1H / 2H:** one / two hands. **SP / CP:** Silver / Copper Pieces.

Up to Range: normal. Beyond Range through twice Range: **-2P**. Farther: no attack.
Thrown weapons retain their Size; arrows, bolts, and sling projectiles are Light.

## Reload crossbows

- **Light:** spend **Movement Action + base Reaction**.
- **Heavy:** spend the **whole round: Combat Action, Movement Action, all Reactions**.

A generic Ready action never shortens these costs. Nocking a bow's arrow uses
the readying rule on page 06.

## Shields and ammunition

A small shield can be strapped to your arm while carrying a two-handed ranged
weapon. It cannot attack, Parry, or provide Active Guard while you wield that weapon.

Per 10: **arrows 1 SP · bolts 2 SP · sling bullets 5 CP**. Each bundle has no
listed ENC.

**Example:** STR 12 gives a javelin **24 m Range**, with **-2P beyond 24 m through 48 m**.
