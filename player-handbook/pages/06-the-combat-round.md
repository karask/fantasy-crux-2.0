# The Combat Round

**One round is five seconds. Close is within 2 metres; farther away is Ranged.**

## Establish the order

At the start of combat, roll **1D10 + Combat Order**. Highest acts first. Ties go
to higher DEX; if DEX also ties, those characters reroll `1D10` between them.

**Example:** Your die shows 7 and your Combat Order is 12: your total is **19**.

## Each round gives you

- **One Combat Action:** attack, start or escape a grapple, Aim, Set Weapon,
  Intimidate, or use a skill/object that takes about five seconds.
- **One Movement Action:** move up to your Movement in metres, stand up, drop
  prone, or disengage. Leaving an enemy can trigger an Opportunity Attack; see
  page 18.
- **One base Reaction:** respond when a trigger occurs, even outside your turn.
  Choose Dodge, Parry, or an Opportunity Attack when eligible; see page 07.

Combat and Movement Actions happen in Combat Order. A Reaction interrupts that
order only to resolve its trigger. Unspent actions never carry into the next round.

## Ready equipment

Use your Movement Action to ready one item or nock an arrow while moving no more
than half Movement. Stowing one item and drawing another, or readying two items,
takes the whole Movement Action. Crossbows use their own reload costs on page 22.

## Delay

Delay your Combat Action until after a chosen participant later in the round.
To interrupt an action already beginning, make an opposed test with the relevant
skills; the winner acts first.

You cannot choose an option that forfeits or restricts an action or Reaction
you have already used incompatibly that round.

## Editorial notes

**Source:** Rounds and Actions → opening, Common Combat Actions, Common Movement
Actions. Keep the more elaborate movement choices on page 18.

**Visual:** Three reusable spending symbols labelled `ACTION`, `MOVE`, `REACTION`
beside a turn-order rail. A shield interruption branches off the rail and returns
to it. Never imply Reactions happen only on the character's turn.

**Exact labels:** `5 SECONDS`, `1D10 + COMBAT ORDER`, `ACTION`, `MOVE`, `REACTION`.

**Must retain:** Initiative is rolled at combat start, not every round. Delay
uses the source's wording; it does not create an extra action or a next-round
action bank.
