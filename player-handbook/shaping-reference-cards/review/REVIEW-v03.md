# Shaping reference cards v03

8 September 2026. Clarifies the timing of free retries against hostile conditions.

## Change

Card 3 now states that a free retry occurs at the **end of the affected subject's
own turn**. Apply that turn's restrictions first; winning ends the condition
without restoring missed Actions. Specific control rules and declared break
conditions retain their existing exceptions.

The canonical Conditions and restoration text and the paralysis example use
the same timing. The existing magical-condition contract checks were updated
to retain the explicit turn owner, order and absence of restored Actions.
