# What Lite leaves out

Compared with the full rules in `src/content/rules/`, as of Lite v03 (19 October
2026). Lite keeps every number it prints identical to the full rules; this list
covers what it omits or shortens. Update it with each Lite version.

## Added in v03

These were the omissions most likely to make a table play a rule wrong:

- Touching an unwilling target with a Shaping: a no-damage Unarmed Combat
  attack first (page 11).
- Escaping incapacitation: retry at the end of your own turn when the effect
  stops your Combat Actions (page 11).
- An unwilling subject under active control moves at most 3 m a round
  (page 11).
- Mind magic deals no HP damage; Unmake·Spirit is Direct only and drains PP
  (page 11).
- Readying two items, or stowing one and drawing another, takes the whole
  Movement Action; no option can conflict with an action already used (page 6).
- Skills keep their scores when a characteristic rises (page 4).
- Creature rules: reading a profile, natural weapon Sizes, Paralysed, creature
  types, Multiattack, Spirit Combat and possession (page 12).
- Eleven more creatures, for 21 in all (page 13).

## Playing the game

- Sorting tests: one roll read against several NPCs' skills.
- Skill details:
  - Athletics: crossing unstable ground at half Movement without a test, and
    -1P per extra metre when jumping.
  - Craft: time and materials (a day per 50 SP of value; materials half that).
  - Deception: picking a pocket needs a minute of access.
  - Influence: cannot force action against basic self-preservation.
  - Natural Lore: one survival test a day to find a necessity, tracking tests
    every 10 minutes, animal training, weather.
  - Trade: bargaining as an opposed test.
  - Healing: reviving an unconscious patient.
- The exact critical-range table (Lite gives the rule and examples).

## Characters

- Random characteristics, for humans and for each ancestry.
- Expertise names (Novice to Master) and starting age.
- Larger Hero Point story benefits, paid by several players.
- Ancestry details: Adrenaline Surge also ignores the first 1 HP of Bleeding;
  Scaled Hide does not stack with Ward AP; Goblins can buy Close-Quarters Knack.
- **30 of the 56 Talents:**
  - Combat: Close-Quarters Knack, Deadly Precision, Defensive Stance, Master
    Assassin, Master Brawler, Mighty Shot, Point-Blank Shot, Signature Weapon,
    Steady Aim, Subdue, Weapon Expertise, Wrestler.
  - Shaping: Confluence, Counter, Enchanter, Indirect, Selective, Shaping
    Expertise, Trigger, Veiled.
  - Crafts and trade: Alchemist, Master Craftsman, Merchant's Eye, Physician,
    Poisoner, Weak Point.
  - Other: Cutpurse, Lockbreaker, Tactician, Wayfinder.

## Combat

- Delay: interrupting an action already starting; Combat Order ties rerolled
  after DEX.
- Charge: no other movement that round, and no off-hand attack. Set Weapon:
  any other action unsets it.
- Intimidate: you must make yourself understood; the GM decides who can be
  targeted.
- Grapple details: committing limbs, +1B for a second limb, a weapon Parry
  against a grab dealing 1 HP, helpers, size modifiers, ending a grapple.
- The extra off-hand Reaction cannot be used for Protector.
- A shield is not ready while you wield a two-handed item; Shield Cover and
  terrain cover do not add.
- Criticals ignore a negative Damage Modifier; fumbles should not decide a
  whole fight.
- Mounted: a lance Charge uses the mount's Damage Modifier; the rider's bonus
  has exceptions.

## Adventuring

- Falling onto soft ground halves the distance.
- Suffocation: poison gas, vacuum, and gasping after a failed recovery test.
- Starvation: Fatigue tests at -1P after three days without food. Damage from
  deprivation or active disease does not heal while the cause lasts.
- Sample poisons and diseases.
- Fire: armour other than metal helps only if it plausibly insulates.
- Travel: an opposed race when it matters who arrives first.
- More object examples, and weapons damaged by hammering stone.

## Equipment

- Transport, food and lodging prices.
- Most of the general gear list, container capacities, tool bonuses, and
  torches as weapons.
- What towns and villages stock, and bartering.
- Armour for very small or very large bodies; ill-fitting plate.
- A small shield strapped on while using a two-handed ranged weapon.
- Sling bullet prices.

## Shaping

- Rituals: Range 4–5, Duration 3–5 and Reach 3–5, helpers and pledged PP, and
  permanent or mythic magic.
- The adjustments that need Talents (Confluence, Indirect, Selective, Trigger,
  Veiled), and Counter.
- Calling spirits, elementals and other beings.
- Indefinite Shapings, which commit maximum PP.
- Conjured being details: strike Size and SIZ, Movement, mount-sized beings at
  Reach 1, each Form's trait and drawback, and the price of extra abilities.
- Outcomes:
  - Vitality reserves (finite magical absorption).
  - Breathing: holding breath, breathing water, not needing to breathe.
  - Illusions, Paths (moving subjects, portals) and Fate.
  - The sixty-cell grid, and which Forms affect undead, Soulless and Mindless
    subjects.
- Healing: curing poison and disease (Intensity at least Potency ÷ 25), no
  regeneration, raising the dead.
- Removing conditions: Fatigue (I3 and I4), curses, mundane conditions.
- Full control: an extra defence before self-harm or betrayal.
- Wards as a defence bonus or a denial; the spiritual class of harm.
- Barriers cannot appear inside an occupied space.
- Direct Harm: selected targets only, never an area, each needing a route;
  unattended objects get no defence.
- Defence: outcomes on one target that use the same defence share a roll; an
  ongoing area is defended once, on entry.
- Costs: a casting stopped by a defence still costs PP; cancelling the
  overreach penalty does not make it safe; a casting that drops you to 0 PP
  still takes effect first.
- Sense Magic's Critical and Fumble results.
- Dispel: the fallback when the original caster's skill is unknown, and that
  completed permanent changes cannot be Dispelled.
- A worked example from casting to damage; Backlash spill details.

## Gamemaster tools

- Enchanted items: making, using, identifying, destroying.
- Treasure (Plunder ratings).
- Mass combat.
- Ships and naval combat.
- Epic characters.
- Threat Intensity, the creature danger rating.

  Giant, Giant Ant, Giant Spider, Goblin, Golem, Horse, Lion, Mummy, Ogre, Orc,
  Skeleton, Troll, Viper, Wolf, Wyvern and Zombie.
- Creature Talents (Exceptional Skill I–III).
- Spirit Sense, and manifesting or fading.
- Nymphs' elemental shells.
- Characteristic dice for varying a creature.

## Not rules
