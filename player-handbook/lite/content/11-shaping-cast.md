# Cast and Sustain

## Common outcomes

Use the lowest Intensity that covers what you want. Your cell must allow it.

| Outcome           | Intensity buys                                                                                                                         |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Harm**          | I D6 damage, no DM. Choose Projected or Direct Harm. Mind deals no HP damage; Unmake·Spirit is Direct only and drains I D6 PP instead. |
| **Heal**          | I D4 HP; stops Bleeding and Dying. I3+ counts as Surgery. Once per subject per rest.                                                   |
| **Protect**       | 2 × I AP against physical or elemental harm; both: +1 I. Use it or armour, whichever is higher.                                        |
| **Barrier**       | 2 × I AP and 5 × I HP; it blocks movement and attacks.                                                                                 |
| **Boost, hinder** | One skill: I2 +1B or -1P; I4 +2B or -2P.                                                                                               |
| **Restrain**      | I1 prone, or half Movement for a turn; I2 hold a person or push 3 m; I3 lose a Combat Action; I4 incapacitate.                         |
| **Sway a mind**   | I1 colour an emotion; I2 a reasonable command; I3 one against their interest; I4 full control.                                         |
| **Move**          | I2 climb, swim or walk a surface; I3 fly; I4 pass through a substance. Speed: I1 +3 m; I2 ×1.5; I3 ×2.                                 |
| **Senses**        | I1 Low-Light Sight; I2 Night Vision; I3 Blind Sight through one sense. Hide: I2 -1P to notice; I3 undetectable.                        |
| **Learn**         | I1 a clue; I2 locate, diagnose or talk; I3 watch afar or read surface thoughts; I4 deep secrets.                                       |
| **Summon**        | A being for the Duration. I1: 3 HP, 25%, 1D3. I2: 6 HP, 1 AP, 40%, 1D6. I3: 10 HP, 2 AP, 55%, 1D8. I4: 14 HP, 3 AP, 70%, 2D6.          |

## Harm: Projected or Direct

**Projected Harm** crosses space like an arrow: Dodge or shield Active Guard,
both at -1P; cover and armour apply. **Direct Harm** (+1 M) arises inside chosen
targets: they resist with Resilience, or Persistence for mind and soul, and a
win cancels it. Only magical protection helps against it.

## Sense Magic and Dispel

**Sense Magic:** Combat Action and 1 PP; a Shaping test finds active magic
within 15 m and its Form. **Dispel:** Combat Action; M is the target's M plus
Range. Cast normally, then also beat a fresh Shaping roll for the original
caster.

## Cast

Spend your **Combat Action**, perform your Practice and roll **Shaping**. You
must have PP to cover the full M. A calm, cosmetic trick (M0) is free and
automatic.

| Result                     | You pay               |
| -------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Critical                   | Half M                |
| Success                    | M                     |
| Failure within Safe M      | 1 PP; no effect       |
| Failure while overreaching | Half M, plus Backlash |
| Fumble                     | M; Backlash           |

**Backlash** (1D4, or the GM's choice): **1** M damage, ignoring armour; **2**
lose M more PP; **3** a weaker effect hits you or something near; **4** one step
more Fatigued.

## Defending against magic

Each target opposes the casting roll; the Shaper must succeed and win.

| Effect                       | Defence                               |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| A bolt at one target         | Dodge -1P, or shield Active Guard -1P |
| An area you can avoid        | Dodge -1P                             |
| A change to body or matter   | Resilience; no Reaction               |
| A change to mind or soul     | Persistence; no Reaction              |
| Willing, yourself, an object | None                                  |

A winning shield Active Guard blocks by Size: I1 Light, I2 Medium, I3 Heavy,
I4 Huge. A Critical casting is not maximised and does not ignore armour.

**Hostile touch:** first hit with a no-damage Unarmed Combat attack in the same
action; the target can React. A miss spends the action, but no PP.

## Keep it going

- All your ongoing effects together cannot exceed your **INT** in M. Dismiss
  one on your turn, freely.
- A lasting benefit needs no action. **Concentration**, or directing another's
  body, costs your Combat Action each round; an unwilling body moves at most
  3 m a round. Damage or distraction calls for Persistence, or it ends.
- A **summon** follows one standing order, such as "guard me". Changing it
  costs your Movement Action within 15 m. Keep one fighting summon, or one
  controlled subject, at a time.
- Held or controlled by magic? Spend a Combat Action to repeat your defence
  against the stored casting roll; if you cannot act, retry at the end of your
  turn. Winning frees you, but returns no lost actions.
