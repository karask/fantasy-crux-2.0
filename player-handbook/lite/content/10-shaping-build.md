# Shaping

## Becoming a Shaper

**Shaper** is a special edge. A human can start with it in place of all three
edges, adding 50 fewer points to Resistance, Combat and Practical skills (at
most 20 fewer from each). Others learn it in play, for 20 IP. It gives:

- the **Shaping** skill, base INT + POW; you can add up to 30 Knowledge points;
- a **specialty**, one Form or one Technique, and two known **cells** that
  contain it; and
- **Sense Magic** and **Dispel**, which need no cell.

Your **Practice** is what you visibly do to cast: speech, gesture, a focus or
prayer. If it is prevented, you cannot cast. Your **Tell** is a sign around you
and your target, seen within 10 × M metres (at least 3 m). It shows magic, not
its purpose.

## Cells

A cell joins a **Technique** (what you do) and a **Form** (what you affect),
such as Unmake·Fire. You can cast only cells you know.

| Technique   | What it does                       |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **Conjure** | Create things, barriers, beings    |
| **Bend**    | Move or control; no damage         |
| **Unmake**  | Damage, destroy, suppress, banish  |
| **Alter**   | Heal, transform, impose conditions |
| **Ward**    | Protect, resist or deny            |
| **Scry**    | Learn, sense or speak; no change   |

| Form             | What it affects                         |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **Fire**         | Flame, heat and light                   |
| **Water/Ice**    | Water, cold and ice                     |
| **Air/Storm**    | Air, wind, sound, weather and lightning |
| **Earth/Stone**  | Soil, stone and natural minerals        |
| **Flesh**        | Bodies, living or dead; plants          |
| **Mind**         | Thought, emotion, memory, senses        |
| **Force/Motion** | Force, movement, weight and mass        |
| **Spirit**       | Souls, spirits and possession           |
| **Paths**        | Routes, thresholds and distance         |
| **Fate**         | Chance, omens and vows                  |

## Rules of thumb

Damage, healing and lost actions happen once; Duration never repeats them.
Same-kind magic does not stack. Magic grants no extra actions or resources.
Conjured things vanish when the Duration ends. Only Dispel ends active magic.

## Build an effect

Say: **using [cell], I cause [outcome] at [Range], affecting [Reach], for
[Duration].** The GM confirms it before you roll.

**Magnitude (M) = Intensity of each outcome + Range + Duration + Reach, +1 for
Direct Harm.**

**Intensity (I)** is an outcome's strength: see Common outcomes. Each different
outcome adds its own Intensity; Range, Duration and Reach count once.

| Rating | Range         | Duration        | Reach                  |
| -----: | ------------- | --------------- | ---------------------- |
|      0 | Self, touch   | Instant         | One target             |
|      1 | 15 m          | A scene, 10 min | 2 targets, 3 m radius  |
|      2 | 50 m          | To a full rest  | 5 targets, 10 m radius |
|      3 | Visible point | Ritual          | Ritual                 |

Instant lasts at most to the target's next turn; a full rest, at most 8 hours.
Reach also limits size: 0 a person or door, 1 a wagon, 2 a room. Range 1–3
needs a clear route: seeing through a barrier is not enough. An area includes
allies.

## Your limits

| Shaping | Maximum I | Safe M |
| ------- | --------: | -----: |
| 0–25%   |         1 |      1 |
| 26–50%  |         2 |      2 |
| 51–75%  |         3 |      3 |
| 76–99%  |         4 |      4 |
| 100%    |         5 |      5 |

No outcome can exceed your maximum Intensity. You can cast up to **Safe M +
2**, at -1P for Safe + 1 and -2P for Safe + 2. This is **overreach**: failing it
brings Backlash. Intensity 5 or anything bigger needs a **ritual** (full rules).

## Two examples

**Firebolt:** Unmake·Fire, 2D6 Projected Harm (I2) at 15 m (Range 1) = **M3**.

**Stone skin for two:** Ward·Earth/Stone, 4 AP against blows (I2), 15 m (Range
1), a scene (Duration 1), two allies (Reach 1) = **M5**.
