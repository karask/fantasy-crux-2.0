# More Combat

## Opportunity Attack

When an adjacent enemy moves away, or gives you a clear opening, spend your
Reaction on one Close Combat attack.

## Surprise

An unaware defender cannot React. Sneaking up is Deception against Perception.
Being behind someone does not by itself make them unaware.

## Ranged attacks

- **Range:** up to the weapon's Range, attack normally; up to twice its Range,
  at -2P; farther, you cannot hit.
- **Aim:** Combat Action. Your next shot at that target gains +1B. You lose the
  Aim if you React, lose sight of the target, or do not shoot with your next
  Combat Action.
- **Reload:** nock an arrow with your Movement Action, moving up to half your
  Movement. A light crossbow takes your Movement Action and your Reaction; a
  heavy crossbow takes the whole round.
- **Into a melee:** apply cover. Only a Fumble hits someone else nearby.
- **At arm's length:** within 2 m, a bow, crossbow, sling or javelin is a club:
  1D6 + DM at -1P. Daggers, hatchets and shortspears work as close weapons.

## Grapple

Combat Action, with a free hand, against an adjacent target: roll **Unarmed
Combat**. An aware target can spend its Reaction to oppose with Dodge or Parry;
otherwise you only need a success. If you win, you hold the target, and while
the hold lasts, both of you:

- cannot take a Movement Action;
- take -1P on every test; and
- fight only unarmed or with a Light one-handed weapon.

**Escape:** Combat Action and an opposed Unarmed Combat test. The holder can let
go at any time on their turn.

## Mounted

Use the mount's Movement. Your attack skill cannot exceed your Riding. A mount
untrained for battle might bolt: test Riding at the start of each round.

## Intimidate

Combat Action: demand surrender or retreat from one foe or a group. Oppose your
**Influence** against the **Persistence** of the foe or its leader. The foe
gets one modifier: at full strength **+2B**; outnumbering you but down 20%
**+1B**; outnumbered and wounded **-1P**; lost half its HP or numbers **-2P**.

If you win, the GM decides how the foe gives way. The wounded might flee or
surrender; healthy foes more often hesitate and lose a turn. A foe at full
strength, or outnumbering you, surrenders only to a Critical against a failed
roll. A fumbled Persistence breaks it at once.

## Major Wounds

One hit that deals **at least your MWL** after armour causes a Major Wound:

- **Wounded:** -1P on every test.
- **Bleeding:** lose 1 HP at the end of every round.

They do not stack, but a new Major Wound can restart Bleeding.

## Dying

At **0 HP** you fall unconscious and are **Dying**. You die at the end of the
**third round** unless someone stabilises you.

**Stabilise:** an adjacent helper spends the whole round, staying put, and
passes **Healing**; -2P without a Healing Kit. It stops Bleeding and Dying but
restores no HP.

## Healing

- **First aid:** after a fight, a patient who is not Wounded can receive one
  Healing attempt before their next full rest. Success restores **1D4 HP**.
- **Surgery:** a Wounded patient heals naturally only after Surgery: a Healing
  test in safety, outside combat. Success restores **1 HP**.
- **Rest:** with only light activity, recover **CON / 4 HP a day**. Wounded ends
  when HP rises above MWL.
- **Power Points:** recover a quarter of your PP per two hours of rest; eight
  hours restores them all. At 0 PP you fall unconscious.

## Hero Points save lives

Spend 1 to keep a Major Wound's HP loss without Wounded or Bleeding, and 1 more
at 0 HP to become stable instead of Dying. One hit can need both.
