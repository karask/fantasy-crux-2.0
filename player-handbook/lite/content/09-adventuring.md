# Adventuring

## Travel

A day is ten hours of travel in fair weather.

| Pace          | Per day | Cost                                      |
| ------------- | ------: | ----------------------------------------- |
| Marching      |   30 km | Arrive ready to fight                     |
| Hiking        |   50 km | A Fatigue test at day's end               |
| Riding slowly |   30 km | With pack animals or wagons               |
| Riding        |   50 km |                                           |
| Riding fast   |   90 km | Rider and mount test Fatigue at day's end |

On rough ground, multiply: light brush 80%; medium brush, light woods or hills
70%; heavy woodland 50%.

**Navigation:** test Natural Lore once per uncertain day. A failure loses a
quarter of the day's progress; a Fumble means no progress, and you are lost.

## Moving about

**Climb** at a quarter of your Movement where there are holds; an Athletics
success doubles it. **Swim** at half Movement in calm water; test Athletics in
currents, bad weather or under a heavy load. **Jump:** with a 5 m run, a success clears about
twice your height across, or half your height up.

## Fatigue

After hard effort, such as a long fight, a sprint, or a hard climb or swim, the
GM can call for a **Fatigue test**: Resilience.

| State     | Tests | Movement       | Combat Order |
| --------- | ----- | -------------- | ------------ |
| Fatigued  | -1P   | Three-quarters | -3           |
| Exhausted | -2P   | Half           | -6           |

Fail once to become Fatigued. More hard effort calls for another test at -1P;
fail it to become Exhausted. A fumbled test knocks you out for 3D6 minutes, and
you wake Fatigued. **20 - CON hours** of rest, at least 1, clears either state.

## Load

You carry up to **STR + SIZ** ENC freely. Above that you take -1P on physical
and Fatigue tests and move at half speed. You can never carry twice that.
Twenty small items in a container count as 1 ENC.

## Hazards

| Hazard                       | Effect                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Falling**                  | 1D6 per full 3 m, ignoring armour; you land prone. If you can control the landing, an Athletics success removes 1D6, a Critical 2D6; a Fumble maximises the dice.            |
| **Fire**                     | Each round: candle 1, torch 1D4, campfire 1D6, bonfire or burning room 2D6, lava 3D6. Metal armour does not help.                                                            |
| **Holding breath**           | CON rounds; then Resilience each round at a cumulative -1P. On a failure take, each round until you reach air: water 2D6, thick smoke 1D6. Armour does not help.             |
| **Poison, disease**          | After the Delay, oppose your Resilience against its Potency. Lose and suffer its effect. A disease returns after each Delay until you win. Treatment first gives +1B.        |
| **Exposure, hunger, thirst** | Safe for CON hours without shelter, CON days without food, CON × 2 hours without water (less in heat). After that, a daily Fatigue test at -1P and 1D6 damage for each lack. |

## Objects

Attacks on still objects hit automatically. Subtract AP, then HP; at 0 HP the
object breaks.

| Object       | AP  | HP  |
| ------------ | --- | --- |
| Wooden chair | 2   | 6   |
| Wooden door  | 2   | 25  |
| Iron door    | 4   | 75  |
| Castle gate  | 4   | 120 |
