# Combat

## The round

A round is five seconds. When a fight starts, everyone rolls **1D10 + Combat
Order** once and acts from highest to lowest; ties go to the higher DEX. Each
round you have:

- one **Combat Action:** attack, Aim, grapple, cast, Intimidate, or use a skill
  or item;
- one **Movement Action:** move up to your Movement, stand up, drop prone, or
  ready an item while moving half as far. Stowing one item and drawing
  another, or readying two, takes the whole Movement Action;
- one **Reaction:** Dodge, Parry or an Opportunity Attack, when something
  triggers it, even on another's turn.

Unused actions are lost. You cannot choose an option that conflicts with an
action you already used: after a Parry, you cannot Sprint. **Delay:** act later
in the round, after someone you choose.

## Moving in a fight

| Option         | Cost and effect                                                                                                         |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Disengage**  | Movement Action: move away; adjacent enemies can make an Opportunity Attack.                                            |
| **Withdraw**   | Both actions: move away without Opportunity Attacks. Keep your Reactions.                                               |
| **Sprint**     | Both actions: move twice your Movement. Your only Reaction is a Dodge.                                                  |
| **Charge**     | Both actions and all Reactions: run straight at least 5 m, up to twice your Movement, and attack. +1D6 damage on a hit. |
| **Set Weapon** | Combat Action: brace a Set weapon. The first foe to Charge you before your next turn takes your attack first, at +1B.   |

## Attack

Roll **Close Combat** for held weapons and shields, **Unarmed Combat** for
fists, kicks and natural weapons, or **Ranged Combat**. Unarmed blows deal 1D3. A result at or under
your skill hits, unless the defender stops it.

## Two weapons

With two ready one-handed items (an empty hand counts as a fist), once per
round choose one:

- right after a normal close or unarmed attack, attack with the other at
  **-1P**, at no cost; or
- keep the other for one extra Parry or Opportunity Attack at **-1P**. It cannot
  Dodge.

## Defend

When an attack hits you and you are aware of it, you can spend one Reaction.
One Reaction answers one attack. Your roll opposes the attack roll; the
attacker does not roll again.

- **Dodge:** if you win, the attack misses. -1P against ranged attacks. Once per
  round.
- **Parry:** Close Combat with a ready weapon or shield, or Unarmed Combat
  against an unarmed attack. If you win, block damage by Size.
- **Active Guard:** Parry a ranged attack. Against a thrown weapon, use a ready
  weapon at -1P or a shield normally; against arrows, bolts and stones, a shield
  only, at -1P.

## Parry Size

Weapons and shields are **Light, Medium, Heavy or Huge**. Fists, kicks,
arrows, bolts and stones are Light.

| Your parrying item is…    | Damage blocked   |
| ------------------------- | ---------------- |
| The same Size or larger   | All              |
| One Size smaller          | Half, rounded up |
| Two or more Sizes smaller | None             |

## Damage

Roll the weapon's damage. Add your **Damage Modifier (DM)** to close, unarmed
and thrown attacks, but not to bows, crossbows or slings. Subtract a winning
Parry, then the target's **Armour Points (AP)**. The rest comes off HP. Damage
and HP never go below 0.

> **Example.** An ogre's Heavy club hits for 12. Your Medium sword Parries and
> blocks half: 6 remain. Leather armour (2 AP) stops 2. You lose 4 HP.

## Criticals and Fumbles

- A **Critical hit** deals maximum weapon damage plus maximum positive DM,
  nothing more, and ignores armour.
- An ordinary successful Dodge or Parry turns a Critical hit into an ordinary
  one: roll damage and apply armour, but the defence blocks nothing.
- A **Critical defence** that wins stops any hit.
- A **Fumble** brings an immediate mishap: a dropped weapon, a fall, an opening.
