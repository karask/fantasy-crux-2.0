# Edges and Growth

## Edges

Choose **two edges**, or three for a human. An edge is a trained knack: most
give a die or an exception in a narrow situation. An edge marked † replaces a
hit's damage, and only if no Dodge or full Parry stops the hit; use one † edge
per attack. Full-rules Talents also work in Lite, at their listed IP cost.

- **Ambusher:** your first attack in a scene, from hiding against an unaware
  target, turns an ordinary hit into a Critical.
- **Battle Awareness:** while you are aware of an enemy, attacks on you from the
  flank or behind gain no +1B.
- **Disarm** †: knock one held item 1D6 m away.
- **Expertise:** in one narrow use of a skill, such as climbing, sneaking,
  persuading, commanding, tracking, or Natural Lore in one terrain, ignore one
  Penalty die. It never adds a Bonus die. Take it again for another use.
- **Favoured Weapon:** +1 damage with one type of weapon.
- **Field Surgeon:** perform Surgery anywhere outside combat; your success ends
  Wounded at once.
- **Iron Fist:** your unarmed blows deal 1D4 and count as Medium.
- **Killing Angle:** once per round, +1D4 damage to a close or unarmed attack
  against a flanked, rear or unaware target; not on a Critical.
- **Missile Guard:** no -1P when you Active Guard with a shield.
- **Off-Hand Mastery:** no -1P on off-hand attacks and Parries.
- **Practised Hands:** your first aid restores 1D4 + 2 HP, and you can retry a
  failed attempt once.
- **Protector:** with a ready shield, spend your Reaction to Parry a hit on an
  adjacent ally.
- **Quick Reflexes:** +2 Combat Order.
- **Rally:** Combat Action: two allies who hear you gain +1B on their next test.
- **Rapid Shot:** Combat Action and all Reactions: shoot a bow or sling twice,
  each at -1P.
- **Shaper:** cast magic; see Shaping. A human can start with it.
- **Shield Cover:** with a ready medium or large shield, ranged attacks and
  Shaping bolts aimed at you take -1P.
- **Shield Rush** †: with a shield, push the target 2 m; it tests Athletics at
  -1P or falls prone.
- **Silent Step:** while you move at half speed or less, anyone trying to notice
  you takes -1P.
- **Steady Casting:** ignore one Penalty die from Wounded or a grapple on
  Shaping tests.
- **Trip** †: knock the target prone.

## Starting gear

Choose one package:

- leather armour, a ranged weapon, a two-handed close weapon and a dagger; or
- leather armour, a shield, a ranged weapon, a one-handed close weapon and a
  dagger.

Everyone also has a backpack, 10 m of rope, two weeks of provisions, flint and
tinder, a waterskin, and **4D6 × 10 SP**.

## Growth

After each adventure the GM awards **3 to 5 Improvement Points (IP)**. Spend
them between adventures; unspent IP carry over.

| Improvement                                                                                    |  Cost |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----: |
| One skill +5%, or +3% if it is above 50%. Each skill once between adventures; never above 100% |  1 IP |
| A new edge                                                                                     |  3 IP |
| +1 to a characteristic other than SIZ, up to your ancestry's maximum                           |  5 IP |
| A new Shaping cell with your specialty                                                         |  2 IP |
| Any other cell, after an ordeal or with a master                                               |  4 IP |
| Become a Shaper, after an initiation                                                           | 20 IP |

After a characteristic rises, recalculate HP, MWL, PP, DM and Combat Order.
Skills keep their current scores.

## Between adventures

Three months of suitable practice or study earns 1 IP, with the GM's approval.
