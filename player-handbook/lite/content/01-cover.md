# Fantasy Crux Lite

## What is Lite?

Fantasy Crux is a gritty percentile game about capable adventurers. Violence is
dangerous, preparation matters, and Hero Points leave room for epic reversals.

Lite is the same game with its side rules folded together. Almost everything is
a skill test, and almost every special case is a reason to add **Bonus or
Penalty dice**. An experienced Gamemaster can read it in an evening and run it
from memory.

## Using Lite

Lite keeps the **same numbers** as the full rules: characteristics, skills,
weapons, armour, wounds and Shaping. Characters, creatures and adventures for
Fantasy Crux 2.0 work in Lite unchanged, and so do any Talents a character has.

When Lite is silent, use the full rules at fantasycrux.org, or make a quick
ruling and keep playing.

## You need

Two ten-sided dice for percentile rolls, one for tens and one for units, plus a
few spare ten-sided dice for Bonus and Penalty dice. A D4, D6, D8, D10 and D12
for damage. A character sheet and a pencil for each player.

## Contents

- **Play** and **Circumstances**: tests and the dice that modify them.
- **Characters** and **Improvement and Talents**: build and improve a
  character.
- **Combat** and **More Combat**: fights, wounds and healing.
- **Equipment** and **Adventuring**: gear, travel and hazards.
- **Shaping**: magic, in two pages.
- **Gamemaster** and **Creatures**: running tests, quick NPCs, creature
  rules and common creatures.
