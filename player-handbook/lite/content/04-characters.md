# Characters

## 1. Concept

Write one sentence: who they are, what they do well and what drives them.

## 2. Ancestry and characteristics

Start from your ancestry's spread and add **30 points**. No score above 18; INT
and SIZ at least 7, the rest at least 3. **Quick human:** assign 16, 14, 13,
12, 11, 10 and 10.

| Ancestry   | STR | CON | DEX | SIZ | INT | POW | CHA |
| ---------- | --: | --: | --: | --: | --: | --: | --: |
| Human      |   8 |   8 |   8 |   8 |   8 |   8 |   8 |
| Elf        |   6 |   8 |  10 |   6 |  10 |   8 |   8 |
| Dwarf      |  10 |  12 |   6 |   4 |   8 |   8 |   8 |
| Orc        |  10 |   8 |   8 |  10 |   6 |   6 |   8 |
| Goblin     |   6 |   7 |  12 |   4 |   9 |   8 |  10 |
| Lizardfolk |  11 |   8 |   6 |   8 |   9 |   7 |   7 |

**Ancestry abilities** cost IP from your starting 10 (step 5). **Human** (0 IP):
none. **Elf** (5 IP): Low-Light Sight; in forests, ignore one Penalty die on
Natural Lore. **Dwarf** (7 IP): Blind Sight (heat); Earth Sense, knowing your
depth and whether a cave is unstable while touching stone. **Orc** (4 IP):
Adrenaline Surge: once per fight, on becoming Wounded or Fatigued, ignore both
penalties until the end of your next turn. **Goblin** (5 IP): Blind Sight
(heat). **Lizardfolk** (6 IP): scaled hide, +1 AP that stacks with armour; a
tail that gives -1P to attempts to trip, throw or push you.

**Blind Sight (heat)** perceives warm bodies without light, but no colour,
writing or fine detail; walls and insulation block it.

## 3. Attributes

| Attribute               | Value                             |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Hit Points (HP)         | (SIZ + CON) / 2                   |
| Major Wound Level (MWL) | HP / 2                            |
| Power Points (PP)       | POW                               |
| Movement                | 15 m a round                      |
| Combat Order            | (DEX + INT) / 2, minus armour ENC |
| Carrying capacity       | STR + SIZ, in ENC                 |

Recalculate them whenever a characteristic changes. **Damage Modifier (DM),**
from STR + SIZ: 1–10 **-1D6**; 11–15 **-1D4**; 16–25
**+0**; 26–30 **+1D4**; 31–45 **+1D6**; 46–60 **+2D6**; 61–75 **+3D6**; each 15
more, another +1D6.

## 4. Skills

Every character has every skill at its base. Add **50 points to Resistances, 50
to Combat, 50 to Knowledge and 75 to Practical** skills, at most 30 to any one
skill. Each Craft, Culture, Language and Lore subject is a separate skill.

| Resistances | Base      | Use         |
| ----------- | --------- | ----------- |
| Dodge       | DEX + 10  | Avoid harm  |
| Persistence | POW + 10  | Resist fear |
| Resilience  | CON + POW | Endure harm |

| Combat         | Base      | Use              |
| -------------- | --------- | ---------------- |
| Close Combat   | DEX + STR | Weapons, shields |
| Ranged Combat  | DEX + INT | Shoot, throw     |
| Unarmed Combat | DEX + STR | Punch, grapple   |

| Knowledge        | Base      | Use           |
| ---------------- | --------- | ------------- |
| Culture (own)    | INT + 10  | Customs       |
| Culture (other)  | INT       | Customs       |
| Language (own)   | INT + 50  | Speak         |
| Language (other) | INT       | 50% is fluent |
| Lore (own field) | INT + 10  | Study         |
| Lore (other)     | INT       | Study         |
| Natural Lore     | INT + 10  | Track, forage |
| Shaping          | INT + POW | Shapers only  |

| Practical     | Base      | Use           |
| ------------- | --------- | ------------- |
| Athletics     | DEX + STR | Climb, swim   |
| Craft (trade) | INT + 10  | Make, repair  |
| Deception     | DEX + INT | Sneak, hide   |
| Driving       | DEX + INT | Carts, wagons |
| Healing       | INT + 10  | Treat wounds  |
| Influence     | CHA + 10  | Persuade      |
| Mechanisms    | DEX + INT | Locks, traps  |
| Perception    | INT + POW | Notice        |
| Performance   | CHA + 10  | Entertain     |
| Riding        | DEX + POW | Mounts        |
| Sailing       | DEX + INT | Boats, ships  |
| Streetwise    | CHA + POW | Rumours       |
| Trade         | INT + 10  | Haggle, value |

## Example

Mara, a human warrior, takes the quick array: STR 16, CON 14, DEX 13, SIZ 12,
INT 11, POW 10, CHA 10. She has HP 13, MWL 7, PP 10, DM +1D4 and Combat Order
12, or 9 in leather. With 30 Combat points, her Close Combat is 59%.
