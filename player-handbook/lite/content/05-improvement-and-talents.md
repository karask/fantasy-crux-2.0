# Improvement and Talents

## 5. Improvement Points

Improvement Points (IP) buy Talents and improvements. You start with **10 IP**,
minus your ancestry's abilities. The GM awards **3 to 5 IP** after each
adventure, and three months of practice or study earns 1 IP. Unspent IP carry
over.

| Spend                                                                                 | Cost   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| A Talent, if you meet its requirement                                                 | Listed |
| One skill +5%, or +3% above 50%; each skill once between adventures, never above 100% | 1 IP   |
| +1 to a characteristic other than SIZ, up to its maximum; 18 at most when you start   | 5 IP   |

**More IP at the start:** keep back up to 20 points from each of your
Resistance, Combat and Practical pools. Every 5 kept back gives 1 IP, at most 12. They buy Talents or characteristics, not skills.

**Shaping costs 20 IP.** Only a human can start with it: all 10 starting IP,
plus 10 from 50 skill points kept back. Other ancestries learn it in play.

## 6. Gear and finish

Take leather armour, a ranged weapon, a dagger, and either a two-handed close
weapon or a shield and a one-handed one. Add a backpack, 10 m of rope, two
weeks' provisions, flint and tinder, a waterskin and **4D6 × 10 SP**. Record
HP, MWL, PP, DM, Combat Order, gear, Talents and **2 Hero Points**.

## Talents

Talents are trained knacks. Each shows its IP cost and the requirement to buy
it; Shaping is on the Shaping page. A Talent marked † replaces a hit's damage,
and only if no Dodge or full Parry stops the hit; use one † Talent per attack.
Any other full-rules Talent can be bought at its listed cost.

- **Ambusher** {{talent:ambusher}}: your first attack in a scene, from hiding
  against an unaware target, turns an ordinary hit into a Critical.
- **Battle Awareness** {{talent:battle-awareness}}: while you are aware of an
  enemy, attacks on you from the flank or behind gain no +1B.
- **Disarm** † {{talent:disarm}}: knock one held item 1D6 m away.
- **Expertise** {{talent:athletics-expertise,deception-expertise,silver-tongue,commanding-presence,tracker,terrain-expertise}}:
  in one narrow use of a skill, such as climbing, sneaking, persuading,
  commanding, tracking, or Natural Lore in one terrain, ignore one Penalty die.
  It never adds a Bonus die. Take it again for another use.
- **Favoured Weapon** {{talent:favoured-weapon}}: +1 damage with one named
  weapon.
- **Field Surgeon** {{talent:field-surgeon}}: perform Surgery anywhere outside
  combat; your success ends Wounded at once.
- **Iron Fist** {{talent:iron-fist}}: your unarmed blows deal 1D4 and count as
  Medium.
- **Killing Angle** {{talent:killing-angle}}: once per round, +1D4 damage to a
  close or unarmed attack against a flanked, rear or unaware target; not on a
  Critical.
- **Missile Guard** {{talent:missile-guard}}: no -1P when you Active Guard with
  a shield.
- **Off-Hand Mastery** {{talent:off-hand-mastery}}: no -1P on off-hand attacks
  and Parries.
- **Practised Hands** {{talent:practised-hands}}: your first aid restores
  1D4 + 2 HP, and you can retry a failed attempt once.
- **Protector** {{talent:protector}}: with a ready shield, spend your base
  Reaction to Parry a hit on an adjacent ally.
- **Quick Reflexes** {{talent:quick-reflexes}}: +2 Combat Order.
- **Rally** {{talent:rally}}: Combat Action: two allies who hear you gain +1B on
  their next test.
- **Rapid Shot** {{talent:rapid-shot}}: Combat Action and all Reactions: shoot a
  bow or sling twice, each at -1P.
- **Shield Cover** {{talent:shield-cover}}: with a ready medium or large shield,
  ranged attacks and Shaping bolts aimed at you take -1P.
- **Shield Rush** † {{talent:shield-rush}}: with a shield, push the target 2 m;
  it tests Athletics at -1P or falls prone.
- **Silent Step** {{talent:silent-step}}: while you move at half speed or less,
  anyone trying to notice you takes -1P.
- **Steady Casting** {{talent:steady-casting}}: ignore one Penalty die from
  Wounded or a grapple on Shaping tests.
- **Trip** † {{talent:trip}}: knock the target prone.
