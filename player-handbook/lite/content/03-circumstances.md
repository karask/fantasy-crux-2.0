# Circumstances

## Add dice for what matters

A clear advantage is **+1B**, a clear problem **-1P**; two dice are for strong
factors. Add up different causes, but use only the strongest of one kind, such
as the worst cover. Then cancel and cap at +3B or -3P. Judge new cases alike.

## Your condition

| You are…                              | Dice                    |
| ------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Wounded, after a Major Wound          | -1P on every test       |
| Fatigued / Exhausted                  | -1P / -2P on every test |
| Held in a grapple, or holding someone | -1P on every test       |
| Below a weapon's STR or DEX minimum   | -1P with that weapon    |
| Overloaded: over STR + SIZ in ENC     | -1P on physical tests   |
| On unstable ground                    | -1P                     |

## Close combat

| Situation                                     | Dice                         |
| --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Target prone, flanked or attacked from behind | +1B to attack                |
| You have higher ground                        | +1B to your own rolls        |
| You are prone                                 | -1P to your own rolls        |
| Fighting underwater                           | -2P                          |
| Rider against someone on foot                 | Rider +1B; foe -1P to defend |
| Target helpless                               | Automatic Critical           |

## Skills and tasks

| Situation                                     | Dice            |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| Good tools: climbing kit, crowbar, lock picks | +1B             |
| A sound plan, or real leverage                | +1B             |
| A successful related Lore test just before    | +1B to one test |
| Skilled helpers                               | +1B in total    |
| Healing without a Healing Kit                 | -2P             |
| Resisting poison or disease after treatment   | +1B             |

## Ranged attacks

| Situation                                                | Dice          |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| You spent a Combat Action to Aim at the target           | +1B           |
| Beyond Range, up to twice Range                          | -2P           |
| Partial / substantial cover                              | -1P / -2P     |
| Target moved 10 m / 30 m since your last turn            | -1P / -2P     |
| Target partly / heavily hidden by mist, smoke or foliage | -1P / -2P     |
| Target prone                                             | -1P           |
| Target helpless                                          | +2B           |
| You are prone                                            | -2P           |
| An enemy is adjacent to you                              | -2P per enemy |
| High wind / fierce wind                                  | -1P / -3P     |

## Light

Light at the target counts for seeing and attacking; light at you, for moving.
Radius: candle 1 m, torch or lantern 3 m, campfire 5 m, bonfire 10 m.

| Light                                            | Effect                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Daylight, or within a light's radius             | None                                             |
| Partial darkness: twilight, or up to 3× a radius | -1P to sight-based Perception and attacks        |
| Darkness: starlight, or up to 5× a radius        | -2P to them; half Movement                       |
| Pitch black                                      | Sight fails; close attacks -3P; quarter Movement |

**Low-Light Sight** counts partial darkness as light and darkness as partial
darkness. **Night Vision** counts natural night outdoors as light.

## Example

An archer aims at a running orc behind a low wall at dusk. Aim gives +1B; the
orc moved 10 m (-1P), has partial cover (-1P) and stands in partial darkness
(-1P). Net: **-2P**. Roll three tens dice and keep the worst result.
