## Weapon tags

**1H / 2H:** hands needed. **Flex:** in two hands, +2 damage and 2 less STR.
**Set:** can be braced against a Charge. **Thrown:** adds DM and keeps its
Size; a thrown dagger or hatchet reaches STR m, a shortspear STR × 2 m, and each
needs DEX 9. Missiles add no DM and are Light. Below a STR or DEX minimum: -1P.
Improvised weapons deal 1 less damage. 10 arrows cost 1 SP; 10 bolts, 2 SP.
