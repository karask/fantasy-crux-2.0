## Retained details checked against the rules

- D100 uses two ten-sided dice, including 00 = 100. Fumble precedence, the
  0% Critical exception and 100% Fumble rule are explicit. Ordinary rounding
  is nearest with halves up; Critical thresholds round down.
- Modifiers cancel, then cap. Candidates use grade before number, including
  higher successful rolls and lower failed rolls. Both 25/65 examples remain
  as native diagrams. Opposed tiebreaks apply to participants, not candidates.
- Assistance is combined, normally requires 26%, and has the exceptional +2B.
- Initiative is rolled at combat start. Movement, readying, Delay, trigger
  Reactions, no action banking and incompatible spending remain.
- One Reaction per attack; once-per-round base Dodge; awareness; all Parry
  Sizes and all seven ordinary/Critical outcome combinations remain.
- Off-hand eligibility includes empty hands, excludes two-handed use and
  grants only one extra option. Missed standard attacks can still follow up;
  Charge, Set Weapon and Opportunity Attacks cannot. Base shield Parry is
  not inherently penalised. Extra defence cannot Dodge.
- Sprint preserves only base Dodge; Charge forfeits all Reactions and uses a
  straight 5 m to twice-Movement route. Set's trigger, expiry and cancellation
  remain. Action costs are never shortened.
- Grappling keeps the player decision loop: seize, the Held effects, release,
  and escape. Defending the initial seizure costs an eligible Reaction; Dodge
  uses the once-per-round base Reaction. An undefended seizure still needs an
  Unarmed Combat success. The controller opposes escape without spending a
  Reaction. Edge cases and adjudication remain in the main rules.
- The ranged pages retain the common player choices: range, Aim, cover, missile
  defence, common difficult shots, position and awareness. Rare circumstances
  remain in the main rules for the GM to adjudicate.
- Intimidate is reduced to demand, opposed test and limits. Resistance modifiers,
  group handling and exceptional outcomes remain GM-facing details.
- Wounds compare a single post-reduction hit to MWL. Conditions do not stack;
  Bleeding can restart. Dying's deadline is the third round after 0 HP.
  Stabilisation costs the helper's whole round, requires stationary adjacency,
  restores no HP and does not wake a patient at 0 HP. Improvised stabilisation
  supplies are distinguished from the general Healing Kit requirement.
- Surgery gates natural Major Wound recovery. Ordinary treatment is one
  attempt, not one success. Recovery rate, maximum HP and strict HP > MWL
  ending condition are explicit. All four Hero Point uses and their distinct
  timing/costs remain, including the two-point example.
- All 24 close profiles, 12 ranged profiles and five armour profiles match
  canonical values. ASCII dashes and soft table wrapping are presentation
  changes only. Reload costs are native diagram text, not omitted. Ammunition
  prices remain a note rather than another table.

## Editorial choices

Repeated explanations were condensed. Dice, action budgets, Parry outcomes,
damage arithmetic, actor-specific modifiers and the wound countdown use native
diagrams. Equipment references favour table space over decorative art. Labels
and numbers remain editable text, not generated lettering.

The cover uses an existing mundane warrior image. New art illustrates the two
handed-item choice and Charge/Set interaction. Existing wrist-hold art shows an
ordinary hold, not a throw or damaging special technique.

The printed pregen's armour adjustment remains an explicit convention to
confirm when actual character sheets are supplied. Creation, advancement,
special abilities, supernatural rules, creatures, GM procedures and gazetteer
content remain excluded. Mentions of agreeing a player action with the GM do
not introduce GM-facing subsystems. The licence is reproduced as legal matter,
not as additional game content.
