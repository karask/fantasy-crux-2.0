-1P**; below both still only -1P.

## Range and hands

Range bands and Aim: {{ref:shot}}. **1H / 2H:** one / two hands. **Missile:** no DM.
Arrows, bolts and sling projectiles are **Light**, despite launcher Size dashes.

## Ready and reload

**Bow:** nock an arrow with your Movement Action while moving up to half
Movement. **Light crossbow:** reloading uses your Movement Action and base
Reaction. **Heavy crossbow:** reloading uses the whole round and all Reactions.
A Ready action never shortens a reload.

## Shields and ammunition

A small shield strapped to your arm cannot attack, Parry or Active Guard while
you wield a 2H ranged weapon.

Per 10: **arrows 1 SP; bolts 2 SP; sling bullets 5 CP**, with no listed ENC.
**SP / CP:** Silver / Copper Pieces. A dash means no listed value.
