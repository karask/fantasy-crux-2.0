## Range and hands

Up to Range: normal. Beyond Range through **twice Range: -2P**. Farther: no
attack. **1H / 2H:** one / two hands. **Missile:** no DM. Arrows, bolts and
sling projectiles are **Light**, despite launcher Size dashes. Aim: page 12.

## Ready and reload

**Bow:** nock one arrow with Movement Action while moving up to half Movement.
A generic Ready action never shortens crossbow reload costs.

## Shields and ammunition

A small shield may be strapped to your arm while carrying a 2H ranged weapon,
but cannot attack, Parry or provide Active Guard while you wield that weapon.

Per 10: **arrows 1 SP; bolts 2 SP; sling bullets 5 CP**. Bundles have no listed
ENC. **SP / CP:** Silver / Copper Pieces. **ENC:** burden. Dash: no listed value.
