# Damage & Critical Hits

**Resolve the Reaction before reducing HP.** Criticals change damage and defence,
not the number of attacks.

## Ordinary damage

Roll weapon damage, adding **DM** for melee, unarmed and thrown attacks (even a
negative DM) but not bows, crossbows or slings, plus declared action damage.
Apply a winning **Parry**, subtract **AP**, then lose the rest as **HP**. Neither
damage nor HP goes below 0.

## An ordinary attack hits

**None, failed or losing Reaction:** damage, then armour. **Winning Dodge:** no
damage. **Winning ordinary Parry:** reduce by Size, then armour ({{ref:defence}}).
**Critical Dodge or Parry:** no damage.

## A Critical attack hits

- **No, failed or fumbled Reaction:** maximum weapon damage plus maximum positive
  DM when normally added; **ignore armour**. Ignore negative DM and all other bonus
  damage, including the Charge die.
- **Ordinary successful Dodge or Parry:** demote it to an ordinary hit: normal
  damage with eligible bonuses, then armour. The Reaction **neither stops nor
  reduces** the hit.
- **Both Critical:** compare rolls, then base skills, then the defender. A winning
  defence stops all damage; a losing one leaves full Critical damage.

## Check the consequences

A Critical gives no free attack or manoeuvre. A Fumble brings a consequence that
fits the risk, such as dropping a weapon. Then check **Major Wound and 0 HP**
({{ref:wounds}}).
