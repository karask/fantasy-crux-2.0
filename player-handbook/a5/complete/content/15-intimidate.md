# Demand Surrender

**Combat Action: pressure an enemy to surrender or flee.**

## Say what you demand

Choose one enemy or a group. State what you want them to do and make the threat
clear.

## Make the test

Oppose your **Influence** against the target's **Persistence**. The GM decides
whether the attempt is possible and applies any modifier.

## Read the response

If you win, the enemy may yield or flee; the GM describes the response.
Intimidation creates pressure, not mind control.

## Editorial notes

Only the player's decision and roll are shown. Enemy modifiers, group leadership
and exceptional rout details remain in the full rules for the GM.
