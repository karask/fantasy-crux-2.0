# Talk, Trade & Go Unnoticed

Describe what you want and what leverage or concealment makes it possible.
Success changes the situation; it does not give control of another person's mind.

## Intimidate: Combat Action

State your demand to one enemy or a group. Oppose **Influence against Persistence**.
The GM applies modifiers and describes whether an enemy yields or flees.
Intimidation is pressure, not mind control.

## Persuade or bargain

Use Influence; resistance usually opposes Persistence or Influence. It cannot
force a choice against basic self-preservation.

**A sale:** oppose Trade. Winning buyer reduces price **10%**; winning seller
raises it **10%**. A Critical changes it **20%**; double the change if the opponent
Fumbles. Appraisal success estimates normal local value within 10%.

## Avoid notice or gain access

**Deception opposes Perception** for sneaking, disguise or sleight of hand.
Picking a pocket or planting a small item normally needs **one minute of access**.

**Locks and traps:** Mechanisms, normally at least one minute; an interference-
resistant device opposes with its maker's Mechanisms.
**Streetwise:** rumours or a contact normally take **1D4 hours**.

## Editorial notes

Only the player's decision and roll are shown. Enemy modifiers, group leadership
and exceptional rout details remain in the full rules for the GM.
