# Recover & Change Your Fate

**Stabilise first (page 16). Treat the injury next. Hero Points are a separate choice.**

## Treat and recover

**Ordinary damage:** after an encounter, a patient who is **not Wounded** may
receive **one Healing attempt before their next full rest**. Success restores
**1D4 HP**, up to maximum. One attempt per patient, even if it fails.

**Major Wound:** once stabilised, successful **Surgery** (Healing, outside combat,
safe conditions) restores **1 HP** and unlocks natural recovery. A Wounded
patient cannot heal naturally before Surgery or equivalent treatment. Ordinary
treatment and Surgery suffer **-2P without a Healing Kit**.

**Rest:** no more than light activity restores **CON / 4 HP per 24 hours**,
rounded nearest with halves up, never above maximum. After treatment, Wounded
ends only when **current HP > MWL**. CON 14 restores 4/day; MWL 6 ends at 7+ HP.

## Spend 1 Hero Point when declaring an effect

**Try again:** reroll a failed test, including a Fumble, after seeing it but
before consequences or costs. The new result stands, even if worse. **At most
one reroll per test**, regardless of source.

**Downgrade a Major Wound:** keep the full HP loss but prevent Wounded/Bleeding
from that hit. It creates no Major Wound needing Surgery. Existing conditions remain.

**Avoid Death:** at 0 HP, become stable and unconscious, not Bleeding or Dying.
Awaken after the scene at **1 HP**; existing Wounded remains.

**Story benefit:** with GM approval, add a plausible favourable detail or
coincidence. Larger benefits may cost several points; companions may contribute.

**Separate effects:** with 5 HP and MWL 6, taking 7 damage needs **two points**
to both downgrade the Major Wound and Avoid Death. Mark spent points off your sheet.

## Editorial notes

Retains all four Hero Point uses and all non-battlefield treatment. The native
two-token example prevents conflating prevention of conditions with prevention of HP loss.
