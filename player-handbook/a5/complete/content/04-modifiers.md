# Better Rolls, Shared Tests

**Bonus dice keep the better result; Penalty dice keep the worse.** Your skill
and Critical range do not change.

## Build and choose

Cancel +B and -P one for one; keep at most **+3B or -3P**.

Roll one units die, one tens die, and an extra tens die per remaining Bonus or
Penalty die. Pair every tens die with the **same units die**; 00 is still 100.

Choose by grade first: **Critical > Success > Failure > Fumble**. Within the
same Critical/Success grade, **higher is better**; within Failure/Fumble,
**lower is better**. Penalties reverse the choice.

## Competing: roll against someone

Both choose their result, then compare **grade**, number and **unmodified skill**.
An exact tie goes to the defender.

Deception **42 Success** beats Perception **31 Success**: same grade, higher
number. Ordinary attacks use attack-then-Reaction (page 7); grappling is opposed.

## Helping: one combined bonus

Useful, timely help gives **+1B total**, not per helper. Assistants normally need
**26% or more** in the relevant skill. Exceptional combined help can give **+2B**.

The native dice example shows identical candidates 25 and 65 at skills 70% and
60%, retaining both Bonus and Penalty choices. This merges draft modules 3-4.
