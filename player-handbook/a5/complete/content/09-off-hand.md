# Your Other Hand

**Two ready one-handed items give one extra option per round. Attack OR defend.**

## What qualifies?

Weapon + weapon; weapon + shield; weapon + free hand; two bare hands.
An empty hand counts as ready; a punch or kick can attack. A **two-handed item
does not qualify**: it leaves no free hand.

## Extra attack: follow immediately

After a **standard Close Combat or Unarmed Combat attack**, immediately make
one free attack with the other item, or unarmed with a free hand, at **-1P**.
Spend no Action or Reaction; add DM to both attacks normally. The first attack
does **not** need to hit.

## Extra defence: reserve the item

Keep the other item for one additional **Parry or Opportunity Attack at -1P**.
This extra Reaction **cannot Dodge**. Your base Reaction remains available
with either choice; a base shield Parry has **no inherent off-hand penalty**.
Other situational penalties still apply.

## One choice, not both

Using either extra option locks out the other until next round. An Opportunity
Attack, Charge or Set Weapon attack never produces an extra off-hand attack.
Forfeiting all Reactions also removes the extra Reaction. Only one Reaction
answers any one attack.

## Editorial notes

New art demonstrates dagger-to-shield contact after a sword swing. The two
annotation strips identify separate rolls; neither is a permanent item penalty.
