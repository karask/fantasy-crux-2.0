# Roll & Read the Dice

Say what you want to achieve and how you try. Roll when uncertainty, pressure or
danger makes failure matter. Routine and impossible actions need no roll.

## Two dice make D100

Choose **two ten-sided dice: tens and units**. Set Bonus/Penalty dice first (page 4).
Read the examples above; **two zeros mean 100**, never zero.

## Grade your result

**Check Fumbles first: they override success.** Skills cannot exceed 100%.

- **Critical:** 01 through one tenth of your skill, rounded down. Any skill
  above 0% has at least 01 as a Critical; a 0% skill has none.
- **Success:** at or below your skill, without a Critical or Fumble.
- **Failure:** above your skill, without a Fumble.
- **Fumble:** 99 or 100 below 100% skill; at 100% skill, only 100.

A Critical achieves your intent exceptionally well. A Fumble fails with a serious,
risk-related consequence. Combat Criticals: page 8. Lower is not always better:
compare **grade first** (page 4).

## Other dice and measures

**2D6+2:** total two six-sided dice, then add 2. D gives the die's sides.
Distances use metres; a grid square may represent 2 m.
Round ordinary fractions to the nearest whole number, **halves up**.
The Critical threshold specifically rounds **down**.
