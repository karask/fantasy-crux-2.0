# Close Weapons / A-L

Add **DM** to damage. Below either STR or DEX minimum: **attacks and Parries -1P**, not -2P for both.

## Hands and tags

**1H:** one hand. **2H:** two hands. **Flex:** two-handed use gives **+2 damage**
and **STR requirement 2 lower**. **Set:** brace against a Charge (page 10).
**Thrown:** use its ranged profile without an improvised-weapon penalty.

**Size:** determines Parry protection (page 7), not physical name or length.
**ENC:** burden. **SP:** Silver Pieces. A dash means no listed value.

## Flex example

Two-handed longsword: **1D8 + 2 + DM**, **STR 11 / DEX 11**. Still **Medium**;
using both hands prevents off-hand options. More close weapons: page 19.
