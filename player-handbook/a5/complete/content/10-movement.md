# Move, Charge & Set

**You must still have every listed Action and Reaction available.**

## Disengage or Withdraw

**Disengage:** Movement Action to leave; an enemy in reach may make an
Opportunity Attack.

**Withdraw:** Combat + Movement Actions; move normally without those Opportunity
Attacks. Keep your base Reaction.

## Sprint

Combat + Movement Actions: move up to **twice Movement**. Keep only your base
Reaction, and use it only to Dodge.

## Charge

Combat + Movement Actions; forfeit all Reactions. Move **5 m to twice Movement**
in a straight, clear route, end adjacent, then attack. A hit adds **+1D6 damage**.
No other movement or off-hand attack.

## Set Weapon against a Charge

Combat Action to set a ready **Set-tagged weapon**. A Charge into reach before
your next turn triggers one Close Combat attack at **+1B before the Charge**.

It works once. Any Action, Movement Action or Reaction before the trigger unsets it.

## Editorial notes

The illustration shows an open gap before impact and a straight route, not a
free attack outside the stated Set trigger. All source action restrictions retained.
