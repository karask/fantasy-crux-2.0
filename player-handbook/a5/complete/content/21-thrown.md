# Thrown Weapons & Whip

Use **Ranged Combat** at distance. Below either STR or DEX minimum: **attacks
and Parries -1P**, only once even if both are low.

| Weapon          | Type         | Damage | Range     | STR/DEX | ENC | Size   | Cost  |
| --------------- | ------------ | ------ | --------- | ------- | --- | ------ | ----- |
| Dagger          | Close/Thrown | 1D4+1  | STR m     | -/9     | -   | Light  | 20 SP |
| Dart            | Thrown       | 1D3    | STR m     | -/9     | -   | Light  | 15 SP |
| Hatchet         | Close/Thrown | 1D6    | STR m     | -/9     | 1   | Light  | 20 SP |
| Improvised rock | Thrown       | 1D4    | STR m     | 5/5     | 1   | Light  | -     |
| Javelin         | Thrown       | 1D6    | STR × 2 m | 5/9     | 1   | Medium | 20 SP |
| Shortspear      | Close/Thrown | 1D6    | STR × 2 m | 5/9     | 2   | Medium | 20 SP |
| Whip            | Close        | 1D3    | 5 m       | -/9     | -   | Light  | 50 SP |

## Read the tags

**Thrown:** ranged attacks add **DM** and retain the listed Size for Parry.
**Close:** within 2 m, use Close Combat and the listed damage. Melee damage
adds DM. A weapon without Close becomes an improvised **Light club, 1D6 + DM,
attacking at -1P** when used within 2 m.

**SP:** Silver Pieces. **ENC:** burden. Dash: no listed value. Dagger melee
requirements differ from this ranged profile; use the profile for the attack.

## Work out the distance

Up to Range: normal. Beyond it through twice Range: **-2P**. Farther: no attack.
With **STR 12**, a javelin has **24 m Range**: normal through 24 m, **-2P beyond
24 through 48 m**. Cover, Aim and defence: pages 12-13.

## Editorial notes
