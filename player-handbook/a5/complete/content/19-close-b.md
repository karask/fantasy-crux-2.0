## Read the profile

**1H / 2H:** one / two hands. **Flex:** two hands give **+2 damage** and a
**STR minimum 2 lower**, without changing Size. **Set:** brace against a Charge
(page 10). **Thrown:** use the ranged profile (page 21).
**SP:** Silver Pieces. **ENC:** burden. Dash: no listed value.

## Protection and improvisation

Small shield is **Medium**; medium shield **Heavy**; large shield **Huge**.
Compare these Sizes when Parrying, not the shield's everyday name (page 7).

Primitive or improvised versions of listed weapons deal **1 less damage**, to
a minimum of 1. A ranged weapon lacking Close has its own improvised use (page 12).
