# Skills at a Glance

Use your sheet's percentages. Routine competent work with time and suitable
tools usually needs no roll.

## Resist and fight

- **Dodge:** evade a perceived attack or physical hazard.
- **Persistence:** resist intimidation, coercion and fear.
- **Resilience:** endure fatigue, shock, deprivation, poison and disease.
- **Close Combat:** attack and Parry with melee weapons and shields.
- **Ranged Combat:** shoot or throw weapons at distant targets.
- **Unarmed Combat:** punch, kick and grapple.

## Know

- **Culture:** a society's history, geography, customs and beliefs.
- **Language:** communicate; 50% gives fluent everyday speech.
- **Lore:** specialist knowledge; successful preparation can give +1B to one
  directly related test soon afterwards.
- **Natural Lore:** nature, tracking, weather, food and shelter.

## Act

- **Athletics:** balance, climb, jump, run, swim and exert force.
- **Craft:** make and repair goods in a chosen trade.
- **Deception:** sneak, disguise, misdirect, conceal and pick pockets.
- **Driving:** handle carts, wagons and similar vehicles.
- **Engineering:** design, build, repair or sabotage large works.
- **Healing:** stabilise, treat injuries and perform Surgery (pages 16-17).
- **Influence:** persuade, bargain, command and intimidate.
- **Mechanisms:** locks, traps and intricate devices.
- **Perception:** notice hidden things, details and danger.
- **Performance:** act, dance, sing, speak or play music.
- **Riding:** control a mount in challenging circumstances.
- **Sailing:** operate boats and ships under oar or sail.
- **Streetwise:** urban rumours, contacts, illicit goods and discreet routes.
- **Trade:** appraise value and negotiate a sale.

Craft subjects, Cultures, Languages and Lore fields are separate skills.

## Editorial notes

All 24 mundane skills, in short descriptions. No skill bases or excluded subsystems.
