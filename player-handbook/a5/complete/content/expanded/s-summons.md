# Summon & Direct a Being

Only **one combat-capable summon or actively controlled subject**, regardless of Reach.

## A generic combat summon

| I   | HP  | AP  | Attack / defence | Damage |
| --- | --- | --- | ---------------- | ------ |
| 1   | 3   | 0   | 25%              | 1D3    |
| 2   | 6   | 1   | 40%              | 1D6    |
| 3   | 10  | 2   | 55%              | 1D8    |
| 4   | 14  | 3   | 70%              | 2D6    |

**Movement 15**, one Movement Action and one base Reaction. It acts immediately
after you. Defence covers Dodge, Resilience, Persistence and possible physical
Parry. No DM; it ignores Major Wounds and vanishes at 0 HP.

It may move, communicate and defend without commands. A consequential act costs
**your Combat Action and its corresponding Action**. Commands need **15 m and
line of effect**. It gains no extra Action from the command.

## Pay for special capabilities

Flight, incorporeality, invisibility, spellcasting, extra armour or special senses
are not included just because the description suggests them. They raise minimum
Intensity or require additional outcomes. Range sets generic arrival; Duration
and the active limit still apply.

## Calling a named being

Its profile sets the necessary Intensity; calling must also reach its location
or realm. An unwilling being resists with Persistence. **Calling is not control**:
binding/command is another resisted outcome. It remembers treatment and may
demand terms. After the magic ends, any willing stay gives no guaranteed service.

Created, summoned, transformed or compelled beings cannot summon, contribute PP,
create magical resources or pay ritual prices. Conjure·Flesh can animate remains
as temporary Mindless, Soulless undead; it restores no former identity or loyalty.

## Editorial notes

The generic player summon profile is necessary to use Conjure; no bestiary is reproduced.
