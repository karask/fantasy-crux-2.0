# Summon & Call a Being

Conjure creates a temporary being from its Form; calling brings an existing one.
Both obey a **standing order**. You can have only **one combat-capable summon or
actively controlled subject**, whatever the Reach.

## Standing orders

Give one short order when casting, such as "guard me" or "hold this door". The
summon acts right after you, spending its own Actions and base Reaction on it.
Changing the order costs your **Movement Action**, once per round, within **15 m**
and line of effect. Out of range, or while you are unconscious, it keeps its last
order. It needs Duration, not concentration, and ends at 0 HP, at Duration's
end, on dismissal or by Dispel.

## A conjured being

| I   | HP  | AP  | Attack / defence | Damage | Size   |
| --- | --- | --- | ---------------- | ------ | ------ |
| 1   | 3   | 0   | 25%              | 1D3    | Light  |
| 2   | 6   | 1   | 40%              | 1D6    | Medium |
| 3   | 10  | 2   | 55%              | 1D8    | Medium |
| 4   | 14  | 3   | 70%              | 2D6    | Heavy  |

Movement 15, PP 0, no DM; it ignores Major Wounds and vanishes at 0 HP. Every
conjured being is Soulless, and its **Form** sets its body, tags, one trait and
one drawback. A mind, or removing the drawback, costs **+1 I** each. Flight,
invisibility or spellcasting cost more. Spirit, Paths and Fate conjure none.

## Call a being

Only beings bound to a Form, or linked to you by name, token or blood, answer.
Intensity equals its **Threat Intensity**. An unwilling being resists with
Persistence or **20 × Threat Intensity %**, whichever is higher. Win and it arrives
bound under a standing order; lose and you still pay. On a Fumble, it may arrive
free and hostile. Summoned beings cannot summon, give PP or pay ritual prices.
