# Alchemy & Poison

Each Talent makes **one dose per day**, spending **50 SP of materials**. A dose
keeps its potency for **one month**. Decide its effect when brewing it.

## Alchemist

Choose one:

- **Antidote:** +2B to one resistance test against a named poison or disease.
- **Salve:** restore **1D6 HP** over an hour's rest, once per patient per day.
- **Alchemical fire:** ready Light flask; Ranged Combat, **Range 10 m**, **1D6
  fire damage**, no DM. Normal Reactions apply. A miss wastes it; a Fumble can
  use the usual crowd-hit consequence.

## Poisoner

The poison's **Potency is half your Lore (Alchemy)**. Choose one Effect:
**1D6 HP damage**, **-2 to one characteristic**, or **unconsciousness**.
An ongoing Effect lasts **1D6 × 10 minutes**.

Choose **smeared**, Delay **1 round**, or **ingested**, Delay **1D6 minutes**.
Applying a smeared dose to a suitable cutting/piercing weapon costs a Combat
Action. It is spent on the **first hit that deals HP damage**, or after one hour.
Ingested poison activates when consumed.

The victim gets the normal **single Resilience-versus-Potency test** after the
Delay. Re-exposure to the same currently active poison neither stacks nor
grants another resistance. Treatment and exceptions: {{ref:hazards}}.

## Editorial notes

This is player manufacture and use; no general GM poison catalogue is included.
