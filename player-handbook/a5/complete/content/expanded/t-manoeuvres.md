# Change the Fight

Declare the Talent before rolling. Each uses your **Combat Action** and replaces
damage when its hit is **not stopped**; see {{ref:improvement}}.

## Disarm

Make a Close Combat attack. On an unstopped hit, deal **no damage**; one chosen
held item lands **1D6 metres away**. It must be something the target can release,
not fused, fixed or securely fastened equipment.

## Trip

Make a Close Combat or Unarmed Combat attack. On an unstopped hit, deal
**no damage** and leave the target **prone**. Impossible Size or anatomy can
prevent the attempt.

## Shield Rush

Attack with Close Combat and a ready shield. On an unstopped hit, deal
**no damage** and push the target **2 metres**. It then tests **Athletics -1P**
or falls prone. Impossible Size or anatomy can prevent the attempt.

## Editorial notes

No free manoeuvres are added to ordinary Critical hits. Each is a purchased action Talent.
