# Make Magic Wait

A Trigger stores **one already-built effect** until a declared observable event.
It is not a way to add damage to every hit or choose a target later.

## Trigger

Add **+1 M plus a second Duration rating for the wait**. The effect still has
its own Duration. State the event when building it; hidden qualities require Scry.

An ordinary casting arms it immediately: fix **target, cell, outcomes, Range,
Reach and all other dials**, then check target/route. These choices cannot change
when it fires. Counter happens at casting; normal target defence happens when
it fires, against the **stored Shaping result**.

For an Activated/Charged item, activation arms one use and fixes its target and
permitted choices. Pay PP or spend the charge then, even if the wait expires.
The item's observable event was fixed when made.

## One hit can be the event

A successful weapon hit on the **already chosen target** may trigger magic.
Resolve the weapon, then the separate Shaping with its normal defence/protection.
It inherits no DM or weapon-Critical benefits. A miss leaves the Trigger waiting.

Damage cannot retaliate for an attack against or harm suffered by the caster,
bearer or another protected subject. It cannot repeat on later weapon hits.

## Example: one prepared follow-up

"When I next hit **that ogre** this scene, sear it for 2D6." Unmake·Fire,
**I2 + R1 + Direct1 + Trigger1 + wait D1 = M6**; effect D0, Reach0.
The hit fires it once; the ogre still opposes the Direct Harm with **Resilience**.
If the scene ends without the hit, the paid Trigger expires.

## Editorial notes

The named-ogre example uses the current canonical M6 build.
