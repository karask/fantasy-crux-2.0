# Counter & Casting Training

These are purchased exceptions. Merely reaching an expertise tier does not
grant its similarly named Talent.

## Counter

Perceive a successful Shaping cast within **15 m**, or its projection entering
within 15 m. Before defence/effect, spend your **base Reaction**; you need
current PP at least its full M. Oppose Shaping against the **original result**;
you must succeed and win to stop it.

Pay **full M either way**; a Fumble also causes Backlash at that M. Only one
Counter can answer a Shaping, and Counter cannot be Countered.

**Counter replaces your own defence.** If it fails to stop the Shaping, you
cannot then Dodge, guard, or roll Resilience/Persistence against it. Another
character's failed Counter leaves your own defence available.

## Steady Casting

Before ordinary cancellation, ignore **one total Penalty die** caused by
Wounded or grappling on Shaping tests. Both conditions together still remove
only one. Other penalties remain; prevented Practice still prevents casting.

## Shaping Expertise

**Once per round**, declare before a Shaping casting roll. After ordinary
cancellation, before the cap, remove one remaining Penalty; never gain a Bonus.
This does not change M, Safe M, maximum I, ritual requirements or Backlash risk.
No benefit to Sense Magic, Dispel or Counter; purchase only once.

If both training Talents apply, use Steady Casting first, then ordinary
cancellation, then Shaping Expertise.

## Editorial notes

Uses current working Shaping Expertise and Steady Casting sources without changing them.
