# Innate Abilities

Buy your ancestry's whole package from starting IP. **Humans buy none and keep
all 10 IP.** Other Talents remain normal purchases if you meet their requirements.

## Elf: 5 IP

**Low-Light Sight (3):** Partial darkness counts as Illuminated; Darkness counts
as Partial darkness. Pitch black and obscurity do not change.

**Terrain Expertise: Forest (2):** remove one remaining Penalty die from Natural
Lore in or about forests, after cancellation and before the cap. This racial
purchase waives the usual Natural Lore prerequisite. It never creates a Bonus.

## Dwarf: 7 IP

**Blind Sight: heat (5):** perceive heat-contrasting targets regardless of light.
Solid barriers, heavy insulation and ambient-temperature targets defeat it.
No colour, writing, fine visual detail or eye contact.

**Earth Sense (2):** touching connected earth or stone reveals approximate depth
and whether the surrounding chamber is structurally unstable.

## Orc: 4 IP

**Adrenaline Surge:** once per combat, immediately after becoming Wounded or
Fatigued, ignore both conditions' test, Movement and Combat Order penalties
through the end of your next turn. Ignore the first **1 HP of Bleeding** in
that time. Bleeding remains; Exhausted, unconsciousness and Dying are unaffected.

## Goblin: 5 IP

**Blind Sight: heat:** as for Dwarves. Close-Quarters Knack is optional, not free.

## Lizardfolk: 6 IP

**Scaled Hide (4):** **1 natural AP, no ENC**, added to worn armour. Use that
combined physical AP or Ward AP, whichever is higher, never both.

**Counterbalancing Tail (2):** tests primarily intended to knock you prone,
throw you or force you to move suffer **-1P**.

## Editorial notes

Innate IP costs and exceptions are copied from the player racial packages.
