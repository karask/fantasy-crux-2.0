# Calculate Your Attributes

Use your final characteristics. Round to the nearest whole number, **halves up**.
Calculate maximum HP first, then use that rounded maximum to calculate MWL.

## Numbers to put on your sheet

| Attribute                 | Calculation                                    |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Maximum Hit Points / HP   | (SIZ + CON) / 2                                |
| Major Wound Level / MWL   | Maximum HP / 2                                 |
| Maximum Power Points / PP | POW                                            |
| Human Movement            | 15 metres                                      |
| Combat Order              | (DEX + INT) / 2, then subtract worn armour ENC |
| Carrying capacity         | STR + SIZ ENC                                  |

Record **current and maximum** HP/PP separately. Injury never lowers MWL.
Add any Talent adjustment to Combat Order; do not subtract armour twice.
For an unusual ancestry, confirm any different Movement with the GM.

## Damage Modifier: STR + SIZ

| Total | DM   | Total           | DM           |
| ----- | ---- | --------------- | ------------ |
| 1-10  | -1D6 | 31-45           | +1D6         |
| 11-15 | -1D4 | 46-60           | +2D6         |
| 16-25 | +0   | 61-75           | +3D6         |
| 26-30 | +1D4 | Each further 15 | Another +1D6 |

Add DM to **melee, unarmed and thrown** damage, including a negative DM.
Bows, crossbows, slings and Shaping do not normally add it.

## Power is a resource

At **0 PP**, a living character falls unconscious until PP returns. Rest restores
one quarter of maximum PP per complete **2 hours**, rounded normally; **8 hours
restores all**. PP matters even if you use a magical item rather than cast.

## Editorial notes

The complete creation example checks HP, MWL, PP, Combat Order, capacity and DM.
