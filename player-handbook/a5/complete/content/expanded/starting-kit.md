# Finish & Equip Yourself

You start with **10 IP**, minus any innate package, plus IP gained by trading
skill points. Spend or save them. **Every PC begins with 2 Hero Points.**

## Spend starting IP

**1 IP:** improve a skill, using {{ref:improvement}}. Only normal starting IP can
do this; do not repeatedly buy the same skill's starting improvement.
**5 IP:** raise a characteristic by 1, not SIZ; creation caps still apply.
**Listed cost:** buy a Talent whose prerequisites you meet.

When you buy a characteristic improvement, update derived attributes; existing
skill scores do not change. Track each expenditure.

## Choose one free equipment package

Both give **leather armour, one ranged weapon and a dagger**. Add either:

- **One two-handed close-combat weapon**, or
- **One shield and one one-handed close-combat weapon**.

Check STR/DEX requirements. A Flex weapon can be used with one or two hands;
ask the GM which starting package it fills. Weapons and armour: {{ref:close-a}}-{{num:armour}}.

## Pack and purse

Also take a **backpack, 10 m rope, two weeks of travelling provisions, flint
and tinder, and a waterskin**. Roll **4D6 × 10 SP** for starting money; the GM
can allow purchases before play. Confirm ammunition and any specialist kit.

**100 CP = 10 SP = 1 GP.** Check the website equipment list for additional goods
and local availability; free starting equipment does not spend your silver.

## Final check

Name, appearance, background, personality, loyalties and an immediate goal.
Humans normally start aged **18-28**, or roll **2D6 + 16**. Note weapons, AP,
carried ENC, armour-adjusted Combat Order, full HP/PP and **2 Hero Points**.

## Editorial notes

Starting purchases use existing improvement rules and the initial IP restrictions.
