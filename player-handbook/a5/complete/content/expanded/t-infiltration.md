# Infiltration & Fine Work

Removing a Penalty happens **after ordinary cancellation, before the cap**.
It never creates a Bonus die or removes a penalty outside the stated scope.

## Deception Expertise

Choose **disguise, hiding objects, picking pockets, stealth or misdirection**.
Remove one remaining Penalty die from that use of Deception. Buy again only
for different applications; record each choice.

## Cutpurse

Pick a pocket, cut a purse or plant a small object in **one Combat Action**,
instead of the usual minute. Oppose **Deception against Perception**.
Failure goes unnoticed unless you Fumble or the missing item is too large to overlook.

## Lockbreaker

Open a lock or disable a trap in **one Action**, instead of an extended attempt.
A resistant device still opposes with its maker's Mechanisms. A Fumble against
a trap springs it.

## Close-Quarters Knack

Remove one remaining Penalty die from **Athletics or Mechanisms** when it is
caused specifically by cramped surroundings. Darkness, footing, inadequate
tools and enemies are not covered.

## Editorial notes

The time-saving Talents do not turn an opposed device or target into an automatic success.
