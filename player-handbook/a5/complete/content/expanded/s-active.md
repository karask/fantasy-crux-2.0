# Keep Magic Active

Total ongoing **Magnitude cannot exceed permanent, unmodified INT**. Record
each effect's **M, Duration and casting result**. Dismiss on your turn without an Action.

## Passive or actively directed?

**Passive benefit:** needs Duration, no maintenance Action. Examples: armour,
subject-directed flight, fixed senses. A Critical still counts **full M**.
An instant effect adds no ongoing M once resolved.

**Concentration / active control:** your Combat Action each round, only one;
keep the subject within Range and a valid route. Damage or serious distraction
forces Persistence or maintenance ends. Summon commands instead use 15 m and
line of effect. Fixed-duration passive magic normally checks route only when set.

## Sense Magic: no cell needed

Combat Action + **1 PP**; Shaping test for active magic within **15 m**. Success
gives location/Form; Critical adds Technique, M and state. Failure: nothing;
Fumble: false impression and obvious Tell, not Backlash. Veiled magic opposes
with its original result. Practice and Tell still apply.

## Dispel: no cell needed

Combat Action; **M = target's full M + Range**, Reach 0 for one Shaping even if
it covers an area. Cast/pay normally. Then you must win an opposed test against a
fresh **Shaping check rolled for the original caster**, using their current unmodified
skill, not the stored casting roll. If unknown, use 10 × original M, capped 100%.

Active anchored enchantments can be Dispelled. Completed permanent transformations
cannot: reversing them needs a new appropriate Shaping.

## Editorial notes

Explicitly distinguishes Dispel's fresh opposition from escape against a stored result.
