# Make an Enchantment

Build a Shaping and bind it to a **vulnerable physical or spiritual anchor**
during a ritual. Duration **4** is ordinary enduring work; **5** is Mythic and
also needs its lasting price. Your expertise still limits ritual M and outcome I.

## Enchanter

Gain **Enchantment Capacity equal to permanent, unmodified POW**. A bound
effect moves its full M into this capacity instead of occupying your PP commitment
or active M. It persists without you. Pay the PP for each Charged use when binding it.

**Continuous/Activated:** count full final M once per effect. **Charged:** count
full M **for every unused charge**. Each separately bound layer counts. Every
effect stays attributed to its original creator, even after sale, gift or theft.
Helpers never inherit its capacity cost. Mythic effects have no exemption.

Creation, restoration and increases must fit capacity. Only a **permanently ended
effect** frees capacity: Dispel, destroyed anchor or spent charge. Reusable
activation and temporary suppression free none.

If permanent POW loss leaves you over capacity, items persist, but you can create,
restore or increase none until the total fits again.

## Example: count uses, not objects

**POW 12:** one Activated M4 effect uses 4 capacity. Two stored M3 charges use
another 6, for **10 total**. A third M3 charge would make 13 and cannot fit.
Using one M3 charge frees 3 capacity; using the Activated item frees none.

## Without Enchanter

Duration 4 **and** 5 enchantments commit the maker's full M from maximum PP and
active M; a Mythic price is extra.

**One-use items** (potions, scrolls) skip Duration 4: price the effect with its own
Duration, plus 1 for binding; a **1D4 healing potion is M2**. Pay its PP when binding;
until used it commits like any enchantment, or uses capacity with Enchanter.

## Editorial notes

Creation is a player option; no treasure generation or GM item catalogue is reproduced.
