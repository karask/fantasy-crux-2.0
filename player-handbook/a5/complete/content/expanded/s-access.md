# Become a Shaper

Shaping builds magic from **what you affect** and **what you do to it**, rather
than giving every caster the same fixed spell list.

## Shaping

Gain the **Shaping Knowledge skill**, base **INT + POW**, one specialty (a Form
or Technique), and **two known cells containing that specialty**. You also gain
Sense Magic and Dispel. Without this Talent, you cannot cast.

**Example:** choose Fire as your specialty, then learn **Unmake·Fire** and
**Ward·Fire**. This grants neither Conjure·Fire nor any other Unmake cell.

## Beginning play with magic

Reserve and pay **20 IP before allocating skills**; you may then put up to
30 Knowledge points into Shaping. The full-price trade example is on {{ref:skill-points}}.
With INT 14 and POW 13, base 27% plus 30 points gives **Shaping 57%**.

## Define Practice and Tell

**Practice:** observable and interruptible speech, gesture, focus, prayer or
similar requirement. If prevented, you cannot cast.
**Tell:** the unavoidable magical sign around you and any ranged target/area.
Watchers notice it; it is also noticeable within **10 × M metres, minimum 3 m**.
It reveals magic, not its purpose. Only Veiled can conceal it.

## Learn more later

New cell containing your specialty: **2 IP**. Other cell: **4 IP**, needing a
significant ordeal or master. Suitable experience is required. Shaping skill
improves normally; new cells do not raise it.

## Editorial notes

The starting Shaping arithmetic is illustrative, not a second partially specified character.
