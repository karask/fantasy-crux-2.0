# Words, Trade & Leadership

Penalty removal comes after cancellation, before the cap, and creates no Bonus.

## Silver Tongue

Remove one remaining Penalty from Influence to **persuade or bargain**. You
need time to talk and an audience willing to listen. Never applies to threats.

## Commanding Presence

Remove one remaining Penalty from Influence to **command or threaten**, including
Intimidate. The target must make you out and have something to lose. This changes
your test, not modifiers on its resistance. No persuasion or bargaining benefit.

## Rally

Combat Action: choose **two allies** who hear and understand you. Each gets
**+1B to their next single test** before their next turn ends. Unused benefits
expire; a new Rally replaces an unused one. No stacking and no self-Rally.

## Merchant's Eye

Know an ordinary item's true worth on sight. A settlement offers the selection
of **one size larger**: village as town, town as city. Cities improve no further;
genuinely unavailable goods do not appear.

## Tactician

An army you command takes **half normal battle casualties**. When your beaten
force passes its rout test, it may keep fighting rather than withdraw; other
commanders need a Critical. The GM handles the battle procedure.

## Editorial notes

Tactician's player benefit is included; the GM's mass-combat subsystem is not.
