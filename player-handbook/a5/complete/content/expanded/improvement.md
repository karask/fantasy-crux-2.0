# Improve & Buy Talents

Spend earned **Improvement Points between adventures**. Unspent IP carry forward.
The GM awards them for progress and achievements; three months of suitable
practice/research may earn 1 IP with approval.

## Buy an improvement

| Spend                        | Gain                                                |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 IP, skill currently 0-50%  | +5 percentage points.                               |
| 1 IP, skill currently 51-99% | +3 percentage points.                               |
| 5 IP                         | +1 characteristic, not SIZ; respect racial maximum. |
| Talent's listed IP           | Buy it if you meet its prerequisites.               |

Improve each skill **at most once between adventures**. Use its score before
the gain; cap at 100%. **50 -> 55; 51 -> 54; 98 -> 100.**

After a characteristic increase, recalculate attributes, including a Shaper's
active limit. **Skills keep their scores**; their bases only seed creation.
New Shaping cells have separate costs on {{ref:shaper}}.

## How to read a Talent

Pay once and record it. Meet skill/characteristic prerequisites when buying;
listed equipment is a requirement when using it. The catalogue starts on {{ref:weapon-training}};
magical Talents appear beside the Shaping rules.

**Passive:** applies when its conditions hold. **Action:** spends your Combat
Action. **Reaction:** spends the Reaction its text names.

Only **one action Talent** may modify an attack. That attack is not a standard
attack for an extra off-hand attack. Passive Talents still apply when eligible.
Forfeiting **all Reactions** includes both base and extra Reactions.

## When a Talent says "not stopped"

An ordinary hit is stopped by a winning Dodge or full-block Parry; partial
Parry does not stop it. A **Critical hit needs a winning Critical Reaction** to stop it.
Apply partial Parry before armour if the Talent deals damage. Replacement
damage/effects follow the Talent, not the normal Critical benefits.

## Editorial notes

This is player expenditure and usage guidance, not GM award advice.
