# Learn Shaping Adjustments

Buy the Talent once; its **added Magnitude is paid in each build that uses it**.
Additional distinct outcomes within one known cell need no adjustment Talent.

## Selective

**+1 M:** name subjects within an area whom the effect excludes. Without it,
an area affects everyone in it, including allies.

## Indirect

**+1 M per barrier crossed**, where the Form permits. Supplies the route for
Range 1-3, concentration/control, or casting past complete cover.

## Veiled

**+1 M and -1P to Shaping:** conceal the Tell. Noticing it requires Perception
opposed by Shaping. Practice stays interruptible; someone detecting the Tell
in time can Counter. Without this Talent, a Tell cannot be concealed.

## Confluence

**+1 M:** combine **exactly two known cells**, each contributing a distinct
permitted outcome. Pay every outcome's I; count shared dials once. One Combat
Action, roll, PP payment and Tell.

Each outcome keeps its own delivery, targets, defence and protection. Same-defence
outcomes against one subject share a defence roll; different defences/subjects
do not. Counter stops the whole Shaping; Dispel ends the whole ongoing Shaping.
One outcome may depend on another succeeding, but never earns a discount/refund.
