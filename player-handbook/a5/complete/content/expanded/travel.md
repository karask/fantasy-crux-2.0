# Travel & Find Your Way

Daily rates assume **ten hours of travel in fair conditions**. Agree the route
and pace with the group; the GM accounts for difficult conditions and interruptions.

## Choose a pace

| Pace        | Per day | Cost or limit                                          |
| ----------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| March       | 30 km   | Remain ready to fight; no end-of-day Fatigue test.     |
| Hike        | 50 km   | On foot, no wagons/animals; Fatigue test at day's end. |
| Ride slowly | 30 km   | Walking mounts, pack animals or wagons.                |
| Ride        | 50 km   | Casual riding.                                         |
| Ride fast   | 90 km   | No wagons; rider and mount test Fatigue at day's end.  |

Multiply by the worst substantial terrain: **road/path 100%; light brush 80%;
medium brush, light woods or rolling hills 70%; heavy woodland 50%**.
If arrival order matters, oppose Athletics on foot or Riding when mounted.

## Navigate, track and forage

**Navigation:** Natural Lore once per uncertain travel day, and again after a
material route change. Success: full progress. Failure: lose a quarter of that
day's progress. Fumble: no progress, and become lost.

**Tracking:** find the trail, then test every **10 minutes** of uncertain following.
**Food, water or shelter:** when a necessity is unavailable, make one Natural
Lore test per day to find it before deprivation takes effect.

## Editorial notes

Encounter generation, campaign maps and travel adjudication stay with the GM.
