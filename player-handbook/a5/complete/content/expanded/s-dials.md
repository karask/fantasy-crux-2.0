# Duration, Reach & Examples

Add each chosen rating **once** to Magnitude. **R** marks a ritual-only rating;
any rating of 5 also requires a declared lasting mythic price.

## Duration: how long

| Rating | Duration                                              |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| 0      | Instant, or through the affected subject's next turn. |
| 1      | One scene or 10 minutes.                              |
| 2      | Until the target next fully rests, maximum 8 hours.   |
| 3 R    | One week.                                             |
| 4 R    | Indefinite until dismissed or Dispelled.              |
| 5 R    | Permanent.                                            |

Duration sustains state; **never repeats damage, healing or a lost Action**.

## Reach: use the highest requirement

| Rating | Count / radius; largest subject          |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| 0      | One; person, door or human-sized object. |
| 1      | 2 / 3 m; wagon or large tree.            |
| 2      | 5 / 10 m; room-sized structure.          |
| 3 R    | 20 / 50 m; building or ship.             |
| 4 R    | 100 / 500 m; settlement.                 |
| 5 R    | Army, city or region; landscape.         |

Areas include allies unless Selective. Direct Harm uses **selected targets, not a radius**.

## Two ready builds

**Firebolt at 15 m:** Unmake·Fire, I2 + R1 + D0 + Reach0 = **M3**. Deal **2D6
Projected Harm** once; normal armour applies.

**Ward two allies at 15 m for a scene:** Ward·Earth/Stone,
I2 + R1 + D1 + Reach1 = **M5**. Grant **4 AP against physical blows**.
At Shaping 60%, the first is safe; the second is **Safe + 2: -2P**.
Ordinary success costs 3 PP or 5 PP respectively.

## Editorial notes

All six ratings are available as player choices; ritual procedures remain separate.
