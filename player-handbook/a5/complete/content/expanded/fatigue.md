# Load, Fatigue & Necessities

You cannot solve every problem by carrying more or pushing on without rest.

## Count what you carry

Add weapons, armour, containers and contents. Capacity is **STR + SIZ ENC**.
Over capacity: **-1P to physical tests and Fatigue tests; half Movement**.
You cannot carry more than **twice capacity**. Every 20 negligible items in a
suitable container count as 1 ENC; containers do not erase their contents' load.

## Fatigue: test Resilience

The GM calls for a Fatigue test after strenuous activity, or during it if
exhaustion could prevent completion. Failure while fresh makes you Fatigued;
failure on another strenuous Fatigue test at -1P makes you Exhausted.

| State     | All tests | Movement       | Combat Order |
| --------- | --------- | -------------- | ------------ |
| Fatigued  | -1P       | Three-quarters | -3           |
| Exhausted | -2P       | Half           | -6           |

Use the current state, not both rows. A Fatigue Fumble means unconsciousness
for **3D6 minutes**, then wake Fatigued. **20 - CON hours** of complete rest,
minimum 1, removes either state. Reduced Movement is at least 1 metre.

## Food, water and shelter

Grace periods: **shelter CON hours; food CON days; water CON × 2 hours**.
Water grace becomes CON hours in arid conditions, **CON / 2** in extreme heat.
After three days without food, Fatigue tests take -1P even within the grace period.

After a grace period: **one Fatigue test at -1P and 1D6 damage per day for each
active deprivation**. That damage cannot heal naturally until you remedy its
cause. Magical removal of Fatigue does not supply food, water or shelter.

## Editorial notes

Movement fractions use the ordinary rounding rule; the minimum is retained.
