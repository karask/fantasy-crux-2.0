# Illusions, Paths & Fate

These methods can look similar to other magic, but have different targets and limits.

## Hide a subject or change a mind

**I2:** -1P to detect through one sense. **I3:** imperceptible through one sense.
**I4:** every sense the cell governs. Impairing a perceiver uses the same grades.
I3+ sight loss uses Pitch black attack/vision limits; general blindness also
quarters Movement, while invisibility does not darken the surroundings.

**Conjure·Mind:** perceive something absent. **Alter·Mind:** change/omit what is
present. Reach counts **each affected mind**; each resists Persistence. Later
observers, devices and Mindless beings perceive normally. Illusions never harm.
A fooled witness may spend an Action to oppose Perception with stored Shaping;
winning identifies falsehood, not hidden matter. Failure needs new evidence.
Fixed illusions need no maintenance; directing behaviour costs the caster's Action.

## Paths: safe destinations only

**Bend·Paths I2:** move a willing subject, or unwilling one up to 3 m. **I3:**
unwilling relocation within Range; **I4** if relocation alone removes it from
the encounter. Unwilling subjects resist Resilience.

**Conjure·Paths I3+:** stationary one-way portal. Crossing costs a Movement
Action and spends its Reach allowance. Range reaches both ends; arrival must
be empty, stable, large enough and safe from unavoidable harm/confinement.
No free collision, falling, restraint or damage. Invalid arrival moves nobody.

## Fate: before uncertainty resolves

**I1:** non-numerical omen/coincidence. **I2: +1B/-1P; I4: +2B/-2P** on a named
test or narrow endeavour. Later events require Trigger; hostile Fate faces
Persistence. Vows do not compel choice. No HP damage, rerolls, automatic success,
chosen dice, extra actions or retroactive changes. Predictions are possibilities.
