# Brawl, Wrestle & Subdue

Unarmed damage still adds DM. Improved strikes affect ordinary bodily attacks,
not the listed damage or Size of a natural weapon.

## Iron Fist

Your punches, kicks and comparable strikes deal **1D4 + DM** and count as
**Medium**, replacing normal 1D3 and Light.

## Master Brawler

Your ordinary bodily strikes deal **1D6 + DM** and count as **Heavy**, replacing
Iron Fist's values. A Medium Parry now blocks only half an ordinary hit.

## Wrestler

While already grappling, spend a Combat Action and win opposed **Unarmed Combat**.
Choose: deal normal unarmed damage (armour applies); drop one held item at the
target's feet; or throw it to an adjacent space, **prone**, ending the grapple.
Impossible Size or anatomy may prevent an outcome.

## Subdue

Make one blunt Close or Unarmed Combat attack. If not stopped, roll listed
damage + DM, then partial Parry and armour. If this reaches the target's **MWL**,
it becomes stably unconscious with **no HP loss**. Otherwise it stays conscious
and loses only the weapon's **minimum listed damage**, reduced by partial Parry
and armour; no DM in that fallback.

Talent damage bonuses affect neither calculation. The target wakes at scene's
end, immediately on damage, or after a successful Healing Combat Action.

## Editorial notes

The threshold and fallback are replacement outcomes, not normal damaging Criticals.
