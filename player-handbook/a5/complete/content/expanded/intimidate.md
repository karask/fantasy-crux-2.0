# Intimidate

**A Combat Action to demand surrender or drive enemies off.**

## Make the demand

State your demand to one enemy or a group. Oppose **Influence against Persistence**;
a group rolls once with its leader's Persistence or Influence, whichever is higher.
Give the enemy's roll the **one** modifier that fits best:

| Modifier | Enemy's condition                                      |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **+2B**  | Full strength, or only minor wounds                    |
| **+1B**  | Outnumbers your side but has lost 20% of numbers or HP |
| **-1P**  | Fewer than your side and already wounded               |
| **-2P**  | Has lost over half its HP, or half the group is down   |

If you win, the GM judges how it gives way:
wounded enemies may flee or surrender, while healthy ones rarely just flee and more
often hesitate and lose a turn. A strong enemy surrenders only to a Critical against
a failed Persistence; a fumbled Persistence breaks it.

You need no shared language, only to make yourself understood by sign, gesture
or bearing. The GM decides who can be targeted and whether it is possible.
