# Harm, Heal & Protect

Use the lowest I that buys the result. Your cell must explain the method;
its description never gives free damage, protection or extra actions.

## Common outcomes

| Outcome                  | What the Intensity buys                                                                                               |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Harm                     | Unmake: **I D6**. No DM. Choose Projected or Direct delivery before M.                                                |
| Heal                     | Alter·Flesh: **I D4 HP**, up to maximum.                                                                              |
| Ward                     | **2 × I AP** against one defined class of harm. Use it or physical armour, whichever protects better; never add them. |
| Barrier                  | **2 × I AP; 5 × I total HP**. Reach sets footprint.                                                                   |
| Vitality reserve         | **3 × I** points absorb qualifying HP damage after other protection, before HP/MWL.                                   |
| Skill / defence modifier | **I2: +1B / -1P. I4: +2B / -2P.** Name one skill or narrow family.                                                    |

## What healing fixes

Healing also removes **Bleeding and Dying**. Above 0 HP, act next turn. **I3+**
counts as Surgery; Wounded ends after treatment when HP exceeds MWL.
Healing never repeats through Duration and creates no regeneration pool.

Poison/disease curing needs **I × 25 at least Potency**, then Shaping must win
against Potency. After failure, the same Shaper needs full rest, new treatment,
discovery or another meaningful change before retrying.

## Protection has limits

Keep only the strongest same-kind magical modifier/protection. A reserve cannot
refill; keep only the larger of a new reserve or existing remaining points.
It changes neither maximum HP nor MWL and absorbs no PP damage or paid sacrifice.

A solid barrier blocks movement and route, never appears through occupied space.
Trapping an unwilling subject is hostile restraint: it chooses Dodge or Resilience.
