# Cast & Pay the Cost

**One Combat Action.** Keep your Movement and eligible Reactions. You need
Practice, Tell, a valid target/route, room in your active limit and PP for **full M**.

## Resolve in order

Agree cell, outcomes, M and defence. Roll Shaping. Resolve **Counter, then
defence**; pay PP, then apply the effect. A defence stopping it does not refund
the casting cost. A Critical keeps its grade, never maximises magical effect dice.

## Risk and payment

**Up to Safe M:** no Magnitude penalty. **Safe + 1: -1P. Safe + 2: -2P**.
Higher M requires a ritual. Removing/cancelling penalties never removes overreach.

| Result              | PP and consequence                                       |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Critical            | Pay **half M**, rounded up; resolve defence normally.    |
| Success             | Pay **full M**.                                          |
| Failure within Safe | No effect; lose **1 PP**.                                |
| Failed overreach    | No effect; lose **half M** and suffer Backlash.          |
| Fumble              | No intended effect; lose **full M** and suffer Backlash. |

Hero Point rerolls happen **before PP loss or Backlash**. At 0 PP, the casting
resolves first, then living Shapers fall unconscious and concentration ends.
PP recovery is on {{ref:attributes}}.

## Hostile touch

First make a **no-damage Unarmed Combat delivery attack** in that Action;
normal Reactions apply. No Talent or off-hand attack. If stopped, spend the
Action but no PP and make no Shaping roll. Contact then needs casting and normal
resistance. A delivery Critical adds nothing; partial Parry does not stop contact.

## Backlash and Tricks

The GM resolves Backlash: harm, extra PP loss, a stray effect or Fatigue.
**I0 is cosmetic only**. A calm M0 Trick is free and automatic; under pressure,
failure does nothing and Fumble gives an inconvenient Tell, not PP loss or Backlash.

## Editorial notes

Backlash outcomes are a player risk reminder; its random adjudication table stays GM-facing.
