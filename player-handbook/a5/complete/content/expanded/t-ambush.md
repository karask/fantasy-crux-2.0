# Strike From an Opening

Being behind someone is not the same as being unseen. These Talents distinguish
position, concealment and genuine lack of awareness.

## Silent Step

While moving at **half Movement or less**, Perception tests to notice you take
**-1P**, whether opposed to Deception or rolled independently. It hides neither
companions nor a light you carry.

## Killing Angle

Add **1D4 damage to one Close or Unarmed attack per round** against a target
you flank, strike from behind, or that is unaware of you. No ranged benefit.
It adds nothing to a hit that remains Critical, including one created by Ambusher.
If an ordinary successful Dodge or Parry demotes it to an ordinary hit, the bonus
applies when the positional condition and once-per-round limit permit.

## Master Assassin

Where Killing Angle applies, increase its bonus to **2D4 total**. Still only
one attack per round, and nothing on a hit that remains Critical. Both dice apply
to an eligible hit demoted to ordinary.

## Ambusher

Declare it before your **first attack of the scene**, from concealment against
a target genuinely unaware of you. An ordinary hit becomes a **Critical**;
an already Critical hit gains nothing further.

Use Close, Ranged or Unarmed Combat, not Shaping. Being distracted, outnumbered
or merely surprised is not enough. This is an action Talent: no extra off-hand
follow-up.

## Editorial notes

The Critical exclusion applies only to hits that remain Critical after defence.
