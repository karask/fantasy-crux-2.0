# Build an Effect

Say: **using [known cell], I cause [outcome], at [Range], affecting [Reach],
for [Duration]**. The GM confirms the build and defence before you roll.

## Choose the outcome first

**Intensity (I)** buys strength: choose a benchmark on {{ref:outcomes}}-{{num:conditions}}.
**I2 healing restores 2D4 HP per target.** Then set Range, Duration and Reach.

Pay each distinct outcome's I and shared dials once. **Confluence** permits two
cells. Never stack an outcome on one subject to evade I limits.

## Your skill limits both strength and safety

| Shaping | Expertise  | Maximum I / Safe M |
| ------- | ---------- | ------------------ |
| 0-25%   | Novice     | 1 / 1              |
| 26-50%  | Apprentice | 2 / 2              |
| 51-75%  | Veteran    | 3 / 3              |
| 76-99%  | Expert     | 4 / 4              |
| 100%    | Master     | 5 / 5              |

Every outcome stays within maximum I. Ordinary casting reaches **Safe M + 2**
with overreach risk. More PP, Talents and rituals never raise maximum I.
I5 itself always requires a ritual.

## Range: how far

**0:** self/touch. **1:** within 15 m. **2:** within 50 m. **3:** any visible point.
**4:** familiar, marked or mystically linked target in the same realm, ritual.
**5:** another realm/plane, Mythic ritual.

Range 1-3 needs an **unobstructed route**. Seeing through a barrier is not enough;
Indirect can cross it when the Form permits.
