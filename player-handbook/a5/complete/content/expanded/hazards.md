# Survive Common Hazards

Know the danger before committing.

## Falling

**1D6 per full 3 metres**, ignoring armour; damage leaves you prone. If conscious,
unsurprised and able to control the landing, test Athletics (Acrobatics): success
removes **1D6**, Critical **2D6**, and either avoids prone. Failure: normal fall;
Fumble: maximise remaining dice. Dice never go below zero.

## Air and fire

Hold breath for **CON rounds**. Then test Resilience each round at cumulative
**-1P**, capped at -3P. First failure deals the effect immediately; repeat at
each later round's end until breathable air, with **no more breath tests**.

| Exposure                               | Damage / effect each round                 |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Water / vacuum inhaled                 | 2D6; armour does not help.                 |
| Thick smoke inhaled                    | 1D6; armour does not help.                 |
| Poison gas                             | Poison exposure; +1D6 if also thick smoke. |
| Candle / torch / campfire              | 1 / 1D4 / 1D6 in direct exposure.          |
| Bonfire, steam, burning room / inferno | 2D6 / 3D6 in direct exposure.              |

Metal armour does not reduce fire/heat; other protection must plausibly insulate.
Shaped fire deals its damage once, when cast; only fire it lights in mundane fuel burns on.
Back in breathable air, test Resilience; failure means retching/gasping for **1D4 rounds**.

## Poison and disease

After the listed **Delay**, oppose **Resilience versus Potency**. Win to avoid
poison; lose to suffer its full Effect/Duration. **Poison gives one test**.
Disease instead repeats after each Delay until you win; its damage cannot heal
while it remains active. Successful Healing gives **+1B to the next resistance**.
