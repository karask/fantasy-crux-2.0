# Skilled Hands & Care

Use normal treatment and tool rules unless the Talent explicitly changes them.

## Practised Hands

After-encounter treatment restores **1D4 + 2 HP**. If it fails, you can try once
more on that patient before their next full rest. Never more than one success
per patient; this does not replace Surgery for a Major Wound.

## Field Surgeon

Attempt Surgery wherever you can stop and work, without a prepared place.
Still **outside combat**, with a Healing Kit, restoring **1 HP**. Your successful
Surgery ends **Wounded immediately**, without waiting for HP to exceed MWL.

## Physician

Your successful Healing gives **+2B instead of +1B** against poison or disease.
A patient you treated can also make **one extra resistance test** against a
poison that beat them; ordinary poison gives no retry.

## Master Craftsman

Materials cost **one quarter of finished value**, not half. Crafted items have
**ENC 1 lower, minimum 1**. Armour you make raises its wearer's Combat Order by 1.

## Weak Point

With access and quiet, examine a structure for **10 minutes**, then test
Mechanisms. Success identifies an accessible load-bearing join: attacks there
**ignore object AP** until repaired or materially changed. Failure gives no
benefit; retry only after a material change.

## Editorial notes

Avoids reproducing structure profiles and GM adjudication tables.
