# Protect With a Shield

Use a **ready shield**. Only one Reaction can answer an attack; a Talent never
makes a shield available while both hands are using another weapon.

## Shield Cover

While aware with a ready **medium or large shield**, impose **-1P** on ranged
attacks and individually targeted Projected Shapings. No Reaction cost.
Use the better of Shield Cover or terrain cover, not both. No benefit against
areas or Direct Harm; Dodge and Active Guard remain available.

## Missile Guard

Ignore shield Active Guard's **-1P** against bows, crossbows, slings and
Projected Shapings. Other penalties, including the extra off-hand -1P, remain.

## Protector

When an adjacent ally is hit by an attack you perceive, spend your **base
Reaction** to shield-Parry for them. Use normal Parry Size; apply Active Guard
eligibility/modifiers for ranged attacks. Failure leaves the ally hit.
The ally cannot also React to this attack, but keeps their Reaction for another
trigger. Protector cannot use the extra off-hand Reaction.

## Defensive Stance

Spend your Combat Action with a ready shield. Until your next turn begins,
gain **+2 AP**, make **no attacks**, and move no more than **half Movement**.
Keep defensive Reactions, including Protector. You cannot declare it after
attacking or moving more than half Movement that round.

## Editorial notes

The passive attack penalty and active defensive roll are kept distinct.
