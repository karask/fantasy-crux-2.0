# Travel & Terrain Talents

Remove Penalties **after ordinary cancellation and before the cap**. Removal
never creates a Bonus. Record any specialism alongside the Talent's name.

## Athletics Expertise

Choose **acrobatics, brute force, climbing, jumping, running or swimming**.
Remove one remaining Penalty from Athletics using that application. Buy again
only for different applications, never twice for the same one.

## Terrain Expertise

Choose a terrain, such as **forest, mountain, desert, swamp, steppe or coast**.
Remove one remaining Penalty from Natural Lore in or about that terrain. You
may buy it again for different terrains. It may combine with Tracker.

## Tracker

Remove one remaining Penalty from Natural Lore to **find or follow a trail**.
No benefit to other Natural Lore uses. With applicable Terrain Expertise,
the two Talents may remove two remaining Penalties, never produce a Bonus.

## Wayfinder

While you can see the sky or read the ground, downgrade a navigation **Fumble
to Failure**. That test does not make the group lost. It does not prevent
other hazards or remove weather, pace, food, water or shelter requirements.

## Editorial notes

The explicit stacking permission belongs to Tracker plus Terrain Expertise only.
