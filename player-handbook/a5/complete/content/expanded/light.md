# Light & Other Senses

For vision and attacks, check light **at the target**. For Movement, check light
at the mover. Smoke, mist and foliage are separate obscurity, not illumination.

## From the light source outward

| Band                 | Effects                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bright / Illuminated | No penalty.                                                                                                                     |
| Partial darkness     | Vision-based Perception and attacks -1P.                                                                                        |
| Darkness             | Those tests -2P; half Movement.                                                                                                 |
| Pitch black          | Sight-based Perception and ranged attacks cannot succeed without another applicable sense; Close attacks -3P; quarter Movement. |

Source radii: **candle 1 m; torch/lantern 3 m; campfire 5 m; bonfire 10 m**.
Inside radius: Illuminated. Outside through **×3**: Partial darkness; through
**×5**: Darkness; farther with no other light: Pitch black.

## Use the best applicable sense

**Low-Light Sight:** improves Partial darkness to Illuminated and Darkness to
Partial darkness, not Pitch black. **Night Vision:** natural surface night is
Illuminated; no help underground, in sealed/supernatural darkness or obscurity.

**Blind Sight:** ignores light only where its named sense accurately perceives
the target. No colour, writing, fine visual detail or eye contact. Its blockers
still apply. Senses never stack.

## Editorial notes

Preserves the distinction between obscurity and light and the limits of alternate senses.
