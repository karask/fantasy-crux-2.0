# Rituals & Lasting Magic

Required for **M above Safe + 2; I5; Range 4+; Reach 3+; or Duration 3+**.
Rituals do not raise maximum I or make powerful effects free.

## Prepare, then test

Spend **one hour per M** in a prepared place, with full Practice and a fitting
focus/offering. Maximum M is the greater of **Safe + 2 or twice Safe**.
Exceeding it needs an exceptional campaign event and a serious lasting price.

| M compared with Safe | Base penalty |
| -------------------- | ------------ |
| Up to Safe           | None.        |
| 1-2 above            | -1P.         |
| 3+ above             | -2P.         |

Then apply the ritual's **+1B**, assistance and circumstances, cancel and cap.
Failure above Safe is still overreach. Use normal PP payment results.

## Share the PP, not the roll

Willing participants stay and join Practice. Pledge full M in a declared order;
the leader pledges at least 1 PP and pays the first point. Critical leaves unused
pledges unspent. One capable Shaper may Assist; only the leader rolls and suffers
Backlash. A Fumble also advances every contributor one Fatigue step; already
Exhausted means unconscious 3D6 minutes, waking Fatigued.

## Indefinite or permanent

**Indefinite:** leader personally commits full M from current and maximum PP,
even on Critical; no contributors. Leave at least 1 available maximum PP unless
declared mythic helplessness. Ending restores maximum, not spent current PP.

**Any dial at 5 is Mythic:** agree a lasting price. Materials are consumed at
the start; lasting changes attach after success, or on Fumble only if declared.
Permanent mundane creation needs equivalent material. Completed transformations
become inactive; supernatural properties remain anchored, Dispellable magic.
A price never discounts M, PP, active load or enchantment capacity.

## Editorial notes

No campaign-price catalogue or GM judgment tables; the obligations a participant accepts remain.
