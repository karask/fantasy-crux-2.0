# Move & Work in the Field

Use **Athletics** for movement and exertion. These are ordinary capabilities,
not automatic success when danger, load or poor conditions matter.

## Athletics at a glance

| Activity             | Usual procedure                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Balance / acrobatics | Half Movement without a test; test for full speed.                                                                                                                         |
| Climb with holds     | Quarter Movement; success doubles that rate when speed or difficulty matters.                                                                                              |
| Swim                 | Half Movement in calm water; test in dangerous conditions.                                                                                                                 |
| Jump                 | With a 5 m run, success clears twice your height horizontally or half your height vertically. Standing distances halve. Each extra horizontal metre: -1P, capped normally. |
| Brute force          | Athletics to lift, drag, bend, break or push.                                                                                                                              |

## Make, prepare or break

**Craft:** roughly one day per **50 SP of finished value**, materials costing
half that value. **Lore:** successful useful preparation gives +1B to one
directly related test soon afterwards.

**Objects:** stationary targets usually need no attack roll. Subtract object
AP from damage, then HP; at 0 HP they break. The GM warns before hard materials
put your weapon at risk. This is not an automatic Critical.

## Tools worth packing

**Climbing kit:** +1B to climbing. **Crowbar:** +1B to brute force.
**Lock picks:** +1B to opening locks. **Fishing kit:** +1B to catching fish.
**Healing kit:** five Healing/Surgery attempts; each attempt uses one charge,
success or failure. Normal kit-less Healing takes -2P.

Walking trained mounts with tack, driving on flat ground and sailing calm water
usually need no roll. Test Riding, Driving or Sailing when conditions become dangerous.

## Editorial notes

Only common player actions and tool effects; no object statistics catalogue.
