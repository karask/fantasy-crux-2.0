# A Character From Scratch

**Ilan, human caravan guard:** protects travelling companions while searching
for his missing brother. This example spends every creation pool exactly.

## 1. Characteristics: 56 + 30 = 86

**STR 14 · CON 13 · DEX 14 · SIZ 12 · INT 13 · POW 10 · CHA 10.**
All scores meet the creation limits. No points are traded for extra IP.

## 2. Allocate the four skill pools

| Pool             | Added points -> final scores                                                                                                      |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Resistances / 50 | Dodge +30 -> **54%**; Persistence +10 -> **30%**; Resilience +10 -> **33%**.                                                      |
| Combat / 50      | Close +30 -> **58%**; Ranged +10 -> **37%**; Unarmed +10 -> **38%**.                                                              |
| Knowledge / 50   | Culture (own) +10 -> **33%**; Natural Lore +20 -> **43%**; Lore (Military Tactics, own) +20 -> **43%**.                           |
| Practical / 75   | Athletics +20 -> **48%**; Deception +15 -> **42%**; Perception +20 -> **43%**; Healing +10 -> **33%**; Mechanisms +10 -> **37%**. |

Every unlisted general skill stays at its base, including Language (own) **63%**.
No pool gives more than 30 points to any skill.

## 3. Spend 10 IP; choose equipment

**Quick Reflexes 3 + Shield Cover 3 + Missile Guard 2 + Disarm 2 = 10 IP.**
DEX 14, Dodge 54% and Close Combat 58% meet all requirements.

Take leather, medium shield, arming sword, shortbow, dagger and the standard pack.
All weapon requirements are met. A starting-money total of 14 on 4D6 gives **140 SP**.

## 4. Record the final attributes

**HP 13/13 · MWL 7 · PP 10/10 · DM +1D4 · AP 2 · Movement 15 m.**
Combat Order: round (14 + 13) / 2 to 14, subtract leather ENC 3, add Quick
Reflexes 2 = **13**. Carrying capacity **26 ENC**. Begin with **2 Hero Points**.
