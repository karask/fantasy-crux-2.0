# Use a Magical Item

**No Shaping skill or cell needed.** Items show their maker's Tell; hostile
effects face the maker's **stored Shaping result**.

## Three ways to power it

| Kind       | What you pay                                                                                   |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Continuous | Active while worn/held; commits your maximum PP equal to M. Removing it restores that maximum. |
| Activated  | Combat Action and **M PP** for each reusable activation. You need enough available PP.         |
| Charged    | Combat Action and **one stored charge**; the maker prepaid it. No PP cost to you.              |

Potions and scrolls are one-charge items, spent on use. A multi-charge item
becomes ordinary or is destroyed after its last charge, as its maker declared.
Continuous items cannot give a depletable vitality reserve; each new reserve
needs an Activated use or stored charge.

## A Trigger arms one use

Activation fixes its target and allowed choices, paying PP or charge even if
the event never occurs. It fires once without another Action. Continuous items
cannot use Trigger. No item retaliates with damage or adds Shaping damage to
repeated weapon hits; each new effect needs a new paid use.

## Identify or end it

**Sense Magic** reveals magic/Form; Critical adds Technique, M and anchored state.
**Scry the matching Form:** I2 diagnoses use and effect; I3 reveals a concealed
curse, limitation or Trigger. Do not assume what an unidentified item does.

Destroy its vulnerable anchor or Dispel through it to end the enchantment.
Completed permanent transformations are different: they cannot be Dispelled.
