# Choose Your Ancestry

These are the six recommended player ancestries. **Ask which fit the campaign.**
Your culture, loyalties and personality are your own, not dictated by ancestry.

## Starting spread: add 30 points

| Ancestry   | STR | CON | DEX | SIZ | INT | POW | CHA |
| ---------- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human      | 8   | 8   | 8   | 8   | 8   | 8   | 8   |
| Elf        | 6   | 8   | 10  | 6   | 10  | 8   | 8   |
| Dwarf      | 10  | 12  | 6   | 4   | 8   | 8   | 8   |
| Orc        | 10  | 8   | 8   | 10  | 6   | 6   | 8   |
| Goblin     | 6   | 7   | 12  | 4   | 9   | 8   | 10  |
| Lizardfolk | 11  | 8   | 6   | 8   | 9   | 7   | 7   |

These are starting spreads, **not finished scores**. Raise INT and SIZ to at
least 7. The usual point-creation maximum is 18, or a lower racial maximum.

## Racial maxima: for later improvement

| Ancestry   | STR | CON | DEX | SIZ | INT | POW | CHA |
| ---------- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human      | 21  | 21  | 21  | 21  | 21  | 21  | 21  |
| Elf        | 18  | 21  | 27  | 18  | 27  | 21  | 21  |
| Dwarf      | 27  | 27  | 21  | 18  | 21  | 21  | 21  |
| Orc        | 27  | 21  | 21  | 24  | 18  | 18  | 21  |
| Goblin     | 18  | 18  | 27  | 15  | 21  | 18  | 21  |
| Lizardfolk | 27  | 21  | 18  | 21  | 21  | 21  | 21  |

SIZ normally cannot be improved with IP, even when its maximum is higher.

## Pay for innate abilities

Deduct the mandatory package from your **10 starting IP**: Human **0**, Elf **5**,
Dwarf **7**, Orc **4**, Goblin **5**, Lizardfolk **6**. The next page explains them.
They are not free extras. Other profiles and racial dice need GM agreement.

## Editorial notes

Only player creation options are extracted from Fantasy Races; no creature
profiles, racial design system or campaign assumptions are reproduced.
