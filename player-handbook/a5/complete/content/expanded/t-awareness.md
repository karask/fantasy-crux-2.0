# Awareness & Your Other Hand

These passive Talents change positioning, acting order or the penalty on an
existing option. They do not give an unrestricted second turn.

## Battle Awareness

While aware of **at least one enemy**, attacks against you gain no +1B merely
for flanking or coming from behind. Genuine surprise still applies: an attack
you do not perceive may still deny a Reaction.

## Quick Reflexes

Add **2 to Combat Order** before rolling initiative. Record the adjusted value
on your sheet so you add it only once.

## Off-Hand Mastery

Remove the core **-1P** from an extra off-hand attack, Parry or Opportunity
Reaction. Every other penalty still applies.

You still choose **extra attack OR extra defence**, once per round. This grants
no extra Dodge or Protector use, and a two-handed item still does not qualify.
See {{ref:offhand}} for the underlying choice.

## Editorial notes

Retains the mutually exclusive extra options and the base Reaction distinction.
