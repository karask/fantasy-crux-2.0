# Allocate Your Skill Points

Each category has its own pool: **Resistances 50, Combat 50, Knowledge 50,
Practical 75**. Add points to bases from the preceding pages: **one point raises
one skill by 1 percentage point**.

## Stay inside the limits

Add **no more than 30 points to any one skill** from these pools. No score can
exceed **100%**. You cannot move points from one category's pool into another.
Different Craft, Culture, Language and Lore subjects are separate skills.

**Example:** DEX 14 + STR 14 gives Close Combat 28%. Adding 30 Combat points
makes it **58%**, leaving 20 Combat points for Ranged and/or Unarmed Combat.

## Trade skill points for starting IP

You can remove up to **20 points each** from Resistances, Combat and Practical.
Each **5 removed gives 1 IP**: at most 4 IP per category, **12 bonus IP total**.
Knowledge cannot be traded.

These bonus IP can buy Talents or raise characteristics, **not raise skills**.
Keep them separate from your normal 10 starting IP, and record each expenditure.

## Want to begin as a Shaper?

Declare Shaping and **reserve its full 20 IP before allocating skills**.
Only then can you put up to 30 Knowledge points into Shaping (base INT + POW).

**Human example:** trade 20 Resistance, 20 Combat and 10 Practical points.
That yields **10 bonus IP + 10 normal IP = 20 IP** for Shaping. Your remaining
pools are **30 / 30 / 50 / 65**. No IP remain for other purchases.

Nonhuman innate packages also cost IP; do not count the same points twice.
If the available budget cannot pay both, start without Shaping and learn it later.

## Editorial notes

The Shaper budget example is the standard full-price creation route, not a discount.
