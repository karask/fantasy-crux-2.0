# Make a Character

Write one sentence: **who you are, what you do well, and what drives you**.
Agree a place in the campaign with the GM and other players. There are no classes.

## Choose characteristics

| Score              | What it measures            |
| ------------------ | --------------------------- |
| STR / Strength     | Power and force.            |
| CON / Constitution | Health and endurance.       |
| DEX / Dexterity    | Coordination and reactions. |
| SIZ / Size         | Height, mass and bulk.      |
| INT / Intelligence | Reasoning and memory.       |
| POW / Power        | Will and inner reserves.    |
| CHA / Charisma     | Presence and leadership.    |

## Allocate 30 points

A human starts at **8 in each score**. Other ancestries start with the spread
on {{ref:ancestries}}. Add **30 points**; lowering scores returns points to spend.
Finish with **INT and SIZ at least 7**, all others at least **3**. No score may
exceed **18 or its racial maximum, whichever is lower**.

All point-built characters start with **86 characteristic points in total**.
Remember weapon STR/DEX requirements when choosing your scores.

## Human random alternative

With the group's agreement, instead roll **4D6, drop the lowest** for STR, CON,
DEX, POW and CHA. For INT and SIZ, roll **3D6, drop the lowest, add 6**.
After all seven rolls, you may swap one pair. Nonhuman random generation uses
the campaign's racial dice, not these human rolls.

## Editorial notes

The point method is the default complete creation path. Nonhuman random dice
remain an optional campaign variant in the main rules, not another full grid here.
