# Master the Ranged Shot

Keep range, cover and readiness rules. An exception changes only what it names.

## Steady Aim

Taking a Reaction no longer loses your Aim. You still lose it on losing sight
of the target, or failing to shoot with your **next Combat Action**.

## Point-Blank Shot

Ignore the **-2P from one enemy** engaging you when making a ranged attack.
Every additional enemy still gives -2P, before normal cancellation and the cap.
One enemy: no engagement penalty; two: -2P. Other causes still apply.

## Mighty Shot

Once per round, add a **positive DM** to one bow or sling attack. Zero or negative
DM gives no benefit; never use it with crossbows. With Rapid Shot it applies to
only one of the two attacks.

## Rapid Shot

Spend your Combat Action and **forfeit all Reactions** for the round to make
**two bow or sling attacks at -1P each**. Resolve them one at a time; they can
have different targets. The weapon must be ready for the first shot; this Talent
includes loading the second missile.

No crossbows. You cannot use it after taking a Reaction that round. Weapon
Expertise cannot modify Rapid Shot, and the usual Aim benefit applies only to
the next eligible attack, not both shots.

## Editorial notes

Aim remains a single-next-attack benefit. No new attack sequence is invented.
