# Conditions & Escaping Them

Price exact consequences. **"Paralysed" gives no free damage, helplessness,
movement or control.**

## Common hostile outcomes

| I   | What it can do                                                                                   |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Prone, or a brief hindrance such as half Movement through next turn.                             |
| 2   | Restrain; -1P to one skill/family or one sense.                                                  |
| 3   | Suppress one sense; remove **one Combat Action once**; serious tactical compulsion.              |
| 4   | No Actions/Reactions while incapacitated; or full bodily/mental control with active maintenance. |

A lost Combat Action never repeats; the target is immune until it completes
a later Combat Action.

## Retry at the right time

Usually spend a **Combat Action** to retry the original defence against **stored
Shaping**; win to escape. If Combat Actions are prevented, retry freely **at
your own turn's end**, after its restrictions.
Winning **does not restore missed Actions**; it does not require being conscious.

**Example:** bind, stored success 42. Resilience 60% rolls 55: success and higher,
so you escape. The Shaper does not reroll.
Magical sleep may instead end on damage or an adjacent ally's waking Combat Action.

## Control and restoration

I1 can colour emotion; I2 compels a limited reasonable act, not a combat Action,
tactical disadvantage or action against interests. Such compulsion needs I3.
Compelled acts use the subject's own turn and Action. Full control needs the
Shaper's Combat Action; serious self-harm, defining betrayal or violated central
belief gives another Persistence defence. Certain death always does.

**Dispel active magic.** For nonmagical Fatigue, I3 removes Fatigued or reduces
Exhausted to Fatigued; I4 removes Exhausted. An ongoing cause remains.

## Editorial notes

Free retries explicitly follow the affected turn, including an incapacitated turn.
