# Position & Difficult Shots

**Use these common modifiers. The GM handles unusual circumstances.**

## Ranged: on the attacker's test

- Target moved since your last turn: **10 m or more -1P; 30 m or more -2P instead**.
- Target partly obscured by mist, smoke, fog or foliage: **-1P**; heavily **-2P**.
- Target prone: **-1P**. Target helpless: **+2B**.
- You are on unstable ground: **-1P**. Prone firing depends on the weapon; check with the GM.
- You are engaged at Close range: **-2P per enemy**.

Range/Aim: page 12. Terrain cover: page 13. Obscurity is not cover.
The GM handles wind and other difficult conditions.

## Melee: attack the opening

- Target prone, flanked or attacked from behind: **attack +1B**.
- Target helpless: **automatic Critical attack**.
- Higher ground: **+1B** to that attacker's or defender's own roll.
- You are prone: **-1P** to your melee attack or defence. A prone defender gives
  the attacker +1B and takes -1P on its Reaction.
- You are on unstable ground: **-1P to the affected test**.

## Behind does not mean unnoticed

An **unaware defender cannot React**. Behind/beside does not automatically mean
unnoticed. An alert defender can React to a rear attack; that attack gains +1B.

## Combine modifiers

Use the strongest version of the same condition; combine different causes.
Cancel +B/-P, then cap at **+3B / -3P**.
