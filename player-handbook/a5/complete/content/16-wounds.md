# Wounds & Saving a Life

**One big hit can wound you. At 0 HP, help is urgent.**

## One hit at least equal to MWL

After Parry/armour, **one hit's damage at least equal to MWL** causes a Major
Wound. Apply HP loss and both conditions:

**Wounded: -1P on every test. Bleeding: lose 1 HP at each round's end.**

Conditions do not stack. Later Major Wounds deal damage and can restart Bleeding.
No Resilience test or injury table. MWL 6: one **6 HP hit** wounds; two **3 HP
hits** do not wound merely by totalling 6.

## At 0 HP: unconscious and Dying

HP stops at 0. Die if still Dying at the end of the **third Combat Round after
reaching 0 HP**. Stabilise before that deadline.

## Stabilise: whole round

An adjacent helper spends **Combat Action, Movement Action and all Reactions**.
Both remain stationary. Test **Healing** with a Healing Kit or suitable
improvised supplies; **-2P without them**.

Success removes **Bleeding and Dying**, restores **0 HP**, and leaves Wounded.
Stable at 0 HP means **unconscious**. Stopping Bleeding does not unlock natural
recovery from a Major Wound. Treatment and separate Hero Point protections: page 17.

## Editorial notes

Combines the wound module with battlefield Healing. The diagram begins the
three-round countdown after 0 HP, not on the round in which the hit occurs.
