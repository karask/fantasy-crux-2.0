# Cover & Defence

**Cover changes the attack.** An aware defender may React only after a hit.

## Terrain cover

**Partial (low wall): attack -1P. Substantial (battlements): attack -2P.**
Complete: blocked attack line, no attack. Cover absorbs no damage.

## Dodge

**Dodge -1P:** once per round, base Reaction only. Perceive the danger and be
able to move away.

## Active Guard

Test **Close Combat** with an eligible ready item:

- **Thrown weapon:** ready weapon **-1P**; ready shield **no guard penalty**.
- **Arrow, bolt, or sling projectile:** shield only, **-1P**.

Carrying a shield adds **no attack penalty**. A strapped shield cannot guard
while you wield a weapon with both hands.

## Resolve the defence

**Ordinary hit:** successful Dodge avoids it; successful Parry blocks by Size.
Any ready shield fully blocks a **Light** projectile on success.

**Critical hit:** ordinary defence success means normal rolled damage after
armour, with no avoidance or Parry reduction. **Critical defence** stops it,
regardless of Size.

## Extra guard

Extra off-hand Reaction: **add -1P** (shield vs arrow: **-2P before other
modifiers**); cannot Dodge. **One Reaction per attack.**
