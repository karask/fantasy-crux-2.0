# Take the Shot

Spend a **Combat Action** and test **Ranged Combat**. Cover modifies this roll;
the defender reacts only after a hit.

## Range

Up to listed Range (**R**): normal. Beyond R through **2R: -2P**. Beyond 2R:
no shot. **Shortbow:** R = 75 m; 2R = 150 m.

## Aim

Spend a **Combat Action** aiming at one visible target: **+1B** to your next
ranged attack against it. Lose Aim on **any Reaction**, loss of sight, or
failure to shoot with your **next Combat Action**.

## Damage and close use

Thrown weapons add **DM** and keep their listed **Size**. Bows, crossbows, and
slings add no DM; their projectiles are **Light**.

Within **2 m**, a **Close**-tagged ranged weapon can use Close Combat and its
listed damage. Without that tag, it is an improvised **Light club: 1D6 + DM**,
attacking at **-1P**.

## Shooting into a crowd

Apply cover normally. An ordinary failure misses. Only a **Fumble** may hit a
fairly or randomly selected nearby target: normal rolled damage, then armour.

## Worked shot

Aim **+1B** and a low wall **-1P** cancel. Roll normally. If the attack hits,
resolve the defender's Reaction separately.

## Editorial notes

**Image labels:** `LOW WALL: ATTACK -1P` identifies terrain's effect on the
archer's roll. The worked shot then includes Aim; it must not imply that the
label is the final combined modifier.

**Scope:** Retains the complete current ranged-attacks module and introduces
projectile Size from the missile-defence module. Other situational
modifiers, crossbow reloads, and the general critical outcomes remain separate
modules in the eventual booklet. Do not silently omit them from the content map.
