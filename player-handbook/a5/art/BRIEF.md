# A5 ranged spread artwork

The left scene reuses the upper panel of the intact
`player-handbook/art/15-terrain-cover/illustration-v01.png`. The original A4
composition and three-panel master remain available. CSS framing does not alter
that source asset.

The right scene is new: an ordinary human soldier actively raises his ready
shield against an incoming arrow. A pointed helmet, mail, rust-red surcoat,
pale ruined stone, and blue sky relate it to the earlier soldier. The image
shows a distinct later moment, not a second simultaneous arrow in the archer's bow.

## Visual intent

The arrow travels from the left into the outward-facing raised shield. The
soldier's gaze, braced arm, and contact splinter make active Parry legible.
The wall shelters only his lower body. There are no passive shield bonuses,
magical effects, extra people, or generated words.

All rule text and modifiers are typeset separately. Oxide identifies the
terrain's effect on the attack; the teal strip identifies the later shield
Parry. Both labels name their roll explicitly. The right page's exact rules
remain in `../content/cover-and-defence.md`, not in the generated artwork.

Review is limited to this one spread. The new artwork does not approve the
layout or authorise generating the rest of the booklet without user review.
