## Major Wounds

Losing HP is ordinary wear. A **Major Wound** is different: it happens when **one hit** deals damage **at least equal to your MWL** after Parry, armour and any magical reserve. Take the HP loss, and you gain both conditions:

- **Wounded:** −1P on every test.
- **Bleeding:** lose 1 HP at the end of every round.

The conditions do not stack. A later Major Wound deals its damage and can restart Bleeding, but you are no more Wounded than before. There is no injury table and no test to resist.

> **Example.** Ilan's MWL is 7. One 7-damage hit wounds him. Two hits of 4 do not, even though they total 8.

## Dying

At **0 HP** you fall unconscious and are **Dying**. If you are still Dying at the **end of the third round** after reaching 0 HP, you die. HP never goes below 0.

**Stabilise.** An adjacent helper spends the **whole round** (Combat Action, Movement Action and all Reactions), both staying where they are, and succeeds at a **Healing** test. Without a Healing Kit or suitable improvised supplies the test is at −2P. Success ends Bleeding and Dying but restores no HP: a patient at 0 HP stays unconscious and Wounded.

## Healing After the Fight

All Healing tests need a **Healing Kit**; without one they are at −2P. Each kit holds five uses, and every attempt spends one, success or failure.

**Ordinary damage.** After an encounter, a patient who is **not Wounded** can receive **one** Healing attempt before their next full rest. Success restores **1D4 HP**. It is one attempt, not one success: a failure cannot be retried.

**Surgery.** A Wounded patient cannot heal naturally until treated. Once stabilised, out of combat and in safe conditions, a successful Healing test (Surgery) restores **1 HP** and lets recovery begin.

**Natural recovery.** With no more than light activity, you regain **CON ÷ 4 HP per day** (rounded, halves up), up to your maximum. **Wounded ends** once you have been treated and your current HP is **greater than your MWL**.

> **Example.** Dori (CON 14, MWL 6) is stabilised at 0 HP. Next morning, Surgery restores 1 HP. She then regains 4 HP a day and stops being Wounded once she reaches 7 HP.

Magical healing of Intensity 3 or more also counts as Surgery (Chapter Eight).

## Hero Points Against Death

Two Hero Point uses protect your life. Each costs 1 point and you declare it when it applies.

- **Downgrade a Major Wound:** when a hit would cause one, keep the full HP loss but gain no Wounded or Bleeding from it. There is no Major Wound to treat.
- **Avoid Death:** at 0 HP, become stable and unconscious instead of Bleeding and Dying. Wake after the scene with **1 HP**, still Wounded if you already were.

They are separate. With 5 HP and MWL 6, a 7-damage hit needs **two** points to avoid both the Major Wound and death.
