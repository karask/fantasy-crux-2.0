## Your Character in Ten Steps

There are no classes. You build exactly the person you imagine, step by step, and the example of Ilan at the end of this chapter follows every step.

1. Write a concept.
2. Choose an ancestry.
3. Set your characteristics.
4. Pay for your ancestry's innate abilities.
5. Work out your attributes.
6. Note your base skills.
7. Allocate skill points (and decide whether you begin as a Shaper).
8. Spend your Improvement Points.
9. Take your equipment.
10. Add the finishing details.

## 1. Concept

Write one sentence: **who you are, what you do well, and what drives you.** Compare ideas with the other players and the GM so everyone fits the campaign; a varied group solves more problems.

> **Examples.** A foolhardy veteran seeking glory. An observant caravan scout paying off an old debt. A disgraced courtier surviving on wit.

## 2. Ancestry

Six ancestries are recommended for player characters. Ask the GM which fit the campaign. Your ancestry sets your starting characteristics and your innate abilities; your culture, loyalties and personality are your own.

## 3. Characteristics

| Score | Measures |
| --- | --- |
| **STR** Strength | Power, lifting and force. |
| **CON** Constitution | Health and endurance. |
| **DEX** Dexterity | Agility, coordination, reactions. |
| **SIZ** Size | Height, mass and bulk. |
| **INT** Intelligence | Reasoning and memory. |
| **POW** Power | Will and inner reserves. |
| **CHA** Charisma | Presence and leadership. |

Start from your ancestry's row and **add 30 points**. You can lower a score to gain points to spend elsewhere. When you finish:

- **INT and SIZ** are at least **7**; every other score is at least **3**.
- No score is above **18**, or your ancestry's maximum if lower.

Check the STR and DEX each weapon needs (Chapter Six) before you settle.

**Rolling instead (humans).** With the group's agreement, roll **4D6 and drop the lowest** for STR, CON, DEX, POW and CHA, and **3D6, drop the lowest, +6** for INT and SIZ. You can then swap one pair of results. Nonhuman random rolls use the campaign's own dice; ask the GM.

## 4. Innate Abilities

Every character starts with **10 IP** (step 8). Nonhumans must first pay for their whole innate package from them. Humans pay nothing and keep all 10.

**Elf (5 IP).** *Low-Light Sight (3):* partial darkness counts as Illuminated and Darkness as partial darkness; pitch black does not change. *Terrain Expertise: Forest (2):* remove one remaining Penalty die from Natural Lore in or about forests (no prerequisite needed; it never creates a Bonus die).

**Dwarf (7 IP).** *Blind Sight: heat (5):* perceive warm or cold targets that contrast with their surroundings, whatever the light. Solid barriers, heavy insulation and targets at the surrounding temperature defeat it; it shows no colour, writing or fine detail. *Earth Sense (2):* touching connected earth or stone tells you roughly how deep you are and whether the space around you is unstable.

**Orc (4 IP).** *Adrenaline Surge:* once per combat, right after becoming Wounded or Fatigued, ignore those conditions' penalties to tests, Movement and Combat Order until the end of your next turn, and ignore the first 1 HP of Bleeding in that time. Bleeding continues afterwards; Exhausted, unconsciousness and Dying are unaffected.

**Goblin (5 IP).** *Blind Sight: heat,* as for dwarves.

**Lizardfolk (6 IP).** *Scaled Hide (4):* 1 natural AP with no ENC, added to worn armour; use that total or a magical Ward, whichever is higher. *Counterbalancing Tail (2):* tests meant to knock you prone, throw you or force you to move are made at −1P.

## 5. Attributes

Use your final characteristics and round halves up.

| Attribute | How to work it out |
| --- | --- |
| Maximum HP | (SIZ + CON) ÷ 2 |
| Major Wound Level | Maximum HP ÷ 2 |
| Maximum PP | POW |
| Movement | 15 m (human) |
| Combat Order | (DEX + INT) ÷ 2, minus your armour's ENC |
| Carrying capacity | STR + SIZ, in ENC |
| Damage Modifier | From STR + SIZ, below |

Work out maximum HP first and round it, then halve that for MWL. Write armour-adjusted Combat Order on your sheet so you never subtract armour twice. Ask the GM about Movement for an unusual ancestry.

| STR + SIZ | DM | STR + SIZ | DM |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1–10 | −1D6 | 31–45 | +1D6 |
| 11–15 | −1D4 | 46–60 | +2D6 |
| 16–25 | +0 | 61–75 | +3D6 |
| 26–30 | +1D4 | each +15 | +1D6 more |

Add DM to **melee, unarmed and thrown** damage, even when it is negative. Bows, crossbows, slings and magic do not add it.

**Power Points.** Each complete 2 hours of rest restores a quarter of your maximum PP; 8 hours restores all. A living character at 0 PP falls unconscious until PP returns.

## 6. Base Skills

You have every general skill below. Its **base** comes from your characteristics; points you add in step 7 raise it. A skill written with a subject, such as Craft (Carpenter) or Lore (Alchemy), is a separate skill for each subject.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Dodge | DEX + 10 | Evade attacks and hazards. |
| Persistence | POW + 10 | Resist fear, pressure and mind magic. |
| Resilience | CON + POW | Endure injury, fatigue, poison and disease. |

| Combat | Base | Use it to… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Close Combat | DEX + STR | Attack and Parry with weapons and shields. |
| Ranged Combat | DEX + INT | Shoot or throw. |
| Unarmed Combat | DEX + STR | Punch, kick, grapple; Parry unarmed blows. |

| Knowledge | Base | Use it to… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Culture (own) | INT + 10 | Know your society's history and customs. |
| Culture (other) | INT | Understand another named society. |
| Language (own) | INT + 50 | Speak your native tongue. |
| Language (other) | INT | Speak another language; 50% is fluent. |
| Lore (own field) | INT + 10 | Know your chosen field of study. |
| Lore (other) | INT | Know another named field. |
| Natural Lore | INT + 10 | Track, navigate, forage, read nature. |
| Shaping | INT + POW | Cast magic; needs the Shaping Talent. |

Choose your **own Lore field** now, such as Alchemy, Magic, Military Tactics, Spirit World, Undead, Law and Heraldry, or Astronomy. A successful Lore test also gives +1B to one directly related test soon afterwards, when the knowledge would help.

| Practical | Base | Use it to… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Athletics | DEX + STR | Balance, climb, jump, swim, use force. |
| Craft (type) | INT + 10 | Make and repair goods. |
| Deception | DEX + INT | Sneak, disguise, hide things, pick pockets. |

| Mechanisms | DEX + INT | Locks, traps, devices, large works. |
| Perception | INT + POW | Notice the hidden and the dangerous. |
| Performance | CHA + 10 | Act, sing, play, speak in public. |
| Riding | DEX + POW | Control a mount under pressure. |
| Sailing | DEX + INT | Handle a boat or ship. |
| Streetwise | CHA + POW | Find rumours, contacts, illicit goods. |
| Trade | INT + 10 | Value goods and haggle. |

Skill percentages also describe expertise: **0–25% Novice, 26–50% Apprentice, 51–75% Veteran, 76–99% Expert, 100% Master.** Many Talents require 51% or 76%.

## 7. Skill Points

You have four pools of points, one per skill category:

{{fig:pools}}

Each point adds 1% to a skill in that pool's category. You cannot move points between pools. During creation:

- add **no more than 30 points** to any one skill;
- no skill can exceed 100%.

> **Example.** With DEX 14 and STR 14, Close Combat starts at 28%. Adding 30 Combat points makes it 58%, leaving 20 Combat points for Ranged and Unarmed Combat.

**Trade points for IP.** You can give up to **20 points** from each of the Resistance, Combat and Practical pools (not Knowledge). Every **5 points gives 1 IP**: at most 4 IP per pool and 12 IP in total. These bonus IP can raise characteristics or buy Talents, but **not** raise skills.

### Beginning as a Shaper

Magic needs the **Shaping Talent, which costs 20 IP**. To begin play as a Shaper, decide now and reserve those 20 IP **before** you allocate skill points; only then can you put up to 30 Knowledge points into Shaping.

> **Example.** A human trades 20 Resistance, 20 Combat and 10 Practical points for 10 bonus IP. With the normal 10 IP that makes the 20 for Shaping. The pools left to spend are 30 / 30 / 50 / 65, and no IP remain.

A nonhuman's innate package also comes out of the same IP. If you cannot afford both, start without magic and learn it later (Chapter Eight).

## 8. Improvement Points

You start with **10 IP**, minus your innate package, plus any IP from traded skill points. Spend them now or save them.

- **1 IP:** improve one skill once, as in Chapter Seven (+5% up to 50%, +3% above). Only your normal 10 IP can do this, not traded IP.
- **5 IP:** raise one characteristic by 1 (not SIZ; 18 is still the creation maximum). Recalculate attributes; skills keep their scores.
- **A Talent's cost:** buy a Talent whose prerequisites you meet (Chapter Seven).

## 9. Equipment

Choose one free package. Both contain **leather armour, one ranged weapon and a dagger**, plus either:

- **one two-handed close-combat weapon,** or
- **one shield and one one-handed close-combat weapon.**

Everyone also gets a **backpack, 10 m of rope, two weeks of travelling provisions, flint and tinder, and a waterskin**, plus **4D6 × 10 silver pieces (SP)**. The GM may let you buy more before play. **100 CP (copper) = 10 SP = 1 GP (gold).**

A Flex weapon can be used in one hand or two; ask the GM which slot of the package it fills. Confirm your ammunition and any specialist kit.

## 10. Finishing Details

Give your character a name, appearance, background, personality, loyalties and an immediate goal. Humans usually begin aged 18–28 (or 2D6+16). Then check your sheet: weapons, AP, carried ENC, armour-adjusted Combat Order, full HP and PP, and **2 Hero Points**.

## Example: Ilan, Caravan Guard

*A human caravan guard who protects travelling companions while searching for his missing brother.*

**Characteristics.** Human 8s plus 30 points: **STR 14, CON 13, DEX 14, SIZ 12, INT 13, POW 10, CHA 10.** He trades no points.

**Skill points.** Base + added points:

| Pool | Spent on |
| --- | --- |
| Resistance 50 | Dodge +30 → **54%**; Persistence +10 → **30%**; Resilience +10 → **33%** |
| Combat 50 | Close +30 → **58%**; Ranged +10 → **37%**; Unarmed +10 → **38%** |
| Knowledge 50 | Culture (own) +10 → **33%**; Natural Lore +20 → **43%**; Lore (Military Tactics) +20 → **43%** |
| Practical 75 | Athletics +20 → **48%**; Deception +15 → **42%**; Perception +20 → **43%**; Healing +10 → **33%**; Mechanisms +10 → **37%** |

Every other skill stays at its base, such as Language (own) 63%.

**Talents (10 IP).** Quick Reflexes 3 + Shield Cover 3 + Missile Guard 2 + Disarm 2. His DEX 14, Dodge 54% and Close Combat 58% meet every prerequisite.

**Equipment.** Leather armour, medium shield, arming sword, shortbow, dagger and the standard pack. He rolls 14 on 4D6: **140 SP**.

**Attributes.** HP **13** (12+13 = 25, halved and rounded up). MWL **7**. PP **10**. DM **+1D4** (STR + SIZ = 26). AP **2**. Movement **15 m**. Combat Order: (14+13) ÷ 2 = 14, minus 3 for leather, plus 2 for Quick Reflexes = **13**. Carrying capacity **26 ENC**. **2 Hero Points.**
