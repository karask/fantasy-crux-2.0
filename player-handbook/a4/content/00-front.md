## Welcome

This handbook is everything a player needs: how to roll, how to make and grow a character, how to fight, survive and explore, every Talent, and the Shaping magic system. The Gamemaster's tools, creatures and settings are in their own books.

Read Chapter One first. Then make a character with Chapter Two, and dip into the rest as play needs it. Shaded boxes hold examples; diagrams repeat a rule shown beside them.
