## Improvement

After each adventure the GM awards **Improvement Points (IP)**, usually 3 for modest progress, 4 for a completed adventure and 5 for a major achievement. Spend them between adventures, or save them. Three months of suitable practice or study can also earn 1 IP, with the GM's approval.

| Spend | Gain |
| --- | --- |
| 1 IP, skill 0–50% | +5% |
| 1 IP, skill 51–99% | +3% |
| 5 IP | +1 to one characteristic (not SIZ), up to your ancestry's maximum |
| The Talent's cost | One Talent whose prerequisites you meet |

Improve each skill **once** between adventures. The score before the gain decides its size, and nothing passes 100%: 50% becomes 55%, 51% becomes 54%, 98% becomes 100%.

After raising a characteristic, recalculate your attributes (HP, MWL, PP, DM, Combat Order, and a Shaper's active limit). Skills keep their current scores: bases only matter at creation.

## How Talents Work

A Talent is a trained exception to the normal rules. Pay its cost once and write it down. You must meet its prerequisite when you buy it; any equipment it names is needed whenever you use it. Each entry lists **cost, prerequisite and type**:

- **Passive:** works whenever its conditions are met.
- **Action:** uses your Combat Action. Only **one** action Talent can modify an attack, and that attack is not a standard attack, so it earns no extra off-hand attack.
- **Reaction:** uses the Reaction it names.

**"Removes a Penalty die."** Several Talents remove one remaining Penalty die. Apply it after Bonus and Penalty dice cancel, before the ±3 cap. It never creates a Bonus die.

**"If not stopped."** A hit is stopped by a winning Dodge or a winning Parry that blocks it completely; a partial Parry does not stop it. A critical hit is stopped only by a winning Critical Reaction. When a Talent replaces damage with an effect, a critical hit adds nothing to that effect.

Shaping and the magical Talents are in Chapter Eight.

## Weapon Training

These four apply to one **exact weapon type**, such as longsword. Buy them again for another type, never twice for the same one.

### Favoured Weapon
*3 IP · Close or Ranged Combat 51% · Passive*

+1 damage with every damaging attack with that weapon type, including shields and primitive versions, but not improvised, unarmed, natural or magical attacks. No bonus on a hit that stays critical, or when a Talent replaces damage. A critical demoted to ordinary does get it.

### Signature Weapon
*4 IP · Favoured Weapon for that type, Close or Ranged Combat 76% · Passive*

Your Favoured Weapon bonus becomes **+2** (not +3). Its limits still apply.

### Weapon Expertise
*4 IP · Close or Ranged Combat 76% · Passive*

Once per round, declare it before an attack, Parry, Active Guard or Opportunity Attack with that type: remove one remaining Penalty die. Your unmodified skill must be 76% or more. It does not work unarmed, with natural or improvised weapons, with magic, Rapid Shot or off-hand options.

### Deadly Precision
*5 IP · all three Talents above for the type; its skill 76% · Once per round*

Before a damaging attack with that type, **double your critical range**: 01–14 at 76%, 01–20 at 100%. Use the wider range when choosing among Bonus or Penalty dice too. Not for Parries, Active Guard, unarmed attacks or magic.

## Awareness and Speed

### Battle Awareness
*3 IP · Perception 51% · Passive*

While you are aware of at least one enemy, attackers gain no +1B for flanking you or attacking from behind. Real surprise still applies.

### Quick Reflexes
*3 IP · DEX 13 and Dodge 51% · Passive*

+2 Combat Order. Write the adjusted value on your sheet.

### Off-Hand Mastery
*5 IP · Close or Unarmed Combat 76% · Passive*

Your extra off-hand attack or Reaction has no −1P. You still choose **one** of the two each round, and it gives no extra Dodge.

## Shields

### Shield Cover
*3 IP · Close Combat 51% · Passive*

While aware and holding a ready medium or large shield, ranged attacks and individually targeted Projected Shapings against you take −1P. No Reaction needed. Use this or terrain cover, whichever is better, not both. No effect against areas or Direct magic.

### Missile Guard
*2 IP · Close Combat 51% · Passive*

Your shield's Active Guard has no −1P against bows, crossbows, slings and Projected Shapings. Other penalties remain.

### Protector
*2 IP · Close Combat 51% · Reaction*

When an adjacent ally is hit by an attack you perceive, spend your **base Reaction** to Parry it with your shield, using normal Size and Active Guard rules. The ally cannot also React to that attack. Not with the extra off-hand Reaction.

### Defensive Stance
*2 IP · Close Combat 51% · Action*

With a ready shield, gain **+2 AP** until your next turn starts. You make no attacks and move at most half your Movement; your defensive Reactions remain. You cannot use it after attacking or moving more than half your Movement this round.

## Combat Manoeuvres

Declare these before rolling. Each uses your Combat Action and replaces the damage of a hit that is **not stopped.**

### Disarm
*2 IP · Close Combat 51% · Action*

Make a Close Combat attack. On a hit: no damage, and one held item of your choice lands 1D6 m away. It must be something the target could let go of.

### Trip
*3 IP · Close or Unarmed Combat 51% · Action*

Make a Close or Unarmed Combat attack. On a hit: no damage, and the target falls prone. Impossible size or anatomy can prevent it.

### Shield Rush
*3 IP · Close Combat 51% · Action*

Attack with a ready shield. On a hit: no damage; push the target **2 m**, and it tests Athletics at −1P or falls prone.

## Unarmed

### Iron Fist
*3 IP · Unarmed Combat 51% · Passive*

Your punches and kicks deal **1D4 + DM** and are **Medium**.

### Master Brawler
*4 IP · Unarmed Combat 76% and Iron Fist · Passive*

Your punches and kicks deal **1D6 + DM** and are **Heavy**. Natural weapons keep their own profile.

### Wrestler
*2 IP · Unarmed Combat 51% · Action*

While grappling, spend a Combat Action and win opposed Unarmed Combat to: deal normal unarmed damage (armour applies); make the other drop one held item; or throw them prone into an adjacent space, ending the grapple.

### Subdue
*3 IP · Close or Unarmed Combat 51% · Action*

Make one blunt attack. If not stopped, roll damage + DM, then apply partial Parry and armour. If the result reaches the target's **MWL**, it falls stably unconscious and loses no HP. Otherwise it loses only the weapon's **minimum** damage (no DM), after Parry and armour. Damage Talents do not add. The target wakes at the scene's end, when hurt, or after a successful Healing Combat Action.

## Ranged

### Steady Aim
*2 IP · Ranged Combat 51% · Passive*

Taking a Reaction no longer spoils your Aim.

### Point-Blank Shot
*3 IP · Ranged Combat 51% · Passive*

Ignore the −2P for **one** enemy engaging you when you shoot; each further enemy still gives −2P.

### Mighty Shot
*5 IP · Ranged Combat 76% · Passive*

Once per round, add your **positive** DM to one bow or sling attack. Never with crossbows.

### Rapid Shot
*4 IP · Ranged Combat 76% · Action*

Give up all Reactions this round to make **two** bow or sling attacks at −1P each, one after the other, at the same or different targets. The weapon must be ready for the first shot. No crossbows, not after a Reaction this round, and Aim helps only the first shot.

## Ambush and Stealth

### Silent Step
*3 IP · Deception 51% · Passive*

While moving at half Movement or less, Perception tests to notice you take −1P. It does not hide your companions or your light.

### Killing Angle
*4 IP · Deception 51% · Passive*

Once per round, add **1D4 damage** to a Close or Unarmed attack against a target you flank, strike from behind, or that is unaware of you. Nothing extra on a hit that stays critical.

### Master Assassin
*5 IP · Deception 76% and Killing Angle · Passive*

Killing Angle's bonus becomes **2D4**.

### Ambusher
*4 IP · Deception 76% · Action*

Declare it before your **first attack of the scene**, from concealment, against a target genuinely unaware of you. An ordinary hit becomes a **critical hit**. Close, Ranged or Unarmed Combat only.

### Deception Expertise
*2 IP · Deception 51% · Passive*

Choose disguise, hiding objects, picking pockets, stealth or misdirection: remove one remaining Penalty die from that use. Buy again for another use.

### Cutpurse
*2 IP · Deception 51% · Action*

Pick a pocket or plant a small item in one Combat Action instead of a minute (Deception against Perception). A failure goes unnoticed unless you Fumble or the item is too big to miss.

### Lockbreaker
*2 IP · Mechanisms 51% · Action*

Open a lock or disable a trap as one Action instead of an extended attempt. Resistant devices still oppose you; a Fumble springs a trap.

### Close-Quarters Knack
*2 IP · DEX 13 and SIZ 9 or lower · Passive*

Remove one remaining Penalty die that cramped surroundings put on Athletics or Mechanisms.

## Words and Leadership

### Silver Tongue
*2 IP · Influence 51% · Passive*

Remove one remaining Penalty die when you persuade or bargain with someone willing to listen. Never for threats.

### Commanding Presence
*2 IP · Influence 51% · Passive*

Remove one remaining Penalty die when you command or threaten, including Intimidate. The target must see or hear you and have something to lose.

### Rally
*4 IP · Influence 51% · Action*

Two allies who hear and understand you each gain **+1B on their next test** before the end of their next turn. It does not stack, and you cannot Rally yourself.

### Merchant's Eye
*3 IP · Trade 51% · Passive*

Know an ordinary item's true worth at a glance. Settlements offer you the goods of one size larger: a village counts as a town, a town as a city.

### Tactician
*4 IP · Lore (Military Tactics) 51% · Passive*

An army you command takes **half** its normal battle casualties. When your beaten force passes its rout test it can keep fighting; other commanders need a Critical.

## Healing and Craft

### Practised Hands
*4 IP · Healing 51% · Passive*

Your after-battle treatment restores **1D4 + 2 HP**. If it fails you may try once more on that patient before their next rest.

### Field Surgeon
*3 IP · Healing 76% · Passive*

Perform Surgery anywhere you can stop and work (still out of combat, with a Healing Kit). Your successful Surgery ends Wounded at once.

### Physician
*3 IP · Healing 76% · Passive*

Your Healing gives **+2B** (not +1B) against poison and disease, and a patient you treat gets one extra test against a poison that beat them.

### Master Craftsman
*4 IP · Craft 76% · Passive*

Materials cost a quarter of the finished value instead of half. Your work is 1 ENC lighter (minimum 1); armour you make raises its wearer's Combat Order by 1.

### Weak Point
*3 IP · Mechanisms 51% · Passive*

Study a structure for 10 minutes and test Mechanisms. Success finds a weak join: attacks there ignore the object's AP until it is repaired or changed.

## Wilderness

### Athletics Expertise
*2 IP · Athletics 51% · Passive*

Choose acrobatics, brute force, climbing, jumping, running or swimming: remove one remaining Penalty die from it. Buy again for another.

### Terrain Expertise
*2 IP · Natural Lore 51% · Passive*

Choose a terrain (forest, mountain, desert, swamp, steppe, coast…): remove one remaining Penalty die from Natural Lore there. Buy again for another.

### Tracker
*2 IP · Natural Lore 51% · Passive*

Remove one remaining Penalty die when finding or following a trail. It combines with Terrain Expertise.

### Wayfinder
*2 IP · Natural Lore 51% · Passive*

While you can see the sky or read the ground, a navigation Fumble becomes an ordinary Failure, so the group is not lost.

## Alchemy

Each Talent brews **one dose a day** from **50 SP** of materials. A dose lasts a month; choose its effect when you brew it.

### Alchemist
*4 IP · Lore (Alchemy) 51% · Passive*

- **Antidote:** +2B to one test against a named poison or disease.
- **Salve:** restores 1D6 HP over an hour's rest, once per patient per day.
- **Alchemical fire:** a Light flask thrown with Ranged Combat, Range 10 m, 1D6 fire damage, no DM. A miss wastes it.

### Poisoner
*4 IP · Lore (Alchemy) 76% and Alchemist · Passive*

Brew a poison whose **Potency is half your Lore (Alchemy)**. Choose its effect: **1D6 damage**, **−2 to one characteristic**, or **unconsciousness**, lasting 1D6 × 10 minutes. Choose **smeared** (Delay 1 round; coating a blade takes a Combat Action and the dose is spent on the first damaging hit or after an hour) or **ingested** (Delay 1D6 minutes). The victim gets one Resilience test against it; a second dose of the same active poison does nothing more.
