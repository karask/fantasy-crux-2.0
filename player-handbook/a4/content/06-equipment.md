## Reading a Weapon

- **Damage:** the dice you roll; add DM for melee and thrown attacks.
- **STR/DEX:** the minimum scores to use it well. Below either (or both), your attacks and Parries with it are at **−1P**.
- **ENC:** weight and bulk. A dash means negligible.
- **Size:** decides how much a winning Parry blocks (Chapter Three). It is not the weapon's length.
- **Cost:** in silver pieces (SP); 10 copper (CP) make 1 SP, 10 SP make 1 gold (GP).

**Tags.** **1H** needs one hand; **2H** needs two. **Flex** works in one or two hands: two-handed, it deals **+2 damage** and its STR minimum is **2 lower**, but its Size does not change. **Set** can be braced against a Charge. **Thrown** can also be thrown without penalty. **Close** lets a ranged weapon attack with Close Combat within 2 m. **Missile** adds no DM.

Primitive or improvised versions of listed weapons deal 1 less damage (minimum 1). The weapon tables are on the next page.

## Using the Weapon Tables

> **Example.** A longsword in two hands deals 1D8 + 2 + DM and needs only STR 11 / DEX 11. It is still Medium, and with both hands busy you have no off-hand options.

Arrows, bolts and sling stones are **Light**. **Ammunition per 10:** arrows 1 SP, bolts 2 SP, sling bullets 5 CP.

A thrown weapon's Range depends on your STR. With **STR 12** a javelin has Range 24 m: normal out to 24 m and −2P out to 48 m.

A small shield can stay strapped to your arm while you use a 2H ranged weapon, but it cannot attack, Parry or Active Guard until you stop.

## Armour

Subtract your armour's **AP** from every hit, after any Parry. Wearing two armours does not add their AP.

| Armour | AP | ENC | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Leather | 2 | 3 | 500 SP |
| Ringmail | 3 | 4 | 1,000 SP |
| Scalemail | 4 | 6 | 1,500 SP |
| Chainmail | 5 | 7 | 3,000 SP |
| Platemail | 6 | 9 | 9,000 SP |

**Leather** is padded, boiled leather or layered linen; **ringmail** is rings sewn on padding; **scalemail** overlapping scales; **chainmail** interlinked rings; **platemail** fitted steel over chain.

Armour ENC counts toward your load **and** comes off your Combat Order. Wearers of **SIZ 1–5** pay half cost and ENC; **SIZ 21+** pay double. Ill-fitting plate can be worn at double ENC; other armour must fit well enough for the GM to allow it.

> **Example.** A hit deals 7 damage after Parry. Chainmail stops 5, so you lose 2 HP. A critical hit would ignore the armour entirely.

### Containers

Contents keep their ENC. A **backpack** holds 20 ENC, a **slingbag** 15, a **large sack** 10 and a **small sack** 5. A **quiver** holds 30 arrows or bolts, and a **waterskin** two days' water for one person. **Rope or a grappling hook** bears 50 ENC, 50 SIZ, or 50 of both together.

### Tools

- **Block and tackle:** needs 10 m of rope. +1B to Mechanisms with large traps, and makes suitable engineering work possible.
- **Climbing kit:** +1B to climb.
- **Crowbar:** +1B to force things with Athletics. As a weapon, a club at −1P.
- **Fish hook:** lets you fish with Natural Lore without an equipment penalty. A **fishing kit** gives +1B.
- **Flint and tinder:** lights an ordinary fire in one minute, with no test in normal conditions.
- **Healing kit:** five Healing or Surgery attempts; each attempt uses one, success or failure.
- **Lock picks:** +1B to open locks.
- **Hammer, mining pick or spade:** as a weapon, a club at −1P. A hammer or pick can strike stone or iron without being ruined.
- **Scythe:** as a weapon, a polearm at −1P.

### Light and fuel

- **Candle:** burns 1 hour and lights a 1 m radius. More than a slight breeze puts it out.
- **Lantern:** burns 2 hours on one flask of oil and lights a 3 m radius.
- **Torch:** burns 1 hour and lights a 3 m radius. Only a fumbled attack puts it out. As a weapon it is a club at −1P that deals **1D4 fire damage** instead of club damage.
- **Lamp oil,** spilled and lit, keeps a small fire going for one minute.
