## The Combat Round

A fight is split into **rounds of five seconds**. Anyone within **2 m** of you is at **Close** range; anything farther is **Ranged**.

### Who acts first

When combat starts, everyone rolls **1D10 + Combat Order**. The highest total acts first. Ties go to the higher DEX; if DEX also ties, those characters reroll 1D10. Keep this order for the whole fight.

### What you get each round

- **One Combat Action:** usually an attack, a skill or a spell.
- **One Movement Action:** move up to your Movement in metres, or something similar.
- **One base Reaction:** used outside your turn when something triggers it, such as an attack that hits you.

Take your Combat and Movement Actions on your turn, in either order. A Reaction interrupts the order only long enough to resolve its trigger. Unused actions are lost at the end of the round.

You cannot choose an option that would give up an Action or Reaction you have already used another way. After Parrying, for example, you cannot Sprint, because Sprint keeps your Reaction for Dodging only.

{{fig:round}}

### Combat Actions

- Make one attack with Close Combat, Unarmed Combat or Ranged Combat.
- Start or escape a grapple.
- Aim, Set a weapon, or Intimidate.
- Cast a Shaping or activate a magic item.
- Use a skill or object that takes about five seconds.
- **Delay:** act later this round, right after someone you choose. To interrupt an action already starting, make an opposed test with the skills involved; the winner acts first.

### Movement Actions

- Move up to your Movement in metres.
- Stand up, or drop prone.
- Disengage and move away (an enemy who can reach you may make an Opportunity Attack).
- **Ready** one item, or nock an arrow, while moving no more than half your Movement. Stowing one item and drawing another, or readying two, takes the whole Movement Action. Readying never shortens a crossbow reload.

## Attack and Defend

**To attack,** roll Close Combat for a held weapon, Unarmed Combat for a punch, kick or natural weapon, or Ranged Combat for a weapon used at a distance. Roll at or under your skill to hit. A miss needs no defence.

**If you are hit** and aware of the attack, you can spend **one Reaction** to defend before damage. Only one Reaction can answer one attack, even if you have more. An unaware defender cannot React.

- **Dodge:** roll Dodge; if you win, the attack misses. Against ranged attacks, Dodge at −1P. You can Dodge **once per round**, and only with your base Reaction.
- **Parry:** roll Close Combat with a ready weapon or shield, or Unarmed Combat against an unarmed attack. A winning Parry blocks some or all of the damage, depending on Size (below). Parrying a missile needs Active Guard.
- **Opportunity Attack:** when an adjacent enemy disengages or gives you a clear opening, make one Close Combat attack.

**The defence is an opposed test** against the attacker's original roll; the attacker never rolls again. Compare grades, then the higher successful roll, then base skill, then the defender wins. A failed defence never stops a hit.

| Incoming attack | Dodge | Weapon Parry | Shield Parry |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Melee or unarmed | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Thrown weapon | −1P | AG −1P | AG |
| Arrow, bolt, sling stone | −1P | No | AG −1P |
| Projected Shaping at you | −1P | No | AG −1P |
| Projected Shaping, area | −1P | No | No |
| Direct Shaping | No | No | No |

*AG: Active Guard (Ranged Attacks, below). Armour protects against all of these except Direct Shaping, which you resist with Resilience or Persistence instead (Chapter Eight).*

### Parry Size

Every weapon and shield has a **Size: Light, Medium, Heavy or Huge.** After a winning Parry, compare your parrying item with the attacking weapon.

{{fig:parry}}

Unarmed attacks and arrows, bolts and sling stones are **Light**. A small shield is Medium, a medium shield Heavy, and a large shield Huge. A losing Parry blocks nothing.

## Damage

When an attack hits, resolve the defender's Reaction first, then:

{{fig:damage}}

1. **Roll damage:** the weapon's dice, plus DM for melee, unarmed and thrown attacks, plus any extra dice from the action or Talent used.
2. **Parry:** a winning Parry blocks all, half (rounded up) or none, by Size.
3. **Armour:** subtract the target's AP.
4. **Vitality reserve:** a magical reserve, if any, absorbs damage next.
5. **Lose HP:** the rest comes off current HP. Neither damage nor HP goes below 0.

> **Example.** An ogre's Heavy club hits you for 12. Your arming sword is Medium, one Size smaller, so your winning Parry blocks half: 6 remain. Your leather armour (2 AP) stops 2 more. You lose 4 HP.

A single hit that deals damage **at least equal to your MWL** after armour causes a Major Wound, and at 0 HP you are Dying (Chapter Four).

### Critical hits

An attack that rolls a Critical is a **critical hit** unless the defence answers it:

| Defender's Reaction | Result |
| --- | --- |
| None, failed, or a losing Critical | **Maximum** weapon damage, plus maximum positive DM; **armour is ignored**. |
| Ordinary successful Dodge or Parry | The hit becomes **ordinary**: roll normal damage, then armour. The defence blocks nothing. |
| Winning Critical Dodge or Parry | No damage. |

A hit that stays critical ignores a negative DM and all other extra damage (such as a Charge's +1D6 or a Talent's bonus). A critical gives no extra attack or manoeuvre.

**Critical defence.** A Critical Dodge or Parry that wins against an ordinary hit stops all damage, whatever the Sizes.

**Fumbles.** On a fumbled attack or defence, the GM applies one immediate consequence that fits the risk: you fall, drop or damage your weapon, or leave yourself exposed.

## Your Other Hand

Holding **two ready one-handed items** gives you **one** extra option each round. An empty hand counts, because a punch is a weapon: sword and dagger, sword and shield, sword and free hand, or two bare fists all qualify. A two-handed weapon leaves no hand free.

- **Extra attack:** right after a standard Close or Unarmed Combat attack (hit or miss), make one free attack with the other hand at **−1P**. It costs no Action or Reaction; DM applies to both attacks.
- **Extra Reaction:** keep the other item ready for one extra **Parry or Opportunity Attack at −1P**. It can be an Active Guard, but never a Dodge.

Using either option rules out the other until next round, and your base Reaction stays available either way. A Charge, Set Weapon attack or Opportunity Attack never earns the extra attack, and losing all Reactions also loses the extra one.

> **Example.** Seris swings her sword (a standard attack), then stabs with her dagger at −1P. She cannot also keep an extra Parry this round, but she still has her base Reaction.

## Moving in a Fight

{{fig:moves}}

**Disengage.** A Movement Action to step away; an enemy who can reach you may spend a Reaction on an Opportunity Attack.

**Withdraw.** Use your Combat and Movement Actions to move your normal Movement **without** provoking Opportunity Attacks. You keep your Reactions.

**Sprint.** Use your Combat and Movement Actions to move up to **twice your Movement**. Your only Reaction this round is your base Reaction, and only to Dodge.

**Charge.** Use your Combat and Movement Actions and give up **all Reactions** this round. Move in a straight line along a clear route, **at least 5 m and at most twice your Movement**, ending next to your target. Then make one Close Combat attack; a hit deals **+1D6 damage**. You make no other movement this round.

**Set Weapon.** Use your Combat Action to brace a ready weapon tagged **Set**, such as a spear. If anyone Charges into its reach before your next turn, you attack them first at **+1B**, before their Charge attack. It works once, costs nothing more, and ends if you take any Action or Reaction first.

## Grappling

Grab an enemy to hold them rather than hurt them.

**Seize.** Spend a Combat Action against an adjacent target, committing one free grasping limb (or two). Roll **Unarmed Combat.** An aware target can spend one Reaction to oppose you with **Dodge or Parry**; if it does not, you need only succeed. If you win, you hold them; if not, your hands stay free.

**Weapon Parry.** If the target beats your seizing attempt with a successful weapon Parry, its weapon also deals you **1 HP**, ignoring armour.

**While held,** neither of you can take Movement Actions, both suffer **−1P on every test**, and both can attack or Parry only unarmed or with a ready **Light one-handed weapon.** A committed limb cannot attack, Parry or count as your other hand.

**Second limb.** Committing a second limb gives **+1B** to seizing, escaping and opposing grapple tests. Either side can add or release one at the start of their turn; the holder cannot release the last without ending the hold.

**Escape.** The held character spends a Combat Action and wins **Unarmed Combat against Unarmed Combat.** Opposing an escape costs the holder no Reaction. The holder can let go freely on their own turn; either way both stay adjacent.

Only one character can hold a target. Allies can assist one grapple test (+1B in total). The GM judges size, anatomy and footing.

## Ranged Attacks

Spend a Combat Action and test **Ranged Combat** against a target in range.

{{fig:range}}

- **Up to the weapon's Range (R):** attack normally.
- **Beyond R, up to 2R:** −2P.
- **Beyond 2R:** you cannot hit.

**Aim.** Spend a Combat Action aiming at one target you can see: your next ranged attack against it gains **+1B**. You lose the aim if you take any Reaction, lose sight of the target, or do not shoot with your next Combat Action.

**Damage.** Thrown weapons add your DM and keep their listed Size. Bows, crossbows and slings add no DM; their missiles are Light.

**At close range.** Within 2 m, a ranged weapon tagged **Close** can attack with Close Combat. Any other becomes an improvised Light club: **1D6 + DM, at −1P.**

### Cover

Cover makes you harder to hit; it does not reduce damage.

| Cover | Attacker's roll |
| --- | --- |
| Partial (a low wall) | −1P |
| Substantial (battlements) | −2P |
| Complete | No attack possible |

### Defending against missiles

Against a ranged hit you can Dodge at −1P, or **Active Guard**: a Parry with a ready item, rolled with Close Combat.

| Incoming | Ready weapon | Ready shield |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thrown weapon | −1P | no penalty |
| Arrow, bolt or stone | — | −1P |
| Projected Shaping | — | −1P |

Missiles are Light, so a winning Active Guard with any shield blocks them completely. Holding a shield does not penalise attackers by itself, and a shield cannot guard while you wield a two-handed weapon.

### Reloading crossbows

- **Light crossbow:** reloading uses your Movement Action **and** your base Reaction.
- **Heavy crossbow:** reloading uses the whole round: Combat Action, Movement Action and all Reactions.

### Shooting into a crowd

Apply cover normally. An ordinary miss just misses. Only a **Fumble** hits someone else: a nearby target chosen fairly or at random, who takes normal damage after armour.

> **Example.** Nerin aims (+1B) at a bandit behind a low wall (−1P). The dice cancel, so she rolls her plain Ranged Combat. If she hits, the bandit may still Dodge at −1P.

## Position and Circumstances

Use a modifier only when the situation clearly calls for it. Different causes add together, but use only the strongest version of the same condition (such as movement or obscurity). Then cancel and cap at +3B / −3P as usual.

| Ranged attack | Modifier |
| --- | --- |
| Target moved 10 m+ since your last turn | −1P (30 m+: −2P) |
| Target partly obscured (mist, smoke, foliage) | −1P (heavily: −2P) |
| Target prone | −1P |
| Target helpless | +2B |
| High wind / fierce wind | −1P / −3P |
| You are prone | −2P |
| You are on unstable ground | −1P |
| You are engaged at Close range | −2P per enemy |

| Close Combat | Modifier |
| --- | --- |
| Target helpless | Automatic critical |
| Target prone, flanked or attacked from behind | +1B to attack |
| Higher ground | +1B to that side's rolls |
| Attacker or defender prone | −1P to that side's rolls |
| Unstable ground | −1P |
| Underwater | −2P |
| Partial darkness / Darkness / Pitch black | −1P / −2P / −3P |

A prone defender therefore gives the attacker +1B and takes −1P on its own Reaction.

**Behind is not unseen.** An attack from behind gains +1B, but the defender can still React if it knows you are there. Only a genuinely unaware defender cannot React.

### Mounted combat

A rider gains **+1B** on Close Combat attacks and Parries against an adjacent opponent on foot, who suffers **−1P** on Reactions against the rider. Neither applies if the opponent is as tall as the rider or uses a weapon at least two Sizes larger. Use the mount's Movement; a lance Charge uses the mount's DM. A rider's weapon skill cannot exceed their Riding. On a mount unused to battle, test Riding at the start of each round; on a failure it Sprints away at its first chance.

## Intimidate

Spend a Combat Action to demand surrender or drive enemies off: **your Influence against their Persistence.** A group rolls once, using its leader's Persistence or Influence, whichever is higher. You need no common language, only to make yourself understood.

Give the enemy's roll the **one** modifier that best fits:

| Enemy's condition | Modifier |
| --- | --- |
| Full strength, or only minor wounds | +2B |
| Outnumbers you but has lost 20% of its numbers or HP | +1B |
| Fewer than you and already wounded | −1P |
| Lost over half its HP, or half the group is down | −2P |

If you win, the GM decides how the enemy gives way. Wounded enemies may flee or surrender; healthy ones usually hesitate and lose a turn. While the enemy is at full strength or outnumbers you, only your Critical against its failed Persistence forces a surrender. If it **fumbles**, it breaks and flees at once.
