## Skills in Play

Describe what you do; the GM picks the skill. Routine work with time and tools needs no roll.

### Athletics

| Activity | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Balance | Half Movement without a test; full speed needs Athletics. |
| Climb | Quarter Movement where there are holds; a success doubles it when speed or difficulty matters. |
| Swim | Half Movement in calm water; test in currents, storms or with a heavy load. |
| Jump | With a 5 m run, a success clears twice your height across or half your height up. Standing jumps go half as far. Each extra metre across: −1P. |
| Brute force | Lift, drag, bend, break or push. |

### People

**Persuade or bargain.** Use Influence; the other side usually resists with Persistence or Influence. Success changes their immediate decision, never their basic self-preservation.

**Haggle.** Oppose Trade. The winner moves the price **10%** their way, **20%** on a Critical; double it if the loser Fumbles. A Trade success also values an item to within 10%.

**Rumours and contacts.** Streetwise takes **1D4 hours** to find information, a contact or illicit goods.

### Stealth and fine work

**Deception against Perception** covers sneaking, hiding things, disguise and sleight of hand. Picking a pocket or planting an item normally needs **a minute** of access.

**Locks and traps** use Mechanisms and normally take at least a minute. A device built to resist tampering opposes you with its maker's Mechanisms.

### Making and breaking

**Craft** takes about one day per 50 SP of finished value, with materials worth half that.

**Objects** usually need no attack roll. Subtract the object's AP from your damage, then its HP; at 0 HP it breaks. The GM warns you before hard materials put your weapon at risk.

### Mounts, wagons and boats

Walking a trained mount with tack, driving on flat ground and sailing calm water need no roll. Test Riding, Driving or Sailing when speed, terrain or weather make it dangerous.

### Tools

The right tool often gives +1B: a climbing kit to climb, a crowbar to force, lock picks to open locks. Without a Healing kit, Healing is at −2P. Chapter Six lists every tool and what it does.

## Travel

Daily distances assume **ten hours** of travel in fair conditions. Agree the route and pace as a group.

| Pace | Per day | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| March | 30 km | Ready to fight; no Fatigue test. |
| Hike | 50 km | On foot only; Fatigue test at day's end. |
| Ride slowly | 30 km | Walking mounts, pack animals, wagons. |
| Ride | 50 km | Casual riding. |
| Ride fast | 90 km | No wagons; riders and mounts test Fatigue at day's end. |

Multiply by the worst terrain on the way: **road 100%, light brush 80%, light woods or rolling hills 70%, heavy woodland 50%.**

**Navigate.** On an uncertain day, test **Natural Lore**, and again after a big change of route. Failure loses a quarter of the day's progress; a Fumble gets you lost.

**Track.** Find the trail, then test every 10 minutes while it is uncertain.

**Forage.** When food, water or shelter is not available, test Natural Lore once a day to find it.

## Load

Add up the ENC of your weapons, armour, containers and their contents. Your capacity is **STR + SIZ**. Twenty tiny items in a suitable container count as 1 ENC.

- **Over capacity:** −1P to physical and Fatigue tests, and half Movement.
- **Twice capacity** is the most you can carry.

## Fatigue

After strenuous activity, or during it if exhaustion might stop you, the GM calls for a **Resilience** test.

{{fig:fatigue}}

| State | All tests | Movement | Combat Order |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fatigued | −1P | ×¾ | −3 |
| Exhausted | −2P | ×½ | −6 |

Failing while fresh makes you **Fatigued**. Failing another strenuous test while Fatigued (at −1P) makes you **Exhausted**. A **Fumble** knocks you unconscious for 3D6 minutes; you wake Fatigued. **20 − CON hours** of complete rest (minimum 1) removes either state.

### Food, water and shelter

You can go without for a while: **shelter, CON hours; food, CON days; water, CON × 2 hours** (CON hours in arid land, CON ÷ 2 in extreme heat). After three days without food, Fatigue tests are at −1P even inside that time.

When the time runs out, each missing necessity costs **one Fatigue test at −1P and 1D6 damage per day**. That damage cannot heal until you fix the cause.

## Light and Darkness

Check the light **at the target** for seeing and attacking, and **at the mover** for Movement.

{{fig:light}}

| Light | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Illuminated | No penalty. |
| Partial darkness | Sight-based Perception and attacks −1P. |
| Darkness | Those tests −2P; half Movement. |
| Pitch black | No sight at all: Perception by sight and ranged attacks cannot succeed; Close attacks −3P; quarter Movement. |

A light source illuminates its **radius**: **candle 1 m, torch or lantern 3 m, campfire 5 m, bonfire 10 m.** Chapter Six gives how long each one burns. Out to 3× the radius is partial darkness, out to 5× is Darkness, and beyond is pitch black unless another light reaches it.

Smoke, mist and foliage are **obscurity**, not darkness: they penalise attacks separately (Chapter Three).

### Other senses

Use only your **best** applicable sense; they never stack.

- **Low-Light Sight:** partial darkness counts as Illuminated and Darkness as partial; no help in pitch black.
- **Night Vision:** a natural night outdoors counts as Illuminated; no help underground or in magical darkness.
- **Blind Sight:** ignores light wherever its sense (such as heat) reaches the target, but shows no colour, writing or fine detail, and its own blockers still apply.

## Hazards

### Falling

Take **1D6 per full 3 m**, ignoring armour, and land prone. If you are conscious, not surprised and able to control the landing, test **Athletics**: a success removes 1D6 (a Critical, 2D6) and you land on your feet; a Fumble maximises the remaining dice.

### Air and fire

You can hold your breath for **CON rounds**. Then test Resilience every round at a cumulative −1P (to −3P). At the first failure you take the effect below at once, then again at the end of every round until you reach air, with no more tests.

| Exposure | Each round |
| --- | --- |
| Water inhaled | 2D6, armour does not help |
| Thick smoke inhaled | 1D6, armour does not help |
| Poison gas | Its poison (plus 1D6 if also smoky) |
| Candle / torch / campfire | 1 / 1D4 / 1D6 |
| Bonfire, steam or burning room | 2D6 |
| Lava or blast furnace | 3D6 |

Metal armour gives no protection from heat. Back in clean air, test Resilience or spend **1D4 rounds** retching.

### Poison and disease

After the poison's **Delay**, oppose your **Resilience against its Potency.** Win and you avoid it; lose and you suffer its full effect. Poison gives **one** test. A disease repeats the test after each Delay until you win, and its damage cannot heal while it lasts. A successful Healing treatment gives **+1B** to your next test.
