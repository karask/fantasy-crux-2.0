## How Shaping Works

There are no fixed spell lists. A Shaper builds each effect from a **cell**: a **Technique** (what you do) joined to a **Form** (what you affect). Unmake·Fire harms with fire; Ward·Fire protects against it; Scry·Mind reads thoughts. Six Techniques and ten Forms make sixty possible cells, and you learn each one separately.

To cast, you describe what you want, price it in **Magnitude (M)**, roll your **Shaping** skill, and pay **Power Points** equal to the Magnitude.

## Becoming a Shaper

### Shaping
*20 IP · a supernatural initiation or discovery · Passive*

The Talent gives you:

- the **Shaping** skill (Knowledge), base **INT + POW**;
- one **specialty**: a Form or a Technique;
- **two cells** that contain your specialty;
- the **Sense Magic** and **Dispel** actions, which need no cell.

> **Example.** Ilyra's specialty is Fire. She learns Unmake·Fire and Ward·Fire. That gives her no Conjure·Fire and no other Unmake cell.

At creation, reserve the 20 IP before you allocate skill points (Chapter Two). With INT 14 and POW 13, Shaping starts at 27%; adding 30 Knowledge points makes it 57%.

**Later.** A new cell with your specialty costs **2 IP**; any other cell costs **4 IP** and a significant ordeal or a master. Shaping itself improves like any skill.

**Practice and Tell.** Decide how you cast (your **Practice**: words, gestures, a focus, prayer, trance) and how your magic shows (your **Tell**: a glow, a chill, whispering). Anyone can interrupt the Practice; if it is prevented, you cannot cast. The Tell appears around you and any ranged target. Anyone watching notices it, and so does anyone within **10 × M metres** (minimum 3 m). It reveals that magic happened, not what it does.

**INT and POW.** POW is your fuel (maximum PP). INT is how much magic you can keep running at once (Keeping Magic Active, below).

## Forms and Techniques

| Form | Governs, and its limits |
| --- | --- |
| Fire | Flame, heat and light. Fire it ignites in mundane fuel then burns by the normal rules. |
| Water/Ice | Water, cold and ice. Dousing flames comes naturally; slowing someone is not free. |
| Air/Storm | Air, wind, sound, weather and lightning. No free knockback. |
| Earth/Stone | Soil, stone and natural minerals; not most worked metal. |
| Flesh | Biological matter, living or dead: people, beasts, plants, bone, corpses, corporeal undead. |
| Mind | Thought, emotion, memory, perception and choice in any mind that is not Mindless, even a Soulless one. |
| Force/Motion | Kinetic force, movement, weight and mass. |
| Spirit | Souls, spirits, possession and spiritual bonds. A Soulless subject is invalid. |
| Paths | Routes, thresholds, distance, destinations and spatial links. Never time travel. |
| Fate | Chance not yet decided, omens, vows and declared contingencies. Never a settled event or a forced choice. |

| Technique | What it does, and its limits |
| --- | --- |
| Conjure | Create a substance, object, barrier, phenomenon, route or being. Nothing it makes strikes on arrival. |
| Bend | Move, direct or control what exists. One paid consequence, no direct damage. |
| Unmake | Damage, destroy, suppress, sever or banish. All direct magical damage belongs here. |
| Alter | Heal, transform, modify or impose a condition. No direct damage, no extra Actions or Reactions. |
| Ward | One protection, resistance or denial. No absolute immunity below Intensity 5. |
| Scry | Learn, sense or communicate, without changing the subject. |

Your Practice and Tell must make sense for both halves of the cell. Only **Dispel** ends an active Shaping; no cell does.

**Bodies, minds and souls.** All undead are Soulless, so Spirit cannot touch them; Mind affects undead that are intelligent. Bend·Flesh restrains or puppets a body but gives it no beliefs or loyalty. Alter·Flesh heals the living and repairs corporeal undead, but restores no soul or memory. Force/Motion handles mass (pushing, lifting, pinning); Flesh works limbs, tissue and actions.

**The cell supplies the method.** The benchmarks below price *what* a result does, not which cell can do it. Alter·Flesh can grow climbing pads, Bend·Force/Motion can hold someone against a wall, and Earth can shape handholds, but none can borrow another's method because the result looks alike. Blindness from changed eyes, altered perception, quenched light or smoke in the way uses different cells, targets and defences.

## Building a Shaping

State it in one sentence, and the GM confirms it and how the target can defend before you roll:

*"Using **Unmake·Fire**, I cause **2D6 harm** at **15 m**, to **one target**, **instantly**."*

Then price it. Each part has a rating from 0 to 5, and Magnitude is their sum:

{{fig:formula}}

### Intensity: how strong

Rate each **outcome** (one thing the Shaping does) at the lowest Intensity that covers it.

| I | Degree | Typical outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Trick | Cosmetic only. |
| 1 | Minor | A small change, clue, hindrance or movement. |
| 2 | Potent | Harm, restraint, +1B/−1P, moving a person. |
| 3 | Major | Flight, a lost Combat Action, distant sight. |
| 4 | Greater | Control, +2B/−2P, impossible changes. |
| 5 | Mythic | Break a fundamental boundary; ritual only. |

### Range, Duration and Reach

| | Range | Duration | Reach |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **0** | Self or touch | Instant, or to the subject's next turn | One person or object |
| **1** | 15 m | A scene or 10 minutes | 2 targets or 3 m radius; a wagon |
| **2** | 50 m | Until next full rest (max 8 h) | 5 targets or 10 m; a room |
| **3** | Any visible point | One week ‡ | 20 targets or 50 m; a building ‡ |
| **4** | A known target in this world ‡ | Until dismissed ‡ | 100 targets or 500 m; a village ‡ |
| **5** | Another world ‡ | Permanent ‡ | An army, city or region ‡ |

*‡ Ritual only (Rituals, below). Any rating of 5 also needs a lasting mythic price.*

- **Range** 1–3 needs a clear route to the target; seeing through a window is not enough.
- **Duration** keeps a state going. It never repeats damage, healing or a lost action.
- **Reach** uses whichever is highest: number of targets, radius, or size of the largest subject. An area includes your allies too.

### Adjustments

| Adjustment | M | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Another outcome | + its I | A second, different outcome from the same cell. |
| Direct Harm | +1 | Harm arises inside the target. |
| Selective ★ | +1 | Leave chosen subjects out of an area. |
| Indirect ★ | +1 per barrier | Cross a barrier, where the Form allows. |
| Trigger ★ | +1 and a wait Duration | Wait for an event, then fire once. |
| Confluence ★ | +1 | Combine two known cells. |
| Veiled ★ | +1, −1P to cast | Hide your Tell. |

*★ Needs the Talent of that name (Shaping Talents, below).*

### Your limits

| Shaping | Expertise | Max I | Safe M | Most M, normal | Most M, ritual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–25% | Novice | 1 | 1 | 3 | 3 |
| 26–50% | Apprentice | 2 | 2 | 4 | 4 |
| 51–75% | Veteran | 3 | 3 | 5 | 6 |
| 76–99% | Expert | 4 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| 100% | Master | 5 | 5 | 7 | 10 |

No outcome can exceed your **maximum Intensity**, whatever PP or help you have. Casting above **Safe M** is **overreach**: harder, and dangerous if it fails. Intensity 5 always needs a ritual.

### Two ready builds

> **Firebolt.** Unmake·Fire: I2 harm + Range 1 + Duration 0 + Reach 0 = **M3**. It deals 2D6 Projected harm once; normal armour applies. Safe for a Veteran.

> **Stone skin for two allies.** Ward·Earth/Stone: I2 + Range 1 + Duration 1 + Reach 1 = **M5**: 4 AP against physical blows for a scene. For a Veteran (Safe M 3) that is overreach at −2P.

### More than one outcome

A second outcome from the same cell needs no extra training: add its Intensity. Outcomes must be different. One Shaping cannot buy damage, healing, movement, restraint, control or another cumulative outcome twice on the same subject; buy a higher Intensity instead.

> **Example.** Alter·Flesh healing at I3 plus +1B Resilience at I2, at touch, is **M5**. It cannot buy healing twice.

### Rules for every Shaping

1. **Price outcomes, not descriptions.** Use the lowest Intensity that covers each outcome. Magic can rival a specialist, but it does not erase skills, equipment or preparation.
2. **Duration never repeats.** Damage, healing, a lost Action or an answer happens once, however long the Duration.
3. **Damage is dealt once.** It never recurs each round, adds to every weapon hit, or strikes back when a protected subject is attacked.
4. **Consequences are real, not free.** A pushed foe can fall, but no consequence can hide free damage, modifiers, conditions or resources.
5. **Same-kind magic does not stack.** Use only the strongest magical modifier, armour or protection of a kind. Mundane circumstances still combine, up to +3B/−3P.
6. **No extra actions or resources.** Shaping never widens a critical range or grants extra Actions, Reactions, Dodges, PP, IP, Hero Points, Talents, skills or memories.
7. **Only Dispel ends active magic.** Unmake can damage a manifestation or exploit a true natural counter, but only Dispel ends a Shaping.
8. **Created matter is temporary.** It vanishes when its Duration ends and leaves no value, materials, PP or anchors. A complex object still needs the relevant Craft or Mechanisms.

## Casting

Casting takes **one Combat Action**; you keep your Movement Action and Reactions. You need your Practice, a valid target and route, room in your active limit, and at least **full M in PP** available.

1. Agree the cell, outcomes, M and the target's defence.
2. Roll **Shaping**: −1P at Safe M + 1, −2P at Safe M + 2. No modifier ever removes this overreach penalty.
3. A **Counter** (if anyone tries) is resolved, then the target's **defence**.
4. **Pay** PP by your result, then apply the effect.

| Your roll | You pay |
| --- | --- |
| Critical | **Half M**, rounded up. The effect is normal, not maximised. |
| Success | **Full M.** |
| Failure, at or below Safe M | Nothing happens; lose **1 PP**. |
| Failure, overreaching | Nothing happens; lose **half M** and suffer **Backlash**. |
| Fumble | Nothing happens; lose **full M** and suffer **Backlash**. |

A defence that stops the Shaping does not refund the cost. A Hero Point reroll comes before any PP loss or Backlash. If you reach 0 PP, the Shaping resolves first and then you fall unconscious.

**Backlash.** Failed overreach and Fumbles cause Backlash. Roll 1D4, or the GM chooses one that fits the Form and situation:

| 1D4 | Backlash |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Take **M damage**, ignoring armour; it can cause a Major Wound. |
| 2 | Lose another **M PP** (to a minimum of 0). |
| 3 | An immediate Reach-0 effect **one Intensity lower** strikes you, the nearest valid subject or your surroundings. At Intensity 0 it is only cosmetic trouble. |
| 4 | One Fatigue step: fresh becomes Fatigued, Fatigued becomes Exhausted, and Exhausted falls unconscious for 3D6 minutes and wakes Fatigued. |

Use the attempt's highest outcome Intensity. Backlash follows the Form you attempted, not a rewritten idea.

**Tricks.** A calm **Magnitude-0** Trick (lighting a candle at touch, a cosmetic glow on yourself) is free and automatic. Under pressure, a failure does nothing and a Fumble only gives an awkward Tell: no PP loss and no Backlash. A Trick at range or on several targets has Magnitude 1 or more and casts normally.

**Hostile touch.** To touch an unwilling target, first make a no-damage Unarmed Combat attack in the same Action; it can Dodge or Parry normally. If that is stopped, you spend the Action but no PP. If it lands, cast and the target defends normally.

## Defending Against Magic

Every defence is an **opposed test** against the caster's Shaping roll. The caster must succeed **and** win. (The critical-hit demotion of Chapter Three does not apply.)

| The magic… | Defence |
| --- | --- |
| Flies at you (one target) | Dodge −1P, or a shield's Active Guard −1P (a Reaction) |
| Fills an area you can escape | Dodge −1P (a Reaction) |
| Changes or restrains your body | Resilience (no Reaction) |
| Works on your mind or soul | Persistence (no Reaction) |
| Is willing, on the caster, or unattended | No defence |

Each subject defends separately. Against one subject, outcomes with the same defence share one roll; outcomes with different defences are rolled separately. A magical Critical keeps its grade in the opposed test, but if it wins it rolls normal effect dice. An ongoing area keeps its first result: leaving it ends its effect on you, and coming back gives no new defence. Attended objects use their bearer's defence.

### Projected or Direct harm

Choose how harm travels before you work out M.

{{fig:harm}}

- **Projected** (no extra M) crosses space. Cover penalises the caster (−1P partial, −2P substantial) and armour protects normally.
- **Direct** (+1 M) arises inside chosen targets, never an area. The target resists with Resilience (or Persistence against Spirit harm), and only magical Wards protect. It still needs a clear route.
- **Spirit harm** is always Direct and drains **I D6 PP**, not HP. Mind magic never deals HP damage.

### Shields against magic

Every Projected outcome has an **Impact Size: I1 Light, I2 Medium, I3 Heavy, I4 Huge, I5 beyond Huge.** A winning Active Guard compares your shield with it as for a Parry: equal or larger blocks the outcome and anything attached to it, one smaller blocks half the damage, two smaller blocks none. A winning Critical guard blocks everything. Weapons cannot guard against magic, and nobody can Active Guard an area, Direct harm or Spirit harm.

| Shield (Size) | I1 | I2 | I3 | I4 | I5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small (Medium) | All | All | Half | None | None |
| Medium (Heavy) | All | All | All | Half | None |
| Large (Huge) | All | All | All | All | Half |

Missile Guard removes the −1P; Shield Cover applies before Active Guard (use it or terrain cover, not both).

## What Magic Can Do

Pick the lowest Intensity that buys the result. The cell explains *how*; it never adds free damage or protection.

### Harm, heal and protect

| Outcome | Intensity buys |
| --- | --- |
| Harm (Unmake) | **I D6** damage, no DM. |
| Heal (Alter·Flesh) | **I D4 HP**, up to maximum. |
| Ward | **2 × I AP** against one class of harm (below). Use it or worn armour, whichever is higher. |
| Barrier | A wall of **2 × I AP** and **5 × I HP**; Reach sets its size. |
| Vitality reserve | **3 × I** points that absorb HP damage after all other protection. |
| Skill bonus or penalty | **I2: +1B / −1P; I4: +2B / −2P** to one skill or narrow family. |

**Healing** also stops Bleeding and Dying; a living subject brought above 0 HP can act on its next turn. Like mundane care, only one HP-restoring Shaping helps a patient between full rests, including items and Confluence outcomes (stopping Bleeding and Dying alone is never limited). Below I3, healing restores HP to a **Wounded** patient but does not end Wounded or start natural recovery; **I3 or more counts as Surgery.** Shaping cannot regenerate: no healing spread over later turns or stored for future wounds.

**Curing** poison or disease needs **I × 25 ≥ its Potency**, and then your Shaping must beat the Potency. After a failure you need new treatment, a discovery, a full rest or some other real change before you try again. Fatigue, hunger and age need their own outcomes.

**Returning the dead** needs Intensity 5, permanent Duration, a mythic ritual, a willing soul within reach and a lasting price. The subject returns at 1 HP and Wounded.

### Wards and barriers

A Ward gives **one** benefit: AP against one class of harm; **+1B (I2) or +2B (I4)** to one defence; or **denial**, priced as restraint, where each crossing opposes the right defence against your stored roll.

| Class of harm | Covers |
| --- | --- |
| Physical | Blows, missiles, falls and crushing. |
| Elemental | Fire and heat, cold, lightning and acid. |
| Spiritual | PP damage. Only Ward·Spirit protects against it. |

A class covers mundane and Shaped harm alike: an elemental Ward stops a burning building and a firebolt. Covering physical **and** elemental harm costs +1 I, and the cell must explain both. Ward·Fire protects only against elemental harm, Ward·Force/Motion only against physical harm. **Mind, Paths and Fate give no AP:** Ward·Mind gives a bonus to Persistence or denies one intrusion such as illusion or control; Ward·Paths bars passage or arrival at a threshold; Ward·Fate gives a bonus against a named doom.

Ward AP never adds to worn armour against one source: use the higher. A **barrier** blocks movement and line of effect until destroyed, and cannot appear where something already stands. Trapping someone in one is restraint: they Dodge or resist with Resilience. Forms that cannot make solid structures make no barriers.

**Vitality reserve.** It absorbs HP damage after Parry, armour and every other reduction; only damage that reaches real HP counts toward a Major Wound. It never refills, cannot be healed, and ignores PP damage and paid HP costs. A subject has **one** reserve at a time: keep the larger. It ends when emptied or when its Duration runs out.

**Transformation** changes appearance and form only. AP, attacks, movement, senses or Size benefits are separate outcomes, guided by a real creature's profile.

### Move, sense and breathe

| Capability | Benchmarks |
| --- | --- |
| New way to move | **I2** climb, swim or walk a named surface at normal Movement; **I3** fly. |
| Pass through matter | **I4** one named substance at half Movement. |
| Faster movement | **I1** +3 m; **I2** ×1.5; **I3** ×2; **I4** ×3. |
| Senses | **I1** Low-Light Sight; **I2** Night Vision; **I3** Blind Sight; **I4** a broad sense. |
| Breathing | **I1** hold breath 10 minutes; **I2** breathe one medium; **I3** no need to breathe. |

You use your own Movement Action for a granted movement and choose where to go; it needs Duration but no concentration. A speed outcome boosts one named movement mode; work it out first, then apply Fatigue, darkness or load. If flight ends in mid-air, you fall. A movement mode is permission, not success: Athletics may still be needed in violent currents, on unstable surfaces or in tight gaps. Passing through matter grants no protection or sight inside it, and if you cannot get out in the same Movement Action you return to where you went in.

Senses never stack: use the best one. Blind Sight needs a named sense and a declared blocker. Breathing water does not let you swim, and not breathing does not protect you from heat, cold, pressure or contact poisons.

**Sensory impairment** works on a perceiver: **I2** −1P with one sense, **I3** that sense suppressed, **I4** every sense the cell governs. A changed body resists with Resilience, a changed perception with Persistence. **Blinded** means sight-based Perception and ranged attacks cannot succeed, Close attacks are at −3P and Movement is quartered, unless another sense supplies what sight would. **Invisible** to sight (I3+ concealment) applies the same attack and Perception limits only against the hidden subject; footprints, opened doors and other traces remain. Concealment and impairment never add their penalties together.

**Scry.** **I1** an immediate clue or presence; **I2** locate, diagnose, communicate or see through ordinary concealment; **I3** distant sight, surface thoughts, deep evidence; **I4** guarded memories, history, motives, possible futures. Watching live, talking or reading thoughts needs concentration. Scry finds only what exists to be found.

### Conditions and control

| I | Hostile outcome |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Prone, or a brief hindrance (half Movement to the next turn). |
| 2 | Restrain; −1P to one skill or one sense; a reasonable small compulsion. |
| 3 | Blind one sense; take **one** Combat Action once; a serious compulsion. |
| 4 | No Actions or Reactions; full control of body or mind. |

Name exactly what the condition does: "paralysed" gives no free damage or helplessness. If a condition truly includes helplessness, price it in and give a way out: magical sleep can make its subject helpless, but ends at once on damage or when someone adjacent spends a Combat Action to wake it. A lost Combat Action never repeats, and the target cannot lose another until it completes one. Conditions of the same kind do not stack.

| I | Influence and control |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Colour an emotion or plant a plausible suggestion. |
| 2 | Compel one small, reasonable act that costs no combat Action and is not against the subject's interests. |
| 3 | Compel an act that costs or denies a combat Action, puts the subject at a disadvantage or goes against its interests. |
| 4 | Full control. |

**Breaking free.** On your turn, spend a Combat Action to repeat your original defence against the Shaper's **stored** roll; win and you are free. If you cannot take Combat Actions, try at the end of each of your turns instead.

> **Example.** A bind was cast with a success of 42. Your Resilience is 60%; you roll 55, a higher success, and break free. The Shaper does not roll again.

**Control.** Compelled actions use the subject's own turn. Full control takes the Shaper's Combat Action each round, and moves an unwilling body at most 3 m a round. Orders of serious self-harm or betrayal give a new Persistence test; certain death always does.

**Removing conditions.** Find the cause first. Active magic ends only by Dispel. Poison and disease use the curing rule, and Wounded still needs Surgery. **Fatigue:** I3 removes Fatigued or turns Exhausted into Fatigued; I4 removes Exhausted. Another mundane condition needs at least the Intensity that could impose it (restoring a suppressed sense is I3) and a cell that can reverse its cause. If the cause remains, the condition can come back. A "curse" is no special category: Dispel an active one, reverse a permanent change with a new Shaping, and settle an oath or debt on its own terms.

### Illusions, Paths and Fate

**Hiding and illusions.** I2 gives −1P to notice through one sense; I3 hides from one sense completely; I4 from every sense the cell governs. Conjure·Mind shows something absent; Alter·Mind changes or hides what is there. Reach counts **minds**, each resisting with Persistence; anyone not targeted, including later arrivals, sees normally, and Mindless creatures are never fooled. A fixed illusion needs no concentration; steering it does. Illusions never harm. A suspicious witness can spend a Combat Action on Perception against the stored roll: success shows it is false, but neither ends it nor reveals what it hides, and after a failure they need new evidence to try again. Physical cells can reach the same result where they allow it (Bend·Fire bends light, Bend·Air/Storm sound, Alter·Flesh appearance), using their real targets and defences. Fog and smoke give ordinary obscurity.

**Paths.** Bend·Paths I2 moves a willing subject, or an unwilling one up to 3 m; I3 relocates an unwilling one anywhere within Range (I4 if that removes it from the fight). An unwilling subject resists with Resilience. Conjure·Paths I3+ opens a **stationary, one-way portal**; making it move or work both ways is another outcome. Crossing takes a Movement Action, and sight, attacks and objects cross too: Range is measured to the entrance plus from the exit to the target, and cover at either opening counts. Reach limits each traveller's size and the total number that can cross; the portal closes when that allowance is used. Range must reach both the subject or entrance and the destination. Arrival must be empty, stable, big enough and safe, or nobody moves. Paths gives **no free falling, collision, restraint, suffocation or damage.** A visible moving opening, or an area, is avoided with Dodge at −1P.

**Fate.** Fate works only on chance not yet decided. Declare it in full: *"When [observable event] happens before [expiry], [named subject] gets [exact paid effect] on [named test, narrow endeavour or consequence]."* I1 gives an omen or coincidence with no numbers; I2 gives +1B/−1P and I4 +2B/−2P. An event later than the casting needs Trigger and fires once. Hostile Fate is resisted with Persistence; vows never compel obedience, and Scry·Fate shows possibilities, not certainty. Fate deals no HP damage and grants no rerolls, automatic success, extra Actions, chosen die results, wider critical ranges, changed damage rolls or changes to the past.

### Summons

**Conjure** creates a temporary being from its Form; **calling** brings an existing being that belongs to a Form, or one linked to you. You can have only **one** combat-capable summon, conjured or called, or one actively controlled subject, at a time. Reach cannot get round this.

**Standing orders.** When you cast, give one short order ("guard me", "attack the ogre", "hold this door"). The summon acts on its own turn, right after yours, with its own Combat Action, Movement Action and base Reaction, including Opportunity Attacks when it is told to guard or attack. A Mindless summon follows the order literally; one with a mind uses judgement. Changing the order costs your **Movement Action**, once a round, within 15 m and line of effect. Out of range, or while you are unconscious, it keeps its last order. It needs Duration but no concentration, so hurting you does not end it. It ends at 0 HP, at the end of its Duration, when you dismiss it, or by Dispel.

**Conjured beings.**

| I | HP | AP | Attack/defence | Damage | Strike Size | SIZ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3 | 0 | 25% | 1D3 | Light | 3 |
| 2 | 6 | 1 | 40% | 1D6 | Medium | 9 |
| 3 | 10 | 2 | 55% | 1D8 | Medium | 13 |
| 4 | 14 | 3 | 70% | 2D6 | Heavy | 18 |

The attack covers its natural attacks; the defence covers Dodge, Resilience, Persistence, Perception and physical Parry. It has Movement 15, PP 0 and no DM, ignores Major Wounds and vanishes at 0 HP. At Reach 0 it is human-sized or smaller; at **Reach 1** it is mount-sized: +50% HP, Movement 20, strike Size one step larger and room for one rider.

Shaping never makes a soul, so every conjured being is Soulless. Its Form sets its body, tags, one trait and one drawback, free:

| Form | Being and tags | Trait | Drawback |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fire | Flame being; Elemental | Immune to fire; sheds torchlight; its strikes ignite exposed fuel | Dousing, or a hit by Water/Ice Shaping, deals +1D6; cannot enter deep water |
| Water/Ice | Water or ice being; Elemental | Swims at full Movement; immune to cold and drowning | Movement 10 on land |
| Air/Storm | Wind being; Elemental | Flies at full Movement at I3+, otherwise hovers; immune to falling | AP 0 at every I; cannot carry, grapple or block a passage |
| Earth/Stone | Stone being; Elemental | +2 AP | Movement 10; cannot swim, fly or climb |
| Flesh | Beast; Living, Corporeal, animal mind | Low-Light Sight and scent; Alter·Flesh heals it | Affected by Mind, poison, disease and Bleeding |
| Force/Motion | Force construct; Mindless | Lifts and carries as STR 18; immune to poison and disease | Perceives only by touch and where you direct it |
| Mind | Phantasm (an illusion) | Any appearance; can speak | Never combat-capable: moves and harms nothing |

Spirit, Paths and Fate conjure no beings; Spirit and Paths call them instead. Conjure·Flesh can also animate remains as a **Mindless, Soulless undead** summon from the table, with no trait or drawback; the remains collapse at 0 HP or when Duration ends. Anything more (flight for another Form, invisibility, extra armour, special senses, spellcasting) raises the Intensity or adds an outcome by its own benchmark. **A mind** for a Mindless being, or **losing the drawback**, costs +1 I each. A conjured being cannot be Incorporeal.

**Calling beings.** Only beings that belong to a Form, or that you hold a true link to, can be called. Ordinary beasts, monsters and people cannot: move one already in Range with Bend·Paths, sway it with Bend·Mind, or conjure a being instead.

| Being | Cell | Range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A Spirit | Conjure·Spirit | The arrival point |
| An Elemental | Conjure·Fire (Salamander or Shade), ·Water/Ice, ·Air/Storm or ·Earth/Stone | The arrival point |
| A servant of a power you are pledged to (a Holy Steed or Holy Warrior) | Conjure·Paths | 4 through that link; otherwise 5 |
| A specific individual linked to you by true name, token or blood | Conjure·Paths | Must also reach where it is; usually 4 |

The Intensity is the being's **Threat Intensity**, and cannot exceed your maximum: **I2** Small Elemental; **I3** Ancestor Spirit, Ghost; **I4** Medium Elemental, Disease, Healing, Magic and Passion Spirits, Holy Steed; **I5** Large and Huge Elementals, Holy Warrior. An anchored being cannot be called beyond its anchor's boundary.

An unwilling being resists with **Persistence or 20 × its Threat Intensity %**, whichever is higher; one that comes by pact or bargain makes no roll. If you win, it arrives bound for the Duration and serves under standing orders with its own profile; afterwards a Spirit or Elemental returns to its Form, while a specific individual stays where it is, unbound, and remembers how it was treated. If it wins, it does not come, and you still pay. On a Fumble the GM may have it arrive free and hostile instead of Backlash. A called being with Shaping casts with its own PP and cells, but its ongoing Shapings count against **your** active limit. No created, summoned, transformed or compelled being can summon, give PP, make magical resources or pay ritual prices.

## Keeping Magic Active

The total M of all your **ongoing** Shapings cannot exceed your **INT**. Instant effects count nothing once resolved. You can dismiss your own magic on your turn, free.

{{fig:active}}

**Everything ongoing counts** its full M: Wards, barriers, summons, control, movement, senses, concealment, conditions, reserves, waiting Triggers and tied anchors. Only enchantments held in Enchantment Capacity are exempt. A Critical still counts full M, and a temporary change to INT does not change the limit.

**Passive effects** (armour, flight, a sense) need only Duration; they cost no further action. Range and route are checked when the Duration starts, and afterwards only for concentration or control.

**Indefinite magic** (and an anchored enchantment made without Enchanter) commits its full M from current **and maximum** PP, even on a Critical, until it ends. Leave at least 1 maximum PP, and nobody else can pay this for you.

**Created matter and hazards.** Created matter can give terrain, obscurity, barriers or restraint, but never automatic damage each round, and a harmful creation cannot appear on top of an unwilling subject. Damage when it appears is a separately paid Unmake outcome. Fire lit in real fuel, a collapsing real wall and similar changed surroundings then follow the normal rules.

**Concentration** (steering an effect, live scrying, controlling someone) takes your Combat Action each round, for one effect at a time, with the subject in Range. If you are hurt or badly distracted, test Persistence or it ends.

### Sense Magic
Combat Action and **1 PP**: test Shaping to detect active magic within **15 m**. Success reveals where and its Form; a Critical also reveals its Technique, M and whether it is concentrated, triggered or anchored. A Fumble gives a false impression and a conspicuous Tell, but no Backlash. Veiled magic opposes you with its original roll.

### Dispel
Combat Action: cast with **M = the target's full M + Range** (Reach 0, whatever its area), paying normally. Then win an opposed test against a fresh Shaping roll for the original caster (or 10 × its M % if unknown). Success ends the whole Shaping. Completed permanent changes cannot be Dispelled.

## Rituals

You need a ritual for **M above Safe + 2, Intensity 5, Range 4+, Reach 3+ or Duration 3+**. A ritual never raises your maximum Intensity.

**Prepare.** Spend **one hour per M** in a prepared place, with your full Practice and a fitting focus or offering. The ritual's limit is Safe M + 2 or twice Safe M, whichever is greater.

**Test.** Up to Safe M: no penalty; 1–2 above: −1P; 3+ above: −2P. Then add the ritual's **+1B**, help and circumstances. Failing above Safe M is still overreach.

**Share the PP.** Willing participants who stay and join the Practice can pledge PP in a declared order, the leader paying at least 1. One capable Shaper can assist the roll. Only the leader rolls and suffers Backlash; on a Fumble every contributor also takes one Fatigue step, and an Exhausted one falls unconscious for 3D6 minutes. On a Critical, unused pledges are not spent.

**Lasting magic.** For an indefinite Duration, the leader alone commits the full M from both current and **maximum** PP until it ends. Any rating of 5 is **mythic** and needs a declared lasting price: permanent POW, a unique treasure, a binding oath, a supernatural debt, a permanent change or a quest. The price never lowers M or Dispel strength. Materials are used when the ritual starts; POW, oaths and debts are taken once it succeeds, and on a Fumble only if announced beforehand.

**Permanent results.** A permanent mundane creation needs equivalent material; it and a finished permanent transformation become real and inactive, and only a new Shaping reverses them. Supernatural properties stay active as enchantments on a vulnerable anchor, which can be Dispelled.

## Shaping Talents

### Counter
*4 IP · Shaping 51% · Reaction*

When you see a successful Shaping cast or arriving within 15 m, spend your **base Reaction** (and have PP at least its M). Oppose your Shaping against its roll; succeed and win to stop it. Pay its **full M** either way; a Fumble also causes Backlash at that M. Counter **replaces** your own defence: if it fails, you cannot also Dodge or resist. Only one Counter can answer a Shaping, and a Counter cannot itself be Countered.

### Steady Casting
*3 IP · Shaping 76% · Passive*

Ignore one Penalty die caused by being Wounded or grappled when you cast.

### Shaping Expertise
*5 IP · Shaping 76% · Passive*

Once per round, remove one remaining Penalty die from a casting roll. It changes no limits and does not help Sense Magic, Dispel or Counter.

### Selective
*3 IP · Shaping 51% · Passive*

**+1 M:** choose subjects inside an area that the Shaping leaves alone.

### Indirect
*3 IP · Shaping 51% · Passive*

**+1 M per barrier:** reach past a barrier where the Form allows, to target, steer or control something behind it.

### Veiled
*3 IP · Shaping 51% · Passive*

**+1 M and −1P:** hide your Tell; noticing it needs Perception against your Shaping.

### Confluence
*5 IP · Shaping 76% · Passive*

**+1 M:** combine **two** known cells in one Shaping, each giving its own outcome. One Action, one roll, one payment; each outcome keeps its own defence.

### Trigger
*4 IP · Shaping 76% · Passive*

**+1 M plus a Duration for the wait:** store a fully built Shaping until an observable event you name. Everything is fixed when you cast it; the target defends when it fires.

> **Example.** "When I next hit that ogre this scene, sear it for 2D6." Unmake·Fire: I2 + Range 1 + Direct 1 + Trigger 1 + wait Duration 1 = **M6**. The hit fires it once and the ogre resists with Resilience. A miss leaves it waiting; the end of the scene ends it.

A trigger can never retaliate when someone is attacked or add damage to every hit.

### Enchanter
*10 IP · Shaping 51% · Passive*

Gain **Enchantment Capacity** equal to your **POW** (Magical Items, below).

## Magical Items

Anyone can use an enchanted item; no skill or cell is needed. It shows its maker's Tell, and a hostile effect uses the maker's stored roll.

| Kind | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Continuous | Works while worn or held; reduces your **maximum PP** by its M until you take it off. |
| Activated | Combat Action and **M PP** for each use. |
| Charged | Combat Action and **one charge**; you pay no PP. Potions and scrolls have one. |

**Identify.** Sense Magic reveals that it is magic and its Form. Scry with the matching Form at I2 tells its use; I3 reveals a hidden curse, limit or trigger. Do not assume what an unknown item does.

**End.** Destroy its anchor, or Dispel it.

### Making enchantments

An enchantment is a Shaping bound to a physical or spiritual **anchor** in a ritual, with Duration 4 (or mythic Duration 5). Your expertise still limits its M and I.

**With Enchanter,** each effect uses capacity instead of your PP: its full M for a continuous or activated effect, or its M **for every unused charge.** Only an effect that ends for good frees capacity.

> **Example.** With POW 12, an activated M4 item uses 4 capacity and two M3 charges use 6 more: 10 in all. A third M3 charge would not fit until one is used.

**Without Enchanter,** an enchantment commits the maker's full M from maximum PP and active M while it lasts.

**One-use items** (potions, scrolls) price the effect with its own Duration plus 1 for binding: a 1D4 healing potion is M2. Binding still takes one hour per M. The maker pays the PP when binding it and, until it is used, it takes Enchantment Capacity, or commits maximum PP and active M without Enchanter, like any other enchantment.
