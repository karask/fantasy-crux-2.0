## How Shaping Works

There are no fixed spell lists. A Shaper builds each effect from a **cell**: a **Technique** (what you do) joined to a **Form** (what you affect). Unmake·Fire harms with fire; Ward·Fire protects against it; Scry·Mind reads thoughts. Six Techniques and ten Forms make sixty possible cells, and you learn each one separately.

To cast, you describe what you want, price it in **Magnitude (M)**, roll your **Shaping** skill, and pay **Power Points** equal to the Magnitude.

## Becoming a Shaper

### Shaping
*20 IP · a supernatural initiation or discovery · Passive*

The Talent gives you:

- the **Shaping** skill (Knowledge), base **INT + POW**;
- one **specialty**: a Form or a Technique;
- **two cells** that contain your specialty;
- the **Sense Magic** and **Dispel** actions, which need no cell.

> **Example.** Ilyra's specialty is Fire. She learns Unmake·Fire and Ward·Fire. That gives her no Conjure·Fire and no other Unmake cell.

At creation, reserve the 20 IP before you allocate skill points (Chapter Two). With INT 14 and POW 13, Shaping starts at 27%; adding 30 Knowledge points makes it 57%.

**Later.** A new cell with your specialty costs **2 IP**; any other cell costs **4 IP** and a significant ordeal or a master. Shaping itself improves like any skill.

**Practice and Tell.** Decide how you cast (your **Practice**: words, gestures, a focus, prayer, trance) and how your magic shows (your **Tell**: a glow, a chill, whispering). Anyone can interrupt the Practice; if it is prevented, you cannot cast. The Tell appears around you and any ranged target. Anyone watching notices it, and so does anyone within **10 × M metres** (minimum 3 m). It reveals that magic happened, not what it does.

**INT and POW.** POW is your fuel (maximum PP). INT is how much magic you can keep running at once (Keeping Magic Active, below).

| --- | --- |

| Fate | Chance, omens, vows and contingencies. |

| Technique | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Conjure | Create matter, phenomena, routes or beings. |

Your Practice and Tell must make sense for both halves of the cell. Only **Dispel** ends an active Shaping; no cell does.

## Building a Shaping

State it in one sentence, and the GM confirms it and how the target can defend before you roll:

*"Using **Unmake·Fire**, I cause **2D6 harm** at **15 m**, to **one target**, **instantly**."*

Then price it. Each part has a rating from 0 to 5, and Magnitude is their sum:

### Intensity: how strong

Rate each **outcome** (one thing the Shaping does) at the lowest Intensity that covers it.

| I | Degree | Typical outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Trick | Cosmetic only. |
| 1 | Minor | A small change, clue, hindrance or movement. |
| 2 | Potent | Harm, restraint, +1B/−1P, moving a person. |
| 3 | Major | Flight, a lost Combat Action, distant sight. |
| 4 | Greater | Control, +2B/−2P, impossible changes. |
| 5 | Mythic | Break a fundamental boundary; ritual only. |

### Range, Duration and Reach

| | Range | Duration | Reach |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **0** | Self or touch | Instant, or to the subject's next turn | One person or object |
| **1** | 15 m | A scene or 10 minutes | 2 targets or 3 m radius; a wagon |
| **2** | 50 m | Until next full rest (max 8 h) | 5 targets or 10 m; a room |
| **3** | Any visible point | One week ‡ | 20 targets or 50 m; a building ‡ |
| **4** | A known target in this world ‡ | Until dismissed ‡ | 100 targets or 500 m; a village ‡ |
| **5** | Another world ‡ | Permanent ‡ | An army, city or region ‡ |

*‡ Ritual only (Rituals, below). Any rating of 5 also needs a lasting mythic price.*

- **Range** 1–3 needs a clear route to the target; seeing through a window is not enough.
- **Duration** keeps a state going. It never repeats damage, healing or a lost action.
- **Reach** uses whichever is highest: number of targets, radius, or size of the largest subject. An area includes your allies too.

### Adjustments

| Adjustment | M | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Another outcome | + its I | A second, different outcome from the same cell. |
| Direct Harm | +1 | Harm arises inside the target. |
| Selective ★ | +1 | Leave chosen subjects out of an area. |
| Indirect ★ | +1 per barrier | Cross a barrier, where the Form allows. |
| Trigger ★ | +1 and a wait Duration | Wait for an event, then fire once. |
| Confluence ★ | +1 | Combine two known cells. |
| Veiled ★ | +1, −1P to cast | Hide your Tell. |

*★ Needs the Talent of that name (Shaping Talents, below).*

### Your limits

| Shaping | Expertise | Max I | Safe M | Most M, normal | Most M, ritual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–25% | Novice | 1 | 1 | 3 | 3 |
| 26–50% | Apprentice | 2 | 2 | 4 | 4 |
| 51–75% | Veteran | 3 | 3 | 5 | 6 |
| 76–99% | Expert | 4 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| 100% | Master | 5 | 5 | 7 | 10 |

No outcome can exceed your **maximum Intensity**, whatever PP or help you have. Casting above **Safe M** is **overreach**: harder, and dangerous if it fails. Intensity 5 always needs a ritual.

### Two ready builds

> **Firebolt.** Unmake·Fire: I2 harm + Range 1 + Duration 0 + Reach 0 = **M3**. It deals 2D6 Projected harm once; normal armour applies. Safe for a Veteran.

> **Stone skin for two allies.** Ward·Earth/Stone: I2 + Range 1 + Duration 1 + Reach 1 = **M5**: 4 AP against physical blows for a scene. For a Veteran (Safe M 3) that is overreach at −2P.

## Casting

Casting takes **one Combat Action**; you keep your Movement Action and Reactions. You need your Practice, a valid target and route, room in your active limit, and at least **full M in PP** available.

1. Agree the cell, outcomes, M and the target's defence.
2. Roll **Shaping**: −1P at Safe M + 1, −2P at Safe M + 2. No modifier ever removes this overreach penalty.
3. A **Counter** (if anyone tries) is resolved, then the target's **defence**.
4. **Pay** PP by your result, then apply the effect.

| Your roll | You pay |
| --- | --- |
| Critical | **Half M**, rounded up. The effect is normal, not maximised. |
| Success | **Full M.** |
| Failure, at or below Safe M | Nothing happens; lose **1 PP**. |
| Failure, overreaching | Nothing happens; lose **half M** and suffer **Backlash**. |
| Fumble | Nothing happens; lose **full M** and suffer **Backlash**. |

A defence that stops the Shaping does not refund the cost. A Hero Point reroll comes before any PP loss or Backlash. If you reach 0 PP, the Shaping resolves first and then you fall unconscious.

**Backlash** is the GM's to describe: harm, extra PP loss, a stray effect or Fatigue.

**Tricks.** A calm Intensity-0 Trick (lighting a candle, a cosmetic glow) is free and automatic. Under pressure, a failure does nothing and a Fumble only gives an awkward Tell.

**Hostile touch.** To touch an unwilling target, first make a no-damage Unarmed Combat attack in the same Action; it can Dodge or Parry normally. If that is stopped, you spend the Action but no PP. If it lands, cast and the target defends normally.

## Defending Against Magic

Every defence is an **opposed test** against the caster's Shaping roll. The caster must succeed **and** win. (The critical-hit demotion of Chapter Three does not apply.)

| The magic… | Defence |
| --- | --- |
| Flies at you (one target) | Dodge −1P, or a shield's Active Guard −1P (a Reaction) |
| Fills an area you can escape | Dodge −1P (a Reaction) |
| Changes or restrains your body | Resilience (no Reaction) |
| Works on your mind or soul | Persistence (no Reaction) |
| Is willing, on the caster, or unattended | No defence |

Each subject defends separately. An ongoing area keeps its first result: leaving it ends its effect on you, and coming back gives no new defence.

### Projected or Direct harm

Choose how harm travels before you work out M.

{{fig:harm}}

- **Projected** (no extra M) crosses space. Cover penalises the caster (−1P partial, −2P substantial) and armour protects normally.
- **Direct** (+1 M) arises inside chosen targets, never an area. The target resists with Resilience (or Persistence against Spirit harm), and only magical Wards protect. It still needs a clear route.
- **Spirit harm** is always Direct and drains **I D6 PP**, not HP. Mind magic never deals HP damage.

### Shields against magic

Every outcome has an **Impact Size: I1 Light, I2 Medium, I3 Heavy, I4 Huge.** A winning Active Guard compares your shield with it as for a Parry: equal or larger blocks all, one smaller blocks half the damage, two smaller blocks none. Weapons cannot guard against magic.

## What Magic Can Do

Pick the lowest Intensity that buys the result. The cell explains *how*; it never adds free damage or protection.

### Harm, heal and protect

| Outcome | Intensity buys |
| --- | --- |
| Harm (Unmake) | **I D6** damage, no DM. |
| Heal (Alter·Flesh) | **I D4 HP**, up to maximum. |
| Ward | **2 × I AP** against one kind of harm: physical, elemental or spiritual (both physical and elemental: +1 I). Use it or worn armour, whichever is higher. |
| Barrier | A wall of **2 × I AP** and **5 × I HP**; Reach sets its size. |
| Vitality reserve | **3 × I** points that absorb HP damage after all other protection. |
| Skill bonus or penalty | **I2: +1B / −1P; I4: +2B / −2P** to one skill or narrow family. |

**Healing** also stops Bleeding and Dying. At I3 or more it counts as Surgery. Like mundane care, only one HP-restoring Shaping helps a patient between full rests (stopping Bleeding and Dying is never limited). **Curing** poison or disease needs **I × 25 ≥ its Potency**, and then your Shaping must beat the Potency.

**Protection.** Keep only the strongest magical effect of the same kind. A reserve never refills. A barrier blocks movement and cannot appear where something already stands; trapping someone in one lets them Dodge or resist with Resilience.

### Move, sense and breathe

| Capability | Benchmarks |
| --- | --- |
| New way to move | **I2** climb, swim or walk a named surface at normal Movement; **I3** fly. |
| Pass through matter | **I4** one named substance at half Movement. |
| Faster movement | **I1** +3 m; **I2** ×1.5; **I3** ×2; **I4** ×3. |
| Senses | **I1** Low-Light Sight; **I2** Night Vision; **I3** Blind Sight; **I4** a broad sense. |
| Breathing | **I1** hold breath 10 minutes; **I2** breathe one medium; **I3** no need to breathe. |

You use your own Movement Action for a granted movement. If flight ends in mid-air, you fall.

**Scry.** **I1** an immediate clue or presence; **I2** locate, diagnose, communicate or see through ordinary concealment; **I3** distant sight, surface thoughts, deep evidence; **I4** guarded memories, history, motives, possible futures. Watching live, talking or reading thoughts needs concentration. Scry finds only what exists to be found.

### Conditions and control

| I | Hostile outcome |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Prone, or a brief hindrance (half Movement to the next turn). |
| 2 | Restrain; −1P to one skill or one sense; a reasonable small compulsion. |
| 3 | Blind one sense; take **one** Combat Action once; a serious compulsion. |
| 4 | No Actions or Reactions; full control of body or mind. |

Name exactly what the condition does: "paralysed" gives no free damage or helplessness. A lost Combat Action never repeats, and the target cannot lose another until it completes one.

**Breaking free.** On your turn, spend a Combat Action to repeat your original defence against the Shaper's **stored** roll; win and you are free. If you cannot take Combat Actions, try at the end of each of your turns instead.

> **Example.** A bind was cast with a success of 42. Your Resilience is 60%; you roll 55, a higher success, and break free. The Shaper does not roll again.

**Control.** Compelled actions use the subject's own turn. Full control takes the Shaper's Combat Action each round, and moves an unwilling body at most 3 m a round. Orders of serious self-harm or betrayal give a new Persistence test; certain death always does.

**Fatigue.** I3 removes Fatigued or turns Exhausted into Fatigued; I4 removes Exhausted.

### Illusions, Paths and Fate

**Hiding and illusions.** I2 gives −1P to notice through one sense; I3 hides from one sense completely; I4 from every sense the cell governs. Conjure·Mind shows something absent; Alter·Mind changes what is there. Reach counts **minds**, each resisting with Persistence. Illusions never harm. A suspicious witness can spend an Action on Perception against the stored roll to see through it.

**Paths.** Bend·Paths I2 moves a willing subject, or an unwilling one up to 3 m; I3 relocates an unwilling one within Range (I4 if that removes it from the fight). Conjure·Paths I3+ opens a one-way portal; crossing takes a Movement Action. Arrival must be safe and empty, or nobody moves.

**Fate.** I1 gives an omen or coincidence; I2 gives +1B/−1P and I4 +2B/−2P on a named test or endeavour before it is rolled. No rerolls, automatic success or changes to the past.

### Summons

**Conjure** creates a temporary being from its Form; a successful **calling** brings an existing one bound to a Form or linked to you by name, token or blood. You can have only **one** combat-capable summon or controlled subject at a time.

| I | HP | AP | Skill | Damage | Size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3 | 0 | 25% | 1D3 | Light |
| 2 | 6 | 1 | 40% | 1D6 | Medium |
| 3 | 10 | 2 | 55% | 1D8 | Medium |
| 4 | 14 | 3 | 70% | 2D6 | Heavy |

A conjured being has Movement 15, no PP and no DM, ignores Major Wounds and vanishes at 0 HP. Its Form gives it a body, one trait and one drawback; a mind, or losing the drawback, costs +1 I each. Spirit, Paths and Fate conjure no beings.

**Orders.** Give one short standing order ("guard me", "hold this door"). The summon acts right after you with its own actions. Changing the order costs your Movement Action, within 15 m. A called being resists with Persistence or 20 × its Threat Intensity %, whichever is higher; on a Fumble it may arrive free and hostile.

## Keeping Magic Active

The total M of all your **ongoing** Shapings cannot exceed your **INT**. Instant effects count nothing once resolved. You can dismiss your own magic on your turn, free.

{{fig:active}}

**Passive effects** (armour, flight, a sense) need only Duration; they cost no further action. A Critical still counts full M.

**Concentration** (steering an effect, live scrying, controlling someone) takes your Combat Action each round, for one effect at a time, with the subject in Range. If you are hurt or badly distracted, test Persistence or it ends.

### Sense Magic
Combat Action and **1 PP**: test Shaping to detect active magic within **15 m**. Success reveals where and its Form; a Critical also reveals its Technique, M and state.

### Dispel
Combat Action: cast with **M = the target's full M + Range**, paying normally. Then win an opposed test against a fresh Shaping roll for the original caster (or 10 × its M % if unknown). Success ends the whole Shaping. Completed permanent changes cannot be Dispelled.

## Rituals

You need a ritual for **M above Safe + 2, Intensity 5, Range 4+, Reach 3+ or Duration 3+**. A ritual never raises your maximum Intensity.

**Prepare.** Spend **one hour per M** in a prepared place, with your full Practice and a fitting focus or offering. The ritual's limit is Safe M + 2 or twice Safe M, whichever is greater.

**Test.** Up to Safe M: no penalty; 1–2 above: −1P; 3+ above: −2P. Then add the ritual's **+1B**, help and circumstances. Failing above Safe M is still overreach.

**Share the PP.** Willing participants who stay and join the Practice can pledge PP in a declared order, the leader paying at least 1. One capable Shaper can assist the roll. Only the leader rolls and suffers Backlash; on a Fumble every contributor also becomes one step more Fatigued.

**Lasting magic.** For an indefinite Duration, the leader alone commits the full M from both current and **maximum** PP until it ends. Any rating of 5 is **mythic** and needs a lasting price agreed with the GM.

## Shaping Talents

### Counter
*4 IP · Shaping 51% · Reaction*

When you see a successful Shaping cast or arriving within 15 m, spend your **base Reaction** (and have PP at least its M). Oppose your Shaping against its roll; succeed and win to stop it. Pay its **full M** either way. Counter **replaces** your own defence: if it fails, you cannot also Dodge or resist.

### Steady Casting
*3 IP · Shaping 76% · Passive*

Ignore one Penalty die caused by being Wounded or grappled when you cast.

### Shaping Expertise
*5 IP · Shaping 76% · Passive*

Once per round, remove one remaining Penalty die from a casting roll. It changes no limits and does not help Sense Magic, Dispel or Counter.

### Selective
*3 IP · Shaping 51% · Passive*

**+1 M:** choose subjects inside an area that the Shaping leaves alone.

### Indirect
*3 IP · Shaping 51% · Passive*

**+1 M per barrier:** reach past a barrier where the Form allows, to target, steer or control something behind it.

### Veiled
*3 IP · Shaping 51% · Passive*

**+1 M and −1P:** hide your Tell; noticing it needs Perception against your Shaping.

### Confluence
*5 IP · Shaping 76% · Passive*

**+1 M:** combine **two** known cells in one Shaping, each giving its own outcome. One Action, one roll, one payment; each outcome keeps its own defence.

### Trigger
*4 IP · Shaping 76% · Passive*

**+1 M plus a Duration for the wait:** store a fully built Shaping until an observable event you name. Everything is fixed when you cast it; the target defends when it fires.

> **Example.** "When I next hit that ogre this scene, sear it for 2D6." Unmake·Fire: I2 + Range 1 + Direct 1 + Trigger 1 + wait Duration 1 = **M6**. The hit fires it once and the ogre resists with Resilience. A miss leaves it waiting; the end of the scene ends it.

A trigger can never retaliate when someone is attacked or add damage to every hit.

### Enchanter
*10 IP · Shaping 51% · Passive*

Gain **Enchantment Capacity** equal to your **POW** (Magical Items, below).

## Magical Items

Anyone can use an enchanted item; no skill or cell is needed. It shows its maker's Tell, and a hostile effect uses the maker's stored roll.

| Kind | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Continuous | Works while worn or held; reduces your **maximum PP** by its M until you take it off. |
| Activated | Combat Action and **M PP** for each use. |
| Charged | Combat Action and **one charge**; you pay no PP. Potions and scrolls have one. |

**Identify.** Sense Magic reveals that it is magic and its Form. Scry with the matching Form at I2 tells its use; I3 reveals a hidden curse, limit or trigger. Do not assume what an unknown item does.

**End.** Destroy its anchor, or Dispel it.

### Making enchantments

An enchantment is a Shaping bound to a physical or spiritual **anchor** in a ritual, with Duration 4 (or mythic Duration 5). Your expertise still limits its M and I.

**With Enchanter,** each effect uses capacity instead of your PP: its full M for a continuous or activated effect, or its M **for every unused charge.** Only an effect that ends for good frees capacity.

> **Example.** With POW 12, an activated M4 item uses 4 capacity and two M3 charges use 6 more: 10 in all. A third M3 charge would not fit until one is used.

**Without Enchanter,** an enchantment commits the maker's full M from maximum PP and active M while it lasts.

**One-use items** (potions, scrolls) price the effect with its own Duration plus 1 for binding: a 1D4 healing potion is M2.
