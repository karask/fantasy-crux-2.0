# Shaping — Freeform Magic for Fantasy Crux

Shapers combine a **Technique** (what magic does) with a **Form** (what it governs), then set **Intensity, Range, Duration,** and **Reach**.

> **Core formula:** `Intensity + Range + Duration + Reach + adjustments = Magnitude.` Intensity sets the effect; Magnitude sets PP requirement and cost, difficulty, active limit, and Dispel strength. Pay PP by result.

Unless noted, Shaping uses core D100 and opposed tests, Bonus/Penalty dice, Reactions, armour, damage, wounds, Power Points, and Improvement.

---

## 1. Becoming a Shaper

Access costs **8 IP**, requires a supernatural initiation or discovery, and grants:

- the **Shaping** skill, base `INT + POW`;
- one specialty: a Technique or a Form;
- two known Technique–Form **cells** containing that specialty; and
- **Sense Magic, Counter,** and **Dispel**.

Cells are exact: Unmake·Fire grants no other Unmake or Fire cell.

| Advancement | IP |
|---|---:|
| New cell containing the specialty | 2 |
| Any other cell, plus a significant ordeal or master | 4 |
| Improve Shaping | Normal skill improvement |

Shaping caps at 100% and may improve once between adventures. At creation it is Knowledge and follows the 30-point limit; later learning requires suitable experience.

### Practice and Tell

- **Practice:** an interruptible requirement—speech, gestures, focus, prayer, or trance. Preventing it prevents casting.
- **Tell:** an unavoidable sign around the Shaper and any ranged target or area. It reveals magic, not purpose.

Cosmetic style is free. Concealing the Tell requires **Veiled**.

## 2. Building a Shaping

Declare one known cell and one clear outcome:

> Using **[Technique]·[Form]**, I cause **[outcome]** at **[Range]**, affecting **[Reach]**, for **[Duration]**.

The Gamemaster confirms the build; useful Shapings require Intensity 1+.

### Intensity

Choose the lowest row that covers the outcome.

| Intensity | Degree | Benchmark |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | Trick | Cosmetic; no practical benefit. |
| 1 | Minor | Ordinary action or tool: small change, hindrance, clue, or movement. |
| 2 | Potent | Specialist: harm, restraint, `+1B`/`-1P`, useful creation, or moving a person. |
| 3 | Major | Superhuman: flight, lost Combat Action, essential transformation, distant observation, or competent summon. |
| 4 | Greater | Encounter-changing: control, `+2B`/`-2P`, impossible transformation, or major threat. |
| 5 | Mythic | Break a fundamental boundary; ritual and lasting price required. |

A Magnitude-0 Trick is free and automatic when calm. Under pressure, failure does nothing; a fumble only causes an inconvenient Tell—never PP loss or Backlash. Other Tricks cast normally.

### Range

| Rating | Range |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Self or touch |
| 1 | Within 15 metres |
| 2 | Within 50 metres |
| 3 | Any visible point |
| 4 | Familiar, marked, or mystically linked target in the same realm; ritual |
| 5 | Another realm or plane; mythic ritual |

Range 1–3 needs an unobstructed route. **Indirect** crosses a barrier when the Form permits.

### Duration

| Rating | Duration |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Instant or until the end of the next turn |
| 1 | One scene or 10 minutes |
| 2 | Until the target next fully rests, maximum 8 hours |
| 3 | One week; ritual |
| 4 | Indefinite until dismissed or Dispelled; ritual |
| 5 | Permanent; mythic ritual |

Duration sustains states or observation, not repeated damage, healing, or answers. Concentration is maintenance; continuing beyond the next turn requires Duration 1+.

### Reach

Use the highest rating required by count, area, or subject size.

| Rating | Targets or area | Largest subject |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | One | Person, door, or human-sized object |
| 1 | Up to 3 or 3-metre radius | Wagon or large tree |
| 2 | Up to 10 or 10-metre radius | Room-sized structure |
| 3 | Up to 100 or 50-metre radius | Building or ship; ritual |
| 4 | Up to 1,000 or 500-metre radius | Settlement; ritual |
| 5 | Army, city, or region | Landscape-scale; mythic ritual |

Areas include allies. Excluding arbitrary subjects requires **Selective**.

### Adjustments

| Adjustment | Magnitude |
|---|---:|
| Additional outcome allowed by the same cell | Add its full Intensity |
| **Selective:** exclude chosen subjects from an area | +1 |
| **Piercing:** ignore otherwise applicable mundane armour | +1 |
| **Indirect:** cross a solid barrier | +1 per barrier |
| **Veiled:** conceal the Tell | +1 and `-1P` to Shaping |
| **Trigger:** wait for one observable event | +1, plus a second Duration rating for the wait |

Noticing a Veiled Tell requires Perception opposed by Shaping. A Trigger uses one Duration for its effect and another for its wait. It fires once; hidden qualities require Scry.

Each Shaping has one outcome. Extras must fit the cell and add their Intensity; otherwise cast separately. Natural consequences remain—a pushed foe may fall—but cannot hide free damage, modifiers, or conditions.

## 3. Techniques and Forms

### Techniques

| Technique | Permitted outcome |
|---|---|
| **Conjure** | Create substance, object, barrier, phenomenon, or being; no strike on arrival. |
| **Wield** | Move or control something existing; choose one consequence and deal no direct damage. |
| **Unmake** | Damage, destroy, suppress, or banish; direct magical damage belongs here. |
| **Shift** | Heal, transform, modify, or impose a condition; no direct damage or extra Actions/Reactions. |
| **Ward** | Grant one protection, resistance, or denial; no absolute immunity below Intensity 5. |
| **Scry** | Gain information, communication, or a sense without changing the subject. |

Unmake can damage manifestations or exploit natural counters; only **Dispel** directly ends an active Shaping.

### Forms

A Form defines valid subjects and usual defence, not free secondary effects.

| Form | Scope and limits | Usual hostile defence |
|---|---|---|
| **Fire** | Flame, heat, and light; ignited mundane fire uses core rules. | Dodge if projected; otherwise Resilience |
| **Water/Ice** | Water, cold, and ice; dousing a flame is natural, slowing is not free. | Dodge if projected; otherwise Resilience |
| **Air/Storm** | Air, wind, sound, weather, and lightning; no free knockback or piercing. | Dodge if projected; otherwise Resilience |
| **Earth/Stone** | Soil, stone, and natural minerals, not all worked metal. | Dodge if projected; Resilience for restraint/change |
| **Flesh** | Living bodies, disease, and biology; not constructs, spirits, or true undead. | Resilience |
| **Mind** | Thought, emotion, memory, perception, and choice; cannot deal HP damage. | Persistence |
| **Force/Motion** | Kinetic force, movement, weight, and mass. | Dodge if projected; Resilience for bodily control |
| **Spirit** | Souls, incorporeal beings, undead, possession, and spiritual bonds. | Persistence |

Direct damage uses worn armour unless **Piercing**. Environmental fire uses core rules; internal Flesh or Spirit damage requires Piercing. Wards may apply.

A doubting witness may spend a Combat Action to oppose Perception with an illusion's stored result. Success recognises falsehood; failure needs new evidence. Illusions do no direct harm, and recognition does not Dispel them.

### Example grid

These are examples, not fixed spells or free effects.

| Form | Conjure | Wield | Unmake | Shift | Ward | Scry |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fire | flame/light | steer fire | burn/extinguish | alter heat | resist fire | see heat/smoke |
| Water/Ice | water/ice | redirect current | crush/freeze | change state | resist cold | locate water |
| Air/Storm | mist/wind | direct air | thunder/lightning | alter weather | deflect wind | hear afar |
| Earth/Stone | stone/wall | move earth | shatter | shape/soften | physical armour | tremorsense |
| Flesh | tissue/life | puppet body | wither | heal/transform | bodily defence | diagnose |
| Mind | illusion | compel | suppress faculty | alter emotion | mental defence | read thoughts |
| Force/Motion | platform/hand | telekinesis | force bolt | alter momentum | kinetic barrier | sense movement |
| Spirit | manifest spirit | bind/command | harm/banish | restore soul | deny spirits | question dead |

## 4. Effect Benchmarks

| Outcome | Effect |
|---|---|
| Direct damage | Unmake deals `Intensity D6`; never add Damage Modifier. |
| Healing | Shift·Flesh restores HP equal to Intensity. |
| Armour or reduction | Ward grants AP equal to Intensity against one defined class of harm. |
| Barrier | AP = Intensity; total HP = `5 × Intensity`; Reach sets footprint. |
| Skill modifier | `+1B`/`-1P` at Intensity 2; `+2B`/`-2P` at Intensity 4. |
| Characteristic modifier | ±1 at Intensity 3; ±2 at Intensity 4, within normal limits. |
| Brief hindrance | Intensity 1 may halve Movement until the end of the next turn. |
| Restraint or forced movement | Intensity 2 restrains a human-sized subject or moves it up to 3 metres. |
| Flight | Intensity 3 moves a willing subject at normal Movement while controlled. |
| Lost Combat Action | Intensity 3, once; the target is immune until it completes a later Combat Action. |
| Full bodily or mental control | Intensity 4 and active control. |

Magic damage follows core Parry, armour, object, HP, and Major Wound rules. Piercing ignores applicable mundane armour; Mind uses conditions or suppressed faculties, not HP damage.

Healing removes **Bleeding** and **Dying**. Above 0 HP, the subject may act next turn in Combat Order. Wounded remains. Intensity 3+ counts as Surgery, unlocking recovery; Wounded ends after treatment once HP exceeds MWL.

Curing poison or disease needs Intensity `ceil(Potency / 25)` and Shaping to succeed and defeat Potency. Failure prevents another attempt until new treatment, discovery, full rest, or meaningful change.

Healing does not remove Fatigue, deprivation, age, or unrelated conditions without another outcome. Returning the dead requires Intensity 5, permanent Duration, a mythic ritual, a willing reachable soul, and a lasting price. They return at 1 HP and Wounded; a destroyed body must be restored separately.

A modifier names one skill or narrow family. Identical modifiers do not stack. Shaping never extends critical range or grants extra Actions, Reactions, Dodges, PP, IP, Hero Points, Talents, skills, or memories.

Temporary characteristics affect direct tests and physical thresholds, never prerequisites or derived values: base skills, HP, MWL, PP, DM, Combat Order, and active capacity. Permanent changes recalculate; permanent POW loss lowers maximum PP and active capacity, forcing dismissals until legal.

Transformation changes appearance and form. AP, attacks, movement modes, senses, Size benefits, and other mechanics are extra outcomes guided by an existing profile.

A restrained subject may spend a Combat Action to retry resistance against the stored result. Full control allows another resistance against serious self-harm, defining betrayal, or central-belief violation; certain death always does.

### Wards and created barriers

A Ward grants one benefit: AP/reduction equal to Intensity; `+1B` at Intensity 2 or `+2B` at Intensity 4 to one defence; or denial priced as restraint and resisted on each crossing attempt.

Ward AP stacks with worn armour only if the armour cannot protect from that source; otherwise use the higher AP. Same-kind magical protection never stacks.

A solid barrier uses the benchmark above and blocks movement and line of effect until destroyed. It cannot appear through occupied space. Placement that traps an unwilling subject is hostile restraint; the defender chooses Dodge or Resilience. A Form unable to make a solid structure cannot use barrier statistics.

### Scry

| Intensity | Information within purchased Range |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Detect a defined presence or reveal one immediate clue. |
| 2 | Locate, diagnose, communicate, or see through ordinary concealment. |
| 3 | Observe a distant scene, read surface thoughts, or reveal deeply hidden information. |
| 4 | Reach guarded memory, obscured history, motives, or uncertain possible futures. |

Scry gives evidence, not omniscience or knowledge absent from every accessible mind, place, spirit, trace, or pattern. Possible futures change when acted upon.

Live observation, communication, or thought-reading requires concentration; a static sense or single fact does not.

### Control and summons

A Shaper may have one combat summon or actively controlled subject total. Directing it costs the Shaper's Combat Action; a creature obeys on its Combat Order using the corresponding Action, never an extra one. Without maintenance, it regains control; an object stops where possible.

A combat summon acts immediately after the Shaper and may move, communicate, or defend uncommanded. A consequential action costs the Shaper's Combat Action and the summon's corresponding Action. It has Movement 15, one Movement Action, and one base Reaction.

| Intensity | HP | AP | Attack/defence | Damage |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 3 | 0 | 25% | `1D3` |
| 2 | 6 | 1 | 40% | `1D6` |
| 3 | 10 | 2 | 55% | `1D8` |
| 4 | 14 | 3 | 70% | `2D6` |

Listed defence covers Dodge, Resilience, and Persistence; damage adds no DM. Summons ignore Major Wounds and vanish at 0 HP. Intensity 5 calls a unique being. Each useful capability—including flight, incorporeality, spellcasting, invisibility, or special senses—raises minimum Intensity or adds an outcome: concept grants permission, not mechanics. Summons cannot summon, contribute PP, create resources, or pay ritual prices.

## 5. Casting and Defence

### Cast

1. Declare the cell and outcome; set the dials and adjustments.
2. The Gamemaster confirms cost, modifiers, defence, armour, and consequences.
3. Confirm current PP—or ritual pledges—can cover Magnitude and any ongoing effect fits the active limit.
4. Spend one Combat Action and test Shaping; the Movement Action remains available.
5. Resolve Counter and defence, pay PP, then apply the effect.

Practice, Tell, target, and line of effect are required unless Scry, Indirect, or ritual Range says otherwise.

| Casting result | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **Critical** | Success; pay half Magnitude, rounded up. Critical grade applies, but effect dice are not maximised. |
| **Success** | Pay full Magnitude. |
| **Failure within Safe Magnitude** | No effect; lose 1 PP. |
| **Failed overreach** | No effect; lose half Magnitude, rounded up, and suffer Backlash. |
| **Fumble** | No intended effect; lose full Magnitude and suffer Backlash. |

A stopped successful cast still pays. Resolve Hero Point rerolls before PP loss or Backlash. Effects begin before 0-PP unconsciousness; concentration then ends.

### Safe Magnitude and overreach

| Shaping | Expertise | Safe Magnitude |
|---:|---|---:|
| 0–25% | Novice | 1 |
| 26–50% | Apprentice | 2 |
| 51–75% | Veteran | 3 |
| 76–99% | Expert | 4 |
| 100% | Master | 5 |

Normal casting reaches Safe Magnitude + 2 at most: `-1P` one over and `-2P` two over. Higher Magnitude requires ritual and remains overreach even if Bonus dice cancel the penalty.

### Defence

Use one defence per outcome.

Outcomes sharing a defence share its roll. One Dodge answers every dodgeable outcome in one Shaping; different defences resolve separately.

| Effect | Defence | Reaction? |
|---|---|---:|
| Self, willing subject, unattended object, empty point | None | No |
| Visible bolt, burst, wave, or projectile | Dodge at `-1P` | Base Reaction only |
| Direct bodily change or control | Resilience | No |
| Thought, emotion, identity, soul, spiritual control | Persistence | No |

Resilience and Persistence are opposed: the Shaper must succeed and win. Neither spends a Reaction.

Dodge uses the casting result and combat matrix. A magical Critical resolves the normal effect, not maximum damage or ignored armour. Dodge remains limited to once per round and the base Reaction.

A hostile Touch Shaping first requires a no-damage Unarmed delivery attack—the sole defence exception. It cannot use a Talent or off-hand attack. The target may react. Failed contact ends without casting or PP loss; success proceeds to resistance. Contact Criticals add nothing.

Roll Shaping once for an area; subjects defend separately. An aware subject may Dodge a visible area at `-1P` using the combat matrix. Dodge grants no movement or prone condition. Attended objects use their bearer's defence.

### Cover and shields

When cover interrupts the route, partial gives `-1P`, substantial `-2P`, and complete prevents casting unless another route or Indirect applies.

The Shield Cover Talent penalises plausibly obstructed projected Shapings; use the better shield or terrain penalty. Internal Flesh, Mind, and Spirit effects gain none.

Tangible magical projectiles are Light for Active Guard—existing objects retain Size—and count as thrown weapons. A full-block Parry stops projectile and outcome; a lesser Parry reduces damage only. Flame, wind, lightning, pure Force, Flesh, Mind, and Spirit cannot be Parried. One Reaction answers an effect.

### Backlash

Roll `1D4` or let the Gamemaster choose:

| `1D4` | Result |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Take Magnitude damage ignoring armour; it can cause a Major Wound. |
| 2 | Lose another Magnitude PP, to a minimum of 0. |
| 3 | An immediate Reach-0 related effect at one lower Intensity strikes the Shaper, nearest valid subject, or surroundings. It gains no beneficial adjustment; Intensity 1 is cosmetic trouble. |
| 4 | Become Fatigued; if Fatigued, become Exhausted; if Exhausted, fall unconscious `3D6` minutes and awaken Fatigued. |

Backlash follows the attempted Form and situation, not a rewritten concept. Counter or Dispel spills use the target's Form at one lower Intensity; Intensity 0 is cosmetic trouble.

## 6. Ongoing Shapings

Active Magnitude cannot exceed POW. Count concentration, buffs, Wards, barriers, summons, control, Triggers, and tied anchors; ignore instant effects and completed permanent transformations. Dismiss freely on the Shaper's turn.

Check Range and route when a set-duration effect begins; it then needs attention only if controlled. Concentration and active control cost the Combat Action each round; only one may be maintained. Its subject must stay within purchased Range and route unless Range 4+, Indirect, or a Form link applies. Damage or distraction requires Persistence or ends it. Summon commands require 15 metres and line of effect. Record each ongoing effect's Magnitude and result.

Only the strongest magical modifier, armour, or protection of the same kind applies. Mundane circumstances combine normally under the `+3B`/`-3P` cap.

Temporary matter vanishes when Duration ends. It cannot leave value, supply permanent crafting, or create PP, IP, Hero Points, components, anchors, or prices. Complex objects still require relevant Craft, Engineering, or Lore.

A created hazard deals fixed damage equal to Intensity per round. It cannot appear overlapping an unwilling subject; drowning, suffocation, restraint, and immediate damage are separate outcomes. Mundane hazards it causes then use core rules.

Indefinite Shaping commits full Magnitude from current and available maximum PP, even on a Critical; the reduced maximum governs recovery. Leave at least 1 maximum PP unless helplessness until external Dispel is a mythic price. Ending restores maximum PP; current PP recover normally. Others cannot commit it.

## 7. Universal Magical Actions

These use the Shaper's Practice and Tell but require no cell.

### Sense Magic

Spend a Combat Action and 1 PP to test Shaping for active magic within 15 metres. Success reveals location and Form; Critical adds Technique, Magnitude, and whether it is concentrated, triggered, or anchored. Failure reveals nothing. A fumble gives a false impression and conspicuous Tell, not Backlash. Veiled magic opposes with its original result.

### Counter

After perceiving a successful Shaping cast or arriving within 15 metres, spend a Reaction before defence or effect. Current PP must at least equal its Magnitude. Oppose Shaping with the original result; Counter must succeed and win. Spend full Magnitude either way; a fumble also causes Backlash at that Magnitude. Only one Counter may answer it, and Counter cannot be Countered.

### Dispel

Dispel Magnitude equals the effect's full Magnitude plus Range to it; Reach is 0 for one effect regardless of area. Test and pay normally. On Success or Critical, oppose the result with the original Shaper's current unmodified skill. If unknown, use `10 × original Magnitude`, capped at 100%. The dispeller must succeed and win.

Completed permanent transformations cannot be Dispelled; active anchored enchantments can.

## 8. Rituals

Rituals are mandatory above normal Safe + 2, or for Intensity 5, Range 4+, Reach 3+, or Duration 3+. A ritual:

- takes one hour per Magnitude;
- needs a prepared place, full Practice, and fitting focus or offering;
- grants `+1B`; and
- allows up to `max(Safe Magnitude + 2, 2 × Safe Magnitude)`.

Beyond this maximum is a unique campaign event requiring exceptional preparation, Gamemaster approval, and a serious price.

| Magnitude compared with Safe | Base modifier |
|---|---:|
| No greater | None |
| 1–2 higher | `-1P` |
| 3 or more higher | `-2P` |

Then apply ritual `+1B`, normal Assistance, circumstances, and the dice cap. A ritual above Safe counts as overreach on failure.

One capable assistant may Assist. Pledged PP must total at least full Magnitude and the leader contributes at least 1. Draw costs in the declared order; a Critical leaves unused pledges unspent. For an indefinite effect, the leader personally commits full Magnitude.

Permanent creation needs equivalent material. A permanent transformation becomes real and no longer counts as active magic. A permanent enchantment needs a vulnerable anchor and remains active, counted, and Dispellable unless its lasting price severs it from the creator.

Any dial at 5 makes the ritual **Mythic**. Declare a lasting price: permanent POW, a unique treasure, binding oath, supernatural debt, permanent change, or consequential quest. Pay material and permanent prices when the ritual begins; oaths, debts, quests, and success-dependent changes attach on success. A fumble exacts them only if announced beforehand. PP alone cannot restore the dead or rewrite the setting. Rituals cannot create their own costs.

## 9. Examples

Builds abbreviate Intensity as I, Range as R, and Duration as D.

| Intent | Build | M | Result |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `2D6` firebolt within 15 m | Unmake·Fire; I2, R1 | 3 | Dodge `-1P`; fire protection applies. |
| `2D6` frost burst, 3 m radius at 15 m | Unmake·Water/Ice; I2, R1, Reach1 | 4 | All subjects, including allies, may Dodge. |
| Scene-long 3 m stone wall | Conjure·Earth; I2, D1, Reach1 | 4 | AP 2, 10 HP total. |
| Telekinetic disarm within 15 m | Wield·Force; I2, R1 | 3 | Target may Dodge; no damage. |
| `2D6` internal wither within 15 m | Unmake·Flesh; I2, R1, Piercing | 4 | Opposed by Resilience; ignores mundane armour. |
| Treat a Major Wound by touch | Shift·Flesh; I3 | 3 | Restore 3 HP, stabilise, and count as Surgery. |
| 3 AP stoneskin for a scene | Ward·Earth; I3, D1 | 4 | Use it or worn armour, whichever is better. |
| Read nearby surface thoughts for a scene | Scry·Mind; I3, R1, D1 | 5 | Opposed by Persistence; each Action maintains it. |
| Competent battle spirit for a scene | Conjure·Spirit; I3, D1 | 4 | 10 HP, 2 AP, 55%, `1D8`; requires commands. |
| Permanently return a willing soul | Shift·Spirit; I5, D5 | 10 | Master mythic ritual and lasting price. |

At Shaping 50%, Safe Magnitude is 2: firebolt is `-1P`, frost burst is `-2P`, and anything larger exceeds the normal ritual maximum and needs a unique campaign event.

## 10. Quick Reference

1. Choose a known cell and state one outcome.
2. Add Intensity, Range, Duration, Reach, and adjustments.
3. Confirm Practice, Tell, target, route, defence, armour, PP, and active capacity.
4. Apply overreach, spend one Combat Action, and test Shaping.
5. Resolve Counter and one defence; pay PP; apply and record the effect.

Price outcomes, not descriptions. Magic may rival specialists through PP and risk, but should not erase skills, equipment, or preparation.
